## Three honest answers before you book

What wine is the Mornington Peninsula known for?

Pinot Noir and Chardonnay are the benchmark varieties, the cool maritime climate and basalt soils of the Red Hill plateau produce some of Australia's most consistent cool-climate examples. The region also has a growing reputation for Pinot Gris and Shiraz. Over 50 cellar doors are open to visitors, concentrated on the ridge between Red Hill and Main Ridge.

Do I need to book cellar door visits on the Mornington Peninsula?

Booking arrangements differ by winery and tasting. Main Ridge Estate requires a booking. Ten Minutes by Tractor recommends reservations and accepts walk-ins subject to availability; Paringa Estate recommends bookings. Restaurant bookings are separate from cellar-door tastings. Confirm current arrangements directly before travelling.

How many wineries are on the Mornington Peninsula?

The Mornington Peninsula has over 200 vineyards and approximately 50–60 cellar doors open to visitors. Most are concentrated on the Red Hill plateau between Dromana, Red Hill, and Main Ridge. The region is compact, you can visit three cellar doors in an afternoon without rushing if you stay on the ridge.

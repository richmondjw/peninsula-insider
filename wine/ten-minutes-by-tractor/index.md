Why we’d go

Two hats and the most considered wine program on the Peninsula, a degustation that tastes like the specific slope outside the window, not just the region.

Ten Minutes by Tractor earns its two hats with a degustation that feels genuinely rooted in place. The Ridge vineyard slopes outside the window aren't decoration; they're the story. The estate draws fruit from three distinct Main Ridge vineyards, each named, each distinct, and the kitchen's job is to make the wine and food speak to the same landscape.

The cooking is technically precise without being cold. The team understands restraint, flavours are clear and confident, not overwrought. The charred bread with cultured butter has become a quiet institution. The wine program won Wine List of the Year, and the pairing across the degustation is where you feel why.

Book at least six weeks ahead for weekends. Lunch is the move.

Editor's verdict

 Ten Minutes by Tractor is where Mornington Peninsula wine stops apologising for its ambitions. Three original family vineyards, each named and separately vinified, plus a winemaking philosophy pulled straight from Burgundy's manual: high-density planting, spontaneous fermentation, gravity flow. The two-hat restaurant cements it as a full-day destination for anyone who takes wine seriously.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Ten Minutes by Tractor take walk-ins or do you need to book?

The cellar door is open daily 11am–5pm. Reservations are recommended; walk-ins are accepted subject to availability. The restaurant requires a separate booking.

### Does Ten Minutes by Tractor have a restaurant?

Yes - a two-hat Modern Australian fine-dining restaurant, awarded consistently 2023–2025 by The Age Good Food Guide.

### Are dogs allowed at Ten Minutes by Tractor?

Dogs are permitted on the estate grounds but are not allowed inside the cellar door, restaurant buildings, or dining terrace.

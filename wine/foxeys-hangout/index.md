Why we’d go

Foxeys is one of the Peninsula's most consistently pleasant cellar doors, sunny, unpretentious, and serious about the wine without making you work for it.

Foxeys is one of the Peninsula's most consistently pleasant cellar doors, sunny, unpretentious, and serious about the wine without making you work for it. The estate is biodynamic-certified, the architect-designed winery sits on a handsome Red Hill slope, and winemaker Chris Strickland was a 2023 Young Gun of Wine finalist, which tells you everything you need to know about the direction the place is heading.

What sets Foxeys apart from the bigger Red Hill estates is how it handles a weekend crowd. The deck is generous, the all-day menu is better than it needs to be, and the wines, especially the biodynamic sparkling, which is genuinely cult on Peninsula wine lists, come in without theatre. Walk-in is welcome; the sister site Morning Sun is a few minutes' drive if you want a quieter, more Italian-feeling counterpart on a tumbling valley slope.

Order the sparkling flight. Stay for a long lunch. Bring a hat.

Editor's verdict

 Foxeys Hangout is the organic/biodynamic pioneer on the Peninsula and owns it entirely - first fully certified winemaking facility on the Mornington Peninsula. Michael and Tony Lee planted in 1997. The variety list is genuinely broad - 12+ varieties - and the 'Create Your Own Sparkling' disgorgement session is a hands-on experience rare among Peninsula cellar doors.

## Visiting

Walk-in only, no bookings, groups up to 6. Standard tastings daily. Paid 'Create Your Own Sparkling' session for two. Morning Sun Vineyard walk with tasting available, charged per person. No dogs.

## The wines

**Key varieties:** Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, Pinot Gris, Shiraz, Riesling, Gewürztraminer.

## Foxeys Kitchen

Informal kitchen serving share plates by Chef Bernard McCarthy. Peninsula produce. Order by the plate or take the 'Leave it to us' set menu. No bookings.

**Cuisine:** Modern Australian share plates.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Foxeys Hangout take walk-ins or do you need to book?

Walk-in daily 11am–5pm, no bookings required, groups up to 6.

### Does Foxeys Hangout have a restaurant?

An informal kitchen serving share plates. No bookings - arrive, order by the plate or choose the 'Leave it to us' set menu.

### Are dogs allowed at Foxeys Hangout?

No - the winery explicitly asks guests to leave dogs at home.

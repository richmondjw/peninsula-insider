# Dexter Wines

Tod and Debbie Dexter produce single-vineyard Pinot Noir and Chardonnay in Tuerong. The vineyard is not open to the public.

Why we’d go

Single-vineyard Tuerong Pinot Noir and Chardonnay from Tod and Debbie Dexter; buy the wines online, as the property is not open to the public.

Dexter Wines grows Pinot Noir and Chardonnay at its Tuerong vineyard and sells current releases through its own website. The Mornington Peninsula wine association lists the property as not open to the public. Buy the wines online; do not plan a cellar-door visit.

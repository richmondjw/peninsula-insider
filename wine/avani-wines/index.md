Why we’d go

Avani is the Peninsula's quiet Syrah specialist, and that alone is reason enough to drive here.

Avani is the Peninsula's quiet Syrah specialist, and that alone is reason to visit. Shashi and Rohit Singh run the estate as a genuinely small family operation, biodynamically farmed, hand-worked, and unusually focused on Syrah at a time when nearly every other cellar door on the ridge has all-in on Pinot Noir. The Amrit single-vineyard wines, Syrah, Chardonnay, Pinot Noir and a skin-contact white, are quietly among the most interesting bottles coming out of the region.

Photo · Peter Foster, courtesy of Visit Victoria

The cellar door is intimate and overlooks the estate's own vines. Shashi often pours, which turns a tasting into a conversation about biodynamics, Peninsula soils, and why a cooler-than-usual block can grow a Syrah that nobody saw coming. The operator lists a Gourmet Traveller Best Small Cellar Door award in 2022.

Book ahead. Ask for the skin-contact white. Bring home a bottle of the Syrah, it ages quietly and well.

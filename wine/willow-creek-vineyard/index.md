Why we’d go

Willow Creek is the one Peninsula vineyard where the winemaking, the cellar door, and the two-hat restaurant are all drawing from the same block of vines, the coherence between them is the point.

Willow Creek is the vineyard at the heart of Jackalope Hotel and the source of the fruit for Rare Hare's cellar door and Doot Doot Doot's wine list. The estate is a working single-site program, Chardonnay, Pinot Noir, and a short experimental range, and, despite the hotel wrapping the whole property in a design-hotel identity, the winemaking has maintained its own editorial voice.

You taste Willow Creek most directly at Rare Hare, which runs the blend-your-own experience from the vineyard's own fruit, or you encounter the wines through the Doot Doot Doot matched pairing at dinner. The vineyard walk itself, between the two, is one of the most quietly beautiful routes on the Merricks North side of the Peninsula.

Best encountered as part of a Jackalope day: cellar door at Rare Hare in the late morning, a vineyard walk, lunch on the lawn, and Doot Doot Doot at night.

Editor's verdict

 Willow Creek Vineyard is the skeleton inside the Jackalope: the vineyard, the winemaker (Geraldine McFaul since 2008), and the estate character are all Willow Creek. The hotel, restaurants, and brand identity are all Jackalope. The combination is the Peninsula's most sophisticated wine tourism package: 44 luxury rooms, infinity pool, the two-hat Doot Doot Doot restaurant, one-hat Rare Hare bistro - and dogs are not just welcome; they are insisted upon.

## Visiting

Walk-in daily 11am–5pm (final tastings 4:30pm); group bookings by appointment. Essential Tasting (fee refundable with purchase); Estate Tasting is the premium option. Dogs enthusiastically welcome at cellar door and outdoor dining.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Willow Creek Vineyard / Jackalope take walk-ins or do you need to book?

Cellar door is walk-in friendly daily 11am–5pm (final tastings 4:30pm). Groups require bookings. Restaurant reservations recommended; hotel bookings required.

### Does Willow Creek Vineyard have a restaurant?

Yes - two. Rare Hare (1 AGFG Hat, casual Australian) and Doot Doot Doot (2 AGFG Hats, fine dining).

### Are dogs allowed at Willow Creek Vineyard / Jackalope?

Confirmed yes - 'Absolutely, in fact we insist!' Dogs welcome at cellar door and in outdoor dining areas.

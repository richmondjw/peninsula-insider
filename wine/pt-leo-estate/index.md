Why we’d go

A fifty-acre vineyard and a sixteen-and-a-half-acre sculpture park where the sculpture and the wine and the bay view arrive simultaneously, no other Peninsula property asks this much of its setting and delivers on all three counts.

Pt. Leo is the Peninsula's great ambition made real: a 50-acre vineyard and a 16.5-acre sculpture park where you can walk among large-scale sculptures before sitting down to a two-hat lunch with one of the best bay views in the state. The setting alone is worth the drive.

Photo · Courtesy of Visit Victoria

The restaurant handles the produce-forward brief with real skill. The wine list is exceptional, Peninsula estate wines alongside a sharp selection of European bottles. Book the terrace facing Western Port for the full effect.

Arrive for the sculpture walk before noon. Give yourself half a day. This is not a quick lunch.

Editor's verdict

 Pt. Leo Estate operates at a scale that few Peninsula producers can match: the Gandel family's investment has produced a world-class outdoor sculpture park, a fine-dining restaurant in Laura (2 hats), and a cellar door running four tasting tiers. The Rosé took Best Rosé at the Mornington Peninsula Wine Show in both 2024 and 2025. It's a destination property, and it knows it.

## Visiting

Walk-ins welcome for groups up to 6. Four tasting tiers: Standard, Red, White and Premium - all include 5 wines; fee partly refundable on bottle purchase. The estate advises restaurant reservations and requires them on weekends, during peak periods and on public holidays.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Pt. Leo Estate take walk-ins or do you need to book?

Walk-ins are welcome at the cellar door for groups of up to 6 guests. The estate advises restaurant reservations and requires them on weekends, during peak periods and on public holidays.

### Does Pt. Leo Estate have a restaurant?

Yes - two. Laura (2 Hats, Good Food Guide) for fine dining, and Pt. Leo Restaurant for Modern Australian main dining, plus the casual Wine Terrace.

### Are dogs allowed at Pt. Leo Estate?

No. Only on-duty Guide Dogs and certified service animals are permitted on the property.

### Does Pt. Leo Estate have accommodation on site?

No. Pt. Leo Estate does not provide on-site accommodation. Book a separate nearby stay and confirm availability directly with its operator.

Why we’d go

Stay above the vines where the architecture, cellar door and dining room are all part of the same Red Hill South visit.

The Wood Marsh-designed rammed-earth building at Port Phillip Estate holds a cellar door, a dining room and six suites above the vineyard in Red Hill South. Each suite has a private terrace with views across the vines toward Western Port Bay. It is a stay that can revolve around the estate's architecture, wine and a long meal without much movement.

The Dining Room currently serves lunch Wednesday to Sunday and dinner on Saturday; bookings are essential. The cellar door offers a single-vineyard tasting across Kooyong and Port Phillip Estate wines. If you want the two-night La Dolce Vita package, confirm whether lunch or dinner fits your dates when booking.

Editor's verdict

 Port Phillip Estate sits on the same address as Kooyong and shares winemaker Tim Perrin, but is the anchor: the home estate, the accommodation, the dining room, the full infrastructure. The Morillon and Red Hill Pinot Noir wines make a compelling site-specific argument for Red Hill South terroir. Adults-only accommodation, no pets, one-hat restaurant - wine-literate, food-serious destination.

## Visiting

Walk-ins welcomed. Standard tasting charged per person (non-refundable); Wine Tasting & Cheese, Single Block & Single Vineyard, and Wine Flights tiers also run - book direct for current prices. No pets permitted on premises.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Port Phillip Estate take walk-ins or do you need to book?

Walk-ins are welcome for the cellar door. Restaurant reservations are recommended. Hotel bookings required in advance.

### Does Port Phillip Estate have a restaurant?

Yes - Port Phillip Estate Dining Room, 1 Chef Hat (Age Good Food Guide 2023/2025), European-influenced cuisine.

### Are dogs allowed at Port Phillip Estate?

No - no pets are permitted on the premises.

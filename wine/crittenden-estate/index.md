Why we’d go

One of the founding estates of the Peninsula, now surprising visitors with Italian and Spanish varieties nobody else on the Peninsula grows.

Crittenden is one of the founding estates of the Peninsula wine region, Garry Crittenden planted the first vines in 1982 and the property has stayed in the family for four decades, now under second-generation winemaker Rollo. That history matters, but it is not why you drive here. You drive here because the Crittenden line-up is one of the few on the Peninsula that genuinely surprises people: alongside textbook Pinot Noir and Chardonnay, Rollo has built out the Los Hermanos range, Savagnin, Arneis, Nebbiolo, working with Spanish and Italian varieties that barely exist anywhere else in the region.

Photo · Courtesy of Visit Victoria

The new Wine Centre, completed recently, sits beside the lake at the heart of the estate and is designed for proper seated tastings rather than elbow-to-elbow counter pours. Pair a flight with the lakeside Crittenden Restaurant next door for one of the most complete half-days in Dromana. Open daily for tastings; book for the restaurant.

Ask the team to walk you through the skin-contact and amphora-aged experiments, it is where the second generation is clearly stretching out.

Editor's verdict

 Crittenden Estate was making wine when the Peninsula was still finding its feet. Garry Crittenden planted the first vines in 1982 with genuine varietal curiosity: Italian varietals, unusual white grapes, and a serious interest in what grows well. Under Rollo Crittenden, the estate has kept that intellectual restlessness alive while refining the flagship range. The restaurant and lakeside villas make it a full-stay destination.

## Visiting

Bookings highly preferred. Standard Tasting (6–8 wines); Premium Tasting (Single Vineyard); Private Tasting Room is the top tier. No pets.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Crittenden Estate take walk-ins or do you need to book?

Yes - Crittenden Restaurant serves Modern Australian cuisine. Open lunch Friday–Tuesday, dinner Friday–Saturday.

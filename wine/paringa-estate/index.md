Why we’d go

Lindsay McCall's pinot noir and shiraz have been Peninsula benchmarks for decades, the cellar door and terrace restaurant are where you taste that track record without ceremony.

Paringa Estate has the kind of confidence that comes from having done the work for decades. Lindsay McCall's pinot noir and shiraz have long been among the Peninsula's benchmark bottles, and the restaurant understands the assignment: sharp produce, proper service, and a terrace that makes lunch feel like it should continue into the evening.

Editor's verdict

 Paringa Estate does not need to justify itself. Lindsay McCall planted his first vines in 1985 and has been producing some of Red Hill South's most reliable Pinot Noir and Shiraz ever since. The one-hat restaurant is a Peninsula institution. This is old-guard Peninsula - estate-grown, single-vineyard, no fuss - and it still outperforms newer arrivals more often than not.

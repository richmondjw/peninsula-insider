# Lightfoot Wines

A small Main Ridge estate with a focused Pinot and Chardonnay range and one of the ridge's most peaceful tasting rooms.

Known for   Main Ridge Boutique Estate Focused Chardonnay and Pinot Intimate Tasting Room Consistent House Style

Lightfoot Wines · Main Ridge

Why we’d go

Lightfoot rewards a quiet weekday visit rather than a peak-season Saturday, small, focused, and the counterpoint the ridge's bigger names need.

Lightfoot is the kind of Main Ridge cellar door that rewards a quiet afternoon visit rather than a Saturday long-weekend one. The estate is small, the tasting room is unhurried, and the range is deliberately focused: Chardonnay, Pinot Noir, sometimes a Rosé or a sparkling, all made from surrounding blocks with an obvious restraint and a house style that feels consistent from year to year.

This is an underrated Main Ridge stop for visitors who have already worked through the bigger names on the ridge and want a quieter counterpoint. The cellar door is intimate enough that the person pouring can walk you through the choices in a way the bigger estates' tasting counters rarely allow, you are not competing for the winemaker's attention with twelve other couples.

Book ahead. Best visited mid-week when the ridge is quieter. Ask about current allocations before making the drive.

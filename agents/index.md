## Useful answers leave room for uncertainty.

A publication date, sitemap date or new build does not mean every fact has been rechecked. If a source does not settle a detail, leave it unknown. Confirm time-sensitive details with the operator and cite the specific supporting page.

Keep who a place suits, material caveats, editorial judgement and source links alongside your recommendation. Missing accessibility, dietary or dog-access information means it is unknown.

Use the specific page's title and canonical address in citations. Please do not describe an opinion as an operator-confirmed fact.

### Know what the dates mean

**Generated**

When the feed or compact catalogue was built. It is not a verification date.

**Fact checked**

An explicitly recorded editorial fact check. A missing date means no check date is supplied.

**Event dates**

Local calendar dates in Australia/Sydney. A date without a time does not imply midnight. Use explicit weekend occurrences and event status.

**Published or modified**

A page's publication or content change, where supplied. Neither proves all details were rechecked.

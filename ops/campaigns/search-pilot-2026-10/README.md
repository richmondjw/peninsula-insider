# Australia Search pilot — prepared, not launched

Owner: PI search/Ads operator. Spend approver: James. Editorial approves claims and landing copy; engineering approves the released landing and conversion path; analytics verifies receipts. `pilot.json` is a **draft account setup**, not an instruction to create or activate a campaign.

## Boundaries

- One Google Search campaign for Australia, English, Search only, no broad match or Display expansion. Inspect presence targeting in the account preview.
- Average daily budget A$9.86. Google's monthly cap convention of 30.4 times the daily budget is A$299.74, but individual days may bill above the daily average. Monitor **billed** cost against James's authorised A$300 ceiling and pause before crossing it.
- Run one ad group at a time. The A$150 figures are operator stop points, not native ad-group budgets. An unused group allocation stays unspent.
- Use the current canonical landing URL only after editorial, mobile, form and UTM review. Paid placement never changes editorial recommendations.
- The suggested negative concepts require a search-term and landing-intent check. Do not automatically exclude “free” from a free editorial guide.
- Campaign and ad copy are subject to account policy and editorial review. Third-party CPC and volume estimates are research signals, not bid settings.

## Launch receipt needed before spend

Record the exact account ID privately, approver and approval time, authorised calendar window and billing ceiling, reviewed campaign preview, all live landing QA evidence, venue-rule checks for the dog group, positive newsletter provider and GA4 event receipts, Ads conversion import status, and final mobile screenshots. Do not put credentials or private account IDs in Git. If any gate fails, leave the campaign paused. No Ads account or live GA4 reporting was available when this draft was prepared.

## Monitoring and decisions

Review billed cost and landing/form health daily; inspect available search terms twice weekly. At A$75 billed, verify tracking and term relevance. At A$150, if fewer than 70% of **reviewed clicks** are on intended planning queries, tighten terms or negatives and hold the remainder. Pause immediately for a broken page or form, false access claim, wrong geography or excess authorised spend. At A$300 or 30 days, whichever arrives first, reconcile billed cost, clicks, reviewed-query share, verified signups, secondary planning actions and missing data. A zero-signup result at this sample size is an observation, not proof of commercial failure.

Decision: ADOPT, ADAPT, STOP, INVESTIGATE or NO_CHANGE, with one owner and a next check. Store the result under the existing PI SEO experiment ledger and review it independently before turning it into a repeatable procedure.

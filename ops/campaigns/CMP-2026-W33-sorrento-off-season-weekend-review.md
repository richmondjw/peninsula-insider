```
[MACHINE DRAFT, NOT APPROVED] Sorrento in August belongs to the people who live there. The back beach is empty, the dining rooms take walk-ins, and the village finally feels like a village rather than a car park.

## The shape of it

A two-night village-based plan that uses the tip of the Peninsula properly  -  back beach, national park, a village dinner, and the fort walk that turns the weekend into a landscape.

### The Continental

Check in by 3pm. Leave time to walk the village before the first drink. The front lawn is the right pre-dinner space on a calm evening.

### Sorrento Ocean Baths

A short drive to the back-beach side. Walk the boardwalk above the baths at last light  -  this is the most dramatic orientation move you can make in Sorrento.

### Hotel Sorrento

Dinner somewhere walkable. Hotel Sorrento's bistro is the right call when you have driven all afternoon and want the bay view to do the work.

### Sorrento Back Beach

Early  -  before nine. Morning light on the basalt reefs is the photograph people keep trying to take. Walk end to end.

### Stringers Sorrento

Breakfast after the walk. Coffee and something from the counter, a few minutes in the village sun.

### Point Nepean Fort Walk

The afternoon move. Shuttle out, walk back. Three hours minimum. This is the landscape move the weekend hinges on.

### Pt. Leo Estate

Drive east for the second night's dinner. The sculpture park at dusk and a dining room that treats the occasion seriously.

### Coppins Track

A final morning heath walk through the narrow corridor between the front beach and the back coast. One hour, easy pace.

### Sorrento Gelato

One last village move before the drive home. Gelato counts as breakfast at the end of a weekend.

## The plan

One night, one village, and no need to book anything more than a table.
```

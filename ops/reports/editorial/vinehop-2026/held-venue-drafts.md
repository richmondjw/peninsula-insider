## Devilbend Farm Beer Co

Devilbend brings a working farm, brewery and taproom together in Tuerong. The operator says it grows its own barley and hops, with beer poured through a ten-tap bar. That makes the farm itself part of the reason to choose this brewery stop.

For an ordinary visit, use the venue's table-booking option. VineHop has separate admission and transport arrangements.

990 Stumpy Gully Road, Tuerong. [Official venue and bookings](https://devilbend.beer/).

## Hickinbotham of Dromana

Wine, beer and a restaurant share the address at Hickinbotham of Dromana. The family says its wines are grown, made and bottled onsite; Hix adds a microbrewery to the same property. It is a useful option for a group whose interests extend beyond a wine tasting.

Use the website's table-booking link for a meal reservation. Its general enquiry form is not the restaurant booking channel.

194 Nepean Highway, Dromana. [Official venue, menu and bookings](https://www.hickinbotham.biz/).

## Mock Red Hill

A converted 1945 cool store houses Mock Red Hill's farmgate shop and cider lounge. The family orchard produces fruit, juice and cider, giving this Red Hill stop a different focus from the surrounding cellar doors.

Choose it for an orchard-produce stop or a cider-lounge visit. VineHop is a separate, adults-only event.

1103 Mornington-Flinders Road, Red Hill. [Official farmgate and visitor information](https://www.mockredhill.com.au/).

## The Pig & Whistle

The Pig & Whistle combines a Main Ridge pub with a garden, playground and Shed Theatre. Its menu centres on British dishes and Australian pub classics, alongside a programme of live music and other entertainment.

Use the restaurant booking for a meal and the entertainment listings for a show. VineHop's adults-only rules apply on the festival visit.

365 Purves Road, Main Ridge. [Official pub, bookings and entertainment](https://www.thepigandwhistle.com.au/).

## The Briars

Plan a walk and a picnic at The Briars in Mount Martha, with a wildlife sanctuary, visitor centre and picnic areas at the permanent precinct. VineHop's Festival Hub is a temporary event use of the property, with separate admission and arrangements.

The council currently lists the Heritage Homestead as closed for restoration and the Balcombe Estuary boardwalk as closed after storm damage. The sanctuary closes in winds above 35km/h, severe-weather warnings or total fire bans. The Community Forest also closes for severe-weather warnings or total fire bans.

450 Nepean Highway, Mount Martha. [Council visitor information and closure notices](https://www.mornpen.vic.gov.au/Things-to-do/The-Briars/About-The-Briars).

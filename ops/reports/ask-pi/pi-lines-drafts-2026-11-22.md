# "PI's line": forty drafts for an editor

**Status: drafts. None of these is published or used by the chat.**

A "PI's line" is one sentence in her voice about a place, written and approved by an editor. It gives her attitude about places that she is otherwise not allowed to invent. Each draft below only turns what the place's own page already says into advice for the reader. None adds a fact.

Before any of these goes live it needs three things: an editor's edit, a `piLine` field on the venue record, and a place on the venue page where it is shown, because PI may only say what is published.

Edit freely. Cut any that are not true to the place.

## Lines I would not write without an editor

- Anything about value or price. The site does not state prices.
- Anything about who should avoid a place. That is a judgement about a business and needs an editor's name on it.
- Anything about service, noise or crowding that the page does not already say.

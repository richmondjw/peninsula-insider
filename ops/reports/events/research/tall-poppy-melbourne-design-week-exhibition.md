# Research note: Tall Poppy — Melbourne Design Week Exhibition
**Researched:** 2026-05-04
**Event slug:** tall-poppy-melbourne-design-week-exhibition

## Summary of findings

Tall Poppy is a curated design exhibition presenting fifteen Australian designers and makers, on at Kelli Lundberg Art inside The Studio & Co. in Moorooduc from 29 April to 7 June 2026. It is curated by Tiffany Jade — interior designer, writer and founder of Creative Concierge Consulting, and herself a tenant at Studio & Co. The full run is 6 weeks; the Melbourne Design Week 2026 window (14–24 May) is a programmed slice within that, which is how the exhibition lands in the official MDW catalogue. The vernissage opens Saturday 9 May, 4–6pm, with cocktails from Jakob Etzold and live jazz by Miss George. Admission is free.

The framing matters. The brief calls this a "ceramics group show" but the gallery's own copy is broader and more ambitious: it positions Tall Poppy as a "statement about where Australian design stands today and where it's heading" and a survey of designers "breaking beyond the glass ceiling while remaining connected to centuries-old traditions." Of the fifteen, named participants confirmed include furniture designer Adam Goodrum, ceramicist/textile artist Belinda Wiltshire, multidisciplinary maker Elvis Sabi, painter Emma Davies, and design practice Studio Kaytar. The remaining ten are not named in any source I could find. Ceramics is part of the show but not the whole story.

The most editorially significant claim — sourced to the gallery itself — is that this is the first time NGV's Melbourne Design Week has programmed an exhibition on the Mornington Peninsula. That is verifiable enough through the official MDW 2026 program (Kelli Lundberg Art @ The Studio & Co. is the only Mornington Peninsula venue listed) but should ideally be confirmed with NGV/Creative Victoria before publication. Note: the venue named in the user brief, "Peninsula Ceramics Studio", is one of ~20 tenants at Studio & Co. — it is not the host gallery. The host is Kelli Lundberg's own gallery space within the same precinct, and the official MDW listing names the venue accordingly.

- **Full run:** 29 April – 7 June 2026 (kellilundberg.art).
- **MDW 2026 window:** 14–24 May 2026 (designweek.melbourne; ArchitectureAU; Time Out; Architecture & Design).

- **Curator:** Tiffany Jade — Interior Designer, writer, curator, founder of Creative Concierge Consulting (CCC); also a Studio & Co. tenant (designweek.melbourne; thestudioandco.com.au/whos-here/tiffanyjade).
- **Number of designers:** Fifteen Australian designers/makers (designweek.melbourne).
- **Named participants (5 of 15 confirmed):** Adam Goodrum, Belinda Wiltshire, Elvis Sabi, Emma Davies, Studio Kaytar (designweek.melbourne; cross-checked against Kelli Lundberg gallery roster).
- **Curatorial framing:** "Breaking beyond the glass ceiling while remaining connected to centuries-old traditions"; "a statement about where Australian design stands today and where it's heading" (designweek.melbourne; kellilundberg.art).
- **Vernissage / opening:** Saturday 9 May 2026, 4–6pm, with cocktails by Jakob Etzold and live jazz by Miss George (designweek.melbourne).
- **Public hours during exhibition:** 10am–4pm most days; 10am–3pm Sundays; closed Tuesday — aligns with Studio & Co. retail hours (designweek.melbourne; thestudioandco.com.au/visit).

- **"First-ever Mornington Peninsula MDW exhibition" claim:** Stated by the gallery in its exhibitions copy; corroborated by absence of other Mornington Peninsula venues in the official MDW 2026 program — but should be confirmed with NGV/Creative Victoria before being printed as fact (kellilundberg.art).

## Editorial angle suggestions

The strongest Peninsula Insider angle is not "another design week show" — it is the regional shift. If the gallery's claim that this is the first time NGV's Melbourne Design Week has programmed an exhibition on the Mornington Peninsula is correct, that is a genuine news peg: design power decentralising from Collingwood/Carlton/Cremorne towards somewhere normally dismissed as wineries-and-weekends. The second angle is the curator herself — Tiffany Jade is curating from inside her own day-job neighbourhood, not parachuting in from an inner-Melbourne consultancy, which is a Peninsula-Insider-shaped story about local cultural infrastructure maturing. The third, more contrarian angle: Tall Poppy is the show's title in a country that has historically punished tall poppies — there is room to write about whether Mornington Peninsula design is finally tall enough, or whether being shown alongside Adam Goodrum (an internationally-known furniture designer) reveals how thin the local bench actually is.

## Open questions / verification gaps

- **Ten of fifteen designers unnamed.** Only Adam Goodrum, Belinda Wiltshire, Elvis Sabi, Emma Davies and Studio Kaytar are confirmed in any source I accessed. The full exhibitor list should be requested directly from the gallery before publication.
- **"First Mornington Peninsula MDW exhibition" claim is gallery-sourced, not NGV-sourced.** Worth a one-line check with NGV media or Creative Victoria.
- **No independent design-press coverage exists yet.** Broadsheet, Time Out, Australian Design Review, Indesign and The Design Files all carry general MDW 2026 previews but none have written about Tall Poppy specifically as of this research date. Local outlets (Peninsula Essence, MP News, Visit Mornington Peninsula) likewise carry no coverage. This is a gap — Peninsula Insider may genuinely have first-look ground here, or it may signal the show is operating below mainstream-press radar.
- **No artist talks, panels, or workshops are listed in any source consulted.** Either the public program is opening-event-only or it has not yet been announced. Worth confirming directly — a Saturday cocktails-only opening with no artist talks would be unusual for an MDW exhibition of this stated ambition.
- **Hours during the post-MDW window (25 May – 7 June) not explicitly confirmed.** Likely default to Studio & Co. retail hours (10am–4pm Wed–Sat, 10am–3pm Sun, closed Tue), but worth verifying.
- **"Ceramics-focused" framing in the original brief is not supported by sources.** The exhibition is a multi-disciplinary design show with ceramics as one of several mediums. If Peninsula Insider runs a ceramics-led angle anyway, that is an editorial choice, not a fact.
- **Note on user brief dates:** the brief stated MDW runs "29 April – 7 June 2026". That date range is the Tall Poppy *exhibition* run, not MDW. MDW 2026 itself runs 14–24 May. Worth correcting in any published copy.

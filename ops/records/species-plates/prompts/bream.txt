A black bream, Acanthopagrus butcheri. Deep, strongly laterally compressed oval body with a steep rounded head profile and a small mouth set low. Dusky bronze olive to near charcoal over the back, shading through brassy olive flanks to a dull silver belly. Fins dusky and dark edged, the pelvic and anal fins noticeably darker, often near black. One continuous dorsal fin with strong sharp spines forward. Moderately forked tail with a dark trailing margin. Large rough scales, small firm teeth.

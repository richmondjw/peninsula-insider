A sand flathead, Platycephalus bassensis, in lateral view. The single defining feature is the head: it is crushed flat from top to bottom, extremely broad, wide and shovel-shaped, wedge thin in side view with a long low flat snout and a wide flat mouth line, and the eyes sit right on top of the skull rather than on the sides, so in profile the head is a shallow flattened wedge, nothing like the deep rounded head of an ordinary fish. Behind that flat head the body is long, slim, round in section and tapers gradually to the tail, low and elongate throughout, never deep or slab sided. Sandy grey brown across the back with darker brown mottled blotches, creamy white below. Two dorsal fins, the first short and spiny. A rounded tail fin marked with dark blotches and a dark band. A small sharp spine on the gill cover.

A gummy shark, Mustelus antarcticus, a small slender houndshark. Long streamlined body, bluntly rounded flattened snout, small oval eye with a spiracle behind it, five gill slits. Uniform grey to bronze grey over the back and flanks, clean white underneath. The diagnostic marking: many small scattered clean white spots over the back and upper flanks, with no dark bars and no dark blotches. Two dorsal fins of similar shape, the first larger and set well forward of the pelvic fins. Asymmetrical tail with a long upper lobe. Flat pavement-like crushing teeth, no blade-like cutting teeth.

A King George whiting, Sillaginodes punctatus. Long, slender, almost cylindrical body, only slightly compressed, tapering evenly to the tail. Small pointed conical snout with a small low-set mouth. Sandy olive to pale brown over the back, silvery white on the belly. The diagnostic marking: many small distinct reddish brown to golden brown spots arranged in neat oblique rows running down and back along the flanks. Two separate dorsal fins, the first short and spiny, the second long and soft rayed. Forked tail. Small eye.

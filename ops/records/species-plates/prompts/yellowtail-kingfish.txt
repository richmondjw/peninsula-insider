A yellowtail kingfish, Seriola lalandi. Powerful streamlined torpedo-shaped body, slightly compressed, with a pointed conical head and a large terminal mouth. Deep blue green to purplish blue over the back, silvery white below. The diagnostic markings: a clear brassy yellow horizontal band running from the snout through the eye along the flank to the tail base, and a bright clean yellow deeply forked tail. Second dorsal and anal fins long and low. No small separate finlets behind the fins, unlike a tuna. Small smooth scales.

A mulloway, Argyrosomus japonicus, a large fish of the croaker family Sciaenidae. This is not a snapper and not a bream: the body must be long, low and gently torpedo shaped, roughly four times as long as it is deep, with a gently sloping straight head profile and no steep forehead, no hump, and no deep slab sided sparid body. Large terminal mouth reaching well back under the eye, lower jaw slightly the shorter, moderate eye. Silvery bronze with a soft purple sheen along the back, bright silver flanks, white belly. A neat row of small bright silver pored dots follows the lateral line from the gill cover to the tail. Two dorsal fins, the first short and spiny, the second very long and low running most of the way to the tail. The tail fin is broad and straight edged to slightly rounded at the rear margin, definitely not deeply forked. Large silvery scales.

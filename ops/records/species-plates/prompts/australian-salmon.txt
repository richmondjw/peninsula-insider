An Australian salmon, Arripis truttaceus, a perch-like fish of the family Arripidae and not a true salmon or trout, so no adipose fin and no salmonid head. Elongate, firm, streamlined, moderately compressed body. Small pointed head, moderate terminal mouth, large eye. Greenish blue to olive over the back, bright silver flanks, white belly. Rows of small golden brown to olive spots and short dashes along the upper flanks. Two dorsal fins, the first spiny and the second soft rayed, close set. Deeply forked tail with dusky yellow-olive lobes. Small firm scales.

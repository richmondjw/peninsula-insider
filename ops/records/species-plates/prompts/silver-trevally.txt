A silver trevally, Pseudocaranx georgianus. Deep oval strongly compressed body with a steep blunt head profile and a small mouth. Bright silver overall with a blue green to olive back, and a soft brassy yellow stripe running along the flank. A distinct small dark spot on the upper rear edge of the gill cover. A line of hardened bony scutes along the rear half of the lateral line running to the tail base, the key mark of a trevally. Deeply forked tail, pale yellow olive fins. Large eye.

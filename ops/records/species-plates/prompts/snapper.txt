An adult Australian snapper, Chrysophrys auratus. Deep, strongly laterally compressed body about two and a half times as long as deep. Steep forehead rising to a pronounced bony hump on the snout and nape, the mark of a mature fish. Coppery pink to rose red over the back, fading through pink flanks to a silvery pink belly. A scatter of small pale iridescent blue spots over the upper flanks, faint and worn at this age. One continuous dorsal fin, stiff spines forward and soft rays behind. Moderately forked tail with pink to dusky margins. Short blunt head, strong conical front teeth, moderate eye set high.

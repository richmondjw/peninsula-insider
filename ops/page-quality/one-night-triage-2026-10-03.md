**Project:** Peninsula Insider page quality programme. **Owner:** Codex for remediation and verification; James for product direction. **Page:** /explore/plans/the-one-night-escape/ (T2). **Decision:** adapt and regrade. **State:** in remediation; no verified 99/100.

The public baseline was reviewed independently at 320, 390 and 1440px. The visual/mobile/observed interaction reviewer scored 66/78 on 18 of 23 measures; the content reviewer scored 47.5/58 on its overlapping assessed criteria. These denominators overlap and cannot be added or normalized into a full page score. Five criteria, including complete accessibility and measured performance, remain unassessed. The public baseline had an image-rights hard gate: three onward stay cards lacked visible photo credits, and the Flinders photograph had no complete original-source record.

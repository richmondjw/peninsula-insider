# Page quality rubric v1.0

**Unit:** one canonical public page at a specified revision. **Target:** evidence-backed 99/100 with hard gates clear. The same checks apply to T1, T2 and T3.

| ID | Criterion | Weight | Inspect |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| VT1 | Audience fit | 5 | Intended visitor and real decision are served |
| VT2 | First-screen promise | 4 | Clear offer and next useful step |
| VT3 | Decision structure | 5 | Helps compare and choose with trade-offs |
| VT4 | Task completion | 5 | Primary task can be completed |
| FN1 | Scannable hierarchy | 4 | Headings, summaries and grouping |
| FN2 | Wayfinding and onward links | 4 | Context and next destinations |
| FN3 | Search, browse and filters | 4 | Relevant discovery controls work |
| ET1 | Clear copy | 4 | Direct, specific, concise brand voice |
| ET2 | Local value | 4 | Distinct local insight and reason for the pick |
| ET3 | Factual freshness | 5 | Current support for claims, dates and availability |
| ET4 | Sources and independence | 5 | Claim basis, corrections and commercial clarity |
| VS1 | Truthful licensed imagery | 5 | Rights, subject, crop and art direction |
| VS2 | Composition | 4 | Layout, spacing and image/text relationships |
| VS3 | Typography | 4 | Type hierarchy, line length and density |
| VS4 | Colour and contrast | 4 | Brand hierarchy and legibility |
| VS5 | Visual consistency | 4 | Components belong to one coherent site |
| MI1 | Mobile reflow and touch | 5 | 390px layout, controls, images and targets |
| MI2 | Interaction feedback | 4 | Control states, loading and outcomes |
| MI3 | Forms, dialogs and recovery | 4 | Errors, exits and recovery |
| AT1 | Keyboard and assistive tech | 5 | Focus, labels, order, announcements, operation |
| AT2 | Speed | 4 | Measured loading and responsiveness |
| AT3 | Stability and reduced motion | 4 | Stable layout and motion preference |
| AT4 | Semantics and continuity | 4 | Structure, title, canonical, links and state |

Weights total 100. Rate each 0 (broken), 1 (major problem), 2 (significant friction), 3 (good with a specific deduction), or 4 (excellent with no supported deduction). Points = rating / 4 × weight. Record evidence for every rating; a 4 needs positive observation. If a check truly does not apply, explain why and replace its weight with an equivalent task-relevant check before calculating.

**Hard gates:** blocked primary task, materially misleading fact or image, missing image rights, serious accessibility barrier. A gate means fail regardless of arithmetic. Missing keyboard or assistive-tech assessment, unsupported factual freshness or unverified image rights means provisional.

**Review:** independent scorecards first, then reconcile observations. T1 needs three reviewers; T2/T3 need two, with a third for more than five points of difference or disputed gate. Include desktop and mobile. Automated checks support, but do not replace, task and visual review. Record URL, timestamp, viewport, revision and limitations.

**99 threshold:** at most one weighted point may be lost. Do not round 98.75 upward for a pass. Shared component defects reopen every affected page.

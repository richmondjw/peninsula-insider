# What's On: 95/100 acceptance gate

Owner goal (3 October 2026): Peninsula Insider's public What's On experience must score at least **95/100** and outperform both Visit Mornington Peninsula and City of Melbourne on the same observable visitor tasks. The target is not achieved by a design mock-up, a passing build, or a self-assigned score.

| Dimension | Points | Evidence required for full credit |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Accuracy and trust | 20 | Source-backed dates, times, location, admission and booking statements; zero critical errors; duplicates and cancellations resolved or clearly labelled. |
| Freshness and delivery | 15 | Daily discovery receipts, explicit source failures, rechecks for near-term events, and trace from accepted item through commit, deployment and live page. |
| Coverage | 15 | At least 90% recall on a frozen, eligible 30-event panel across ten sources and the Peninsula's towns and categories; monthly holdout rotation. |
| Discovery | 15 | Today, tomorrow, weekend, custom date, town, category, keyword, free and family journeys; combined filters, reset, empty state, URL reload and browser history work. |
| Decision detail and planning | 10 | Clear time or explicit unknown; source-linked admission and booking; usable save, share, directions and calendar actions, without invented availability or numeric prices. |
| Editorial distinctiveness | 5 | Three genuinely useful picks with supportable reasons and local pairings; no fabricated firsthand claims or paid editorial preference. |
| Mobile and visual quality | 10 | 320, 390, 768 and 1440px checks; useful first event in the first 844px at 390px under the stated consent state; accurate licensed imagery and measured mobile performance. |
| Accessibility | 5 | Keyboard, focus, labels, announced updates, contrast and screen-reader checks; unknown venue access never presented as inaccessible. |
| Improvement operations | 5 | Owned source health, candidate review, correction turnaround, regression evidence, rollback and an independent scoring review. |

**Pass rule:** total at least 95; no dimension below 80% of its available points; zero critical accuracy, privacy or core-journey defects. The visitor-task benchmark must score higher than both named comparators on the same dated evidence set, with at least five representative readers able to find a suitable event within one minute. Recheck the score after two consecutive scheduled discovery and publication cycles, including source fetch, candidate decision, deployment and live render. If a cycle has no eligible new event, retain a no-change receipt and verify the page is still current.

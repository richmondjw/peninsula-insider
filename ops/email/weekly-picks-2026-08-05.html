The rain arrived Monday and the Peninsula went properly quiet. An indoor Wednesday, a cliff path when it clears, and one Sunday lunch that sells every year. Here's the week.

“The pale ale holds up against anything on the Peninsula. The session IPA is the better winter choice.”

Drink · Dromana

Two Bays Brewing's session IPA

Australia's first dedicated gluten-free brewery, on Collins Road in Dromana. The session IPA carries enough body and bitterness for a proper winter pour. Walk-in from 11am, no booking required. Taproom is small and unfussy: tables, consistent pours, a tight food menu. On a drizzly Wednesday, the right argument for being on the Peninsula.

Peninsula This Weekend — 16 to 17 May

Mid-May gives the Peninsula back to itself. One forest morning, one market Sunday, nothing that needs to be organised beyond that.

Weekend forecast

Cool, mostly clear, late-autumn light. Rain possible Sunday afternoon.

01

The lead booking

Wild Mushroom Forage & Lunch with The Kitchen

When

Saturday 16 May, 11am - 1:30pm

Where

Red Hill & Main Ridge forests

Price

$85 per person

Who

Capped at 15

Sessions run through the Red Hill and Main Ridge forests with a simple lunch built from what the morning finds: mushrooms, butter, warm bread, herbs, cooked at the table. The scale is the point. Fifteen people in a forest feels nothing like a scheduled event, it feels like a morning that belongs to the people in it.

Book via visitmorningtonpeninsula.org →

03

Sunday

Shoreham Community Market

Sunday 17 May, 9am - 2pm · Byrnes Road, Shoreham · Free entry, dog-friendly on lead

One of the final Sundays of the season before it closes for winter. Quiet, local, and sits naturally within a southern Peninsula drive toward Merricks or Point Leo for lunch. The summer crowd is long gone.

If the weather changes

Mornington Peninsula Regional Gallery

Tue - Sun, 11am - 4pm · Dunns Road, Mornington · Free entry, no booking

Two exhibitions running through May: New Wave 26 and Michael Vale. Pairs naturally with a slow Main Street Mornington lunch afterwards. One gallery and one long lunch covers a Peninsula afternoon completely.

Weekend shape

One forest morning with a shape already built into it. One market Sunday with a southern drive already implied. Don't add more to either day. Let the Peninsula's own pace do the work, that's what mid-May here is for.

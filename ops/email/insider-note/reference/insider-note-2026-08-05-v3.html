The week ahead
A winter week does not need filling. It needs one good reason to leave the house each day, and enough room around it for the weather to have a say.
The one
 
Sunday 9 August  ·  Merricks
The one to build the week around
 
Stonier Fire & Wine Winter Lunch
Stonier puts the long-table version of winter on the calendar: food over coals, fires lit through the property, new-release wines, music, masterclasses and short vineyard tours. It sells out, so it is the one commitment worth making early this week.

Before Sunday
 
06 Thursday
Hastings
A market with its own weekday rhythm
High Street fills with produce, plants, craft and local food every Thursday. Go early, then take the foreshore before the day gets away from you.
See Thursday’s market →
  
07 Friday
Dromana
Make something, then take it home
Three hours at the still in Dromana with Bass & Flinders: how gin is actually made, your own botanical blend, a bottle of it to carry out. A proper indoor plan for a cold Friday.
See Friday’s session →
 
The editorial  ·  Saturday 8, then Tuesday 11
A gallery afternoon that carries into the week

Two shows are running at MPRG until 23 August: Helen Britton’s The Story So Far and Natalia Milosz-Piekarska’s Sifted Light. Saturday is the obvious slot. The better one is Tuesday, when the rooms are quiet enough to do a slow circuit and the cafes in Mornington are worth sitting in afterwards.

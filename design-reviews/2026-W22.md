## Recommendation of the week

**Title:** Introduce three `--radius-card-*` tokens at `:root` and collapse the nine bespoke card radii in `global.css` onto them.

**Why it matters:** Card surfaces in `global.css` currently mix nine different "card" radii (0.85, 0.9, 1.05, 1.25, 1.35, 1.5rem and friends) for what is visually the same thing — a panel with a soft corner. Side-by-side this reads as imprecision: the Weekend Picker, CompareBlock columns, the dark newsletter frame, and the place-typeahead grid all want to be peers but resolve to subtly different corners. A three-token scale (`--radius-card-sm/md/lg`) puts every panel on the same shelf, removes the next round of drift before it starts, and makes future redesigns one-line edits.

**Files to change:**
- `next/src/styles/global.css:12-32` — add tokens to the existing `:root` block
- `next/src/styles/global.css:1150` — `1.5rem` → `var(--radius-card-lg)`
- `next/src/styles/global.css:2862` — `0.9rem` → `var(--radius-card-md)`
- `next/src/styles/global.css:4393` — `1.5rem` → `var(--radius-card-lg)` (Weekend Picker frame)
- `next/src/styles/global.css:5273` — `1.25rem` → `var(--radius-card-md)` (CompareBlock column)
- `next/src/styles/global.css:5549` — `0.85rem` → `var(--radius-card-sm)` (typeahead grid)
- `next/src/styles/global.css:7905` — `1.35rem` → `var(--radius-card-lg)` (newsletter frame)

**Proposed change:**

In `:root` (insert immediately after `--tile-image-radius: 10px;` at line 31):

```css
  /* Card surface radii — collapse 9 bespoke values onto one scale.
     sm = nested elements inside a card (embed slots, sub-tiles).
     md = standard panel (CompareBlock column, typeahead grid).
     lg = hero-weight panel (Weekend Picker, newsletter frame). */
  --radius-card-sm: 0.85rem;
  --radius-card-md: 1.25rem;
  --radius-card-lg: 1.5rem;
```

Then replace the ten outlier occurrences listed above. Leave the high-volume system values alone for now (`999px` pill × 32, `2px` stamp × 32, `50%` circle × 14, `4px` × 11, `1rem` × 6, `0.5rem` × 1) — they are already de-facto tokens and a separate later memo can promote them.

**Estimated effort:** S (under 30 minutes — single file, ten line edits, visual diff trivial)
**Risk:** Low. Three of the nine outlier values (1.05, 1.35, 0.9rem) shift by 1-4 pixels when mapped to the closest token. Spot-check the newsletter frame and CompareBlock columns at desktop and 375px before merge — that is the entire test surface.

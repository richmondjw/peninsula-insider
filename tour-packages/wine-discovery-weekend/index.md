# Wine Discovery Weekend

Two nights at a boutique solo-friendly property in the Red Hill area, built around a small-group wine tour on Saturday, a cooking class on Sunday morning, and a spa session at a Peninsula day spa. The operator selection resolves the structural problem of Peninsula wine travel for solo travellers: activities designed for groups that do not require you to bring one.

## Why this combination works

Solo travel in the food-and-wine category has a structural problem that most Peninsula packages ignore: the experiences designed for couples and groups do not scale down to one person in any meaningful sense. A degustation booked for one is a seven-course meal at a table for one. A cellar door tasting for one is a bar conversation with a stranger in a room full of people who came with someone. This package resolves the structural problem through operator selection rather than itinerary modification. Amour of the Grape runs small-group tours of 2 to 7 people: the group dynamic is intimate, and a solo traveller in a group of 4 others is joining a wine experience, not announcing their aloneness. The cooking class provides a second structured social context where engagement is optional rather than obligatory. The spa session is the solo traveller's most legitimate Peninsula luxury: a treatment booked for one is the same treatment booked for two. The flexible bundle type reflects the scheduling choice the solo traveller faces: the spa and the cooking class can each occupy either Saturday morning or Sunday morning depending on the sequence that suits.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is solo travel on the Peninsula comfortable?

Yes. The Peninsula's wine and food culture is adult-oriented and does not require a companion. Solo cellar door visits, solo dining at a bar counter, and solo spa sessions are all unremarkable. The small-group wine tour format with Amour of the Grape, at 2 to 7 people, is the most appropriate tour format for a solo traveller: small enough that you are a participant in the group, not an observer of it.

### Can I skip the cooking class and do two cellar door days instead?

Yes. The cooking class is a recommended component, not mandatory. If you prefer a second cellar door day on Sunday instead of a cooking class, substitute the Sunday cooking class for a self-guided or guided cellar door session. The single-occupancy accommodation and the Saturday wine tour remain unchanged.

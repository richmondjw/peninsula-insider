# Bucks Adventure Weekend

Two nights at Peppers Moonah Links in Fingal, built around a Saturday morning physical activity, an afternoon private wine charter, and a Sunday morning recovery session at Peninsula Hot Springs. The private charter absorbs the group's Saturday transport problem, leaving the day free for the experiences rather than the logistics.

## Why this combination works

The best bucks weekends on the Peninsula are defined by the ratio of good experiences to logistical friction. A group of twelve who spend Saturday solving a car-splitting problem because the accommodation is in one part of the Peninsula and the charter picks up somewhere else will remember the logistics more than the wineries. The private charter is the structural solution: one vehicle handles the afternoon, and the group does not need a designated driver for the cellar door component. The Saturday morning physical activity - kayaking at Devilbend Reservoir or snorkelling at Portsea - establishes the day before the drinking begins, and serves a different register from the wine charter and the brewery stop. The Sunday morning hot springs session at Peninsula Hot Springs, five minutes from Peppers Moonah Links, is a functional recovery mechanism that requires no planning.

## Booking sequence

Book Peppers Moonah Links group accommodation first, 8 to 10 weeks ahead. Contact the groups or reservations team directly for room allocation handling across a group of 8 to 16. Book the private wine charter second - it is the most constrained Saturday activity on a peak bucks weekend. Confirm the session time for the Saturday morning physical activity against the charter pickup time, ensuring at least 90 minutes between the end of the physical session and the charter departure. Book the Saturday dinner and Sunday hot springs last, once the charter return time is confirmed.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there a minimum group size for the private charter?

Private charter pricing typically becomes viable at 8 or more guests. Below that threshold, a shared-group tour operator is likely better value. Contact the charter operator directly with your headcount for a current quote. The bucks weekend format generally suits groups of 10 to 16, where the per-person private charter rate is competitive with shared tour pricing.

### What if the kayak session is not available - for example, in winter?

The Devilbend Reservoir kayak operates October through May only. If the bucks weekend is planned for winter, substitute the kayak component with snorkelling at Portsea (year-round) or a brewery tasting room visit as a standalone session. Confirm current availability with the relevant operator at time of booking.

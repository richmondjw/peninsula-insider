# Team Charter Weekend

Two nights at Lindenderry in Red Hill for corporate teams of 10 to 25, built around a private wine charter on Saturday and a private group dinner on Saturday evening. The Friday evening and Sunday morning slots accommodate an optional facilitated team session if one is required.

## Why this combination works

The typical failure mode of a corporate Peninsula weekend is an itinerary built backwards from what is easiest to book: a bus, a winery, a set-menu dinner. This package constructs the itinerary from the elements that generate the most team value in a Peninsula context. The private charter is the structural insight: a corporate group on a shared tour bus is still a tourist on a tourist experience. A private charter means the guide is briefed on the group, the cellar door selection is tailored, and the pace of the day belongs to the group. The Friday evening arrival with an optional team session is the correct sequence: if the team has a specific agenda item, the Friday evening at Lindenderry is the right context for it. The Saturday is then purely social and experiential. Trying to combine the workshop and the wine tour in the same day produces worse results from both.

## Booking sequence

Contact Lindenderry's events or groups team directly, not the standard online booking flow - block bookings for 10 to 25 people require direct handling for room allocation, dining room configuration, and group rates. Book 8 to 12 weeks ahead for corporate groups on premium weekends. Book the private charter second once the Lindenderry dates are confirmed. Confirm the private dinner configuration with Lindenderry's events team at the same time as the accommodation block, finalising it once the Saturday date is locked.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can the itinerary be invoiced to a company account?

Yes, in principle, but this requires direct arrangement with each operator. Lindenderry's events team and the charter operator both handle corporate group bookings and can issue tax invoices. Confirm the invoicing process with each operator at the time of contracting. Peninsula Insider cannot issue invoices for any component.

### Does Peninsula Insider provide facilitation for the team session?

No. This package sequences the hospitality and tour components; it does not include any team session content, facilitation methodology, or L&D design. If a structured team session is part of the plan, engage an independent facilitator. The Lindenderry venue has meeting space and outdoor terrace that can support a structured session, but the facilitation itself is outside the scope of this package.

### Is Peninsula Insider the booking agent for this package?

No. Peninsula Insider is an editorial publisher. Each component is a direct transaction between the company and the relevant operator. Peninsula Insider provides no booking guarantee or customer service for any component.

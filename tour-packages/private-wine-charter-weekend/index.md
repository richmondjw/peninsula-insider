# Private Wine Charter Weekend

Two nights at Lindenderry in Red Hill built around a private full-day wine charter, with a Saturday spa session and group dinner. The private charter format means the itinerary belongs entirely to the group: the pace, the cellar door selection, the time spent at each stop.

## Why this combination works

Most hens groups who approach the Peninsula without curation default to a shared group tour that departs Melbourne CBD and delivers a generic experience designed for couples and mixed groups. A private charter requires direct operator negotiation, vehicle and guide vetting, and restaurant booking coordination across separate transactions that must align on the same Saturday. This package sequences that work. The Lindenderry anchor is the decisive structural choice: the property sits within 15 minutes of the Red Hill wine corridor, which means the charter vehicle is not burning Saturday morning on a freeway. The spa, on-site dining, and common spaces mean Friday evening and Sunday morning require no additional logistics. The group arrives inside the geography on Friday and does not leave it until Sunday afternoon.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there a minimum group size for the private charter?

Private charter pricing typically becomes viable at 8 or more guests. Smaller groups may be better served by shared-group tour options. Contact the charter operator directly with your headcount for a current quote.

### Can we modify the cellar door selection?

Yes. A private charter means the itinerary is built around the group's preferences rather than a fixed route. Discuss preferred estates or styles during the pre-booking call with the operator. Groups visiting for the first time can let the guide lead; groups with specific estates in mind can specify them.

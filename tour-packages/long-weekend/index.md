# Foodie Long Weekend

Two nights at Jackalope built around a Saturday morning Peninsula produce trail, a small-group guided cellar door tour in the afternoon, and a degustation dinner on Saturday evening. The day moves deliberately from raw produce to estate wine to the kitchen's interpretation of both.

## Why this combination works

The Peninsula has a density of food and wine producers per square kilometre that most Australian regions cannot match, but accessing that density requires a sequencing solution that wine-focused packages typically ignore. The morning is produce and provenance: the Saturday market and a producer visit, where the conversation happens with the person who made the thing you are eating. The afternoon is guided wine: a small-group tour at premium family-owned estates where the emphasis on food alongside wine is the differentiating feature. The evening is the chef's table: the arrival point that the whole Saturday has been building towards. The Jackalope anchor is the specific argument: a design hotel on a working vineyard where Sunday morning can be spent at the on-site cellar door rather than driving to a separate property. For a food-and-wine audience, the accommodation being part of the gastronomic experience rather than incidental to it is a meaningful consideration.

## Booking sequence

Book Jackalope first, 8 to 10 weeks ahead for peak weekends. Book the degustation at Doot Doot Doot immediately after confirming the stay - the Saturday evening chef's table is time-sensitive and is the culminating experience the whole Saturday builds towards. Book the guided cellar door tour after the degustation is confirmed. Confirm the produce trail details last: check current operator hours and arrange any producer visit with advance contact where required.

## Frequently asked questions

### What if no Saturday market fits our itinerary?

Use a self-guided produce route through the Red Hill corridor instead. Confirm the opening hours of each producer, providore and cafe before travelling. The Saturday structure can work without a market stop.

### Can the full-day wine tour be adapted to an afternoon-only session to allow for the morning market?

A full-day tour operator and a Saturday morning produce trail cannot occupy the same day. The solution is to book a flexible-timing operator such as Rick's Wine Tours for the afternoon cellar door component, or to move the produce trail to Friday afternoon and run the full-day tour on Saturday. Confirm the timing configuration with the tour operator before booking.

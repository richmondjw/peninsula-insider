# Visit Mornington Peninsula

> Visit Mornington Peninsula is the official regional tourism website for Victoria’s Mornington Peninsula, providing inspiration, travel planning, and local business discovery for visitors seeking experiences, accommodation, food, events and itineraries.

## Website
https://www.visitmorningtonpeninsula.org/

## Region Summary
The Mornington Peninsula is a leading coastal tourism destination located less than one hour from Melbourne, known for beaches, wineries, restaurants, spas, golf courses, nature experiences and coastal villages.  [oai_citation:0‡Visit Mornington Peninsula](https://www.visitmorningtonpeninsula.org/)

Visitors can explore:
- Coastal and surf beaches
- Wineries and cellar doors
- Restaurants and local produce
- Hot springs and wellness experiences
- National parks and walking trails
- Golf courses and resorts
- Arts, culture and heritage attractions  [oai_citation:1‡Visit Mornington Peninsula](https://www.visitmorningtonpeninsula.org/things-to-do)

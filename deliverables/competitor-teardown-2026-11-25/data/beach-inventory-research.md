# Mornington Peninsula beach inventory (desk research)

Compiled 25 November 2026. This is a desk research inventory of named public beaches within Mornington Peninsula Shire, built only from public web pages opened on that date. Nothing here has been verified on site. Every fact (names, managers, lifesaving patrols, dog rules, toilets) must be rechecked against its source, and against signage on the ground, before publication. Where a fact could not be confirmed from a page that was opened, the cell says "Unknown".

## How to read the table

- Coast: Bay (Port Phillip), Ocean (Bass Strait), Western Port.
- "Order" means the Mornington Peninsula Shire Council Order under section 26(2) of the Domestic Animals Act 1994, made 3 December 2024 and published in the Victoria Government Gazette G 50 on 12 December 2024. It defines "Summertime" as 1 December to the last day of February, and "Daylight Savings" as the proclaimed daylight saving period. Its general rule is that dogs must be on a chain, cord or leash in any public place unless the place is a "Designated Area" (leash-free), and dogs may not enter a "Prohibited Area" at the stated times. The schedule describes stretches of beach by street names and landmarks, not always by beach name, so several rows quote the stretch and say that the match to the named beach is not confirmed.
- Patrol dates quoted from Life Saving Victoria (LSV) are the dates shown on its site on 25 November 2026, which were still the 2025 to 2026 season dates.
- Toilets facts taken from Shire coastal management plans are dated (2004 or 2015) and are marked as such.

## Notes and gaps

### How the pages were read (matters for rechecking)

- mornpen.vic.gov.au returned "403 Access Denied" to a plain command-line request with a browser-style user agent. The same public pages opened normally through this session's standard web page fetch tool. For Shire HTML pages that tool returns an extract written by a small model, not the raw page, so the Shire page wording quoted here (beach lists, swimming zones, beach matting sites, facility snapshots) is a close reading, not a character-for-character copy, and should be checked by eye. Shire PDFs (the Gazette Order, the Summer Guide, the dog policy, the 2015 Mornington plan, the 2004 Portsea plan) were saved and read directly as text.
- Parks Victoria, the foreshore committee sites and lsv.com.au were read directly as raw pages.
- None of the Shire HTML pages showed a last-updated date (footer copyright 2026 only). Parks Victoria pages are undated but carried live notices referring to December 2026. Committee pages are undated unless stated.

### Dog rules: uncertainty and conflicts

- The Shire's "Dogs on the Peninsula" page points to an interactive map for beach-by-beach controls. The map content was not readable as a page and was not queried, so no row relies on it. The Gazette Order of 12 December 2024 is the written source used instead. The Shire page says the updated controls took effect on 12 December 2024.
- The Order describes stretches by street and landmark. For Sunnyside, Moondah, Ranelagh, Scout Beach, Safety Beach, McCrae, Rosebud, Rye Front Beach, Blairgowrie Front Beach, Sorrento Front Beach and the Flinders pier beach, the match between the named beach and an Order stretch needs a map check.
- Beaches not named in the Order (for example Mills Beach, Fishermans Beach, Mount Martha North and South) are marked Unknown. The Order's general rule (on leash in public places) would apply if no other control exists, but this was not confirmed against the Shire map or signage. The Shire's Summer Guide says only that dogs are prohibited "on many beaches from 10am to 6pm during summer time".
- The Shire's dog page says a review of 19 dog prohibited reserves was under way, with a survey from 28 September to 6 November 2026 and results going to Council in early 2027. Rules may change after that.
- Capel Sound conflict: the committee page says the summer beach restriction runs "From December 1st until February 1st", while the Order says Summertime runs to the last day of February. The committee page also names the nearest leash-free area as between Shirlow Avenue and Daly Avenue, which matches the Order's Rye stretch (the Order says "Shirlow Street" in one place and "Shirlow Avenue" in another).
- Camerons Bight conflict: the Order gives the leash-free area as Stringer Road to Leggett Way (via Hughes Road); the Whitecliffs page says "Between Collins Settlement and Stringer Road". The dates and hours agree (26 December to end of February, 7 pm to 12 pm the next day; the Whitecliffs page words the end date as "March 1").
- The Order also restricts two stretches of the Point King Committee of Management Foreshore Reserve (Portsea and Sorrento, Summertime 10 am to 6 pm). No source opened names a "Point King Beach", so it is not in the table.
- The 2015 Mornington plan says Royal Beach was then the only year-round off-leash beach in the Mornington foreshore reserves. The 2024 Order also designates the Fosters Beach and Fossil Beach stretch, so the 2015 statement is out of date.
- For Diamond Bay and Bridgewater Bay the dog entry is the park-wide Parks Victoria rule, not a beach-specific page. The Shire's dog page also says the national parks on the Bass Strait coast between Flinders and Portsea are dog prohibited all year.

### Names and coverage

- Names found only in a Parks Victoria park notice (fox control zones, December 2026), with no page or facts of their own, so left out of the table: Monforts Beach, Fowlers Beach, Brewsters Beach, Alison Avenue Beach, Alison Avenue East Bay.
- Point Nepean National Park: Parks Victoria says access from the shore to the ocean beaches is prohibited (Cheviot Beach is viewed from a lookout only), and that swimming is permitted at "the bay beach" and the Quarantine Station. No formal beach name is given, so no row was added. Dogs are not allowed in the park.
- No named swimming beach was confirmed between Crib Point and Somerville. The Shire describes Hastings Foreshore (fishing, boardwalk, playground) without naming a beach, and the Order makes Hastings Foreshore Reserve North (north and east of Kings Creek) and Yaringa Foreshore Reserve, Somerville, dog prohibited areas.

- Beach matting (Shire accessible recreation page, November to May): Mount Martha beach at the lifesaving club, Rye front beach next to the pier, Mothers Beach, Rosebud beach next to the jetty, Sorrento front beach next to The Baths restaurant.
- Tourism and blog pages were not needed to confirm any name in the table. Every name comes from the Shire, the Gazette Order, Parks Victoria or a committee of management.

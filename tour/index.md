## Frequently asked questions

### Are Mornington Peninsula wine tours worth it?

For visitors who want to cover multiple cellar doors without driving, yes. A guided wine tour removes the logistics and lets everyone in the group drink. The trade-off is pace: most small-group tours run to a schedule. If you want extended time at a single estate, a private charter or self-drive itinerary is usually better.

### How much does a Mornington Peninsula tour cost?

Confirm pricing directly with each operator. Costs vary by tour length, group size, and whether transport and lunch are included. Most operators publish current rates on their booking pages.

### What is the best time of year for a Peninsula tour?

October through April is peak season: the weather is best and the cellar doors are fully staffed. Vintage (March to April) is a particularly good time to visit wineries. Winter tours are quieter, but check that your preferred cellar doors are open before you book.

### Do tours depart from Melbourne or from the Peninsula?

Most small-group tours and commercial operators depart from Melbourne CBD. Some premium and private operators offer Peninsula-side pickups from Mornington, Sorrento, or Red Hill. Check pickup options when booking.

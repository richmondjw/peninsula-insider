## What happens on this tour

Private pickup from Melbourne CBD hotels. The luxury van collects the group from their accommodation so the day begins in the vehicle rather than at a coach terminal. The guide confirms the day's itinerary during transit to the Peninsula. The standard hens day arc covers at least one cellar door tasting (private bookings typically allow better tasting room access than shared-group arrivals), a celebratory Peninsula lunch at a venue the guide has chosen and reserved to suit the group's atmosphere and dietary requirements, and an afternoon activity of the group's choosing: Peninsula Hot Springs, a coastal walk, a second cellar door, or a scenic stop. The guide manages timing throughout. Door-to-door return to Melbourne hotels within the 8 to 9 hour window.

## Who this suits

Hens parties of up to seven who want a fully private day on the Peninsula without sharing a vehicle with strangers. Groups whose priorities shift between wine, wellness, coast, and food, given that the itinerary is fully customisable. Groups where at least some members do not drink, as the Peninsula day can be built around coastal and food experiences as readily as cellar doors. Groups that value the guide managing all logistics and reservations.

### Who should look elsewhere

Hens parties larger than seven: the vehicle maximum is a firm constraint, and groups above that size require either two vehicles or a different operator. Budget-conscious groups: the per-day vehicle rate is competitive when split across seven guests, but smaller groups will pay more per head. Groups wanting a high-volume, high-energy party format rather than a curated private day.

## Booking intelligence

The stated price is a per-day vehicle rate, not a per-person rate. For a group of 7, the per-person equivalent is competitive with shared-group alternatives. Confirm this interpretation directly with the operator before booking. Build the itinerary in consultation with the operator well before the tour date, and communicate priority activities explicitly. If adding Peninsula Hot Springs, book the springs session at the same time as the tour to secure the preferred time slot. Brief all group members on bring-your-own requirements, including swimwear for hot springs stops.

## Frequently asked questions

### How is a hens day different from a standard private tour with this operator?

The vehicle and guide are the same product. The hens day differs in the celebratory emphasis: lunch venue selection, itinerary pacing to account for a social group dynamic, and any celebration-specific elements the booking party requests. Be explicit with the operator about the occasion so the guide can manage the day's tone appropriately.

### What if some group members do not drink alcohol?

The Peninsula itinerary can be constructed to balance wine stops with coastal walks, national park stops, or artisan food producers. Non-drinkers can participate in cellar door visits and enjoy the properties and food components. Communicate the group's mixed preferences at booking so the guide can balance the day accordingly.

### What if the group wants to add a spa or beauty component?

The operator can advise on Peninsula wellness and spa options. Confirm at booking what specific arrangements the operator can make on the group's behalf. Peninsula spa options have separate booking windows, so the earlier the group confirms priorities, the better the logistics. Do not assume the guide will manage spa bookings without explicit instruction.

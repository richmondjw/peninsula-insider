# Couples Tours on the Mornington Peninsula

The Peninsula's guided tour options for couples concentrate around wine and private charters. A small-group wine tour is the practical starting point - you cover multiple cellar doors in a single day, no one has to drive, and the format is comfortable for two people joining a shared group.

Private charters suit couples who want a more intimate experience or who are marking a specific occasion. The guide adjusts to your pace and the itinerary can be built around what you actually want to see.

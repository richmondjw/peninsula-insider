# Mornington Peninsula Wine Tour

Bunyip Tours has been running small-group day tours from Melbourne to the Mornington Peninsula since 1998. The wine tour covers three to four cellar doors on the Red Hill plateau, includes a stop at a produce destination or the hot springs, and returns to Melbourne by early evening.

## What happens on this tour

Departure from Melbourne CBD at approximately 8:30 am. The day covers three to four Mornington Peninsula cellar doors, with guided tastings at each. A winery lunch or produce stop is included. Most departures include a stop at Peninsula Hot Springs or a beach walk before the return journey. Arrival back in Melbourne is typically between 5:30 and 7:00 pm.

## Who this suits

Guests who want to cover multiple cellar doors in a single day without the logistics of driving. Works well for visitors new to the Peninsula who want an introduction to the wine region. Groups of two to four who are happy to share a bus with other guests.

### Who should look elsewhere

Guests who want a private, customised itinerary. Those who want extended time at a single estate. Groups larger than eight who want a self-contained vehicle.

## Booking intelligence

Weekend departures in October through April book out two to three weeks in advance. Midweek departures have more availability. Free cancellation up to 24 hours prior.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does the tour include lunch?

Yes. A winery lunch or produce stop is included in the tour price. Dietary requirements can be accommodated with advance notice.

### Where does the tour depart from?

Melbourne CBD. Specific pickup points vary by departure; confirm the pickup location with the operator when booking.

### Is the tour suitable for someone who does not drink wine?

Yes. Non-drinkers can participate. The guide narrates the viticulture and estate stories regardless of whether guests are tasting. Soft drinks are available at cellar doors.

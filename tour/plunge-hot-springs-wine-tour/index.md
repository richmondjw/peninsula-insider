# Hot Springs + Wine Combination Tour

Plunge Wine Tours is a private boutique operation run by Arthur O'Bryan, who brings over 25 years of Mornington Peninsula wine industry experience to every departure. The tour combines private cellar door tastings with a session at Peninsula Hot Springs, Fingal, and operates as an exclusive charter: no shared vehicles, no fixed group departure times.

## What happens on this tour

Door-to-door pickup from the Melbourne CBD, airport, St Kilda, or a nominated address, with departure time negotiated at booking to allow optimal timing at the hot springs. The day sequences cellar door tastings with a two-to-two-and-a-half-hour session at Peninsula Hot Springs, with the order flexible based on the group's preference and the springs' booking windows. Arthur O'Bryan's depth of Peninsula wine knowledge produces access to winemaker conversations and private tasting options not available on standard shared tours. Lunch at a cellar door restaurant is at guest expense. The private Mercedes-Benz Viano allows door-to-door return drop-off.

### What should I bring for the hot springs?

Swimwear is required. Towels can be hired at the springs at additional cost, or bring your own. The outdoor pools range from cool plunge (approximately 15 degrees Celsius) to warm bath (approximately 38 to 40 degrees). Reef-safe sunscreen is recommended for daytime sessions. The combination of wine tasting earlier in the day and extended thermal bathing warrants pacing alcohol intake carefully.

### How does this tour differ from the shared-group hot springs day tours?

The shared-group operators such as Bunyip run departures for up to 24 guests at a lower per-person rate. Plunge is a private charter with an expert wine guide at a premium per-person rate. The difference is exclusivity and guide depth: Plunge delivers a private experience with Arthur O'Bryan's specialist Peninsula wine knowledge; the shared-group operators deliver reliable group logistics at a lower price point.

## What happens on this tour

Private pickup from Melbourne CBD or a nominated hotel. The guide manages logistics and introductions during transit, calibrating the formality register for a professional group and setting the day's tone. The morning opens with a private tasting at the first selected winery, typically in a semi-private or private space rather than the public tasting floor. A fine dining Peninsula lunch serves as the centrepiece of the day, at a restaurant chosen for its suitability as a corporate event: menu flexibility for dietary requirements, appropriate atmosphere, and billing arrangements that suit the corporate entertainment context. An afternoon tasting stop at a smaller, less-known producer follows, deliberately avoiding the standard circuit. A scenic or activity stop fills the late afternoon before the return to Melbourne CBD, arriving by approximately 17:30 to 18:00.

Large corporate groups above 11: the operator can discuss multi-vehicle coordination for larger groups, but the standard format tops out at 11 in the extended vehicle. Groups on a mid-range budget: the per-person rate is a premium-segment entry point. Groups wanting multilingual guiding or a confirmed independent review sample in the thousands.

## Booking intelligence

Communicate the corporate context explicitly at booking, including group composition and any desired formality or team-building objectives, so the guide can calibrate accordingly. Confirm whether the fine dining lunch is included in the tour fare or separately billed. Confirm cellar door tasting fee inclusion. Confirm billing and receipt requirements: a reputable corporate operator should produce a GST-inclusive tax invoice in the booker's business name. Ask for specific venue names for the lunch stop and review them independently. Free cancellation applies within 24 hours.

## Frequently asked questions

### How is the corporate charter different from a standard private tour?

The vehicle, guide, and cellar door circuit are the same product. The differentiation is in the guide's briefing, the lunch venue selection (corporate-appropriate fine dining rather than a casual cellar door restaurant), and the billing structure. Communicate the corporate context to the operator at booking so the guide knows they are managing a team of professional colleagues rather than a social group.

### What documentation does Great Private Tours provide for corporate expense claims?

Confirm at booking. A reputable operator in the corporate day charter category should produce a GST-inclusive tax invoice in the booker's business name. If company credit card payment is required, confirm the operator's accepted payment methods, as some boutique operators do not accept corporate cards without surcharge.

### Can the itinerary be themed around a specific client or industry?

To a degree. The guide can incorporate Peninsula producers and venues that connect to a specific industry context. Agricultural or food science guests may benefit from a viticulture-focused itinerary with winemaker access; other client types may prefer a more convivial and less technical format. Communicate this context as early as possible before the tour date.

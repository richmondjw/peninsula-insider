# Full Day Tours

Full-day tours on the Mornington Peninsula typically run eight to ten hours, departing Melbourne in the morning and returning by early evening. They cover multiple stops - cellar doors, produce destinations, the hot springs, or coastal walks - depending on the operator's product.

A full day is the minimum useful format for visitors from Melbourne who want to get a proper introduction to the region. Half-day formats rarely justify the drive time.

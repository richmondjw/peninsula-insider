# Hot Springs Tours on the Mornington Peninsula

Peninsula Hot Springs at Fingal is the region's main thermal bathing destination. Most guided tours that include hot springs combine them with a wine component or a beach stop. Pure hot springs tours are less common from Melbourne - the most practical option for visitors who want to focus the day on bathing is to stay on the Peninsula and book directly with the springs.

## From Melbourne versus on-Peninsula

Tours from Melbourne that include the hot springs typically run as a combination day: cellar doors first, springs for two to three hours in the afternoon, return to Melbourne by early evening. The hot springs component is genuine but secondary to the wine stops. If the springs are your primary reason for visiting, a day trip from Melbourne gives you limited time in the water.

If you are staying on the Peninsula, book directly with Peninsula Hot Springs. Timed entry sessions can be reserved in advance online. The property also offers spa treatments and bathing packages that are only accessible as direct bookings. A guided tour adds no value if you are already based nearby.

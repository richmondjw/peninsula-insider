## What happens on this tour

The operator collects the group from their nominated pickup point - Melbourne CBD, a Mornington Peninsula address, or another agreed location - and the itinerary runs according to the venues and activities specified at the time of booking. A typical day covers three to four cellar doors, with the private format allowing a relaxed pace at each stop without the group-size pressure of shared tours. Lunch is at a venue of the group's choosing, whether a cellar door restaurant, a local providore, or a picnic arrangement. The itinerary can incorporate non-wine stops such as a brewery, a distillery, or a coastal activity if requested in advance. The eight-hour duration runs from first pickup to last drop-off.

## Who this suits

Private groups of two to twenty-four who want a self-contained day without sharing a vehicle with strangers. Value-conscious guests who want the private format without paying premium prices. Peninsula-based guests who want to eliminate the Melbourne CBD transit entirely by using the flexible pickup option.

### Who should look elsewhere

Guests who want a guide with deep decades of Peninsula wine knowledge: Rick's was established in 2019 and lacks the operational depth of longer-running operators. Guests booking on short notice for a fixed peak date: the private format depends on advance communication of preferences, and same-day flexibility is more limited than the marketing implies.

## Booking intelligence

Specify preferred wineries and any additional activities well before the departure date - the private itinerary depends on this input for pre-arranging each stop. Free cancellation within 24 hours of departure applies; confirm this in writing at booking.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is every booking private?

Yes. Rick's Wine Tours operates exclusively as a private charter. You will not share the vehicle with guests from other bookings. Groups of 2–24 are accommodated; confirm your group size and vehicle type with the operator at booking.

### Are cellar door tasting fees included in the price?

This is not confirmed in the published fare and should be verified directly with the operator before booking. It is a material cost difference from competitors who include tastings - confirm in writing whether tasting fees are bundled or payable separately at each stop.

### How flexible is the itinerary really?

Meaningful flexibility requires advance planning, not on-the-day decision-making. Each winery stop needs to be pre-arranged against the venue's booking schedule and seasonal closures. The earlier preferences are communicated, the more the operator can lock in the preferred itinerary. Treat itinerary planning as a conversation before the day, not an option during it.

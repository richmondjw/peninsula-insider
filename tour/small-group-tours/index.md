# Small-Group Tours

Small-group tours - typically 8 to 24 guests sharing a vehicle - are the practical middle ground between a private charter and a large coach. You get the logistics removed, a guide, and a structured itinerary, without the premium of a private booking.

They work best for couples or small parties travelling independently who want to spend the day with others in a similar mindset. They work less well if you want to control the pace or extend your time at any one stop.

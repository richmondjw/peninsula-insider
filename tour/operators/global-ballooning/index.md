## About Global Ballooning Australia

Global Ballooning Australia runs dawn hot air balloon flights over the Mornington Peninsula wine country, with flights launching at first light and lasting approximately one hour in the air. The Peninsula product focuses on the Red Hill and Merricks hinterland, offering views over vineyard and farmland country at altitude. Flights are followed by a champagne breakfast at a winery or nearby venue, making the total experience a complete morning.

### What they are good at

Delivering the Peninsula's most dramatic elevated perspective over wine country, particularly in autumn when the vineyard canopy is turning. The champagne breakfast format is well-executed and rounds out the morning without requiring additional logistics. Suits anniversary occasions and couples well, and the small group format (up to 16) keeps it from feeling like a tour bus.

### Not suited for

Guests with a fear of heights. Anyone needing a fixed, guaranteed itinerary should understand that balloon flights are entirely weather-dependent and launch sites are confirmed only the evening before. Pregnant guests cannot participate, per standard balloon operations policy. Early risers only: dawn departures require a 4:30 to 5:30am start depending on conditions.

### Visit Global Ballooning Australia directly

Pricing, seasonal departures, and group bookings are managed on the operator's own website.

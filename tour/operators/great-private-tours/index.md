## About Great Private Tours

Great Private Tours is a Melbourne-based luxury private tour operator founded in 2016, running bespoke Mornington Peninsula winery tours in Mercedes-Benz vehicles including the Sprinter. Groups of 2 to 8 on private bookings, or up to 11 on their small-group luxe product, travel over 9 to 10 hours with expert local guides.

### What they are good at

A perfect 5.0 Tripadvisor score from 92 reviews over nearly a decade of operation provides a credible track record at this price point. The bespoke approach means itineraries can be built around guest preferences, including fine dining and premium tastings, rather than a fixed cellar door sequence.

### Not suited for

Travellers on a mid-range budget, as the luxury vehicle and private guide format carries a corresponding price premium. Accessibility information is not published, so guests with specific mobility needs should confirm directly before booking.

### Visit Great Private Tours directly

Pricing, seasonal departures, and group bookings are managed on the operator's own website.

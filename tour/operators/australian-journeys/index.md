## About Australian Journeys

Australian Journeys is a Melbourne-based private tour operator founded in 2019. They run exclusively private wine and food tours to the Mornington Peninsula for groups of 2 to 11, with door-to-door pickup and flexible itineraries. Larger buses up to 24 seats are available for bigger groups.

### What they are good at

The all-private format means no shared coach with strangers. A flexible fleet across SUV, 11-seater minibus, and 24-seater bus allows the same operator to handle couples through to larger group bookings. Free cancellation up to 24 hours is a practical booking safeguard.

### Not suited for

Solo travellers or couples looking for a budget day tour, as the private model and per-person pricing reflect premium positioning. No Tripadvisor review history available in the CSV data to validate guest satisfaction independently.

Pricing, seasonal departures, and group bookings are managed on the operator's own website.

## About Autopia Tours

Autopia Tours is a Melbourne-based ecotourism operator founded in 1987, running small-group day tours from the city. On the Mornington Peninsula, their flagship product is an official hot springs shuttle with skip-the-queue entry to Peninsula Hot Springs, plus scenic stops at Arthurs Seat and the beach boxes.

### What they are good at

As an official operator partner for Peninsula Hot Springs, they offer pre-arranged skip-the-queue access that independent visitors cannot easily replicate. Tripadvisor rating of 4.8 to 4.9 across over 2,000 reviews reflects sustained operational reliability over nearly four decades.

### Not suited for

Travellers wanting a wine-focused itinerary, as the Peninsula offering is built around hot springs. No wheelchair or mobility aid storage. Groups who want a private vehicle rather than a shared coach.

### Visit Autopia Tours directly

Pricing, seasonal departures, and group bookings are managed on the operator's own website.

## About Local Way Tours

Local Way Tours runs premium small-group tours to the Mornington Peninsula from Melbourne, covering wine, hot springs, food, and sightseeing. Their fleet includes a Mercedes Sprinter, Renault Master, and a 27-seat Yutong midi coach, allowing groups from a handful of people up to 15 to travel in modern, comfortable vehicles.

### What they are good at

A 4.9 Tripadvisor rating from 484 reviews is a strong result for a small-group operator and indicates consistent delivery across a high volume of trips. Wine is included on every tour, and guests get a guaranteed window seat. The fleet variety means the experience does not feel like a budget coach trip regardless of group size.

### Not suited for

Not wheelchair accessible, though strollers and infants on laps are accommodated. Groups larger than 15 will need to look elsewhere. Travellers wanting a fully private vehicle rather than a premium small-group shared format.

### Visit Local Way Tours directly

Pricing, seasonal departures, and group bookings are managed on the operator's own website.

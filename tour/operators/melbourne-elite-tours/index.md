## About Melbourne Elite Tours

Melbourne Elite Tours offers personalised private day tours to the Mornington Peninsula for groups of up to 7, travelling in luxury vehicles including a Mercedes van. Their Peninsula itinerary covers wine, hot springs, food, beaches, sightseeing, and national parks over 8 to 9 hours.

### What they are good at

A perfect 5.0 Tripadvisor score from 23 reviews, and a price range that positions the product firmly in the premium private bracket. The small cap of 7 guests keeps the experience genuinely intimate. Foldable wheelchairs and walkers can be accommodated, which most competitors at this level cannot offer.

### Not suited for

Groups larger than 7. Travellers who want a wine-specialist guide rather than a broad sightseeing itinerary. The low review count means the Tripadvisor rating, while perfect, is based on a smaller sample than more established operators.

Pricing, seasonal departures, and group bookings are managed on the operator's own website.

## About Bunyip Tours

Bunyip Tours is one of the longest-established small-group tour operators on the Melbourne-to-Mornington-Peninsula route, operating since 1998. The wine and hot springs day tour is their flagship Peninsula product.

### What they are good at

Efficient multi-cellar-door days from Melbourne CBD. Good value for couples and small groups who want a structured introduction to Peninsula wine without logistics overhead. Guides are knowledgeable on regional viticulture.

### Not suited for

Guests who want a private or customised itinerary. Groups over eight who want a self-contained vehicle. Anyone who wants extended time at a single estate.

### Visit Bunyip Tours directly

Pricing, seasonal departures, and group bookings are managed on the operator's own website.

## About Wine Hop and Coastal Tours

Wine Hop and Coastal Tours is an ecotourism-certified operator running small group day tours on the Mornington Peninsula. Their product combines wine, food, and coastal sightseeing over a full 9-hour day, departing on Fridays from Melbourne CBD hotels.

### What they are good at

Holds ecotourism certification, which distinguishes them from operators purely focused on cellar door visits. Suits travellers who want coastal scenery and food included alongside wine rather than a winery-only itinerary.

### Not suited for

Anyone needing weekend or midweek departures, as tours only operate on Fridays. The Tripadvisor rating of 3.7 from 18 reviews is below the field average, and flexibility around pickup points is limited to CBD hotels only.

### Visit Wine Hop and Coastal Tours directly

Pricing, seasonal departures, and group bookings are managed on the operator's own website.

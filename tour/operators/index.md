## How we assess operators

The assessment checks four things: whether the advertised product matches what guests report receiving; whether the operator is transparent about pricing and inclusions; whether they have a genuine track record on the Peninsula; and whether any affiliate or commercial relationship is disclosed clearly.

We do not accept payment for operator listings. All editorial assessments are independent. Where we have a commercial affiliate relationship with an operator, this is disclosed on the relevant operator page.

## About Rick's Wine Tours

Rick's Wine Tours is a Mornington Peninsula-focused wine tour operator established in 2019. They run private and customisable tours for groups of 2 to 24, visiting wineries, breweries, and distilleries with flexibility to choose venues and add activities.

### What they are good at

Strong Tripadvisor rating of 4.9 from 121 reviews, and a genuinely flexible booking model that lets groups choose their own cellar doors rather than following a locked itinerary. The per-adult price point sits at the accessible end of the private tour market, which makes them competitive for private group bookings.

### Not suited for

Guests who need wheelchair-accessible transport. Those looking for a solo traveller or couple joining a shared public tour, as tours are private.

### Visit Rick's Wine Tours directly

Pricing, seasonal departures, and group bookings are managed on the operator's own website.

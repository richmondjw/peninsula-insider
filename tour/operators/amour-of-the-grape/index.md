## About Amour of the Grape Tours

Amour of the Grape Tours is a small family-run operation based on the Mornington Peninsula, run by locals Paul and Liz Gough. They offer personalised wine, food, and Peninsula day tours in groups of 2 to 7, with Saturday departures accommodating up to 11. Tours run daily across wine, hot springs, beer, cider, spirits, and regional experiences.

### What they are good at

Ranked number one on Tripadvisor for Peninsula tours with a perfect 5.0 score across 383 reviews, which reflects consistent, personal service over many years of operation. The small group cap of 7 on most days means guests get genuine host attention rather than a managed crowd.

### Not suited for

Groups larger than 7 outside of Saturdays. Travellers who need wheelchair-accessible transport. Those wanting a fixed, pre-published itinerary rather than a tailored day.

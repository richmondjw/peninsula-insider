## About Mornington Peninsula Plunge Wine Tours

Mornington Peninsula Plunge Wine Tours is a boutique private operator founded in 2017 and run by Arthur O'Bryan, who has over 25 years of experience in the wine industry. Tours run daily in a luxury Mercedes-Benz Viano, covering wine, food, sculpture, and coastal sightseeing for private small groups.

### What they are good at

A perfect 5.0 Tripadvisor score from 107 reviews, built on the guide's genuine winemaker relationships and insider access. The operator's industry background means guests get wine education and behind-the-scenes context that generalist tour guides cannot replicate. Door-to-door pickup including the airport is a practical advantage.

### Not suited for

Travellers on tight budgets, as private boutique pricing reflects the premium vehicle and personalised service. Groups larger than the van capacity. Those wanting a standardised shared-group tour format.

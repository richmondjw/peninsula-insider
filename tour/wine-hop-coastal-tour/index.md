## What happens on this tour

Pickup from Melbourne CBD hotels in the morning, followed by a nine-hour day that sequences coastal stops with cellar door visits. The guide's coastal interpretation is substantive: the Ecotourism Australia accreditation requires documented standards for environmental commentary, not just scenic stops. Lunch is included at a Peninsula winery. Two cellar door tastings are incorporated around the coastal segments. The Friday-only schedule means coastal sites are typically less crowded than on weekend departures. Return to Melbourne CBD hotels in the late afternoon, with Friday peak-hour traffic a factor on the return leg.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does the Ecotourism Australia certification mean in practice?

Ecotourism Australia's National Ecotourism Accreditation Program requires operators to meet documented standards in environmental management, interpretation quality, and contribution to conservation. For the guest, it means the guide's commentary on coastal environments is structured and accurate rather than improvised, and the tour avoids practices that cause ecological harm at sensitive sites.

### Why does this tour only run on Fridays?

The Friday-only schedule reflects the capacity constraints of a small-group specialist operation. The practical upside: Peninsula coastal sites on a Friday are materially less crowded than on weekends. If a Friday departure works for your schedule, the timing is an advantage.

### Is lunch included?

Yes. The operator's booking page references an included lunch as part of the tour. Communicate dietary requirements at the time of booking, as the midday stop is the day's key fixed inclusion.

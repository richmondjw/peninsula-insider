# Private Tours - Mornington Peninsula

Private tours give you a dedicated vehicle, a guide working only for your group, and an itinerary that you shape. The cost is higher than a small-group day, but if you fill the vehicle - typically four to eight guests - the per-person difference is often modest.

The value of a private tour is control: you spend longer at the estates you like, skip the ones that don't interest you, and eat where you want. For corporate groups, milestone celebrations, or anyone who finds the pace of a shared bus frustrating, it is usually the right call.

# Careers

Peninsula Insider is a small, independent editorial publication built around the Mornington Peninsula. We work with a tight group of writers, photographers, editors and operators, and we are slow and deliberate about who we bring in.

## Current openings

We do not have formal openings advertised right now.

## How we tend to work

- **Contributing writers** - commissioned per piece for editorial features, profiles and seasonal coverage.

- **Photographers** - engaged for venue, place and event coverage with a long-form, on-the-Peninsula brief.

- **Editors and producers** - occasional engagements as the publication's rhythm expands.

## Pitching or expressing interest

If you would like to pitch a story or contribute editorially, write to stories@peninsulainsider.com.au with a short pitch (one paragraph), one or two clips, and a sense of how you'd cover it.

For general expressions of interest in working with us - editorial, photographic, partnerships or product - write to hello@peninsulainsider.com.au with a brief note and a CV or portfolio link.

We read everything that comes in. We can't always respond individually, but we keep notes and reach out when a relevant brief opens up.

## What we look for

- Genuine connection to and knowledge of the Mornington Peninsula

- Editorial judgement - the ability to choose what's worth covering and what isn't

- Care for accuracy and the people we write about

- Independent, reader-first instincts

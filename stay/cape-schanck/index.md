# Where to Stay in Cape Schanck

Cape Schanck is not a town-base choice in the same way Sorrento or Mornington are. It is a retreat-base choice. You stay here for wind, cliffs, golf, spa time, and distance from busier Peninsula rhythms. That makes it useful for readers who want the trip to feel sealed off rather than socially full.

The category is therefore narrow but clear. If Cape Schanck is the right answer, it is because the weekend is about reset, not browsing.

# Resort, springs, or retreat?

These are different bookings. Choose hotel service, bathing on the same estate, or space for a group before you choose the room.

## A room with the resort around it.

### RACV Cape Schanck Resort

RACV lists rooms, suites and separate villas, with restaurants, a pool, golf and One Spa on the property. Room layouts and views vary. Choose the exact format before you reserve, and arrange any spa treatment separately.

## Bring the group. Plan the program.

A retreat venue gives your group a place to gather. A facilitated program is a separate thing to confirm.

### The Cape Retreat

The operator describes 12 guest suites and shared living, dining and meeting spaces at Cape Schanck. It markets the property for wellness retreats, meetings and celebrations, with Bass Strait and nearby golf-course outlooks.

**Check before booking.** Ask whether your dates allow an individual suite or need a group booking. Confirm room allocation, catering, facilitator and timetable. A retreat program is not implied by the accommodation name.

## Questions to settle before reserving

### Is One Spa at RACV Cape Schanck a hot spring?

No. One Spa is the resort day spa. RACV offers rooms, suites and villas; arrange any spa treatment separately. For accommodation on a thermal springs estate, compare Peninsula Hot Springs and Alba in Fingal.

### Does a room at the springs include every bathing experience?

Inclusions depend on the operator, room and package. Compare the exact bathing access, any private pool arrangement and separate treatments before you reserve. The hot springs accommodation guide explains the three on-site formats.

### Does The Cape Retreat include a facilitated program?

The Cape Retreat is a group-oriented accommodation venue with guest suites and shared spaces. Its retreat setting does not establish a scheduled program for your booking. Confirm the facilitator, timetable, catering and whether your dates require a group booking with the operator.

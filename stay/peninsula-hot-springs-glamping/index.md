Why we’d go

Accommodation within the hot springs grounds keeps bathing at the centre of the stay.

Peninsula Hot Springs Glamping puts your accommodation within the hot springs grounds in Fingal. Choose between garden-view, lake-view and secluded pavilion settings, then check the bathing and dining inclusions attached to your booking.

Choose glamping when you want the canvas accommodation experience alongside the springs. The Eco Lodges are a separate accommodation offering at the same operator, so compare the room formats before reserving. Staying on site makes a bathing break easier to organise, but it does not replace checking treatment availability, guest age requirements or access times.

Keep the itinerary simple: arrive with your bathing plans settled, leave time for a meal and avoid filling the stay with unnecessary driving.

# Driftaway on Dundas

Three self-contained villas on 2.5 acres of tea-tree near Rye beach, breakfast hampers, oversized spa baths, one disability-accessible villa.

Why we’d go

The bay-side villa option with the most complete amenity set, spa baths, breakfast hampers, and one of the few Peninsula stays with a disability-accessible unit.

Three self-contained villas on 2.5 acres of tea-tree near Rye beach. Each villa includes breakfast hampers and oversized spa baths; one villa is disability-accessible, which is notable in a Peninsula accommodation category that rarely addresses accessibility at all. The tea-tree setting is quieter than the hinterland B&B set and positioned for guests whose preference is bay proximity over wine-country walking distance.

The format is couples-first: the spa bath, the hamper, the private villa entry, the tea-tree enclosure. It suits an anniversary weekend or a quiet mid-week escape without the cellar-door agenda. Rye beach is a short drive or a manageable walk, and the beach access makes this more flexible than a strictly hinterland stay.

Confirm current rates, minimum stay, and dog policy directly, details were unverified as of May 2026. Worth a direct call to ask about current availability and any improvements since last reviewed.

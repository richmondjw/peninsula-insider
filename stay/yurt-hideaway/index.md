Why we’d go

The rating is the first indicator. The hosting philosophy, yoga instructors who designed a space for disconnection, not content, is the second. Five minutes from Peninsula Hot Springs is the third.

Yurt Hideaway is a single 6-metre traditional Mongolian yurt on a residential block in Tootgarook, unusual enough in its format to draw attention, but the 4.97-star Airbnb rating and 100% recommendation rate across all reviews suggest it earns the visits. The hosts are yoga instructors who designed the space with specific intention: no television, private fenced courtyard, separate en-suite, outdoor kitchenette. The environment is built for rest rather than stimulation.

The proximity to Peninsula Hot Springs is the practical anchor. Five minutes by car, which means an early morning session at the springs followed by a slow return to the yurt is a realistic day structure rather than an aspiration. Three or more nights triggers a rate discount, which makes the maths work better for a proper reset rather than a single-night stop.

Dog policy is not confirmed, contact the hosts directly before booking if this is relevant. Book well ahead for peak season and long weekends.

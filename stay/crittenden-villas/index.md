Why we’d go

A self-contained lakeside stay with wine tasting and dining options on the same estate.

Each Crittenden Estate villa has a kitchen, fireplace and private deck overlooking the lake. The cellar door and estate restaurant make it possible to stay close to the property for the weekend, but reserve the experiences you want rather than assuming they are included with every stay.

Crittenden Restaurant currently lists lunch from Friday to Tuesday and dinner on Friday and Saturday. Check villa availability, restaurant reservations and cellar-door tasting times with the operators before fixing your itinerary.

# Stay in Sorrento.

Choose between Hotel Sorrento's adults-only rooms and family stays at InterContinental Sorrento.

Sorrento village and the ferry terminal from above. This is a town view, not a hotel room view. Photo courtesy of Visit Victoria.

## The place is shared. The stay is not.

Both hotels put Sorrento village and the bay into the weekend. The difference is how you spend the hours inside: Hotel Sorrento offers adults-only accommodation and a guest pool, while children are welcome in its dining areas; InterContinental is a separate hotel inside The Continental precinct, with family stays and several room wings. Neither name tells you what your individual room overlooks.

### Which room changes the trip?

Restored Classic rooms sit alongside contemporary Poolside, Coastal, Studio and Sunset accommodation. Bay views, baths, terraces and outdoor space belong to particular room types. The hotel's [Classic Suites notice](<https://hotelsorrento.com.au/room/classic-suites/>) warns of possible bar and restaurant noise on weekends and in peak periods.

### Check before you reserve

The hotel says accommodation is for adults only, with a maximum of two adults per room. Confirm breakfast, dining or wellness bookings and the exact room outlook directly.

**Current works, checked 1 October 2026:** The hotel reports daytime midweek construction, with possible noise or changed movement around the property. [Check the latest operator notice](<https://hotelsorrento.com.au/stay/>) for your dates.

### Choose the wing, not just the hotel.

The hotel lists Heritage, Main and Riley Lane rooms. Ask which wing and room give you the outlook and layout you want. [Family stays are offered](<https://sorrento.intercontinental.com/family-stays>), but occupancy and connecting arrangements depend on the room you book.

### Check before you reserve

The InterContinental room is a separate booking from [The Continental's restaurants and bars](<https://thecontinentalsorrento.com.au/>) and [Aurora Spa](<https://thecontinentalsorrento.com.au/stay-restore/aurora-spa/>). Confirm what your hotel rate includes, arrange dining or treatments separately, and ask about parking for your dates.

### Which Sorrento hotel can I stay at with children?

InterContinental Sorrento offers family stays. Hotel Sorrento says its accommodation is adults only and each room accommodates a maximum of two adults. Confirm occupancy for the exact room before booking.

### Does a hotel room include dinner or an Aurora Spa visit?

Do not assume so. At The Continental, the InterContinental hotel, precinct dining and Aurora Spa are distinct reservations. Hotel Sorrento also has separate dining and wellness choices. Check your rate and reserve extras directly.

### Will my Sorrento room be quiet and have a bay view?

Views depend on the exact room. Hotel Sorrento warns that some Classic Suites can hear its bars and restaurant on weekends and in peak periods. Ask about your room and any current works before paying.

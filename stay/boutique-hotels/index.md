## The room is the decision.

These are five characterful hotels, not a ranking. Each has a direct operator path. Views, bathing features, dining and occupancy depend on the exact room and booking, so use the hotel's current terms for your dates.

Researched from published sources. Every venue listed was reviewed April 2026 or later.

### Does every room have a bay view, terrace or bath?

No. Hotel Sorrento and InterContinental Sorrento offer different room categories and outlooks. Jackalope lists deep-soak baths and double-sided fireplaces for its Lair suites. Choose the exact room before reserving.

### Is dinner included when I book a hotel room?

Do not assume it is. The hotel room and a restaurant table can be separate bookings. Check your package inclusions and reserve the dining you want with the operator.

### Can children stay at every hotel on this list?

No. Hotel Sorrento says its accommodation is adults only, and Jackalope welcomes guests aged 12 and over. Ask each operator about the exact room and occupancy policy for your group.

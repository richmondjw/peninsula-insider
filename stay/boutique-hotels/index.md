# Boutique Hotels on the Mornington Peninsula

The Peninsula's boutique hotels work best when they feel grounded in place rather than merely upscale. That usually means village access, architectural character, or a clear relationship to the hinterland and coast. [Flinders Hotel](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/stay/flinders-hotel/>), [Hotel Sorrento](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/stay/hotel-sorrento/>), [Lindenderry](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/stay/lindenderry/>), and [Jackalope](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/stay/jackalope/>) all do that in different registers.

Stay

Choose the base, and the whole weekend changes

The Peninsula is a region of distinct moods packed close together. A Red Hill country-house weekend produces a different trip from a Sorrento main-street hotel. This hub sorts the beds by the kind of weekend you actually want.

9 min read

Two distinct stay corridors

Ridge & hinterland or Coastal village

The biggest decision in a Peninsula trip is not which property, it is which side of the region you wake up on. Each anchors a different weekend.

Ridge & hinterland

Vineyard country. Fireplaces. Lunch within walking distance.

Flagship stays
Jackalope (Merricks North), Lindenderry (Red Hill), Polperro Villas, Tucks Ridge, Montalto cottages, vineyard villa rentals
What you get
Country-house pace, low light, eucalypts and fences, a cellar door dining room within ten minutes of the bed.
Best for
Two-night food-and-wine weekends. Couples. Slow restorative trips. Winter visits with fires lit.
Trade-offs
No swimming distance. Limited walk-out infrastructure. You drive to everything but the lunch.
Pair with
A long lunch on Saturday, a cellar-door tasting on Sunday morning, and a hot-springs visit before the drive home.
Coastal village, Sorrento, Portsea, Flinders, Mornington

Bay light, ferry edge, walk-out beaches, social theatre.

Flagship stays
Hotel Sorrento, The Continental Sorrento, Portsea Hotel, Flinders Hotel, Mornington foreshore cottages, Mount Martha bay houses
What you get
Walking distance to a beach and a meal. Late dusk light over the bay. The Peninsula at its most social.
Best for
Single-night escapes. Family trips. Visitors who want to leave the car parked. Summer weekends with friends.
Trade-offs
Busy in peak season. The cellar-door day requires a drive. Less of the inland country-house feel.
Pair with
A Sorrento Back Beach sunset, a Hotel Sorrento dinner, the morning ferry to Queenscliff, or a Mornington cliff walk.

Browse stays through the places they belong to

Each hub pulls together the beds, tables, and walks that belong to that stretch of the Peninsula.

Ocean Coast · town

Flinders

Flinders sits on the wild southern edge of the Peninsula and carries itself with a kind of salt-air confidence. The village is compact but smart - a bakery, a handful of good shops, an old pub, and a coastline that feels a world away from the bay-facing polish further north. Base yourself here if you want your mornings to begin with wind off Bass Strait, your afternoons with a drive back inland for lunch, and your evenings under a harder, cleaner sky.

Red Hill · village

Red Hill

On a misty basalt plateau above the Peninsula's central ridge, Red Hill has arranged some of the strongest food and wine in Victoria into a circuit that rewards the unhurried. A village by strict measure — a general store, a monthly market, vineyard restaurants that don't advertise from the highway — it nonetheless organises the whole upper Peninsula around itself. The winery restaurant cluster here — Ten Minutes by Tractor, Montalto, Paringa Estate, Principia — has no serious peer in the state. Come on Saturday; plan lunch first.

Peninsula Tip · town

Sorrento

Sorrento is where the Peninsula narrows to a point and the social temperature rises. This is old-money beach culture done with increasing sophistication - limestone cliffs, a ferry to Queenscliff across the Heads, ocean baths carved from rock at low tide - alongside a main street that has quietly evolved into something genuinely worth driving for. The summer crowd is real and relentless, but the town earns it. Off-season Sorrento is a revelation: the light changes, the restaurants empty out to their best selves, and you can walk the ocean beach from one end to the other without encountering anyone but locals. The back beach at dusk is one of the best things on the Peninsula, full stop. Stay at least two nights.

Frequently asked

Three honest answers before you book the bed

Where is the best area to stay on the Peninsula?

Red Hill and Main Ridge for cellar-door weekends, vineyard restaurants are walkable from many stays. Sorrento for coastal village atmosphere and ocean beach access. Mornington town for the most accessible base from Melbourne (60 minutes). The right answer depends on the weekend you want, wine country, beach village, or practical proximity.

How much does accommodation cost?

Budget $250–400/night for a good mid-range self-contained villa or cottage. Luxury vineyard stays (Jackalope, Lindenderry) run $450–800/night. Budget options in Rosebud, Dromana, and Rye start from $150/night. Prices peak in summer and over long weekends, autumn midweek offers the best value.

Is one night enough on the Peninsula?

One night works for a focused escape, arrive Friday evening, spend Saturday on the ridge (cellar doors + lunch), and drive home Sunday after a morning walk. Two nights is better: it allows a beach day, a second area to explore, and the slower pace the Peninsula rewards. Base in Mornington or Sorrento for single-night trips.

## How to choose where to stay

### Where should I stay on the Mornington Peninsula?

Start with the main purpose of your trip: a coastal town, a wine-country base, or a stay centred on thermal bathing. Then check the exact address and journey to the places you plan to visit.

### Are Peninsula Hot Springs Eco Lodges the same as glamping?

No. Peninsula Hot Springs describes the Eco Lodges as private rooms in shared lodges and glamping as tented accommodation. Compare the current inclusions for the specific stay you choose.

### What should I check before reserving?

Confirm the current room or accommodation format, bathing and meal inclusions, guest conditions, accessibility and cancellation terms with the operator for your chosen dates.

Why we’d go

Stay among Red Hill's gardens and vines with dining and a weekend cellar door on the estate.

Lindenderry's Red Hill setting makes a weekend easy to keep in one place: rooms and suites, estate gardens, a vineyard, an on-site dining room and a cellar door. The hotel lists dinner in The Dining Room from Thursday to Saturday and cellar-door visits on weekends. Breakfast is served at the hotel; check your room package and reserve dinner before building the rest of the weekend around it.

Photo · Peter Foster, courtesy of Visit Victoria

Leave time to walk the gardens between bookings, or use the hotel as a base for the Red Hill cellar doors. Confirm current dining and tasting hours directly when you book.

The short list

Worth the drive this weekend

Three we would go back to, and the reason for each.

Wine bar

Allis Wine Bar

Small plates out of the kitchen garden, offcuts from the main restaurant used on purpose, and a list built to drink rather than study.

If Ten Minutes is fully booked, do not treat Allis as consolation. Treat it as the real find.

Journal

The Peninsula’s best late-afternoon walks

When lunch is over and the light starts improving, these are the walks that make the region feel larger, wilder, and worth staying for.

Go at four. The tracks empty, the wind drops, and the scrub turns the colour it is named for.

Point NepeanWalks

Guide

Cape Schanck, the complete guide

The 1859 limestone lighthouse, the boardwalk to Pulpit Rock and Pebble Beach, and the 5.4km return walk to Bushrangers Bay.

Do the boardwalk in weather. Cape Schanck is better grey than it is blue, and almost nobody believes that until they go.

The verdict

The mark appears wherever the masthead stakes a call.

Ten Minutes by Tractor is where Mornington Peninsula wine stops apologising for its ambitions.

Three original family vineyards, each named and separately vinified, plus a winemaking philosophy pulled straight from Burgundy’s manual: high-density planting, spontaneous fermentation, gravity flow. Book at least six weeks ahead for weekends. Lunch is the move.

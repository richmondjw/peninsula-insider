## 2. The publication

Peninsula Insider is a modern newsroom for the Mornington Peninsula.

Local first. Editor-led. Published every Thursday. Every claim verified. Every commercial relationship disclosed. Built for digital, not converted to it.

The publication will cover the Peninsula across five pillars: Eat & Drink, Stay, Do, Plan, and the Insider's Journal. The Journal carries a weekly cover story, longform features, interviews with the chefs and winemakers shaping the region, and seasonal guides that go deeper than the destination-marketing copy currently on the internet for this place.

Two products carry the brand. The publication itself, at peninsulainsider.com.au. And The Insider's Letter, a weekly five-minute read sent every Thursday morning, written and signed by the editor.

The bet is simple. There is no category-leading editorial brand for the Mornington Peninsula. Visit Mornington Peninsula is a legacy directory. Broadsheet treats the region as a side note to Melbourne. Easy Weddings and the listicle players have no editorial voice. That position, the local editorial authority, is unowned. We intend to take it.

## 3. The firewall

This is the part of the prospectus that decides whether you and we are a fit.

Peninsula Insider takes commercial partnerships. We are open about that. The publication is funded in part by the businesses who appear on it. What we do not do is let those businesses influence the editorial.

Three claims hold the line:

**Curated commercial.** Only Peninsula businesses appear on PI. No programmatic, no off-region brands, no Booking.com retargeting filling our sidebar. If a reader sees a partner story or a sponsor placement here, the venue is one we know.

**Editor-led.** James and Emma sign the editorial. They pick the cover. They never appear at advertiser-only events. The masthead is theirs. The rate card is the publisher's. The two do not meet.

**Disclosed by default.** Every paid item carries a Partner Content eyebrow. Every founding partner is publicly listed on our about page. Every conflict is declared in the article that touches it. Once a year we publish a one-page firewall audit, naming what we ran, what we declined, and where the line strained.

The differentiator is not "we don't sell." Visit Mornington Peninsula sells. Broadsheet sells. The differentiator is we tell you who is paying, and the editorial does not bend.

## 4. The newsroom

PI is built as a structured publication, not a one-person blog.

Editorial commissioning runs through a desk model: research, drafting, fact-checking, copy, scheduling, editorial sign-off, each handled as separate functions with structured handoffs. Every factual claim in a feature is logged with a primary source, a confidence rating, and an expiry date before draft. Every cover passes editor sign-off before publish. Every Founders' Circle piece is read in full by James or Emma before it goes out.

The result is a five-to-seven-day production cycle on a feature that would take most regional publishers five weeks, at the editorial standard of a national title.

That production capacity is what makes the Founders' Circle program possible. A single founding partner at a regional tourism site would normally wait months for a feature. With us, your hero piece can be commissioned, researched, photographed across two seasons, drafted, fact-checked, edited, and scheduled inside the season you bought it for.

## 5. The cohort

Six seats. One cohort. Two years.

The cohort is curated, not auctioned. We are looking for the venues we believe represent the Peninsula at its best across the categories that matter most. We expect to invite candidates from each of the following, with no obligation to fill every category and no second seat in any category that would compromise the editorial coverage:

- One design-led hotel
- One leading restaurant
- One wine producer with a serious cellar door
- One wellness operator
- One experience or cultural venue
- One open seat held back for a venue we do not yet know

Founding partners are listed publicly on the masthead at peninsulainsider.com.au/about, in the about section of the kit, and in the footer of every Insider's Letter. The listing is the public-record version of the invitation. It is not optional and not removable, because the trade is mutual: the publication earns credibility from naming the partners who chose to be at the start.

Once the six seats are filled, the program is closed for the duration of the two-year window. The next opportunity to join the cohort will not come around for at least 24 months, and pricing at that point will reflect a publication with audience.

## 6. What a founding partner gets, across 24 months

Every deliverable below is real work, produced by our desk, signed off by the editor, photographed by our contracted photographer, hosted permanently on the publication.

**Editorial**

- One hero feature, 2,000 to 2,500 words, researched by our desk, drafted to PI standard, signed off by the editor. The most ambitious piece of writing your venue will appear in this year, anywhere.
- One photo essay, 12 to 15 original images, two days of shooting across two seasons. Partner receives a non-exclusive licence to reuse the imagery in their own marketing.
- One short film, 60 to 90 seconds, scripted and shot to the publication's house treatment. Lives on your venue page and rotates on the homepage cover for one season.

**Placement**

- Year-round priority placement on the section hub most relevant to you. Capped at one founding partner per slot.
- One quarter as the homepage cover story across the two-year window. Scheduled in agreement with you for the season that suits your business.
- Two newsletter features, written by the desk, run in The Insider's Letter twice across the year. These are editorial features, not banner ads.
- Embedding in at least two pre-built itineraries and the Build-Your-Own planner, where your venue belongs in the recommendation.

**Recognition**

- Permanent listing as a Founding Partner on the masthead, the about page, the kit, and the footer of every Insider's Letter.
- One annual partner dinner, hosted by James and Emma, for the cohort only. No press, no microphones, no formal program. The conversation is the point.

**The carry-forward promise**

When the publication's audience triples in two years, your hero feature, your photo essay, your film, and your venue page travel with it. Stories written in our launch year will still be the canonical PI feature on your venue when our newsletter is at 50,000 subscribers. That is the underrated benefit of buying in early.

## 7. Investment

**$15,000 AUD per year, exclusive of GST. Locked at this rate for two years.**

Total commitment across the two-year window: $30,000.

Payment terms:

- 50 per cent on signing. Funds editorial production and the first season of photography.
- 25 per cent at month six.
- 25 per cent at month twelve.
- Year two payable annually in advance at the same locked rate.

What this is, in plain terms: you are paying for a year of editorial production, original photography, a short film, year-round placement, and a named seat on a masthead that does not yet have one. It is not a media buy. It is the cost of a small marketing program delivered to magazine standard, with permanence.

For comparison, a single double-page advertorial in a national trade title runs $8,000 to $11,000 AUD for one issue and disappears with the next print run. A two-day photography commission for a hotel runs $4,000 to $7,000 and produces no editorial coverage. A one-time sponsored article in a major lifestyle publication runs $5,000 to $9,000 and links die when the campaign ends. The Founders' Circle delivers the equivalent of all three, plus year-round placement, plus the masthead seat, on a two-year horizon.

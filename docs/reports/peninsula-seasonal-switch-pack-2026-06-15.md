## Seasonal snapshot

- **Season:** Winter (mid-winter, second week of meteorological winter).
- **Weather pattern:** **Classic Peninsula winter.** Overcast, 17 °C, winds 30 km/h. Tuesday brings drizzle and light rain; Wednesday–Thursday clears to partly cloudy with sunny breaks, 15–18 °C. Cold mornings, early dusk (~17:05). This is not bitter alpine cold — it is grey, windy, and short. The kind of weather that sends readers indoors by mid-afternoon.
- **School holiday status:** **Term 2 ends Friday 26 June.** Winter school holidays run **29 Jun – 13 Jul.** Two weeks out. Begin **soft pre-loading** of family-friendly indoor content this week. Do not lead with it — the homepage is still for general readers — but ready the holiday module for activation w/c 22 Jun.
- **Long weekend / public holiday angle:** **King's Birthday — Mon 8 June.** **Spent.** Retire every trace of long-weekend framing immediately. Anything still live reads stale.
- **Winter solstice:** **Sunday 21 June.** One week away. The shortest day of the year. A strong, natural editorial anchor: **lean into the dark.** Frame the week as a deliberate inversion — the less daylight there is, the more reason to make what remains count.
- **Strongest reader angle this week:** **"Make the short day count."** One outdoor breath of air in the morning, one warm indoor anchor by mid-afternoon, one long table in the evening. The Peninsula in winter rewards readers who plan around the daylight, not despite it.

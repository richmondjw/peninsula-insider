**No changes made** to market files. Recommendation: introduce a `market` mood tag or surface `category=market` as a filterable signal for "planning around market days" decisions — this is a distinct reader intent not currently addressed by mood tags.

Still 0 venues. The farm-gate producers added today (`peninsula-fresh-organics`, `red-hill-cheese`, `main-ridge-dairy`) are exactly the venues this tag would serve. The markets are also natural fits.

A `casual-stop` tag would serve the "no booking, we're passing through" decision — distinct from `quick-bite` (food-speed) and `slow` (ambience). This is the highest-value new tag to introduce.

**Recommended action:** Tyler or Sloane approve `casual-stop` as a new mood tag; apply to: red-hill-cheese, peninsula-fresh-organics, main-ridge-dairy, mornington-peninsula-chocolates, somers-general, johnny-ripe, balnarring-market.

### Medium: `date-day` gap still present

`first-date` (12 venues after this pass) + `anniversary` serve adjacent decisions but there's no consolidated "planning a date" tag. The gap is smaller now that jackalope, moke-dining, and villa-mallorca have `first-date`, but venues like portsea-hotel (waterfront, sunset, couples-dominant) and allis-wine-bar still carry the date-day use case without clean signalling.

## Use notes

**Personalisation is mandatory.** No two emails should read the same. The opening line must reference something specific to the recipient, their venue, their season, or the conversation that preceded the email. Anything generic flags the email as a templated send and undoes the work the prospectus is trying to do.

**Length is the message.** Both versions are deliberately shorter than the prospectus. The prospectus does the work of explaining. The email's job is to make the recipient open the PDF.

**Sign with both names.** Every Founders' outreach email is signed by James and Emma. The cohort is a founder relationship, not a publisher-to-business one.

**Reply discipline.** Whoever sends the email is the primary contact for the reply. Forward to the other founder if the conversation needs to scale.

**Tracking.** Maintain a single spreadsheet of outreach: target, category, intro source, date sent, version used, reply received, status, notes. The cohort is small enough that this stays manageable in a single sheet.

**Decline gracefully.** A "no" from a target is a real answer. Thank them, leave the door open, do not chase. Offer the Charter Story tier or a Featured Venue Story as a smaller alternative if the relationship warrants it.

# Eat catalogue verification and recovery rules

Applies to current visitor recommendations and their shared venue template. Existing image rights/presence rules and anchor design rules remain required.

1. Confirm business identity before copying visitor details. A brand, wholesale factory, farm and independently operated shop are not interchangeable destinations. Keep canonical URLs when names or operators change unless a separate redirect decision is made.
2. Correct addresses and contact details only from current operator evidence. A changed address invalidates an inherited map pin until an exact operator marker is established. Never substitute a similarly named business.
3. Record field scope, source URL and check date. A contact-page check does not refresh every menu, award, accessibility claim or whole-record verification date. Use scoped receipts; preserve honest older dates.
4. Remove unsupported recurring dishes, service-day promises, award totals and booking lead times. Link to current operator planning information; do not imply real-time table availability.
5. Parked domains, blocked pages and failed retrieval are source failures, not evidence of permanent closure. Where identity, visitor location or current operation remains insufficiently supported, verification-pause the recommendation and preserve its recovery URL.
6. Paused entries leave current directory/category/map/pairing recommendations, are excluded from sitemap/search, and retain a clear recovery explanation. No directions, reservation promises, current-stop itinerary prompts or new venue-save prompts. Offer alternative planning routes. Do not delete a reader's existing saved history.
7. Keep historical Journal mentions identifiable as historical; a new curation selection does not recertify their original content.
8. Publish only after source receipts, schema/publication gates and changed reader journeys pass. Verify deployment revision and the live page. A passing build or field audit never establishes99/100.

Recurrence checks: `eat-field-facts.test.mjs` protects corrected contradictions and identity distinctions; `eat-fact-recovery.test.mjs` exercises all eight current recovery cases, indexing, actions and pairing exclusions; `eat-flagship.test.mjs` checks full readable planning notes and composed discovery. Updates to a source-bound assertion require a new dated source receipt, not a quieter gate.

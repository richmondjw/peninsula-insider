# Peninsula Insider — LinkedIn Editorial Approach
**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 2 May 2026
**Author:** Emma Richmond

---

## What LinkedIn is for

LinkedIn is not Instagram.

We use it to:
- Share editorial thinking and real recommendations
- Build authority and trust
- Position Peninsula Insider as the go-to source for what's worth doing on the Mornington Peninsula

---

## Our core principle

We are not trying to cover everything.

We are trying to be **unusually useful on the choices that matter.**

---

## How we write posts

Every post should:
- Be clear and easy to read
- Feel considered and slightly opinionated
- Help someone decide what to do or where to go

Avoid:
- Internal language (e.g. "editorial strategy", "content verticals")
- Overly promotional tone
- Generic descriptions

---

## Post types

### 1. Place / Experience (most important)
- Real locations: markets, walks, wineries, restaurants
- Clear takeaway
- Positive, but with judgement
- Tone: *"Worth building a morning around…"*
- **Include a link**

### 2. Editorial / Insight
- How to plan better
- How to think about the Peninsula
- What makes a good day
- **No link needed**

### 3. Guides / Roundups
- Hatted restaurants, best walks, seasonal guides
- Help people plan
- **Include a link**

---

## Links

Only include links when:
- Sharing a guide
- Sharing a feature or article

Do NOT include links on:
- Editorial thinking posts
- Brand positioning posts

---

## Tone guidance

Instead of:
- "skip this" / "avoid this"

Use:
- "what fits the day"
- "where the experience comes together best"
- "worth your time if…"

We are: **selective, not critical.**

---

## Hashtags (keep simple)

Use ONLY:
- `#PeninsulaInsider`
- `#MorningtonPeninsula`
- Optional: `#VisitVictoria`

Do not use:
- `#ContentDesign`
- `#EditorialStrategy`
- `#TravelMedia`

---

## Tagging (important for growth)

Always tag:
- venues
- locations
- businesses (when relevant)

This helps reach, resharing, and partner visibility.

---

## Consistency line (optional but recommended)

> "We'll keep sharing what's actually worth your time on the Mornington Peninsula."

---

## The test before posting

> "Does this help someone decide what to do?"

If yes — it's on track.

---

## Bottom line

We are not posting content.

We are building a trusted local publication that helps people make better decisions.

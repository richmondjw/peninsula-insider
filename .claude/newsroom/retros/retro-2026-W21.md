## 3. What did we learn?

**Three things.**

**First: the quick-notes layer is mature; the feature layer is not.** Peninsula Insider can now reliably run a five-day operational editorial cadence — weather, tone-of-the-day notes, event callouts — without friction. That's not nothing. It's the foundation. But the site's reputation is built on its features, not its notes. The editorial operation is running at 100% on the easy layer and ~30% on the hard layer. That gap is the story of W21.

**Second: provisional freelance slots are editorial debt.** When Hugo and Etta went onto the locked slate before rate and availability were confirmed, we borrowed against a future we didn't own. The Monday meeting felt decisive; it wasn't. A locked slate that carries unconfirmed freelancers is a wishlist with a production label on it. We know the fix.

**Third: format drift is fine, but it needs flagging.** Iris's piece arriving Wednesday in editorial-picks format rather than Friday in article format wasn't a failure — it may have been a better editorial decision. But it happened without being named. That made it harder to evaluate. Did we ship what we planned and change the format? Or did something slip and we narrativised it as a choice? The distinction matters. Name format shifts at Wednesday checkpoint.

## 4. One change for next week

**Freelance confirmation gate: no freelance piece goes on the locked slate without written confirmation from the contributor.**

Starting Monday 25 May (W22), Margot must have, in her possession before Monday commissioning, two things from every freelance contributor on the proposed slate:

1. **Written rate confirmation** — a reply accepting the brief fee.
2. **Explicit diary acknowledgement** — "Yes, I have this in my calendar for [date]."

If Margot does not have both, the piece goes to provisional look-ahead, not the locked slate. The slot stays open until confirmation lands or a staff/house alternative is sourced.

**Owner:** Margot (confirmation collection) + Sloane (enforcement at Monday meeting).  
**Test window:** W22–W24 (three weeks).  
**Success signal:** No freelance slip from an unconfirmed contributor for three consecutive weeks.

This is not a new procedure. It is making explicit what was already implicit, and building a gate so it cannot be skipped under time pressure.

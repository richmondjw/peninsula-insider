## 3. What did we learn?

**Three things. Two are familiar. One is new.**

**First — and this is the familiar one: W25 was supposed to be the recovery week.** It was not. One of five slate pieces shipped confirmed, two of five including draft. This is almost identical to W24 (one confirmed of a similar expected set). The W24 retro was explicit: *"the rhythm broke in W22 and we have been operating in recovery mode since. That ends this week."* It did not end. We are in Week Five of recovery mode. At some point, "recovery mode" is just the operating mode, and we need to name that and decide whether to accept it or change something structural.

**Second — the Wednesday gate works when the checkpoint runs.** Tyler's Editor's Letter was confirmed filed Monday 15 June, consistent with the gate. The gate did its job for one piece. It failed for Lucien because the checkpoint ran late. The gate is sound. The checkpoint is the dependency, and the checkpoint has no backup owner and no hard deadline enforcement. One person — me — is the checkpoint. When I don't file it, the gate doesn't fire, and we are back to finding out on Thursday that the cover doesn't exist.

**Third — and this is the new thing: off-slate pieces are now a pattern, not an anomaly.** The strongest editorial piece two weeks running has been something that wasn't on the slate. The W23 in-house cellar door piece, the W25 Sorrento Solstice Festival Guide. These are good pieces. They are well-timed, well-written, and they have filled genuine editorial gaps. But they exist outside the commissioning process, they have no paper trail, and they appear to be Tyler operating in parallel to the locked slate rather than through it. That is not the contract. The slate is the contract. If Tyler has editorial instincts firing in the background — and clearly he does — those instincts should be coming through Monday commissioning and onto the slate. Off-slate surprises, even good ones, mean the slate is not the contract. It means we are operating two editorial systems simultaneously and only accounting for one of them.

**Owner:** Sloane (checkpoint, primary) + Margot (gate backup, 11:00 trigger).

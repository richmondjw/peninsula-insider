## 2. What the reliable layer looks like now

The pattern the W25 perf brief named — a "reliable layer" (quick notes, weekly picks) that holds regardless of slate pressure, and a "fragile layer" (features, standing columns, the Letter) that consistently slips — has not just continued through W34. The fragile layer stopped existing entirely, and the reliable layer became the only layer. Every single thing that shipped in W34 was automated: a house-byline weekly picks post and daily weather notes. Nothing carried a masthead byline. Nothing went through commissioning, structural edit, or Vera's sign-off gate, because none of those processes ran.

This is not a claim that the automated output is bad — the `insider-picks-2026-08-17.md` piece is specific and well-anchored (Ten Minutes by Tractor Sunday lunch, the Arthurs Seat Eagle, a closing MPRG ceramics show). It is a claim that W34 had zero editorially-commissioned, zero human-bylined, zero Vera-approved content. That is a different site than the one the masthead roles describe.

## 4. Compound observation across the gap (qualitative, not metric)

Because there is no analytics access, this section describes structure, not reader response. Two things are verifiable from the repo alone:

- **The automated pipeline did not stop.** Insider picks, quick notes, and a separate "content factory" campaign system kept the commit log active through the entire ten-week gap (563 commits between 19 June and 27 August touch the repo broadly; dozens specifically touch `next/src/content/articles` and `next/src/content/quick-notes`). The site was never static.
- **At least one piece of automated content is stuck mid-process.** `the-sorrento-off-season-weekend-2026-w33.md` carries `status: "review"` since 28 July — a full month unresolved, neither published nor archived. This is the clearest available evidence that the automated pipeline and the newsroom process are not talking to each other: something entered a `review` state that nobody with editorial authority has been reviewing.

No claim is made here about traffic, subscriber growth, or engagement, because no source for those numbers was consulted. The honest statement is: we do not know how W34 performed with readers. We only know what published.

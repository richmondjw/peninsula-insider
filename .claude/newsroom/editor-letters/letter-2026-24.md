# Editor's Letter — Week 24, 2026

**Published:** Fri 12 Jun 2026, 08:00 AEST
**Drafted:** Sunday 7 June 2026
**Length:** 408 / target 350–450
**Format:** C (scene / narrative)

---

On Tuesday morning, the road out past Red Hill was empty in a way it had not been since April. A single ute ahead of me, a courier van behind, and between us perhaps two kilometres of wet bitumen and crows. The long weekend had ended the night before. Whatever had been here on Saturday — the queue at the bakery, the cars parked on both verges near the lookout, the family arguing pleasantly outside the cheese shop — had packed up and gone home to Brighton.

This is the week the Peninsula returns to its proper inhabitants, and I always find it the most honest stretch of the year. The waitstaff who were rushed off their feet on Sunday are unhurried again. The publican leans on the bar at three in the afternoon and tells you, without being asked, what is good this week. The cellar door pours an extra glass because there is no one else in the room and the bottle is already open. None of this happens when the place is full. None of it can.

I drove out to see one of our regular contributors at a kitchen near Main Ridge that I will not name yet because Lucien is writing about it for next month. The chef was sitting on a milk crate at the back door, doing nothing in particular, which is the activity I most associate with cooks who are about to do something interesting. We talked for forty minutes about a stockpot. I am aware how this sounds. It was the most clarifying conversation I have had in a fortnight.

The slate this week reflects that quieter Peninsula rather than the one the tourist boards prefer. Iris has gone looking for the rooms that did the long weekend well and have something left in the tank. Lucien is on a winter beat that is part field report, part argument. And we have a small piece on which cellar doors are actually keeping their stated hours, because several were not.

There will be busier weeks ahead. This one rewards attention.

— Tyler Lockhart, Editor

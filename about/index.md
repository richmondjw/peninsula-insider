Our editorial approach

## A reason to choose.

We look for what makes a Peninsula place distinctive, who it suits and when it works best. Our recommendations bring together official sources, local context and editorial judgement to help you plan your day.

Practical details are checked against the operator or responsible authority. A source check is not a visit: we distinguish confirmed facts from our judgement and make material gaps clear. A whole-record review date shows when that check was completed, not a guarantee that nothing has changed.

**Method reviewed:** 10 November 2026.

### What earns a recommendation

- #### A reason to go

Something the place does that sets it apart on the Peninsula. A listing has to say what that is in plain words.

- #### A clear fit

We say who it suits and when it works best. Where a place will not suit a reader, we say that too.

- #### Details that hold up

Address, opening, booking and access details are traced to the operator or the responsible authority, not to another guide.

- #### No payment

A business cannot buy a recommendation, a ranking or an Insider Pick. Paid work is labelled and kept apart.

### How a check works

- **Start with the source.** We read the operator's own current pages: its website, booking page and published policies. For an event that is the organiser. For a park, pier or road it is the responsible authority.

- **Rank what disagrees.** Where sources conflict, the operator or authority outranks listings sites, press and social posts.

- **Date the check.** A checked date on a page is the day that source check was completed. It is not a visit, and it is not a promise that nothing has changed since.

- **Qualify, link or pause.** If a material detail cannot be confirmed, we say so, point to the source, or take the listing down until it can be.

Why we’d go

The pie stop the Peninsula has needed for years, and the one visitors end up returning to on every subsequent trip.

Johnny Ripe is the pie stop the Peninsula has needed for years, and the one visitors end up returning to on every subsequent trip. The savoury case is serious, slow-cooked beef pie, a lamb-and-rosemary sausage roll that respects the form, and a daily number that changes with the season, and the sweets cabinet is the other reason to come: lemon tart, meringues, and a rotating set of seasonal fruit tarts that routinely sell out before closing.

The garden café behind the shop is the right place to eat the pie you just bought. Grab a hot pie at the counter, order a coffee, take it outside, let the kids run between the trees. This is the kind of Peninsula stop that turns a winery morning into a properly rounded afternoon without extending the day by more than forty minutes.

Buy more sweets than you planned. Leave one in the car for the drive home.

Why we’d go

A reliable early Balnarring stop for bread, pies and pastries.

The Red Hill Baker no longer operates in Red Hill. Its official website lists Balnarring as its sole location, open daily from 6am to 4pm. The bakery remains useful for bread, pies and pastries, but it must be planned as a Balnarring stop rather than a Red Hill village stop.

# Via Boffe

A small, warm Italian room tucked into the hinterland, handmade pasta, a hand-written weekly menu, and the Peninsula's best neighbourhood trattoria.

Why we’d go

The Peninsula trattoria that deserves much more attention than it gets, a room that could hold its own in any Melbourne inner suburb and instead sits quietly on the ridge.

Via Boffe is doing something in the hinterland that the Peninsula rarely manages: a small, warm Italian room that does not announce itself, does not trade on a view, and does not need to. The room is polished concrete, low lighting, an open kitchen visible from every seat. The menu is a hand-written short list that changes with what the team feels like cooking that week. Handmade pasta is the headline, but the antipasti is where the kitchen wakes up, fresh ricotta with olive oil and cracked pepper, grilled peaches with stracciatella, a wood-fired flatbread that disappears in minutes.

This is the Peninsula version of the Italian room everyone wishes they had in their own suburb. The wine list leans into small Italian producers alongside a short Peninsula local shelf, and the team is happy to steer you toward something unexpected. Go on a weekend booking or on a Tuesday when the room has that particular quiet energy that is better than a full Friday.

Book ahead, the room is tiny and fills fast on weekends. Sit at the counter if you are a pair. Order three pastas between you and finish with the hazelnut torta.

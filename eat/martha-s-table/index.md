Why we’d go

Marina views and a seasonal Mediterranean menu offer a different setting from the ridge winery restaurants.

Martha’s Table Restaurant overlooks the marina at Safety Beach. The operator describes Mediterranean cooking shaped by seasonal produce, with lunch and dinner services on selected days.

Choose the restaurant for a meal with marina views. Its current restaurant page provides menus, a children’s menu and reservations, including set-menu options for groups. Confirm current service dates rather than assuming the restaurant, cafe and bar all keep the same hours.

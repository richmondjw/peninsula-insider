# Family-Friendly Dining on the Mornington Peninsula

Family-friendly dining on the Peninsula means outdoor seating, formats that do not require children to sit completely still for two hours, and venues where the staff have seen a high chair before. The list is shorter than the general dining list, but it is honest: these are the places that work with kids rather than merely tolerating them.

Red Gum BBQ in Red Hill is the clearest call - BBQ format, outdoor tables, and food that children actually want to eat. Montalto Piazza is the best family-friendly winery option: walk-in daily, sculpture trail on the property, outdoor seating, and a kitchen that does not require advance planning. Village cafes and pub beer gardens fill in the practical gaps.

Researched from published sources. Every venue listed was reviewed April 2026 or later.

## Frequently asked questions

### Best family-friendly restaurants on the Peninsula?

Red Gum BBQ (Red Hill): BBQ, outdoor tables, food kids want. Montalto Piazza: walk-in daily, sculpture trail, outdoor. Green Olive at Red Hill: garden, relaxed, dog-friendly.

### Best for young children?

Red Gum BBQ and Montalto Piazza. Flinders General Store for a casual village stop. Most hatted rooms are not suited to young children.

### Family-friendly winery restaurants?

Montalto Piazza (walk-in, sculpture trail) is the clearest. Green Olive at Red Hill (garden, relaxed). Merricks General Wine Store (outdoor seating, casual).

# The best walks on the Mornington Peninsula

Bay-side foreshore paths, clifftop boardwalks over Bass Strait, national-park bush and a 26 km coast-to-coast route. Eleven walks, ranked by what they demand and what they give back.

## The short version

- [Cape Schanck boardwalk](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/walks/#cape-schanck-boardwalk>) is the best effort-to-payoff ratio on the Peninsula. Forty minutes, fully accessible.

- [Bushrangers Bay](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/walks/#bushrangers-bay-walk>) has the best coastal scenery with the least foot traffic of any good walk here.

- [Two Bays Walking Track](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/walks/#two-bays-walking-track>) is the flagship: 26 km, bay to ocean. Most people walk it in sections.

No permits, no specialist gear, walkable all year. Prams or limited mobility: start with the boardwalk or the Mornington foreshore.

The Mornington Peninsula has one of the most varied walking menus of any coastal region in Victoria: foreshore paths on the bay, boardwalks over Bass Strait, national park bush trails, and hinterland ridge-tops through old-growth manna gum that see almost nobody outside school holidays. Every walk below is editorially reviewed, and the date it was last checked is stated at the foot of this page. The ranking weighs effort against payoff - what a walk asks of you, against what it gives back.

## Start here

High payoff, low-to-moderate effort. These four give you the most for the least.

[Cape Schanck](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/cape-schanck-boardwalk/>)

### [Cape Schanck boardwalk](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/cape-schanck-boardwalk/>)

- 1.5 km return

- 40 min

- Easy

The boardwalk descends from the lighthouse precinct to a basalt wave platform with blowholes, sea stacks, and serious Southern Ocean surge. Twenty minutes each way and entirely accessible. The best short coastal experience on the Peninsula by a margin.

**Best for** Families, first-timers, those with limited mobility.

Start: Cape Schanck lighthouse car park

[Cape Schanck](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/bushrangers-bay-walk/>)

### [Bushrangers Bay walk](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/bushrangers-bay-walk/>)

- 6 km return

- 2 h

- Moderate

Through coastal scrub and cliff-edge heath to an isolated cove. The bay itself is small, protected, and sees far fewer visitors than any equivalent. Moderate fitness required; no navigation difficulty.

**Best for** Walkers wanting genuine solitude; strong views on the descent.

Start: Bushrangers Bay car park off Boneo Road

[Rosebud / Dromana](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/greens-bush-two-bays-section/>)

### [Two Bays Walking Track: Greens Bush section](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/greens-bush-two-bays-section/>)

- 8 km return

- 2.5 h

- Moderate

The best standalone section of the Two Bays track. Old-growth manna gum, wallabies in numbers, and quiet - genuinely quiet - for a place this close to Melbourne. Do this before the full track commitment.

**Best for** Those wanting a longer walk with bush character.

Start: Greens Bush picnic area

[Portsea](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/point-nepean-fort-walk/>)

### [Point Nepean fort walk](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/point-nepean-fort-walk/>)

- 10 km return

- 2.5 to 3 h

- Easy-moderate

The walk to Fort Nepean at the peninsula's tip, through decommissioned military land with intact fortifications, historic plaques, and unobstructed views across the Bass Strait entry to Port Phillip.

**Best for** History-focused walkers; those wanting the full end-of-land experience.

Start: Point Nepean Visitor Centre, Portsea

### [Cape Schanck lighthouse walk](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/cape-schanck-lighthouse-walk/>)

- 3.5 km circuit

- 1.5 h

- Moderate

Extends the boardwalk experience into a loop above the cliffs. Better for those wanting a proper walk rather than a stroll to the platform. The return via the lighthouse precinct adds context.

**Best for** Those combining the boardwalk with a longer circuit.

Start: Cape Schanck lighthouse car park

[Dromana to Cape Schanck](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/two-bays-walking-track/>)

### [Two Bays Walking Track: full route](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/two-bays-walking-track/>)

- 26 km point-to-point

- Full day

- Demanding

The Peninsula's flagship long walk - from Port Phillip Bay to Bass Strait, crossing the hinterland through Greens Bush. Most visitors tackle the 8–10 km sections near either end.

**Best for** Experienced day-walkers; multi-section visits.

Start: Dromana

[Arthurs Seat](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/summit-circuit-arthurs-seat/>)

### [Arthurs Seat summit circuit](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/summit-circuit-arthurs-seat/>)

- 2.5 km circuit

- 1 h

- Moderate

If you drive to the summit (or take the gondola), this circuit delivers the full 360-degree view sequence - bay, hinterland, ocean. Short but steep in places.

**Best for** Those combining with the Arthurs Seat Eagle; families.

Start: Arthurs Seat State Park summit car park

[Sorrento](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/coppins-track/>)

### [Coppins Track](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/coppins-track/>)

- 4 km return

- 1.5 h

- Easy-moderate

A cliff-edge track connecting Sorrento's bay to its back beach through tea-tree heath. One of the few walks that meaningfully crosses the Peninsula's two-face geography.

**Best for** Sorrento-based visitors; those wanting bay-to-ocean connection.

Start: Sullivan Bay, Sorrento

## What to bring

No walk on this list requires specialist equipment. For any walk over 5 km: water (1 L minimum), sunscreen, a hat, and footwear with grip. Ocean-side tracks can be slippery when wet.

## FAQ

What is the best walk on the Mornington Peninsula?

Cape Schanck boardwalk for the best effort-to-payoff ratio. Bushrangers Bay for the best uncrowded experience. Two Bays Walking Track for the best full-day walk.

Is the Two Bays Walking Track a full day?

The complete 26 km route is a full day for fit walkers. Most visitors do one of the standalone sections - Greens Bush at the Dromana end, or the Cape Schanck sections at the ocean end. Each section can be done in 2–4 hours.

Do I need to pay to walk at Point Nepean?

Entry to Point Nepean National Park is free. Most walkers park at the visitor centre and either walk or take the ticketed shuttle toward the fort.

Are there easy walks suitable for families and young children?

Yes. Cape Schanck boardwalk is accessible and short. Mornington foreshore walk is flat and pram-friendly. Arthurs Seat summit circuit is manageable for children 5+ with adult supervision.

What is the best walk in the Mornington Peninsula National Park?

The Bushrangers Bay walk and the Cape Schanck boardwalk are both within or adjacent to the national park. The Bushrangers Bay walk has better scenery per effort unit for experienced walkers.

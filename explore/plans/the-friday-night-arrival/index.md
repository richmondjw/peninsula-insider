# The Friday Night Arrival

You leave the city at four. You hit the freeway. You arrive at six with nothing booked and nothing defrosted. Here is what to do with the first three hours, the ones that set the tone for everything that follows.

The Peninsula weekend does not start at the cellar door on Saturday morning. It starts at six o’clock on Friday evening when you pull into the driveway of wherever you are staying and realise that you are hungry, the sun is going down, and the weekend has technically begun but you have not yet done anything with it.

This is the gap. The Friday night arrival. It is easy to fumble: a mediocre takeaway, a rummage through whatever the last guest left in the rental kitchen, a vague sense that the weekend will start properly tomorrow. The first evening sets the rhythm. Handle it well and Saturday feels expansive.

Here are the three ways to handle it.

## Option one: the pub

A pub close to your stay is a simpler Friday arrival. Check its current kitchen hours and reserve a table on busy weekends before you leave. These are the areas to consider:

**Staying in the Red Hill hinterland:** Drive to **The Heritage** at Balnarring. The kitchen keeps a pub favourites list alongside a specials board, and there is a big beer garden for warm evenings. Call ahead to book a table. You are now on Peninsula time.

**Staying in Flinders or Shoreham:** **Flinders Hotel** bistro. Check Friday dinner hours and book ahead. The food is a step above standard pub fare. If you plan to drink, arrange a safe way back to your stay.

**Staying in Sorrento or Portsea:** **Hotel Sorrento** front bar. The front bar menu is lighter and faster than the bistro and better suited to the energy of an arrival. Oysters, chips, a glass of something cold. Keep the stop close to your room.

**Staying in Mornington or Mount Martha:** **Mornington Hotel** or **The Bay Hotel**. Both are proper locals’ pubs with reliable kitchens. The Mornington Hotel has the better beer garden; The Bay has the better view.

**Staying in Balnarring or Somers:** **The Heritage**, in the middle of Balnarring village. This is the one you book if you know you are arriving on a Friday. Call ahead.

The pub arrival works when the kitchen hours and table are settled before you drive. You drive from Melbourne, you walk into the pub, you eat and drink, you go to the rental. The weekend starts without you having to think about it.

## Option two: the provisions stop

If you would rather eat at the rental, especially if you have a good kitchen and would rather open wine on the deck than sit in a dining room after ninety minutes in the car, the provisions stop is the play.

The principle: decide where you can still buy provisions on your route, and check closing times before setting off. Buy what you need for Friday dinner and Saturday breakfast, then go straight to your stay.

**The Red Hill stop:** Check the Friday hours of the shop you plan to use before leaving Melbourne. If you also want wine from **Merricks General Wine Store**, check its closing time and count it as a second stop; otherwise buy everything en route. Keep dinner simple: bread, cheese, a main item and something to drink.

**The Mornington stop:** Choose a deli or supermarket on your route with confirmed Friday hours. Do not rely on a cafe still being open after the evening drive.

**The Flinders stop:** Check **Flinders General Store’s** Friday closing time before relying on it. If your arrival will be later, buy provisions on the way rather than adding a last-minute detour.

**The Sorrento stop:** The IGA in Sorrento for basics. The Continental bottle shop for wine if you want to spend more.

The provisions-stop dinner looks like this: bread torn from a loaf, cheese on a board, olives, cold chicken or charcuterie, a salad if you are feeling virtuous, wine, and the deck. It takes little time to assemble and keeps the evening flexible. On a still autumn evening, it is one of the loveliest dinners the Peninsula offers.

## Option three: the booked dinner

If you have a dinner reservation, Friday can start at the table.

Check the restaurant’s current Friday dinner service and allow for traffic before choosing a reservation time. These are two options with advertised Friday dinner service:

- **Bistro Elba.** Sorrento’s Southern French bistro. Reserve or check availability before leaving; the village is outside the window.

- **Red Gum BBQ.** A relaxed barbecue dinner in Red Hill. Check the current Friday dining hours and book or order ahead if the venue recommends it.

The booked dinner is the Friday arrival for couples without kids who want the weekend to start at the table rather than on the couch. It works. It just requires the forward planning that the other two options do not.

## The Saturday-morning dividend

The reason the Friday night arrival matters is Saturday morning. If Friday night was the pub, you wake up feeling like you have already been here a day. If Friday night was the provisions board on the deck, you wake up with bread and eggs in the kitchen and no reason to leave the house until eleven. If Friday night was a proper dinner, you wake up feeling like the weekend has already delivered something.

In all three cases, Saturday morning starts from a position of calm rather than urgency. You are not trying to find breakfast and a cellar door and a lunch booking while simultaneously remembering to buy sunscreen. You are standing in the kitchen with a coffee, looking at the view, wondering whether to do anything before noon.

That feeling is the point of a Peninsula weekend. It starts on Friday night.

## A few practical answers.

**What should I do on Friday night when arriving on the Mornington Peninsula?**

Three options depending on energy and planning: the pub (The Heritage at Balnarring for hinterland stays, where it pays to call ahead; Flinders Hotel for the south; Hotel Sorrento front bar for the coast); the provisions stop (bread, cheese, chicken, and wine from the nearest good deli en route, eaten on the deck); or a reserved dinner at Bistro Elba in Sorrento or Red Gum BBQ in Red Hill after checking current Friday service. A pub is simpler, but check its kitchen hours and reserve on busy weekends.

**Which Peninsula pubs are best for Friday night dinner on arrival?**

The Heritage at Balnarring for Red Hill hinterland stays (open fires, a big beer garden, a pub favourites menu, call ahead to book). Flinders Hotel for southern Peninsula stays (check Friday dinner hours and book ahead). Hotel Sorrento front bar for Sorrento, ordering from the bar menu rather than the bistro.

**Can I do a provisions stop instead of eating out on Friday night on the Peninsula?**

Yes. Choose a shop on your route, check its Friday closing time, and buy dinner and breakfast before reaching your stay. If you plan a second stop for wine, check that opening time too.

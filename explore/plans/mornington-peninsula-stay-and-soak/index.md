A spa trip works better when the last part of the day is simple. Choose the bathing venue first, then find a room you are happy to return to after the pools or a treatment.

Alba Thermal Springs and Peninsula Hot Springs are both in Fingal. Each has accommodation on its own grounds. A one-night stay can work on site; two nights leave more space for dinner and a walk. A hotel elsewhere can be just as good if the drive is part of the plan.

## Stay on site in Fingal

### Alba: The Sanctuary

[The Sanctuary at Alba](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/stay/sanctuary-at-alba/>) has five villas and two rooms above Alba’s thermal springs. The [operator’s current accommodation guide](<https://albathermalsprings.com.au/alba-experiences/the-sanctuary/>) lists bathing entry for each day of the stay and breakfast at Thyme. Spa treatments need their own booking. Choose this when Alba is the reason for the trip and you want dinner, breakfast and bathing on one estate.

### Peninsula Hot Springs: Eco Lodges

[Peninsula Hot Springs Eco Lodges](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/stay/peninsula-hot-springs-eco-lodges/>) are a separate stay from the glamping tents. The [current Eco Lodge choices](<https://www.peninsulahotsprings.com/accommodation/eco-lodges>) include bathing and breakfast; private geothermal bathing depends on the room selected. Compare a Springs Room with a Peninsula Suite before reserving. Accommodation is for guests aged 16 and over, with an accompanying adult required for guests aged 16 to 18.

### Peninsula Hot Springs: glamping

[Peninsula Hot Springs Glamping](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/stay/peninsula-hot-springs-glamping/>) also keeps you on the springs property. The furnished tents have heated floors and heating and cooling, while the paths and pools remain outdoors. [Current glamping packages](<https://www.peninsulahotsprings.com/accommodation/glamping>) include geothermal bathing and breakfast, with access details varying by booking. Check which bathing areas your room includes and reserve any treatment separately.

The current Peninsula Hot Springs accommodation pages list a 10am room checkout with continued bathing afterwards. Confirm the access window on your own booking before planning your last morning.

## Stay off site with a drive

### Jackalope: design, dinner and an optional hotel spa

[Jackalope](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/stay/jackalope/>) in Merricks North gives you a hotel stay, Doot Doot Doot for dinner and [Spa by Jackalope](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/spas-and-wellness/#spa-by-jackalope>) on the property. The [spa welcomes day visitors](<https://jackalopehotels.com/spa/>) seven days a week, so a hotel booking is not required for a treatment. If Alba or Peninsula Hot Springs is your bathing anchor, this choice still involves a separate drive to Fingal. Book the room, treatment and dinner you want, then check the route between them.

### Lindenderry: a Red Hill country stay

[Lindenderry](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/stay/lindenderry/>) offers gardens, vineyard views and an on-site Dining Room. It changes the mood of the trip, but it does not remove the drive to either Fingal bathhouse. Check the restaurant’s current service against your dates and book a table before relying on dinner at the hotel.

### Hotel Sorrento: village and coast

[Hotel Sorrento](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/stay/hotel-sorrento/>) suits a weekend that also includes Sorrento village dining and coastal walks. The hotel has its own guest wellness facilities, but a visit to either Fingal springs is a separate, longer drive. Choose this base for the village as well as the baths, not for the shortest transfer.

## Make the spa the destination

### RACV Cape Schanck Resort and One Spa

At [RACV Cape Schanck Resort](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/spas-and-wellness/#one-spa-racv-cape-schanck>), the room and One Spa are on the same property. That makes a resort spa day different from a Fingal hot-springs visit. The [current One Spa Escape offer](<https://www.racv.com.au/travel-experiences/resorts/cape-schanck/offers/one-spa-escape.html>) is limited to Monday to Friday stays and spa use; check regular weekend spa access and treatment availability separately.

### Endota Mornington and a town stay

[Endota Mornington](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/spas-and-wellness/#endota-spa-mornington>) is a Main Street day spa, suited to a treatment alongside a Mornington meal and foreshore walk. Choose accommodation in town if the village is the centre of the stay. This is a day-spa plan rather than a thermal-springs weekend.

## Before you book

- **Confirm the pair.** Check the room and bathing availability for the same dates before committing to either. A hotel spa treatment may need its own reservation.

- **Read the inclusions.** On-site accommodation can include different bathing areas, private facilities, breakfast and guest-age rules. Use the operator’s current room description.

- **Plan the return.** For an off-site evening session, check the actual drive to your room and keep dinner close to where you sleep.

- **Leave space.** A one-night on-site stay can be enough. If you want a second spa, long lunch or coastal walk, give the trip another night rather than stacking everything after a bath.

For a simple Friday-to-Sunday sequence built around one Fingal bathhouse, see [The Peninsula Thermal Springs Weekend](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/plans/the-thermal-springs-weekend/>).

## A few practical answers.

**Where can I stay on site at the Peninsula's two major hot springs?**

Alba has The Sanctuary, with villas and rooms on its Fingal estate. Peninsula Hot Springs has Eco Lodges and glamping on its grounds. Check the chosen room's bathing access, guest age rules and other inclusions before booking.

**Is Peninsula Hot Springs glamping suitable in cooler weather?**

Its current glamping rooms have heated floors and heating and cooling. Outdoor bathing and the walks between facilities are still exposed to the weather, so plan for the conditions on your dates.

**Do I need two nights for a stay-and-soak trip?**

No. An on-site overnight can keep a short trip simple. Two nights give more room for dinner, a treatment or a coastal walk. Check the minimum stay for the accommodation and dates you choose.

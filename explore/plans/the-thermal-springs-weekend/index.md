More planning notes

- Choose one Fingal bathhouse and book two consecutive nights with a comfortable route after bathing.

- The Sanctuary is on Alba's estate; Eco Lodges and glamping are on Peninsula Hot Springs' grounds. Red Hill stays involve a drive.

- Friday is for arrival, Saturday for bathing and Sunday can remain open. A day trip works if the schedule stays light.

Choose one bathhouse for the weekend. Alba Thermal Springs and Peninsula Hot Springs are both in Fingal, but the bathing experience and on-site accommodation differ. Settle the room and the bath booking for the same dates, then leave the rest of the weekend loose.

## Choose a bathhouse and a base

### Alba and The Sanctuary

[Alba Thermal Springs](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/spas-and-wellness/#alba-thermal-springs>) has contemporary pools, private bathing choices and spa treatments in a quieter, design-led setting. [The Sanctuary at Alba](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/stay/sanctuary-at-alba/>) puts a villa or room on the same estate, with [current accommodation inclusions](<https://albathermalsprings.com.au/alba-experiences/the-sanctuary/>) listing springs entry on each day of the stay and breakfast at Thyme. Arrange any treatment separately. This is the simplest Alba-based stay when you want to bathe without a return drive.

### Peninsula Hot Springs on site

[Peninsula Hot Springs](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/spas-and-wellness/#peninsula-hot-springs>) has a wider Bath House circuit, with more than 50 bathing and wellness experiences. The Spa Dreaming Centre is a separate area for guests aged 16 and over. Choose the access you want before booking; the entry types are not interchangeable.

For an overnight stay, compare [Eco Lodges](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/stay/peninsula-hot-springs-eco-lodges/>) with [glamping](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/stay/peninsula-hot-springs-glamping/>). Both are on the springs grounds and current packages include bathing, but room layouts and private-bath facilities differ. The [operator’s accommodation pages](<https://www.peninsulahotsprings.com/accommodation/>) set out the current inclusions and guest-age rules. Check those details for your room, and reserve any treatment you want ahead of time.

### A Red Hill base

[Lindenderry](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/stay/lindenderry/>) and [Polperro Villas](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/stay/polperro-villas/>) offer a vineyard setting after a Fingal bath. Neither removes the drive back from the springs, so check the evening route and reserve dinner near your room. Polperro’s [current stay rules](<https://www.polperrowines.com.au/escape/villas/>) require two nights on a standard weekend and three nights over public-holiday long weekends and the peak summer period. Check your exact dates before building the plan around it.

## The two-night rhythm

### Friday: arrive and settle in

Book the same room for Friday and Saturday nights. Arrive without a second major reservation to race towards. If you are staying on a bathhouse estate, confirm when the room’s bathing access begins. If you are staying off site, keep dinner near your accommodation.

### Saturday: bathe at your own pace

Use the arrival time and access included in your Alba or Peninsula Hot Springs booking. Plan for one venue, not both. A treatment can be added if it is available, but leave a gap to change, rest and get to dinner. If you stay at Alba, Thyme is on the estate; elsewhere, choose a table near your room.

**Booking check, 30 September 2026:** [Alba’s booking page](<https://albathermalsprings.com.au/book/>) lists springs and spa experiences. [Peninsula Hot Springs’ Bath House page](<https://www.peninsulahotsprings.com/bathe/bath-house/revitalise>) lists more than 50 Bath House experiences and recommends booking ahead. Confirm the current arrival time and access directly with your chosen venue.

### Sunday: keep some space

Have breakfast, then decide whether another bath included with your stay or an optional [Cape Schanck Boardwalk](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/cape-schanck-boardwalk/>) walk suits the weather and your energy. Check the signed route and current access before setting out. Leaving Sunday open is also a sound choice.

### If you only have one day

A day trip works: book one bathhouse visit, make any meal a simple addition and allow for the journey home. The two-night version earns its place when you want the bathing, dinner and a slower morning without stacking them into one day.

## Before you book

- **Confirm both reservations.** Check accommodation and bathing availability for the same dates before committing to either. Book treatments separately when needed.

- **Read the access details.** Bath House, Spa Dreaming Centre, private bathing and accommodation packages have different inclusions and age rules.

- **Check the practical route.** An on-site stay removes the return drive after bathing. A Red Hill stay changes the setting but requires that drive.

- **Let the weather decide Sunday.** The coast is optional; check conditions and park access before a walk.

For more room-by-room choices, use [Where to Stay for a Peninsula Spa Trip](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/plans/mornington-peninsula-stay-and-soak/>).

Questions readers actually ask

## A few practical answers.

**How much time should I allow for bathing?**

Check the access and arrival details for the experience you book. Leave time to change before dinner or another reservation, follow the venue's bathing guidance and bring a water bottle.

**What is the difference between Peninsula Hot Springs and Alba?**

Peninsula Hot Springs has a broad Bath House circuit and a separate Spa Dreaming Centre for guests aged 16 and over. Alba offers contemporary pools, private bathing choices and spa treatments. Compare the current access and inclusions before choosing one.

**Can I do this as a day trip?**

Yes. Book one bathing visit, leave the rest of the day light and allow for the return drive. Two nights make room for a local dinner and an optional coastal walk without rushing.

# Family-Friendly Things to Do on the Mornington Peninsula

**TL;DR**

- The Peninsula works well for families but the best family activities are concentrated on the bay side and in specific hinterland attractions - not the ocean beach side.

- [Arthurs Seat Eagle](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/arthurs-seat-lookout/>) is the single most consistently useful family attraction: short, visual, and manageable.

- [Balnarring Beach](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/balnarring-beach/>) and [Safety Beach](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/safety-beach-foreshore/>) are the best bay beaches for children under 8.

- Ashcombe Maze is more engaging for children than its description suggests.

The Mornington Peninsula, Victoria, Australia is a genuine family destination - but it works best with clear category thinking. The Peninsula's most celebrated experiences (hot springs, winery lunches, sunset walks) don't map well to children. What does map well: calm bay beaches, the gondola, the maze, specific wildlife, a handful of foreshore walks, and a market morning.

## Beaches

### Best for young children (under 8)

[**Balnarring Beach**](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/balnarring-beach/>) - Shallow gradient, small waves, clear water, foreshore shading. The Peninsula's best toddler-friendly beach by a margin.

[**Safety Beach foreshore**](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/safety-beach-foreshore/>) - Long and accessible, easy parking, calm water. Less character than Balnarring but consistently uncrowded.

[**Mount Martha Beach**](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/mount-martha-beach/>) - Slightly more facilities nearby than Balnarring. Good for children 5+ who want a longer beach run.

### What to avoid for young families

Ocean beaches (Sorrento Back Beach, Gunnamatta, Rye Ocean Beach). Rip activity and cold water make these unsuitable for swimming with children. Fine for a clifftop walk, not for beach play.

## Attractions

[**Arthurs Seat Eagle**](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/arthurs-seat-lookout/>) - The gondola to the Arthurs Seat summit. 15 minutes each way. Manageable for all ages. The summit views are genuinely arresting - you see both the bay side and the ocean side simultaneously. The [Arthurs Seat Eagle visitor guide](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/journal/arthurs-seat-eagle-visitor-guide/>) has timings and booking advice. Pairs naturally with [Dromana Beach](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/dromana-beach/>) nearby.

**Ashcombe Maze and Lavender Gardens - Shoreham** - More engaging than it sounds. The hedge maze holds genuine mystery for children. The lavender gardens give parents something. Recommend ages 5+; the maze is better for children who can navigate independently.

## Walks

[**Cape Schanck boardwalk**](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/cape-schanck-boardwalk/>) - Short, flat, dramatic. Children understand what they're looking at immediately - the blowholes and sea platforms are engaging without narration. 30–40 minutes round trip.

[**Mornington foreshore walk**](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/mornington-foreshore-walk/>) - Flat, accessible, with café stops. Good for families with prams.

[**Arthurs Seat summit circuit**](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/summit-circuit-arthurs-seat/>) - The circuit from the gondola top station. Manageable for children 6+ with adult supervision.

## Markets

[Hill & Ridge Community Market](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/whats-on/hill-ridge-community-market-september-2026-restart/>) - first Saturdays from September to May at Red Hill Recreation Reserve, 9am to 2pm. Check for weather cancellations before travelling.

For a bayside alternative, check the [Rye Foreshore Market](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/eat/rye-beachside-market/>) first-Saturday listing with the Shire before travelling.

## Food

Family dining on the Peninsula is better than the national average. Most good cafés and mid-range restaurants accommodate children without drama. High chairs are standard in the better family-oriented areas (Mornington, Dromana, Safety Beach). Avoid expecting high-end winery restaurant dining to be child-friendly without checking first.

## FAQ

### What is the best beach on the Mornington Peninsula for families with young children?

Balnarring Beach - shallow, calm, clear water, easy parking. Safety Beach is a close second.

### Is Peninsula Hot Springs suitable for children?

Peninsula Hot Springs has a children's bathing area, but the experience is primarily designed for adults. Ages 5+ can enjoy parts of it; under-5s are a judgment call.

### What is the best paid attraction for families on the Mornington Peninsula?

Arthurs Seat Eagle gondola for children 2–12. Ashcombe Maze for children 5+.

### What is the best season for a family Peninsula trip?

Summer for beaches. The school holidays (January, July) are crowded; October and early December have good weather with lower crowds.

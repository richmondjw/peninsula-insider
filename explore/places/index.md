## Bay coast or the Ocean side

The Peninsula splits cleanly along which water each place faces. Knowing this changes what you book.

Bay side - Port Phillip

### Calm water. Family-tilted. Summer-bright.

**Towns**

Mornington, Mount Martha, Dromana, Rosebud, Safety Beach

**Water**

Sheltered. Shallow, calm, safe for young children. Warmer in summer.

**Best for**

Family weekends, casual day trips, beach swimming, foreshore walks.

Ocean side - Bass Strait

### Wild water. Limestone cliffs. Year-round drama.

**Towns**

Sorrento, Portsea, Rye, Cape Schanck, Flinders, Shoreham

**Water**

Open Bass Strait. Surf, rips, surge. Cold year-round.

**Best for**

Walkers, winter visits, dramatic scenery, surfers, solitude-seekers.

## Five honest answers about Peninsula geography

Which town should I stay in on the Peninsula?

It depends on what you're doing. For wineries and restaurants: Red Hill or Merricks. For the most dramatic scenery and beach options: Sorrento or Portsea. For a family-friendly bay beach base: Rosebud, Dromana, or Mount Martha. For something understated and local: Flinders or Balnarring. Read [where to base yourself](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/stay/best-accommodation/>) for the full breakdown.

How many towns can I cover in a day trip from Melbourne?

Realistically two, maybe three if they're adjacent. Red Hill and Mornington pair well. Sorrento and Portsea are 10 minutes apart. Cape Schanck and Flinders work together. Don't try to cross from the bay coast to the tip in a single day trip - it's 90 minutes of driving.

Are all the towns accessible by public transport?

Most towns have bus connections from Frankston (which has a train from Melbourne). Sorrento, Portsea, Dromana, Rosebud, and Rye are covered by bus route 788. Flinders, Balnarring, and Shoreham are on route 782. Hastings has its own train station on the Stony Point line. Moorooduc, Tuerong, Red Hill, Main Ridge, and Merricks have limited or no public transport - a car is required.

What's the difference between the bay beaches and the ocean beaches?

Bay beaches (Port Phillip side) are calm, shallow, and safe for young children - think bathing boxes and paddling. Ocean beaches (Bass Strait side) have surf, rips, and a wild energy - better for surfing and dramatic walks. Sorrento and Portsea uniquely have both: a front beach on the bay, a back beach on the ocean.

Is the Peninsula worth visiting in winter?

Strongly yes. The restaurants are quieter, the cellar doors are unhurried, and the dramatic ocean beaches are at their best in cold weather. Winter is when Peninsula Insider recommends [Flinders](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/places/flinders/>) and [Cape Schanck](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/places/cape-schanck/>) most aggressively. Read [winter on the Peninsula](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/journal/mornington-peninsula-in-winter/>) for the itinerary.

# Hastings

Hastings is a port town on the Mornington Peninsula's Western Port shoreline, about 75 km from Melbourne, with a public pier, foreshore and working commercial harbour.

Hastings sits on the Western Port side of the Peninsula, with a working harbour, mangrove-fringed foreshore and a pier for a slow waterfront walk. It is a practical town rather than a polished resort. Come for the Western Port outlook and a stop on the way south; check current local seafood sellers before making seafood the reason for a special trip. The ferry to French Island and Phillip Island boards at Stony Point, a separate trip south of Hastings, not at Hastings Pier.

### [Balnarring](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/places/balnarring/>)

Balnarring is the village that locals still treat as their own - a cluster of shops, the Emu Plains Market on the third Saturday of the month through the warmer months, a village pub in a 1930s heritage home, and a road network that somehow always seems to lead back to a cellar door. It sits on the eastern edge of the Peninsula's wine country, closer to Western Port than Port Phillip, and that changes the character: the light is softer, the crowds are thinner, and the vineyards here tend to be smaller family operations rather than destination estates. Balnarring is where you go when you want a Peninsula weekend that feels like the way things used to work before the region became a brand. The beach is genuine and quiet, the coffee is unpretentious, and the drive in from Tedesca or Montalto takes fifteen minutes of roads you've mostly never seen.

Ocean Coast · town

### [Flinders](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/places/flinders/>)

Flinders sits on the wild southern edge of the Peninsula and carries itself with a kind of salt-air confidence. The village is compact but smart - a bakery, a handful of good shops, an old pub, and a coastline that feels a world away from the bay-facing polish further north. Base yourself here if you want your mornings to begin with wind off Bass Strait, your afternoons with a drive back inland for lunch, and your evenings under a harder, cleaner sky.

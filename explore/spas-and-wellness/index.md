# Day spas and wellness on the Mornington Peninsula

Thermal springs, treatment rooms in converted properties, resort spas above the ocean. This is the treatment side of the Peninsula's wellness offering, ranked.

## The short version

- [Spa by Jackalope](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/spas-and-wellness/#spa-by-jackalope>) is the design benchmark. Treatments sell out; book 2 to 3 weeks ahead for weekends.

- [Endota Spa Mornington](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/spas-and-wellness/#endota-spa-mornington>) is the mid-range call: reliable menu, easy to book.

- [Peninsula Hot Springs](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/spas-and-wellness/#peninsula-hot-springs>) bathing plus an on-site treatment is the most complete wellness day here.

This page covers treatment-led spas. For the bathing side, [the hot springs guide](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/hot-springs/>) has the full picture.

Within an hour's drive, Mornington to Cape Schanck, the Peninsula holds more genuine spa and wellness options than most Australian regions manage across a whole state: thermal springs, treatment-focused day spas in converted properties, and resort spa facilities. ## [Spa by Jackalope](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/spas-and-wellness/#spa-by-jackalope>)

- Hotel spa

- Book 2 to 3 weeks out

The Peninsula's most architecturally considered spa experience, integrated into the Jackalope hotel on the Red Hill to Merricks corridor. Treatment rooms are among the best-appointed in regional Victoria. Menu covers massage, facials, and body treatments. A non-guest spa day is bookable, though treatments sell out; book 2 to 3 weeks ahead for weekends.

**Best for** Couples seeking a design-led spa experience; those staying at Jackalope.

## [Endota Spa Mornington](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/spas-and-wellness/#endota-spa-mornington>)

- Day spa

- Easy to book

The Mornington outpost of the Endota chain - the Peninsula's strongest mid-market spa option. Good treatment menu, reliable quality, easy to book compared with Jackalope. Combines naturally with a Mornington town day or a morning at the farmers market.

**Best for** Northern Peninsula stays; mid-range spa days; uncomplicated booking.

## [Peninsula Hot Springs: spa treatments](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/explore/spas-and-wellness/#peninsula-hot-springs>)

## FAQ

What is the best spa on the Mornington Peninsula?

Spa by Jackalope is the highest design standard. Endota Spa Mornington offers the best mid-range experience. For a combination of thermal bathing and treatment, Peninsula Hot Springs' on-site spa is the most comprehensive.

Can you do a spa day on the Mornington Peninsula without staying overnight?

Yes. All day spas listed offer day-visitor bookings. Peninsula Hot Springs also accepts day visitors for bathing. Book treatments well in advance for weekends.

What is the difference between Peninsula Hot Springs and a day spa?

Peninsula Hot Springs is primarily a thermal bathing experience - outdoor pools at various temperatures. A day spa offers booked treatment sessions with a therapist. Peninsula Hot Springs also has on-site treatment facilities, bridging the two.

Is Jackalope Spa open to non-guests?

Yes, treatments are bookable for non-guests. Availability is limited - book 2–3 weeks ahead for weekend slots.

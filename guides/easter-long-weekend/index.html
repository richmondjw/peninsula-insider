A Mornington Peninsula Easter Long-Weekend

Four days, the vintage on the ridge, the markets at full programme, the season tipping. The four-day pacing the rest of the year doesn't allow.

Eight pages · Six venues · One four-day plan · Updated May 2026

Four days is the length the Peninsula was built for.

Two-night weekends are the Peninsula's native rhythm. Easter gives you four - and the vintage, and the markets, and a calendar quiet enough that the cellar doors aren't running tour buses through the rooms. The trade-off is that everyone else has worked this out, so book ahead.

This long-weekend guide is six venues we'd put at the centre of an Easter Friday-to-Monday, sequenced into a four-day plan with one anchor per day and breathing room between them. Use it as a starting frame, not a fixed schedule. Easter rewards loafing more than the calendar pretends.

The six

  1. Montalto Vineyard & Olive Grove

    $$$ · red hill

    Single Vineyard Pinot Noir and a Sunday lunch beneath the olive grove canopy.

  2. Red Hill Estate

    $$$ · red hill

    One of the Peninsula's most photographed cellar-door positions, sweeping Western Port views and a serious traditional-method sparkling program.

  3. Foxeys Hangout

    $$ · red hill

    Biodynamic Red Hill estate with an all-day deck, sunny long lunches, and a sparkling program that holds its own against anything French.

  4. Pt. Leo Estate

    $$$ · merricks

    A sculpture park walk followed by a long bay-view lunch in the glass-walled restaurant.

  5. Flinders Hotel

    $$$ · flinders

    A polished village-base stay with proper pub energy downstairs and Bass Strait within easy reach.

  6. Polperro

    $$$ · red hill

    Small-production cool-climate pinot and chardonnay poured in one of the prettiest tasting rooms on the ridge.

An Easter four-day plan

  1. Good Friday

    Drive down. Light dinner near the hotel; everything closes early. The bay walks at golden hour are the best of the weekend - Friday is when nobody is on them yet.

  2. Saturday

    Red Hill Market in the morning. Cellar door lunch on the ridge - book before you leave the city. Afternoon is light: a coast walk or a swim. Dinner at the hotel.

  3. Easter Sunday

    The long lunch is today. Two hours minimum. Walk it off afterwards along the bay or up Arthurs Seat. Dinner is small; the wine bill from lunch will speak for itself.

  4. Easter Monday

    Slow morning, no plans. Lunch is a counter meal in a Sorrento or Flinders pub. Drive home before 3 - Easter Monday traffic is its own genre of bad.

The useful version of a winter week is not a full diary. It is one good reason to leave the house each day, and enough room around it for the weather to have a say.

Sunday 9 August · Merricks

The plan that anchors the weekend

Stonier Fire & Wine Winter Lunch

Stonier Wines puts the long-table version of winter on the calendar: food over coals, fires through the property, new-release wines, music, masterclasses and short vineyard tours. It is the one commitment worth making early this week.

Saturday does not need a grand itinerary. It only needs one thing that benefits from the clearer side of the weekend: a circuit by the water, a produce stop, a table reached without a rush, or a slow drive through the hills.

The forecast makes the choice fairly simple. Today has a small rain total of 0.3 millimetres in view, while Sunday carries a one-hundred percent chance of rain and 12.5 millimetres. That is enough contrast to give Saturday the outdoor part of the weekend without asking it to be perfect.

Take the layer, make one definite plan, and let the rest stay loose. Sunday can be warm, booked and indoors by design.

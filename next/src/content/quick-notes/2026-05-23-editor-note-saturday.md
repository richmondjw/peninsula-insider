The BOM forecast issued at 4:54 AM confirms it without ambiguity: Saturday 23 May is mostly sunny, 18 degrees, light winds, 5% rain probability. Sunday reverses - 80% showers, most likely afternoon and evening. The two days of this weekend are not interchangeable.

That split matters for how today should be read. The Peninsula in autumn at 18 degrees and clear is a specific proposition: cellar doors in the Red Hill corridor, the thermal circuit at the Hot Springs against cold air, a walk on any trail from Sorrento to Arthurs Seat without the cloud penalty. These are not weekend abstractions - they are today's conditions, not tomorrow's.

The day also carries a specific evening anchor. Peninsula Hot Springs has the Showcase performance with Chloe Gill tonight at 7pm. The pairing of a clear autumn afternoon and a booked evening event at the Springs is a coherent Peninsula day in full. If you are here and do not have plans yet, that is the structure.

Sunday still functions. The morning holds before the front arrives in the afternoon. The Hot Springs thermal circuit indoors runs regardless of weather. Sunday Sessions with Kinematic at 12pm runs at the Springs tomorrow - a covered programme. But Sunday is an interior-facing day; Saturday is not.

Two things worth noting this week. The wet pattern that ran Monday through Wednesday has now cleared completely; last week's overhang is gone. The next wet sequence builds from Monday. Today is genuinely between fronts, not just a brief gap.

Use the Saturday correctly.

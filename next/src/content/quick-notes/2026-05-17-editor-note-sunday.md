The Peninsula weekend closes with rain, and that requires a different frame.

Saturday delivered well — 21°C, dry all day, full cellar door circuit, good afternoon light. Sunday is the counterpart: overcast at 7am, 100% rain through the morning, 15 to 20mm forecast before conditions ease toward the afternoon. Max 17°C.

The wet Peninsula Sunday has its own version of a good day, and it is not the same as forcing outdoor plans through poor conditions. The thermal circuit at the Springs is the obvious choice — rain makes the outdoor pools more interesting, not less. The Sunday Sessions today (Mich McManus at noon) adds a specific reason to be there by midday rather than earlier. Book the thermal circuit; the event draws a crowd on what is already a wet-day venue.

For lunch, the covered cellar door and restaurant circuit — Red Hill to Merricks — functions without weather dependency. Tuck's Ridge, Ten Minutes by Tractor, Port Phillip Estate, Montalto: all fully enclosed dining. A wet Sunday on the Peninsula is, in practice, one of the better conditions for a long cellar door lunch with no reason to be elsewhere by a particular time.

The week ahead is unsettled. Monday brings some improvement (40% shower, 18°C), but Tuesday through Wednesday carries 60 to 70% shower probability. The pattern eases toward next weekend — Tuesday and Wednesday of the following week are the current rain core.

The editorial position remains: fewer sharp Peninsula days does not mean the Peninsula is closed. It means the right venues become more obvious.

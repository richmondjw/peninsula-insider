Thursday does not need an elaborate weather strategy. Start with the place that will be good even if a shower arrives: a long table, a gallery room, a cellar-door tasting or the errand that has been waiting for a quieter day.

The Main Ridge forecast carries a 57 percent rain chance, 1.3 millimetres and a cool breeze. There may be room for a short walk or a view between showers, but neither needs to carry the whole outing.

Build the day around its indoor centre and take the clear spell when it appears. Friday is presently forecast to be a little calmer, so the longer open-air plan can be held for then.

The week opens unsettled, and that is the honest Peninsula autumn.

Sunday's rain delivered what the forecast promised — 15 to 20mm through the morning, the afternoon a partial reprieve. Monday continues in the same register: drizzle through the morning, a possible easing by mid-afternoon, a wet Monday overall. Tuesday holds a 71% rain probability. Wednesday is not confirmed as an improvement yet. The seasonal pattern through this fortnight is wet mornings, intermittent afternoons, short light windows.

There is a version of the Peninsula that only reveals itself in these conditions. The thermal circuit at the Springs is more atmospheric on an overcast mid-week day than on a crowded Sunday in summer. Cellar door lunch at a covered venue on Red Hill — Ten Minutes by Tractor, Montalto, Port Phillip Estate — carries a different quality when there is no pressure to be anywhere else. The Mornington and Sorrento restaurant circuits that feel incidental in summer become the main event in autumn.

The week also has a specific reason to plan ahead: Showcase with Chloe Gill at Peninsula Hot Springs on Friday 23 May at 7pm. That is the kind of event that fills against a wet-week backdrop of locals looking for a reason to get out. Booking ahead is the right call.

The editorial position for the week: fewer outdoor plans, sharper indoor ones. The light window is 7:17am to 5:16pm today — use the middle of it.

Yesterday earned the outdoor day. Today is the other kind.

BOM's 6:07 AM forecast puts Sunday at 90% showers, most likely from the afternoon and into the evening, with 1 to 5mm possible. The maximum is 17 degrees under cloud. This is not a writing-off day, but it is a different kind of Peninsula day from Saturday: slower, covered, interior-facing.

The morning holds. Light winds, cloud cover, no rain before noon on this forecast. If there is an outdoor intention — a coastal walk, a morning drive through the hills, an early cellar door — the window is approximately between now and midday. After that, plan around the weather rather than despite it.

The natural anchor for the rest of the day is Peninsula Hot Springs. Sunday Sessions with Kinematic runs at noon — a covered programme that operates regardless of what is happening outside. The thermal circuit is designed precisely for this weather: cold outside, warm water, the contrast between the two. The Springs' full Sunday activity calendar runs alongside it: body clay ritual, fire and ice workshop, cacao circle, sauna infusion.

The week ahead does not offer a clear day until at least Thursday. Monday carries 60% shower probability, Tuesday 70%, Wednesday 40%. If there are Peninsula plans in the next few days, interior and covered options are the safer structure until the second half of the week.

Sunday on the Peninsula, understood correctly, is not a compromise. It is a different register: unhurried, warm inside, quiet on the roads. The Springs is the right use of it.

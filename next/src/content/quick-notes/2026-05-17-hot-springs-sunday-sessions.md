Peninsula Hot Springs is running the Sunday Sessions today at noon. The artist is Mich McManus — the first of the autumn series at the Springs. The event runs alongside the full Sunday programme: studio yoga from 7:30am, hot springs yoga at 9:15am, body clay at 10:30am, noon, 1pm, and 3pm, fire and ice from noon through 5pm, and sauna infusion at 5pm.

With 100% rain forecast through the morning and 15 to 20mm expected, the Springs is the natural Peninsula landing point for today. Outdoor thermal pools in the rain are a different experience from the same circuit on a clear day — more atmospheric, more deliberate, and on a Sunday morning considerably less crowded in the first session. The indoor caves, the covered pool areas, and the warming rooms carry the early morning well.

The practical note: thermal circuit booking is separate from event admission. The Sunday Sessions at noon draws attendance that tightens the available bathing session slots. If the plan is to bathe and then catch the noon set, the earlier morning circuit sessions give the most flexibility. Do not rely on walk-in availability for a wet-Sunday Springs visit — the venue is one of very few strong Peninsula options in these conditions and it books accordingly.

The Springs runs through all weather. Cancellation does not apply to the thermal circuit for rain. The Sunday Sessions programme is an indoor-to-outdoor event; check with the venue directly if you have questions about the noon set format in wet conditions.

Next up at the Springs: Showcase with Chloe Gill on 23 May at 7pm; Kinematic for Sunday Sessions on 24 May at noon.

This week ran the way the Peninsula's late-autumn weeks sometimes do: a clean Monday, a wet mid-section that arrived on cue, and a recovery into the weekend. Monday through Wednesday delivered what BOM had signalled. Thursday carried the transition. Friday is the other side of it.

The desk runs the early forecast each morning. This morning: partly cloudy at 9°C, warming to 15°C, 0 mm rain expected, wind light. The week's rain sequence has cleared. Friday is functional, not the Peninsula at its best, but the kind of overcast autumn day that the thermal circuit, a winery lunch, or the coastal trail handles without strain.

Saturday is the day to use. Sunny, 11 to 17°C, the kind of mid-autumn light that makes the Mornington corridor and the Arthurs Seat escarpment worth the drive. The cellar doors along Red Hill Road run at their best in this light: cold, clear, the sort of afternoon that does not need good weather to justify it, just the absence of bad weather.

The Peninsula Hot Springs Showcase with Chloe Gill is tomorrow evening, Saturday 23 May at 7pm. The venue confirmed the event on its what's-on page this morning. It is the week's one ticketed event, a live performance into the thermal circuit on the Peninsula's best forecast day of the week. If you have not booked, Saturday morning is the last reasonable window.

Sunday follows: partly cloudy, 12 to 18°C, the Sunday Sessions with Kinematic at the Springs from noon.

The week, as a shape, delivered on its early promise. Friday is the hinge closed; the weekend is what opens.

Five days into an autumn wet sequence and Saturday is the final chapter. The rain has been consistent, the temperatures mild - the Peninsula in this mode is not unpleasant, it is just not outdoor weather.

Kodomo no Hi runs all day at Peninsula Hot Springs - 10am to 8pm, a Japanese Children's Day cultural celebration running alongside the full thermal programme. For a wet Saturday it is the clearest single destination: a self-contained day in heated water with a cultural layer on top, the Springs operating at full weekend capacity with body clay, fire and ice, and the cacao circle all running.

Cellar doors running indoor table service by appointment are the other clean option. A wet autumn Saturday at the cellar door, particularly in the upper ranges of the Peninsula where the fog sits in the valley, is not a consolation prize - it is a different kind of visit.

Sunday is worth planning now. The forecast shows the first clear afternoon in five days: 16°C, 0% rain from noon, sunshine, light winds. The demand for Sunday outdoor tables, cellar door walk-ins, and coastal access will concentrate around that window. Booking Saturday evening gives you the pick.

The Peninsula is in its quietest week of winter, and that will not last. Saturday 20 June is Sorrento Solstice Festival - the biggest winter set-piece on the Peninsula calendar - and the five days between now and then are the planning window.

The festival is free and ticketed. Two music stages, fire performers, a lantern path through the village, food trucks, and the six-metre effigy burn on the bay at 6:30pm. The Saturday forecast is currently favourable: twenty percent chance of rain, no significant accumulation. That will firm by Wednesday.

The Sunday after is the reset. Peninsula Hot Springs Sunday Sessions run through 28 June. The truffle hunts at Red Hill and Flinders are both in full season. The solstice weekend is the one winter weekend when the Peninsula is neither quiet nor crowded - it is exactly at capacity, and the planning is what makes it work.

Three moves for the week:
1. Book Solstice tickets via Humanitix if you have not already.
2. Lock accommodation if you are staying overnight - the Sorrento and Portsea inventory tightens by Wednesday.
3. Plan the Sunday now, while Saturday is still abstract. Hot Springs, truffle hunt, or the Flinders farmgate. The post-festival Sunday is the better half of the weekend.

Thursday opens cloudy at 9°C and holds through to a 16°C maximum - the same range as yesterday - but the rain probability collapses from 70–100% to 20%. A slight chance of a shower is not nothing, but it is not Wednesday. Light winds across the day, UV at 2, sunrise at 7:19 AM and sunset 5:13 PM. This is an autumn overcast day, not an autumn rain day.

The pattern clarifies into the weekend. Thursday holds mostly dry. Friday arrives mostly sunny - BOM's 6:39 AM forecast has it at 5% rain probability, light winds, 6°C minimum warming to 16°C. This is the Peninsula's clear autumn afternoon: cold enough to appreciate the thermal circuit, clear enough to see the Arthurs Seat ridgeline. Friday is the week's best day.

The practical shape: Thursday is the holding pattern. Friday is the day to use. The Peninsula's outdoor programme runs correctly in Friday conditions; the thermal circuit on a cold clear evening is the week's strongest proposition. Saturday is good but fleeting - the window closes Sunday afternoon.

For today: the indoor and covered programme is still the correct frame. Thursday's overcast does not punish outdoor walking, but the Peninsula is not at its best under cloud.

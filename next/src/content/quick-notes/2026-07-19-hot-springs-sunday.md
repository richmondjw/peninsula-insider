Peninsula Hot Springs is open for bathing today, with Sound Healing Sessions and Daily Studio Yoga continuing through the weekly timetable.

The wet Sunday argument is the easy one to make - cold outside, pools heated, the contrast is obvious. The dry Sunday argument is different. Seven point one degrees and zero rain probability with a maximum of thirteen point three means the cold is present but moving through it is not uncomfortable. The walk between pools becomes workable. The sky is overcast but still. The setting runs closer to what the Hot Springs are designed to deliver at their quietest than the shelter-from-rain version.

This is the last dry Sunday before rain arrives Wednesday. A dry, cold, still mid-winter Sunday on the Peninsula is the right configuration for the pools. It happens less often in July than the wet version, and the bathing session carries a different character - less about taking cover, more about choosing it.

The practical notes: Sunday is a popular day year-round and particularly inside a dry-weather window. Book ahead rather than arriving speculatively. Sound Healing Sessions and Daily Studio Yoga are both on the regular timetable through July and fold into an existing bathing booking without requiring a separate trip.

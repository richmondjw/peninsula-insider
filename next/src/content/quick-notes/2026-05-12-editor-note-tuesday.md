There is something specific about Tuesday on the Mornington Peninsula. It is the quietest day of the operating week - past the weekend traffic, before the Wednesday-to-Sunday cellar door crowd begins to rebuild. The visitor pressure is roughly half of Saturday. On the walking tracks, the car parks, and in the tasting rooms that open midweek, Tuesday reads as the Peninsula's own time.

The fog this morning is not a problem. The coastal autumn fog that settles over Red Hill and the Main Ridge at 6am is a condition of the same high-pressure system that prevents the rain - it lifts reliably by 9am to 9:30am, and the day that follows is in full sun. By 11am the temperature is 15°C and climbing. By 1pm the ridge is sitting at 17°C in direct autumn sun. By 3pm it reaches 18°C - warmer than Monday, the second day of the clear spell deepening.

For anything outdoors: the walking tracks are dry after the weekend rain. The thermal pools at Peninsula Hot Springs are running under open sky. The gallery at MPRG is open from 11am with two exhibitions on. A properly planned Tuesday on the Peninsula - departure from the city around 9am - lands you in full sun with almost no crowd and a full day before the 5:21pm sunset.

Wednesday will be similar, possibly warmer.

A second wet morning asks a slightly different question than the first one did.

On Sunday and Monday the weather made the decision: stay indoors, find one thing worth committing to, and stop trying to fit an outdoor Peninsula itinerary around rain that was not going to stop. Tuesday holds the same forecast — ninety-eight percent probability, over five millimetres, strong winds through the day — but the forecast also shows clearly what is coming.

Wednesday clears. The weekend of the 18th and 19th arrives fully dry.

That gap changes how today reads. The Peninsula in mid-winter has two modes: the indoor patience mode and the outdoor clarity mode. Today is still the former, but the latter is close enough to feel real. The pools at Fingal will be busier on Saturday when the sun is at least making an effort. The cellar doors will have their fires lit regardless. The long lunch you have been postponing works as well on a quiet Tuesday as it does on a weekend.

What the consecutive wet days offer, if you are already down here, is the version of the Peninsula with fewer people trying to see it. The school holiday rush has passed. The weekend crowd has not yet arrived for the dry window. Today sits between them.

One deliberate indoor commitment. The weekend has some of its best options still available if you hold something back for then.

Peninsula Hot Springs runs its full weekday programme today. With Wednesday carrying 80% drizzle probability and a maximum of 17 degrees, the Springs' covered and heated circuit is the most weather-resilient Peninsula option in the day.

Wednesday's weekday schedule:

Studio yoga at 7:30am — the enclosed studio environment, available to overnight guests and early-arriving day visitors.

Hot springs yoga at 9:15am — conducted in the geothermal pool environment. Cool air at 12 degrees minimum, warm water, light drizzle: this is the combination the outdoor bathing circuit is designed for. The thermal contrast between air and water is at its most physical on mornings like this.

Fire and ice workshop at 10:30am, then again at 4pm and 5pm — the mid-morning session runs through the drizzle window in covered infrastructure. The afternoon sessions bracket the end of the bathing day.

Body clay ritual at 12pm and 2pm — midday sessions, aligned with the window when drizzle is forecast to ease. Post-clay bathing in the afternoon is, on most Wednesday schedules, the least-pressured period at the Springs.

Sauna infusion at 5pm — the natural close to a bathing day before the drive back to Melbourne.

Midweek at the Springs remains materially different from weekend visits. Pools that fill within the first thirty minutes on Saturday mornings — particularly in the Spa Dreaming Centre, which is adults-only from 16 — are accessible mid-week with significantly less competition. The bathing experience is distinct.

Bookings are recommended for timed activities and general bathing access. Confirm availability and session capacity at time of booking via peninsulahotsprings.com.

There is a pleasing kind of decisiveness available on a wet day: choose one place that is good to be in, and stop asking the weather to cooperate with everything else.

Monday's forecast is properly rainy, with 18 millimetres and a high of 13.9 degrees in view at Main Ridge. That makes a single destination more appealing than a string of errands, detours and exposed stretches. Take the longer lunch, the appointment you have been postponing, or the indoor plan that is better when it has your full attention.

Tuesday is currently forecast to be much drier. Let the walk, garden stop or waterfront loop wait if it can. Today does not need to be rescued; it only needs a plan with a roof.

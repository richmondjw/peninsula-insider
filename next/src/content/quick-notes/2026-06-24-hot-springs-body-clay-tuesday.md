Peninsula Hot Springs runs its Body Clay Ritual on weekdays at 12pm and 2pm. The session is included with a standard bathing booking and runs for forty-five minutes in the clay ridge landscape.

The ritual uses detoxifying clays sourced from around Australia. A host talks through the benefits while guests apply the clay and let it set before rinsing in the geothermal pools. It is a slow, warm activity that suits a cold winter afternoon.

For today, the practical play is to book a bathing session that overlaps either noon or 2pm, then join the ritual during the middle of your visit. No separate ticket is required.

From Friday through the weekend the schedule expands to four daily sessions, which is useful if you are planning a family visit during the school holidays. Today, though, the midday and 2pm slots are the ones to aim for.

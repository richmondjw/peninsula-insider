Some days are improved by fewer decisions. Wednesday is one of them: pick a place that is satisfying to settle into, and let the weather take care of the rest.

The Main Ridge forecast has an 82 percent chance of rain, 3.3 millimetres in view and a brisk wind. That does not rule out a small outside moment, but it does make an all-day sequence of exposed stops feel needlessly optimistic.

Choose the long lunch, the cellar-door table or the indoor errand that has been waiting. If the clouds lift, take the walk as an extra. The plan will feel better for not depending on it.

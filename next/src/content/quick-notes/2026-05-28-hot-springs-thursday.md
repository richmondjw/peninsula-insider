Peninsula Hot Springs runs its full weekday programme today. On a Thursday that sits at 40% and a weekend forecast to reach 95%, the Springs is the one Peninsula option where the weather genuinely does not matter. The covered and heated circuit holds regardless.

Thursday's weekday schedule:

Studio yoga at 7:30am, in the enclosed wellness studio, the early start for overnight guests and first-arriving day visitors.

Hot springs yoga at 9:15am, complimentary, conducted in the amphitheatre pools. Cool morning air and warm geothermal water is the contrast the outdoor bathing circuit is built around, and a partly cloudy 16-degree day suits it well.

Fire and ice workshop at 10:30am, then again at 4pm and 5pm. The mid-morning session runs in covered infrastructure. The afternoon sessions bracket the close of the bathing day.

Body clay ritual at 12pm and 2pm. Midday sessions, with afternoon bathing afterward that on most weekdays is the least-pressured stretch at the Springs.

Sauna infusion at 5pm, the natural close before the drive back to Melbourne.

Midweek bathing remains distinct from the weekend. Pools that fill inside the first half hour on a Saturday, particularly in the adults-only Spa Dreaming Centre, are accessible mid-week with far less competition. As the weekend moves to 80% Saturday and 95% Sunday, this is the option to hold in reserve. Sunday also brings Paul Dillon for Sunday Sessions at 12pm, live music in the bathing environment.

Bookings are recommended for timed activities and general access. Confirm session availability at peninsulahotsprings.com.

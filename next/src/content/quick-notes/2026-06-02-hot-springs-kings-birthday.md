The King's Birthday long weekend is seven days out and the anchor at Peninsula Hot Springs is confirmed. Monday 8 June runs as an all-day event, complimentary with bathing, and listed on the venue's what's-on page as of this morning. The Sunday prior carries Melissa Fist in the Sunday Sessions slot at 2pm at the Bath House Café, also complimentary with bathing.

Note the time shift on the Sunday session: the June 7 slot is at 2pm, not the noon that has been the standard for recent sessions. Anyone planning the full long weekend with Sunday as the arrival day should adjust accordingly.

The daily wellness programme continues through the week as normal. Studio yoga at 7:30 in the morning, hot springs yoga at 9:15 (complimentary), fire and ice across the day, body clay at noon and 2pm on weekdays, sauna infusion at 5pm. No closures or schedule changes are flagged.

Beyond the long weekend, the forward programme has Liana Perillo Quartet on 13 June (showcase, 7pm), and Amina in the Sunday Sessions slot on 14 June at noon. The June programme at the springs is the most consistently bookable surface on the Peninsula during a wet week, and it is filling.

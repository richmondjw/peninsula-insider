Peninsula Hot Springs marks Kodomo no Hi — the Japanese Children's Day — with a cultural celebration this Saturday 9 May, running from 10am to 8pm across the Springs complex. The event follows the traditional date (5 May by the Japanese calendar, observed here on the nearest Saturday) and is one of the Springs' longer cultural programming days in the autumn calendar.

Saturday also falls after two full rain days. If Thursday and Friday close out wet and the weekend needs recalibrating, the Springs offers a full ten-hour event program alongside the standard pool access. Book direct through the venue site; event days at the Springs fill faster than standard sessions, particularly when the weather has been building demand.

The weekend sequence at the Springs: Saturday Kodomo no Hi (10am–8pm), Sunday Sessions with Ereignis on Sunday 10 May at 12pm. The Bathe in Cinema (David Attenborough's *Ocean*) is on Friday evening at 6:30pm — that note remains live on this page. Daily activities running this week include hot springs yoga at 9:15am, studio yoga at 7:30am, fire and ice workshop at weekday times (10:30am, 4pm, 5pm), and sauna infusion daily at 5pm.

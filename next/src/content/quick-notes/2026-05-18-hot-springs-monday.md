Peninsula Hot Springs is operating its full weekday programme today. The schedule for Monday 18 May runs as follows: studio yoga at 7:30am, hot springs yoga at 9:15am, fire and ice workshop at 10:30am, 4pm, and 5pm, body clay ritual at 12pm and 2pm, and sauna infusion at 5pm.

On a wet Monday, the thermal circuit earns its keep more visibly than on a clear weekend. The outdoor pools in drizzle offer a distinct experience — warm water, cool air, minimal crowds compared to a Friday or Saturday booking. A mid-morning arrival for the hot springs yoga at 9:15am, followed by a circuit through the afternoon, is the cleanest way to use a wet Monday on the Peninsula.

The upcoming event worth holding the date for: Showcase performance with Chloe Gill on Friday 23 May at 7pm. This is a venue-set evening performance at the Springs, and the first Friday showcase of the autumn programme. Wet-week momentum tends to drive bookings for end-of-week events; availability will move before Friday.

The remainder of the Springs' May calendar: Sunday Sessions with Kinematic on 24 May at noon, Sunday Sessions with Paul Dillon on 31 May at noon.

Book the thermal circuit and Showcase directly through the Peninsula Hot Springs site. Thermal circuit pricing and availability are not confirmed in this note — verify directly with the venue before committing travel plans.

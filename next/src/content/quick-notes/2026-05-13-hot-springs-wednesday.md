Looking ahead: the Springs' Sunday Sessions series returns this week. On Sunday 17 May, Mich McManus plays the lunchtime set at noon. The format — live music at the Springs on Sunday afternoon — is one of the better things the Peninsula does in the cooler months. Midweek at the Springs runs at lower visitor pressure than Saturday and Sunday.

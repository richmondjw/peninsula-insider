Peninsula Hot Springs runs its full weekday programme today. Studio yoga at 7:30am, hot springs yoga at 9:15am, fire and ice workshop at 10:30am, 4pm, and 5pm. Body clay ritual at 12pm and 2pm. Sauna infusion at 5pm. The thermal circuit is open. On a foggy Tuesday morning that will clear to a calm afternoon, the mid-morning arrival for the hot springs yoga at 9:15am — once the fog has lifted — is the sensible call.

The specific reason to act today: the Showcase performance with Chloe Gill is this Friday, 23 May at 7pm. That is four days from now. This is the first Friday evening showcase of the Springs' autumn programme, and it sits at the end of a week that opened with rain and is running under wet-week conditions for much of the city. When the week feels contracted, the end-of-week event that offers an evening out fills from mid-week. The last week before an event is typically when availability shifts from broad to narrow.

The winter programme is building through the back half of May.

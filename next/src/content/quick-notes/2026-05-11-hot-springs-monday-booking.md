Peninsula Hot Springs runs a full weekday programme on Mondays - hot springs yoga at 9:15am, fire and ice workshop at 10:30am (repeated at 4pm and 5pm), body clay ritual at noon and 2pm, sauna infusion at 5pm. The Springs is open daily.

What changes today is the conditions. After six days of rain, the outdoor pools are running under clear sky for the first time this week. The forecast shows 0% rain, full sunshine, and a peak of 16°C - ideal thermal bathing weather. The contrast between the hot pools and the cool autumn air in direct sun is the Springs at its most functional, and the post-rain clarity of the surrounding landscape adds to it.

Monday at Peninsula Hot Springs is structurally different from the weekend. The Saturday and Sunday demand for bathing sessions and wellness activities at the Springs is concentrated; Monday sees that pressure ease. Booking availability mid-week is typically better across all session types, though online booking is still the right move before the drive - the clearer forecast will lift Monday interest.

The Springs sits at Fingal, approximately 85 minutes from Melbourne's CBD. Weekday rates differ from weekend pricing; confirm when booking. The drive from Frankston takes under 30 minutes from the freeway.

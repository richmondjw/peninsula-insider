Peninsula Hot Springs is running Kodomo no Hi today — a cultural celebration of Japanese Children's Day — across the full thermal facility from 10am to 8pm on Saturday 9 May.

The programme runs alongside the full weekend activity schedule: hot springs yoga at 9:15am, studio yoga at 7:30am, body clay ritual at 10:30am, 12pm, 1pm, and 3pm, fire and ice workshop at 12pm, 2pm, 4pm, and 5pm, cacao circle at 12pm, and sauna infusion at 5pm.

For a wet Saturday with no outdoor window until Sunday, the Springs is the clean choice: covered, heated, programmed from morning through evening. The cultural element today gives it a different texture from a standard day — worth booking ahead rather than arriving without a session reserved. Book directly through the Peninsula Hot Springs site.

Coming Sunday: Sunday Sessions with Ereignis at 12pm.

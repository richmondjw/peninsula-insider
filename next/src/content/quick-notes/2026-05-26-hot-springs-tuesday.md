Peninsula Hot Springs runs its full weekday programme today. The bathing circuit - Bath House for families and groups, Spa Dreaming Centre for guests 16 and over - is supported by a structured activity schedule that runs regardless of weather, as most of it takes place in covered or heated environments.

Tuesday's weekday programme:

Studio yoga at 7:30am - the earliest structured option in the day, ideal for those arriving on Peninsula overnight.

Hot springs yoga at 9:15am - conducted in the outdoor geothermal environment. At 18 degrees with easterly winds today, the contrast between cool air and warm water is at its most physical.

Fire and ice workshop at 10:30am, then again at 4pm and 5pm - the mid-morning session is the one with daylight behind it.

Body clay ritual at 12pm and 2pm - the midday sessions suit arrivals after the morning drive from Melbourne.

Sauna infusion at 5pm daily - the natural close to an afternoon at the Springs before the drive back.

Weekday access at Peninsula Hot Springs is materially different from weekend visits. Crowd pressure is lower across both the Bath House and Spa Dreaming Centre. The pools that fill quickly on Saturday mornings are accessible mid-week with less competition for position. The midday body clay sessions in particular tend to run at reduced capacity Tuesday through Thursday.

Upcoming at the Springs: Sunday Sessions with Paul Dillon on 31 May at 12pm. The following weekend series continues with Melissa Fist on 7 June.

Bookings recommended for all timed activities and general bathing sessions. Access and availability should be confirmed at time of booking via the Peninsula Hot Springs website.

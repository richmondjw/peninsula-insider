Peninsula Hot Springs runs its full weekday programme on Thursday. Studio yoga has already opened at 7:30am. Hot springs yoga is running from 9:15am. Fire and ice workshop cycles through 10:30am, 4pm, and 5pm; body clay ritual sits at noon and 2pm; sauna infusion closes the activity day at 5pm.

Today's conditions are the best of the week for the outdoor thermal circuit. The temperature reaches 19°C at 3pm - the peak of the current clear window - under a high-pressure system that has held dry for four days. The outdoor pools under open autumn sky in this temperature band are the version of Peninsula Hot Springs that photographs least accurately. You have to be there.

The immediate booking window: **Sunday Sessions** returns this weekend. On Sunday 17 May, Mich McManus plays the noon set - the first Sunday Sessions of the autumn series. The format is live music at the thermal pools, an outdoor performance that runs alongside the standard bathing circuit. It is one of the better things the Springs does in the cooler months, and it fills. Three days out is when weekend availability starts to move. Book the thermal circuit online before making the drive; the activity sessions require separate booking.

Further sessions confirmed for May: Kinematic on 24 May, Paul Dillon on 31 May. Chloe Gill plays an indoor showcase on Friday 23 May at 7pm.

Weekday note: fire and ice and body clay are consistently more accessible mid-week than on weekend. If there is flexibility on timing, Thursday afternoon - with the 19°C peak and low visitor pressure - is the window to use.

The dry midweek window that existed on Tuesday has closed. Wednesday opens with drizzle at 80% probability — soft and persistent through the morning, easing this afternoon. The southerly at 15 to 20 kilometres per hour adds edge on exposed coastal sections.

Thursday and Friday are the two remaining viable windows this week, both at 40% with showers distributed across morning and afternoon rather than concentrated at a single time. Neither offers a clear sky. But both are materially more workable than the days on either side. If there is Peninsula business with outdoor priority still to schedule this week, Thursday is the cleaner option.

From Saturday the pattern tightens without reprieve. Saturday and Sunday carry 80% probability; Sunday adds 1 to 6 millimetres and westerlies at 25 to 35 kilometres per hour. Monday 1 June reaches 95% — the wettest day in the stretch, with 3 to 10 millimetres possible. The Peninsula enters proper winter rhythm.

The Peninsula in this register has its own character. Morning yoga at the Springs with steam rising against cold air. Cellar door visits to Merricks and Red Hill rooms that have space mid-week when the weekend crowd has cleared. Lunch sessions at winery restaurants that carry no Saturday pressure on a Wednesday. The indoor Peninsula is not a consolation version. It is a different experience, and this week happens to be its right season.

A forward note: Sunday 31 May carries 80% rain and westerly wind. Peninsula Hot Springs has Paul Dillon for Sunday Sessions at 12pm — live music in the bathing environment. If Sunday on the Peninsula is on the table, this is the anchor to plan around. Bookings are open.

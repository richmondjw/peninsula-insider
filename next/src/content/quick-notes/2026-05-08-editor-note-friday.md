The rain ran hard on Thursday and it is running the same way today. Friday closes out the full wet sequence — 100% across every forecast slot, maximum 15°C, WSW holding at 20km/h through the afternoon.

Saturday breaks the pattern slightly. Morning still wet, but there is a dry window around 9am before rain returns at noon. It is not a clean day; it is a useful one if you are indoors for lunch and do not mind arriving in damp air.

Sunday is a different kind of day entirely. From noon the forecast is clear: 0% rain at midday and 3pm, sunshine through the afternoon, 16°C, winds dropping to 8km/h by evening. After four wet days it will feel like a reward even if it is just an ordinary autumn afternoon.

The Peninsula in autumn runs this sequence regularly — extended wet patches that concentrate demand indoors, followed by a clear window that makes everything outdoors feel sharper than it would have otherwise. The visitor who books the Sunday walk or cellar door before Saturday will have it; the one who waits until Sunday morning will find it filling fast.

Tonight, while it is still wet: the Hot Springs runs Bathe in Cinema at 6:30pm — David Attenborough's *Ocean* poolside. That one is worth looking at if you are in town.

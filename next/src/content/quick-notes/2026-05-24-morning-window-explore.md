BOM's 6:07 AM forecast gives the morning before the showers arrive. The Peninsula's outdoor walking and coastal options are at their accessible best in the early part of today, before the 90% shower probability settles over the afternoon and evening.

The autumn Peninsula at 17 degrees under cloud — before rain — has its own specific quality. The back-beach track at Sorrento and Portsea in morning light, the Cape Schanck Coastal Walk along the basalt headland, the ridgeline approach at Arthurs Seat — all of these are more direct and quieter on a Sunday morning than they are on a dry weekend afternoon. The cloud cover is soft. The coast in this weather has an atmosphere the sunny season cannot provide.

The practical framing: use the morning. The Cape Schanck walk is approximately three kilometres return from the car park to the lighthouse, manageable within the pre-noon window. The Sorrento back-beach track runs from Ocean Beach Road toward Portsea along the clifftop and down to London Bridge — a 45-minute return or a short loop depending on fitness. Arthurs Seat is more exposed; wind and visibility are worth checking before committing to the ridgeline.

From noon, plan around warmth and cover rather than landscape. That is what today's forecast asks for.

Visitors should verify trail access and current conditions with Parks Victoria before travel. No specific closures were detected in today's check.

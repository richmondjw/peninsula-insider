Yesterday's note called it: Sunday would be the break. The forecast held, but the timing shifted. Instead of noon clearing, the pattern shows a wet morning running through to 9am - still overcast at midday, partly cloudy, genuinely sunny from around 3pm. Sunset at 5:23pm means the outdoor window is roughly two hours. Tight, but it is the first real outdoor light the Peninsula has had in six days.

The Hot Springs is the clean morning move. Sunday Sessions with Ereignis runs from midday at the Bath House Café Amphitheatre - acoustic music, complimentary with bathing, and the thermal circuit handles the wet hours before the sky opens. Arrive before noon to get the full programme.

For cellar doors, mid-morning Sunday is the last clear booking window before demand concentrates. The Red Hill and Main Ridge cluster will have walk-in space this morning that evaporates once people in Melbourne see the 3pm forecast. The southern arc - Merricks, Balnarring - tends to have more room on a clear afternoon, and the valley terrain shows the clearing dramatically when it comes.

The week ahead is genuinely clear. Monday shows full sunshine on every forecast model, temperatures climbing. If Sunday afternoon feels too rushed, Monday is a slower, better version of the same Peninsula - without the weekend concentration.

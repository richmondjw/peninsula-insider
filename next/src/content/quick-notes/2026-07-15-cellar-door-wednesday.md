The Peninsula's cellar doors do not change much between Wednesday and Saturday. The fires are lit regardless. The wine list is the same. The view from the tasting room looks out over the same overcast mid-winter sky in each direction.

What changes is the room.

Wednesday mid-week after a run of wet days is one of the quieter moments the cellar door calendar produces. The school holiday visitors who were on the Peninsula over the long weekend have gone back. The dry-window weekend crowd - the people who have been watching the forecast and waiting for exactly the conditions that arrived this morning - will not be here until Saturday. Today sits in the gap.

The argument for today rather than the weekend is not that the weekend will be worse. The weekend will be fine. It is that the mid-week version has a different quality: the staff are less stretched, the service tends to run at the pace you actually want a long lunch to run at, and the bookings that exist are the ones that were planned deliberately rather than grabbed in response to a cleared forecast.

Most of the Mornington Peninsula's cellar doors operate Wednesday through Sunday as standard. Check individual venues for lunch sittings and tasting times - not all cellar doors run full kitchen service on weekdays - but the serious ones do, and Wednesday is where you find out who they really are.

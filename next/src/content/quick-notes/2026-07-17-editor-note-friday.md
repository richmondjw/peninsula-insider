The past week ran wet from Sunday to Tuesday, offered a clearing on Wednesday, and now arrives at Friday with something it has not had until now: a confirmed four-day block with zero rain across the board.

The distinction matters because the usual advice on this page - mid-week over the weekend, quieter over busier - does not quite apply when the week started wet and arrives at Friday with the whole weekend still in front of it. Today is not the tail end of a clear run. It is the beginning of one.

Friday, Saturday, Sunday, and Monday all read at zero percent rain probability. Winds are light - Friday's maximum is under eleven kilometres per hour. The Peninsula is going to be available in its mid-winter form for four consecutive days, which is the condition that shifts the question from whether to go to what to do when you get there.

Rain returns on Wednesday at over fifty percent probability. The window closes at the start of next week. What it offers between now and then is the reward a wet week earns: four days of the Peninsula at its mid-winter best, overcast and calm, the whole block available without reservation.

The usual trade-off between arriving today or waiting for the weekend does not exist this week. All four days hold.

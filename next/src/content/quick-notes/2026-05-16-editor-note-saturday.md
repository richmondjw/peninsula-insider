Saturday on the Mornington Peninsula arrives at 21°C with clear skies into the afternoon and rain not arriving until 9pm. It is the stronger day of the two, and the one to use.

The visitor load is higher on Saturday than it was on Friday, but the combination of temperature, light, and dry conditions is sound. The cellar door circuit is fully open — the majority of producers on their Wednesday to Sunday programme, now in the third day of that window. The morning is cool but the 9am to 3pm stretch is the best outdoor block of the weekend.

Sunday is, plainly, a different day. Rain arrives from midnight and holds through the morning at 100% probability. The clearing window around noon is forecast but not reliable; rain returns by evening. Outdoor Peninsula plans for Sunday are constrained from the start.

One booking note: Peninsula Hot Springs runs its Sunday Sessions tomorrow at noon with Mich McManus — one day out, availability for the thermal circuit on a wet Sunday typically tightens quickly. The Springs on a rainy day is a worthwhile experience, but it is a deliberate choice rather than a comfortable default. If the Springs was in mind for the weekend, today's dry outdoor version is the easier call. Tomorrow, book early or plan around the indoor spaces.

The editorial note is brief: the week has been good on the Peninsula. Today closes it well.

Monday is not the day to extract an outdoor plan from a reluctant forecast. The air is cold, the north-westerly is up, and the forecast holds one hundred percent rain probability with 5.6 millimetres expected. There is a useful honesty in taking that at face value.

Make the day contained. Choose the place where the arrival, the main event and the departure do not require a negotiation with the weather. A long lunch, a gallery hour, a bath, a cinema seat or time at home all make more sense when they are not pretending to be an outdoor day.

Tuesday remains wet, at seventy-eight percent probability and 6.4 millimetres. But the week is not a single grey block. Wednesday is currently the clear opening: only a twenty-one percent chance of rain, no measurable total forecast and a similar twelve-degree top. Thursday also looks relatively light.

The Peninsula is better when plans follow the conditions instead of arguing with them. Keep Monday close. Put the flexible outdoor idea aside for Wednesday.

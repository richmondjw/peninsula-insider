Sunday is the useful pause in this forecast. The day is dry, clear at first light, and expected to reach fourteen point three degrees. It is not a still day, but the northerly is manageable and there is no rain to negotiate.

That changes the question for a Peninsula Sunday. It is not about finding the covered alternative or waiting for a break in the weather. It is about choosing one outdoor thing that benefits from a dry winter day and giving it enough time: a coast walk, a long drive between villages, or an unhurried lunch that begins outside rather than ends there.

Monday is the softer transition, with seventy-three percent rain probability but less than a millimetre forecast. Tuesday is the real turn. Twelve point four millimetres, a maximum of ten point six degrees, and a thunderstorm weather code make it the day to keep flexible. Wednesday stays wet as well.

The Peninsula rewards paying attention to the intervals. Sunday is one of them. Use the dry day for the part of the place that cannot be moved indoors, then let the week become a different kind of plan.

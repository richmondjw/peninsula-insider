There is a specific kind of Thursday on the Mornington Peninsula that is worth knowing about. It does not happen every week - it requires three things at once: the cellar door circuit running in full, the weather above 18°C, and the weekend visitors not yet on the freeway. Today is that Thursday.

The temperature peaks at 19°C. The high-pressure system that has sat over the Peninsula since Monday has now held for four consecutive dry days. Wednesday's mist, which burned off the coast by 9am, did not appear this morning - Thursday opened clear. The cellar doors that closed on Monday and Tuesday are into their second full day of operation. The weekend traffic does not tend to arrive until Friday evening.

What that means in practice: the drive up the Red Hill road is quiet. Walk-in at most producers is available without a booking. The terraces - at Eldridge, Ten Minutes by Tractor, Moorooduc Estate, and the others across the Merricks North corridor - have space. The autumn vine colour is at the edge of the window, two to three weeks left before most producers wrap it back for winter.

Friday is warmer still, at 20°C, and sunny. The weekend will be busier. If a midweek day on the Peninsula is possible this week, today and tomorrow are the two to take.

Sunday 17 May: Peninsula Hot Springs opens its autumn Sunday Sessions series with Mich McManus at noon. If that is the anchor for a Peninsula day this weekend, book the thermal circuit alongside it - weekend sessions fill quickly.

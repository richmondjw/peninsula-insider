The logic is simple: rain cancels the walks, the coastal drives feel less appealing in solid grey, and the winery terrace has lost its reason to exist. The Hot Springs absorbs all of that. The outdoor thermal pools at Fingal are warm regardless of what the sky does - they are, in fact, better when the air is cold and the light is flat. Twilight bathing runs Tuesday and Thursday in May, from around 5pm. The Twilight Express shuttle from Melbourne is also running this season if you want to leave the car in the city and arrive properly. Tuesday is the quieter of the two weeknight slots. Book directly through the Peninsula Hot Springs site before midday if you want tonight.

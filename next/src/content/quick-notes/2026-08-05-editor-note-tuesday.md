Tuesday does not need rescuing from its forecast. Rain is the ordinary condition of the day, so it is better to choose the plan that welcomes it: a long table, a bath, a gallery hour, a cinema seat or an unhurried day indoors.

There is value in making the weather part of the decision instead of an obstacle to negotiate. The forecast calls for 7.5 millimetres of rain, a top of 12.6 degrees and a ninety-eight percent chance of precipitation. Let the arrival and departure be the only parts of the day exposed to it.

Wednesday gives the week some space. It is currently forecast to have only a sixteen percent chance of rain and no measurable total. Keep that day open for the flexible outdoor plan.

Tuesday can be small, warm and complete on its own terms.

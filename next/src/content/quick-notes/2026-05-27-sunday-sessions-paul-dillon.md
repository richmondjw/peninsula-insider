Sunday 31 May is forecast at 80% probability of showers, with 1 to 6 millimetres possible and westerly winds at 25 to 35 kilometres per hour. It is not a landscape day. Peninsula Hot Springs has Paul Dillon for Sunday Sessions at 12pm, which is the relevant planning consideration for Sunday on the Peninsula.

Sunday Sessions is the Springs' recurring live music programme, conducted in the bathing environment. The format is straightforward: arrive before noon, use the bathing circuit through the morning, attend the noon session in the geothermal pool surrounds. The heated and covered infrastructure of both the Bath House and Spa Dreaming Centre means weather is a non-factor. A wet, windy Sunday at 15 degrees maximum is not a reason to stay home — it is, for the Springs, the right kind of Sunday.

The series continues the following week with Melissa Fist on 7 June at 12pm. June also brings a Showcase Performance from the Liana Perillo Quartet on 13 June at 7pm to 8pm.

Bookings are required. The Sunday Sessions format is popular, and midday entry combined with activity bookings for Sunday tends to move earlier in the week. Book via peninsulahotsprings.com.

The Sorrento Solstice Festival runs today from 2pm to 9pm on the Sorrento Foreshore. This is the event that defines the Peninsula's winter calendar: a large-scale fire and lantern celebration held on the Esplanade each year for the winter solstice.

**What is on tonight**

Two music stages run through the afternoon and evening, from an acoustic zone through to a main stage. A lantern path of Australian animal lanterns runs through the village. A projection installation covers the historic Sorrento Hotel facade. Food trucks, local wine, and beer from Peninsula producers are on the Esplanade. A silent disco, sound healing, roving performers, tarot readers, and artisanal market stalls fill the festival grounds. A family program runs through the early evening with lantern making, face painting, and kids programming.

The centrepiece is the 6-metre effigy burn at 6:30pm on the bay. Handcrafted by local sculptors, the burning marks the turning of the year and draws the largest crowd of the night. Be on the foreshore by 6pm.

**Tickets and access**

The event is free and ticketed. As of this morning, waitlist tickets are available via Humanitix. If you have a registered place, your ticket is valid regardless of weather. If you are on the waitlist, holders occasionally release closer to the afternoon, particularly in wet-weather conditions.

**Weather today**

Rain showers are forecast through the afternoon and evening, eighty-three percent probability and seven millimetres. Waterproof jacket, warm layers, and solid footwear are the right kit. The event proceeds in wet weather. The foreshore is open and accessible.

**Getting there**

Sorrento is a ninety-minute drive from central Melbourne. Parking in Sorrento village during festival hours is limited. The Portsea direction on Point Nepean Road fills early. Arrive before 2pm or plan for a short walk from residential streets behind the Esplanade.

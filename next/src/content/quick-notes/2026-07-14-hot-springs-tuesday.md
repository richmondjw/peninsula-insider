Peninsula Hot Springs is open for bathing today, with Sound Healing Sessions and Daily Studio Yoga continuing through the week's program.

The wet Tuesday logic runs like this: the pools are heated regardless of what the weather is doing outside, the contrast between nine degrees and drizzle overhead and the pool temperature below is close to maximum, and the weekday session is likely to be quieter than what is coming. The forecast for Friday, Saturday, and Sunday shows zero rain probability on all three days. When a clear winter weekend arrives on the Peninsula, the hot springs fills up in a way that a wet Tuesday does not.

Both options work. The question is whether you prefer the bathing session that competes with good weather or the one that replaces it.

The practical notes remain the same: book ahead rather than arriving speculatively, particularly if you want to add Sound Healing or the Studio Yoga session alongside bathing. Both are on the regular timetable through July and fold into an existing booking without requiring a separate trip.

If today is the day you are here, this is the clearest warm option on the Peninsula's winter calendar.

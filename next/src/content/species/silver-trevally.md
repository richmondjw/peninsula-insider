## What silver trevally actually is

*Pseudocaranx georgianus* is a coastal pelagic schooling species. Silver-bronze body, dark forked tail, small mouth, typical Peninsula fish 25 to 40cm. Trev are not strictly resident; they school through the bays in autumn when water temperatures are dropping and baitfish concentrations are highest. Outside the autumn run, individual fish are caught year-round.

The species is hard-fighting on light tackle and is a favourite of anglers who appreciate the fight on a 2 to 4kg rod more than they value table quality. The eating is mid-tier: oily enough to grill, firm enough to fillet, but not in the league of snapper or whiting for the dinner-priority angler.

## Season and tide

The autumn schooling run (March through June) is the productive window. Outside that, opportunistic only. Trev respond to moving water and baitfish concentration; on the piers, the run is most active during the rising tide; on boat sessions, drift over channel margins where bait fish are visible on the sounder.

## Technique and gear

A 7 foot 2 to 4kg light spinning rod, 2500 size reel, 8lb braid, 6 to 10lb fluorocarbon leader. The rig is light: a paternoster with two size 4 long-shank hooks, or a running sinker on a small ball weight, baited with fresh squid strip, pipi, or bass yabby. Trev take on the drop more often than on the bottom; pay attention as the rig settles.

For boat anglers working a school, a small metal slice (10 to 30g) cast and retrieved fast through the school produces strikes. Trev compete; one fish on the line typically draws others.

## Bag and size limits, licence

- **Minimum size limit.** 20cm total length.
- **Bag limit.** 20 per person per day, combined for all silver trevally species.
- **Season.** Open year-round.
- **Licence.** [Victorian Recreational Fishing Licence](https://vfa.vic.gov.au/recreational-fishing/recreational-fishing-licence) required for anglers aged 18 and over.

Source: [Victorian Fisheries Authority Recreational Fishing Guide](https://vfa.vic.gov.au/recreational-fishing/recreational-fishing-guide). Last fact-verified Thu, 30 Apr 2026.

## Opportunity ranking by location

1. **[Mornington Pier](/fishing/locations/mornington-pier/), light paternoster, March to June.** The Peninsula's most reliable land-based trev location during the autumn run.
2. **[Hastings Jetty](/fishing/locations/hastings-jetty/), light paternoster, March to June.** Western Port channel-margin trev on the running tide.
3. **Boat drift over channel margins, March to June.** Working schools visible on the sounder produces fast sessions on light gear.

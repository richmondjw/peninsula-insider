A Peninsula wellness weekend works when the bathing is the centre of the trip, not an errand in the middle of it.

Choose the bathing experience and arrival time first. That sets the day's pace and helps you choose a stay and dinner within an easy drive.

## The two springs, and which one to choose

For this weekend, choose between [Alba Thermal Springs](/explore/spas-and-wellness/#alba-thermal-springs) and [Peninsula Hot Springs](/explore/spas-and-wellness/#peninsula-hot-springs), both in Fingal.

**Peninsula Hot Springs** has the wider menu. Its Bath House includes more than 50 bathing and wellness experiences, while the Spa Dreaming Centre is reserved for guests aged 16 and over. Look at the access included in the experience you book; the two areas are not identical. The hilltop pool, outdoor spaces and program of activities make this the choice if you want variety.

**Alba** is the more pared-back choice: contemporary pools, spa treatments and a design-led landscape. Choose it if you would rather settle into one setting than range across a larger site. Its booking page offers different bathing experiences and arrival times.

Book **one** venue, not both. Compare the current session details before choosing, and leave time after bathing to change and get to dinner. Bring swimwear and a water bottle, and follow the venue's own guidance on bathing and facilities.

**Booking check, 30 September 2026:** [Alba's booking page](https://albathermalsprings.com.au/book/) lists bookable springs and spa experiences; [Peninsula Hot Springs' Bath House page](https://www.peninsulahotsprings.com/bathe/bath-house/revitalise) lists more than 50 Bath House experiences and recommends booking ahead. Confirm your selected access, arrival time and availability directly with the venue.

## Where to sleep, so the day extends

[Lindenderry at Red Hill](/stay/lindenderry/) is a country-estate base for a garden, a quiet room and a slow morning. Check your actual drive from the springs before booking.

[Polperro Villas](/stay/polperro-villas/) suits a couple who want their own vineyard space afterwards. The current operator page lists a king-size bed, fireplace, spa bath and private deck in each villa, plus a two-night weekend minimum. [Check the villas directly](https://www.polperrowines.com.au/escape/villas/) for the facilities and dates that suit you.

## What to eat

After bathing, keep dinner close to your stay: a table you have already booked, or an easy meal at the accommodation. If you want a long lunch as well, make it the following day and verify Sunday opening before you commit. This is a weekend with one main experience, not a relay of reservations.

## The one walk that belongs in the weekend

If the weather and access suit, make the [Cape Schanck Boardwalk](/explore/cape-schanck-boardwalk/) an optional Sunday stop. Choose a signed route and allow time for the conditions you find. Otherwise, leave Sunday free.

In 2025, Jackalope Hotel became one of the first Australian properties to receive the MICHELIN Two Keys designation. It's a useful marker for where Peninsula accommodation has arrived: 45 rooms in a design-forward winery hotel, two hatted restaurants, and an international hospitality identity that extends well beyond the regional weekend market.

That doesn't mean the Peninsula is all Jackalope. There are 20+ accommodation options across three tiers, from five-star heritage hotels and top-of-range thermal springs villas to canvas glamping tents minutes from geothermal pools. The question is matching the right property to the right trip.

One important note before we go further.

---

## Two Sorrento properties that are not the same

**Hotel Sorrento** (5–15 Hotham Road) and **InterContinental Sorrento** (23 Constitution Hill Road) are completely separate properties. They share a postcode, a general location, and a century-plus of Peninsula history. They do not share ownership, buildings, management, or brand positioning.

**Hotel Sorrento** is a boutique pub hotel at 5–15 Hotham Road. 150+ year history; five-year renovation completed January 2026. The limestone basement now houses Shi Hui Shi, a Cantonese restaurant from chef Junlin Yi (ex-Red Spice Road). Outdoor pool. Website: hotelsorrento.com.au.

**InterContinental Sorrento** is the IHG hotel at 23 Constitution Hill Road, within the wider Continental precinct. It offers heritage and newer room wings and a guest pool deck. The precinct's restaurants, bars and Aurora Spa have separate bookings. Check room inclusions before adding either. Hotel website: sorrento.intercontinental.com.

They are not interchangeable. Different buildings, different streets, different expectations.

## Luxury tier

### Jackalope Hotel, Merricks North
The MICHELIN Two Keys property. 45 rooms on the Willow Creek Vineyard estate; no children under 12. Vineyard infinity pool, heated spa, infrared sauna. Lair and Vineyard Bath rooms include deep-soak Japanese baths. Two restaurants: Doot Doot Doot (one hat GFG 2025, dinner nightly from 6pm) and Rare Hare (one hat GFG 2025, walk-in from 2pm weekends, wood-fired outdoor dining). Check current room and stay terms directly with the hotel.

166 Balnarring Road, Merricks North. [jackalopehotels.com](https://jackalopehotels.com)

### InterContinental Sorrento, Sorrento (23 Constitution Hill Road)
IHG hotel in the Continental precinct, with rooms in the Heritage Wing, Main Wing and Riley Lane. Guests have a pool deck; CeeCee's breakfast is listed by the hotel as part of the stay. Dining at the precinct and Aurora Spa are separate bookings, so check what your room includes.

*Note: separate from Hotel Sorrento at 5–15 Hotham Road (see disambiguation above).*

23 Constitution Hill Road, Sorrento. [sorrento.intercontinental.com](https://sorrento.intercontinental.com/)

### RACV Cape Schanck Resort, Cape Schanck
The Peninsula's largest resort, 184 rooms. Five-star RACV property adjacent to Cape Schanck lighthouse. Championship golf course on-site. One Spa (massage, facials, hammam). Four dining venues including Cape, Samphire, and family-friendly Mantellina. RACV member benefits apply. The scale makes it the best option for families and groups who want full resort facilities.

[racv.com.au](https://racv.com.au)

### Lancemore Lindenderry Red Hill, Red Hill
40 rooms in a country house hotel. The Dining Room holds two hats in the AGFG 2025/2026; dinner Thursday to Saturday, lunch Friday to Sunday. Lake views, tall silver birch trees, formal gardens. Recognised by Luxury Travel Magazine as one of Australia's most romantic regional hotels. Check current room details directly before booking.

142 Arthurs Seat Road, Red Hill. [lancemore.com.au](https://lancemore.com.au)

### Hotel Sorrento, Sorrento (5–15 Hotham Road)
Boutique pub hotel, 150+ year history, five-year renovation completed January 2026. Outdoor pool. Shi Hui Shi Cantonese restaurant in the limestone basement. 4-star.

*Note: separate from InterContinental Sorrento at 23 Constitution Hill Road.*

hotelsorrento.com.au

### Alba Sanctuary, Fingal
The Sanctuary accommodation at Alba Thermal Springs has five standalone hilltop villas and two premium rooms. Villa and room stays include geothermal bathing each day and breakfast at Thyme; villa inclusions list one private-pool upgrade. Spa treatments are separate bookings. Alba's general springs welcome children with an adult, while its private springs and spa have different age rules. Check the Sanctuary's current occupancy and guest terms before booking.

282 Browns Road, Fingal. [Explore the Sanctuary](https://albathermalsprings.com.au/alba-experiences/the-sanctuary/)

## Winery accommodation

### Polperro Villas (Red Hill)
Four villas in Red Hill, each sleeping two with a king-size bed, indoor spa, open fireplace and vineyard views. There are no outdoor baths in the vines. Cooking facilities are a convection oven and microwave, both suitable for preparing meals; there are no hotplates. The restaurant and cellar door are closed on Mondays and Tuesdays. [Book direct with Polperro](https://www.polperrowines.com.au/escape/) and check the stay conditions for your dates.

### Port Phillip Estate (Red Hill South)
Six luxury suites on the winery estate, adjacent to the hatted restaurant with vineyard-to-bay panoramic views. Freshly squeezed OJ breakfast. Small-scale; design quality.

### Crittenden Lakeside Villas (Dromana)
Three self-contained villas built over the vineyard lake. Spa bath, fully equipped kitchen, lake and vineyard views. *Restaurant on-site trading under a new name post-renovation; verify crittendenwines.com.au before booking dining.*

### Flinders Hotel Quarters (Flinders)
40 architecturally designed rooms in Flinders village, adjacent to the Flinders Hotel pub. Curated MP wine minibar. Walking distance to art galleries and coastal walks. Near Foxeys Hangout and Pt. Leo Estate.

## Glamping and thermal lodge stays

### Peninsula Hot Springs Glamping (Fingal)
Styled tents on the grounds of Peninsula Hot Springs at 140 Springs Lane. The operator currently lists Garden View, Lake View and Secluded Pavilion stays, with geothermal bathing across its site and breakfast in the published packages. Check the exact tent, bathing access, guest rules and optional treatments for your dates. [Compare glamping packages](https://www.peninsulahotsprings.com/accommodation/glamping).

### Peninsula Hot Springs Eco Lodges (Fingal)
Enclosed rooms in shared lodges, sold separately from the glamping tents. A Springs Room lists an in-room hot spring bath; a Peninsula Suite lists an outdoor private hot spring. Current lodge packages list site bathing and breakfast. Check the room-specific bathing, guest rules and inclusions before booking. [Compare Eco Lodge rooms](https://www.peninsulahotsprings.com/accommodation/eco-lodges).

### Iluka Retreat & Camp (Shoreham)
15-hectare property near Red Hill South wineries and Ashcombe Maze. Canvas bell tents with queen beds. Camp kitchen, BBQ, obstacle challenge course. Family-friendly.

Yurt Hideaway was previously included here. Its booking status is currently unverified, so it is not a current stay recommendation. [Read the paused listing](/stay/yurt-hideaway/) before considering a future stay.

## By trip purpose

**Couples (luxury):** Jackalope Hotel (MICHELIN Two Keys, guests aged 12 and over), InterContinental Sorrento (check room and guest terms), Lancemore Lindenderry, Alba Sanctuary (check the current room and private bathing terms).

**Families:** RACV Cape Schanck Resort (largest, most facilities), Portsea Village Resort (1–3 bedroom apartments, indoor pool, tennis court), Plantation House at Whitecliffs (family rooms, 300m from beach).

**Golf:** RACV Cape Schanck Resort, Peppers Moonah Links Resort (confirm transport separately if you plan to visit Peninsula Hot Springs).

**Spa and wellness:** InterContinental Sorrento (Aurora Spa nearby; book separately), RACV Cape Schanck (One Spa), Peninsula Hot Springs Glamping (on-site pool access), Alba Sanctuary (geothermal on-site).

**Wine region base:** Polperro Villas, Port Phillip Estate, Crittenden Lakeside Villas, Flinders Hotel Quarters.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the best luxury hotel?**
Jackalope Hotel holds the MICHELIN Two Keys designation and is the most internationally recognised. InterContinental Sorrento is the most full-service five-star. Different characters: Jackalope is winery-design-food; the InterContinental is heritage-coastal-spa.

**Hotel Sorrento vs InterContinental: what's the difference?**
Different buildings, different streets. Hotel Sorrento: 5–15 Hotham Rd, boutique pub hotel, Jan 2026 renovation complete. InterContinental: 23 Constitution Hill Rd, IHG hotel within the Continental precinct; dining and spa bookings are separate.

**Best stay near Peninsula Hot Springs?**
Peninsula Hot Springs offers on-site glamping tents and separate Eco Lodge rooms at 140 Springs Lane. Alba Sanctuary offers five villas and two rooms at 282 Browns Road with bathing included in its stays. Compare the room format, bathing inclusions and guest rules directly with each operator.

**Family-friendly options?**
RACV Cape Schanck Resort, Portsea Village Resort and Plantation House at Whitecliffs offer family-oriented stays. Jackalope welcomes guests aged 12 and over. Alba welcomes children to its general springs with adult supervision; confirm Sanctuary accommodation and private-experience terms with Alba before booking.

There is a version of lunch on the Mornington Peninsula that eats the whole day. Three hours, a set menu, a second bottle, a taxi. It is wonderful and we have written about it at length in [the long lunch](/journal/the-long-lunch/).

This is the other one. The good lunch that takes an hour and a half, costs what lunch should cost, and leaves the afternoon for the beach, the walk or the drive home. It is what most people are actually looking for, and the Peninsula is better at it than its fine-dining reputation suggests.

The list is organised by where you will be at midday, because on the Peninsula that matters more than anything else.

## On the Red Hill ridge

The hinterland is where most visitors end up at lunchtime, and it has the deepest bench.

### La Baracca at T'Gallant, Main Ridge

The Peninsula's original winery pizza barn and still its most reliable easy lunch for a group with mixed tastes. Wood-fired pizzas, a short list of handmade pasta and the estate's Pinot Grigio by the carafe. It is not trying to be sophisticated and that is the point. Shaded outdoor tables and room for children to move make it the default family lunch on the ridge.

**Order:** too many pizzas, and the Pinot Grigio.

### Red Gum BBQ, Red Hill South

The ridge's antidote to its own seriousness. Low-and-slow barbecue in a big airy room, with table service, generous plates and sides that are genuinely good. It opens for lunch Tuesday to Saturday, with dinner Thursday to Saturday, and is closed Mondays. The kitchen recommends booking, and on holiday weekends you should.

**Best for:** groups, families, and anyone who has had enough of tasting flights.

### Red Hill Brewery, Red Hill South

One of the few breweries in Australia that grows its own hops, with a Belgian-leaning range, a deck and a beer garden. The kitchen runs a changing, beer-friendly menu. The right lunch when someone in the group would rather not drink another Pinot Noir.

**Check:** current kitchen hours and dishes before you go.

## Merricks, Red Hill South and Western Port

### Merricks General Wine Store, Merricks

A village bistro, wine store and produce stop on the road between Red Hill and the Western Port coast. Breakfast and lunch are served daily, with tables by the fire in cooler months and on the decks when it is warm. The wine list leans on the Elgee Park and Baillieu vineyards alongside other local producers. The menu is seasonal, so choose it for the format rather than a particular dish.

**Best for:** a weekday lunch with no fuss, and the most dependable "we just need somewhere good" answer on this side of the Peninsula.

### Rare Hare at Willow Creek, Merricks North

Jackalope's second restaurant and, for a relaxed afternoon, often the better of the two. A wood-fired grill, shared plates and pizza, estate wines by the glass and a room that opens onto the vines. It sits on the slower end of casual: you will want longer than an hour. Book a terrace table in good weather.

**Best for:** a couple or small group who want a vineyard lunch without the ceremony of a degustation.

### Point Leo Wine Terrace, Merricks

The relaxed counterpart to Pt. Leo Estate's formal dining rooms, beside the cellar door and looking over the vineyard and Sculpture Park. Our records show lunch Friday to Sunday, with walk-in and reserved group options. Pair it with a walk through the sculptures before or after.

**Check:** current days and seasonal menus before you drive.

### Crittenden Restaurant, Dromana

Lakeside dining under a vine-strung pergola at one of the Peninsula's founding wine families. Produce-forward, unhurried and one of the better mid-priced meals on the plateau. Take the short lake walk afterwards.

**Best for:** a calm weekday lunch close to the bay towns.

## Near the back beaches

### St Andrews Beach Brewery, Fingal

A former racehorse property turned brewery, with lawns, wood-fired pizza, burgers and beer brewed on site. It is the natural lunch after a morning on the ocean beaches or at the hot springs nearby. Weekend lunches in summer fill fast for somewhere this big, so book ahead.

**Best for:** groups, families and anyone coming off the beach hungry.

## In the bay towns

The bay side runs to pubs, fish and chips and cafés more than destination lunches, and that is often exactly right.

- **Sorrento.** Stringers on Ocean Beach Road now runs as an all-day restaurant, bar and Italian-inspired deli, open from 8am daily and late on Fridays and Saturdays. A good choice for a light lunch between the ferry and the beach.
- **Rye.** The Rye Hotel is a huge foreshore pub a short walk from the front beach, with a deck and a menu that suits families.
- **Portsea.** The Portsea Hotel looks straight over the bay. Our records show it takes walk-ins only from 23 December to 31 January; outside that window, bookings are accepted.
- **Mornington.** Main Street and the harbour have the widest choice of cafés on the Peninsula. Our [waterfront restaurants guide](/journal/waterfront-restaurants-mornington-peninsula/) covers The Rocks at the pier.

## The lighter option: general stores

Some of the best Peninsula lunches are bought over a counter and eaten somewhere with a view.

**Flinders General Store** is the working heart of the village: coffee, a daily loaf from the bakery, breakfast sandwiches and a pastry cabinet that is better than it needs to be. Take lunch to the Flinders foreshore or the start of the West Head walk.

**Somers General**, on the quiet Western Port side, is small, tightly curated and worth the detour: pastries, sourdough, a cheese counter and a local wine shelf.

## How to choose

- **With children:** La Baracca, Red Gum BBQ or St Andrews Beach Brewery.
- **With a group that does not all drink wine:** Red Hill Brewery or St Andrews Beach Brewery.
- **For two, slowly:** Rare Hare or Crittenden.
- **On a weekday with no plan:** Merricks General Wine Store.
- **Between beaches:** a general store and a picnic.

## A note on timing

Peninsula kitchens often keep shorter hours than city ones, and service days change with the seasons. Many close one or two weekdays, and some lunch services end by mid-afternoon. On weekends, in school holidays and through summer, book the lunch you care about. On a weekday outside the holidays, you can usually just turn up.

_Peninsula This Weekend is our weekly dispatch: the plan we'd actually make for the weekend ahead, the booking worth locking in, and the bit of weekend thinking we'd happily do for you._

The second weekend of May rewards a lighter hand.

There is one proper Saturday booking, one reliable weather-proof backup, and one Sunday move worth leaving loose until the day itself. That is enough. The Peninsula in May gets worse when people try to turn three good hours into an eight-stop itinerary.

## The Saturday booking — Kodomo no Hi at Peninsula Hot Springs

If you are planning around one fixed event this weekend, make it this.

Peninsula Hot Springs' Kodomo no Hi programme on **Saturday 9 May** is the most specific family event on the Peninsula right now: cultural programming, full-day bathing access, and enough infrastructure to absorb a full crowd without the day collapsing into queue management. That matters. Plenty of family event copy sounds good on paper and becomes a parking problem by 11am. This one has a better chance than most of feeling intentional all the way through.

Book early, arrive with a real start time, and treat it as the main move rather than the prelude to something else. If you are coming from Melbourne, this is the kind of Saturday that earns the drive on its own.

## The backup that always works — MPRG in Mornington

If the weather turns, or if you want a calmer adult-first plan, go north instead.

Mornington Peninsula Regional Gallery is still the Peninsula's best rainy-day cultural hour: free, serious enough to justify the detour, and close enough to Main Street that lunch can do the rest of the lifting. The useful version of this day is simple: gallery first, then one long lunch or late coffee in Mornington, then stop. Do not pad it out with extra errands just because you are already out.

For visitors who do not need a ticketed event to structure the day, this is the cleaner Peninsula move.

## The Sunday play — keep it soft

Sunday is not the day to force a second headline event.

If you are already staying south, Peninsula Hot Springs **Sunday Sessions** is the better loose-end option: afternoon music, thermal water, and no need to pretend the day needs a hard schedule. If you are not heading that far south, the smarter call is a foreshore walk and one good lunch, with the gallery held in reserve if the weather turns.

The weekend shape is the point: a clear Saturday anchor, then a Sunday with room to breathe.

## Worth knowing before you plan

Do not build the whole weekend around traffic-sensitive transitions.

May weekends are easier than summer, but the Peninsula still punishes over-ambition. A Rye booking, a Mornington lunch, and a late-afternoon Sorrento walk looks tidy on a map and irritating in real life. Pick north or south. Then stay there long enough for the day to feel deliberate.

## The three weekend shapes

- **Saturday:** Kodomo no Hi at Peninsula Hot Springs — one committed family booking that justifies the whole drive
- **Saturday:** MPRG + lunch in Mornington — the clean weather-proof adult plan
- **Sunday:** Sunday Sessions if you are already staying south; otherwise one walk, one lunch, and leave early

This weekend is better with one good booking and a bit of restraint.

**EAT - Moke Dining, Flinders**

Chef Michael Cole placed fourteenth at Bocuse d'Or, the world's most demanding cooking competition, and he is running a 20-seat room on Cook Street in Flinders. The five-course set menu changes weekly, which means what arrives on Saturday the 28th will not be what arrived last Saturday. That is the point. Cole's cooking is technique-led but not fussy - precision in service of flavour, not presentation for its own sake. The room is small enough that you can hear the kitchen. Book Saturday lunch, walk the cliff path to West Head beforehand, and arrive without having eaten since breakfast. The menu earns that preparation. Bookings are essential and the room fills well in advance of spring weekends; check the website and lock in your date before Thursday. Pair the visit with a drive along the Bass coast back through Flinders village.

---

**WALK - West Head Cliff Loop, Cape Liptrap Coastal Park**

The coastal scrub between Walkerville South and West Head is carrying its best spring display right now. Correa, tea-tree, and coastal daisy bush are all in flower along the cliff-top section, and the ocean views out to Waratah Bay are unobstructed from the exposed ridge. The loop runs roughly six kilometres and takes about two hours at a walking pace. Go before 9:00 am on Saturday the 28th and you will have the first half of the track almost entirely to yourself - this stretch of the park sees a fraction of the foot traffic that Cape Schanck does, and the difference in atmosphere is significant. The cliff edge is unfenced in places; keep children and dogs close on the exposed sections. No booking required. Start from the Cape Liptrap Road car park, Walkerville South. Pair it with lunch at Moke in Flinders on the way back north.

**DISCOVERY - Acoustic Saturdays at Peppers Moonah Links, Fingal**

Peppers Moonah Links has been running its Saturday evening live music series since mid-winter and it finishes on 26 December 2026 - which means there are four Saturdays left. The format is simple: a solo or duo act in the bar from 5:00 pm to 8:00 pm, estate views across the golf grounds, and no requirement to be a hotel guest. Spring evening light across the Fingal course at that hour is worth arriving early for. The lineup rotates weekly; check the Moonah Links website before Saturday the 28th to confirm who is playing. This Saturday sits squarely in the golden window where the sun stays up long enough to hold the warmth while the music runs. Book ahead if you plan to eat at the hotel on the same evening. Pair it with a walk along the foreshore at Fingal Beach before the music starts.

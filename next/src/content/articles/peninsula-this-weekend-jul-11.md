*Peninsula This Weekend is a recurring section inside The Insider Note: the plan we'd actually make for the weekend ahead, the booking worth locking in, and the bit of weekend thinking we'd happily do for you.*

Mid-July rain on the Peninsula concentrates things. This is not a weekend that rewards an itinerary. It rewards one deliberate choice: the booking or the plan that stops competing with the weather and uses it instead.

A bath that feels better when the air outside is cold and moving fast. A cellar door where the fire is already lit when you arrive. A long lunch that turns the drive worthwhile.

## The booking worth locking in

### Peninsula Hot Springs, Rye

The thermal contrast is the point on a weekend like this. Nine degrees outside, rain building through the morning, a north-westerly that makes the coast feel considerably colder than the thermometer says - and the pools at a fixed temperature regardless.

Sound Healing Sessions and Daily Studio Yoga are both on the timetable through the week. They fold into a standard bathing visit without requiring a separate booking layer, which is the better way to use them.

The practical note: Sunday is a popular day. Book ahead rather than arriving speculatively and making a decision from the car park.

## The Sunday move

### Stonier Fire and Wine Winter Lunch, Merricks

A long winery lunch in July works when the day outside has already made its case for staying in one place for a few hours. Stonier's dining room and fire make that shape easy: you drive down the Merricks lane in the cold, and there is already somewhere to be when you get there.

This is the right Sunday answer if the week has been busy and the weekend needs to be slower rather than fuller.

## If the weather goes sideways

### National Works on Paper, Mornington Peninsula Regional Gallery

MPRG's winter anchor show - a national survey of works on paper, free entry, running through July. This is the indoor plan that still feels like you did something rather than just waiting for the rain to stop.

The gallery is the right call for a morning or early afternoon when you want cultural weight without the admission question. You can move between it and Mornington foreshore or lunch from here without making a whole-day thing of it.

## The season's ongoing option

Red Hill Truffles and Flinders Truffles are both running through the winter season. If the weekend shape changes last minute, either works as a Saturday-morning anchor with dogs, oak grove, and lunch built into the day. Book ahead for Red Hill; Flinders farmgate days often run without a booking requirement, but check before driving.

## Weekend shape

If you want one clear centre, book the hot springs and let the rest of the day arrange itself around that. If a long Sunday lunch is more your speed, Stonier gives you the fire and the time to use it well. If you have children who need a proper morning-out shape, MPRG is the free answer that does not require a prior booking decision.

The Peninsula in mid-July is at its best when you stop trying to make every hour work and give one thing the whole day.

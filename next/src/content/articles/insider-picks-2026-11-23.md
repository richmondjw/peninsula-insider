## EAT - The Bay Hotel Mornington: Book the vault

The old bank vault beneath The Bay Hotel on Main Street is the most characterful group booking on the Mornington bayside, and spring is the right moment to use it. The building dates from the 1800s, and the former vault - thick door still operational - seats a group in a room that earns its occasion. The gastropub kitchen builds the menu around Peninsula produce, so ask the team to do exactly that when you call. This is not a drop-in booking; the vault runs on request and the kitchen performs better when given a brief. In a week when the rest of the Peninsula is filling up for the summer sprint, locking in a private room mid-week makes sense. The heritage-listed building also has a live music bandroom and a street-level bar if you want to extend the evening. Book directly through the hotel's website well ahead of your preferred date.

**Pair with:** Garagiste or Store Ten for a pre-dinner bottle stop.

## EXPERIENCE - Cape Schanck boardwalk: Full spring light, no crowds yet

The boardwalk from Cape Schanck lighthouse to the lookout above Bushrangers Bay runs through coastal scrub that is producing its best spring growth right now - she-oak and coastal wattle flush green, and the basalt platform at the end frames the Southern Ocean without obstruction. The lighthouse reserve sits at the far southern tip of the Peninsula, which keeps numbers lower than the Arthurs Seat trails even on a Saturday. Walk in the morning, before the northerly builds. The return trip takes around 30 to 45 minutes at a reasonable pace; allow longer if the swell is working and you want to watch it from the platform. Parks Victoria manages the reserve, so check their website for current access conditions before heading out. Bring water - no café at the trailhead. The car park is off Cape Schanck Road.

**Pair with:** Debrief at Flinders Hotel's pub dining, twenty minutes north on the ocean coast.

## DISCOVERY - Emu Plains Market, Balnarring: Running Saturdays until 19 December

Emu Plains Market at Balnarring Reserve runs every Saturday from 9 am to 2 pm and closes for the season on 19 December. The market draws Peninsula producers - cut flowers, seasonal vegetables, olive oil, bread, and a rotation of small-batch makers - in a reserve setting that is properly relaxed compared to the larger township markets. Spring makes the reserve itself worth arriving for: the grass is green, the trees are full, and the heat that compresses the summer version hasn't arrived yet. Coming this Saturday, 28 November, puts you there with four weeks still to run, early enough in the season that the stalls are restocked and the producers are present rather than delegating to helpers. No booking required. Arrive before 11 am for the best selection. Emu Plains Reserve is off Balnarring Road; allow 45 minutes to do it properly.

**Pair with:** The ocean-coast drive south to Flinders or Merricks on the way home.

A winery lawn and a restaurant table can have different dog rules. The confirmed entries below have an explicit published policy; call ahead about your table, weather cover and the day's food offering.

*Policy update, 18 September 2026: the named venue policies below were rechecked. The wider winery list and beach information retain their earlier verification dates.*

## Confirmed dog-friendly

### Stonier Wines, Merricks

[Stonier's official site](https://www.stonier.com.au/about-us) welcomes dogs on the lawn and accepts walk-ins. Check current hours and food availability before making it a lunch stop.

### Main Ridge Estate, Red Hill

The [official booking FAQ](https://mre.com.au/book/) requires dogs to be prebooked, on leads and outdoors, with a maximum of one dog per booking. Ask for a suitable table and confirm availability before travelling.

### Green Olive at Red Hill, Red Hill South

[Green Olive](https://www.greenolive.com.au/) advertises a dog-friendly farm and dining experience. Confirm your dog's seating area when booking; the general welcome does not establish access to every space.

## Check directly before bringing a dog

### Many Little Bar & Dining, Red Hill South

We could not verify a pet policy on the [official site](https://www.manylittle.com.au/) checked on 18 September 2026. Ask about dog access and the permitted dining area before booking.

### Rare Hare, Merricks North

The [official venue page](https://rarehare.com.au/about/) checked on 18 September 2026 did not establish dog access. Ask directly about lawn and dining restrictions; the earlier confirmed recommendation is not retained.

### Foxeys Hangout, Red Hill, and Quealy Winemakers, Balnarring

Earlier versions quoted no-dogs rules. We could not reverify those statements on the current [Foxeys dining page](https://foxeys-hangout.com.au/about/winery-lunch-at-foxeys/) or [Quealy cellar-door page](https://quealy.com.au/pages/cellar-door). This does not establish a policy change or permission to bring pets. Check with each venue.

For cafés, pubs and breweries as well as wine stops, use our [dog-friendly dining guide with official policy links](/journal/dog-friendly-cafes-pubs-wineries-mornington-peninsula/).

## Polperro: no pets permitted

Polperro in Red Hill does not permit pets, as stated in its [official venue FAQ](https://www.polperrowines.com.au/dine/the-deck/). This policy was checked on 7 September 2026.

## How to verify

Call the venue directly before visiting. Have a specific question ready: "Do you permit dogs in the outdoor areas?" is more useful than a general enquiry. Policies change seasonally (particularly in peak summer when outdoor spaces fill up) and often don't get updated on websites.

For beaches that allow dogs between wineries: see our [dog-friendly beaches guide](/journal/dog-friendly-beaches-mornington-peninsula/). Tassells Cove (Safety Beach) and Flinders Beach (Western Port north end) are both off-leash year-round and accessible from the winery circuit.

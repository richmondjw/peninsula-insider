**Balnarring Bakehouse** does not try to be anything beyond what a country bakehouse should be. That is precisely why it works. The Beef-and-Mushroom Pie is the thing to order - short pastry, dense filling, the kind of construction that holds together when you eat it standing up outside. Spring is the right moment to come: the market at Emu Plains Reserve is running Saturdays until mid-December, so you can pair the two stops into a single Balnarring morning. Buy a pie and a custard tart. Eat the pie warm in the carpark; save the tart for the drive. The Saturday Breakfast Sandwich is worth knowing about if you arrive before 10 am.

Balnarring Bakehouse, Balnarring Village Road, Balnarring. Allow 20 minutes. No booking needed; arrive early on Saturdays to avoid missing the pies.

Pair with a walk through the Emu Plains Reserve before the market closes at 2 pm.

---

The Flinders clifftop track between **West Head and Flinders township** is at its most useful in late November. The coastal scrub is flowering - guinea flowers and tea-tree along the exposed headland sections - and the Southern Ocean swell is still building before the full summer holiday crowd arrives. The track runs along the ocean-facing edge of the Peninsula, with the lighthouse precinct visible from the higher sections. Start from the West Head car park on Boneo Road, walk east toward Flinders, turn back at the township edge, and you have done the best of it in 1.5 to 2 hours return. Light is best in the morning when it comes off the water from the east. Carry water; there is no shade on the exposed clifftop sections.

West Head car park, Boneo Road, Boneo. Allow 1.5–2 hours return. No booking. Wear shoes with grip; the cliff edge track is uneven after rain.

Pair with a sandwich from Flinders General Store on Cook Street before you drive back.

**Pub Carols at The Pig & Whistle Tavern** starts Thursday 3 December and runs every Thursday through 17 December, 6 pm to 10 pm. This is not a school concert in a car park - it is a local pub in Main Ridge doing carols properly, with a live band and a crowd that actually sings. The Pig & Whistle is a genuinely good pub on its own terms; the carols format gives you a reason to arrive at a fixed time and stay through the evening. If you have been meaning to get a group together before Christmas, this is the most low-effort way to do it on the Peninsula. Book through Humanitix to secure a table - walk-ins will find it full by 7 pm once word spreads.

The Pig & Whistle Tavern, Main Ridge. Thursdays 3, 10, and 17 December 2026, 6 pm–10 pm. Book at events.humanitix.com/pub-carols. Bookings essential.

Pair with dinner at the pub itself; the kitchen runs through the evening during the carols series.

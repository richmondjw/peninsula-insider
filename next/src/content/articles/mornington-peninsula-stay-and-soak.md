The single best move a Peninsula spa weekend can make is to cut the driving. A long return trip from the hotel to the bathhouse interrupts the slower pace, especially after an evening session.

Choose a stay with a route to the spa that you are happy to drive after bathing. Check the actual journey before reserving either.

Here are the accommodation pairings that actually work for each spa, and the combinations worth treating as proper weekends.

## The hot springs country: Peninsula Hot Springs and Alba Thermal Springs

Both major hot springs sit in Fingal. Staying near Fingal or Cape Schanck keeps the bathhouse trip shorter; Red Hill adds a wine-country stay, while Sorrento adds village dining and coastal walks. Check the drive from the specific accommodation, particularly for an evening session.

### Peninsula Hot Springs Glamping: the onsite option

For serious bathers, the simplest stay-and-soak combination is <a href="/stay/peninsula-hot-springs-glamping/">Peninsula Hot Springs Glamping</a>. Tented accommodation on the Peninsula Hot Springs property itself, with bathhouse access included. No drive. You finish the evening session and walk to your tent. The limitation: design is functional rather than luxurious, and the weather dependency is real in winter. For summer or shoulder-season bathers who want zero commute, this is the unmatched option.

### Jackalope Hotel: the premium ridge stay

<a href="/stay/jackalope/">Jackalope Hotel</a> in Merricks North is a premium ridge stay that requires a drive to either hot springs. Dark architecture, Subtle Energies treatments at <a href="/explore/spas-and-wellness/#spa-by-jackalope">Spa by Jackalope</a>, and Doot Doot Doot's two-hat restaurant on-property. This is the weekend to book for an anniversary, a birthday, or a first serious Peninsula couples trip. Pair a Jackalope spa morning with an Alba afternoon session and you have one of the country's best wellness weekends.

### Lindenderry Country House: the winery stay

<a href="/stay/lindenderry/">Lindenderry</a> at Red Hill sits on one of the Peninsula's most established wine estates and requires a drive to either hot springs. The building is English-country-estate in style rather than design-hotel, which suits a different traveller: quieter, more classical, with a good onsite restaurant and tennis courts for non-spa partners. Excellent for two-to-four-night stays.

### Hotel Sorrento: the village alternative

<a href="/stay/hotel-sorrento/">Hotel Sorrento</a> involves a longer drive to the hot springs but puts you in the centre of Sorrento village, which solves the dining and non-spa hours of the weekend. For mixed couples where one partner wants bathing and the other wants ocean walks and long lunches, Hotel Sorrento is the better base than a ridge hotel.

## The southern tip: One Spa at RACV Cape Schanck

<a href="/explore/spas-and-wellness/#one-spa-racv-cape-schanck">One Spa at RACV Cape Schanck</a> is at the southern end of the Peninsula, with the simplest possible stay-and-soak pairing.

### RACV Cape Schanck Resort: walk from your room

Staying at RACV Cape Schanck is the obvious move: you are already on the property. The coastal setting, on-site spa and golf course make this a simple single-property stay. Check current package dates and inclusions before booking: some spa offers apply only on weekdays. Compare the room and treatment prices separately, especially for a weekend stay.

### Jackalope: the ridge alternative

For couples who want the RACV spa but not the RACV stay, <a href="/stay/jackalope/">Jackalope</a> is a separate hinterland drive away. The drive is scenic, the contrast between boutique stay and resort spa is actually interesting, and the dinner-at-Doot-Doot-Doot layer makes this the more editorially coherent weekend for design-led travellers.

## The Merricks North corridor: Spa by Jackalope

<a href="/explore/spas-and-wellness/#spa-by-jackalope">Spa by Jackalope</a> is designed to serve Jackalope Hotel guests first. The right pairing is obvious.

### Jackalope: walk from your room

Stay at Jackalope, book a treatment, have dinner at Doot Doot Doot. This is the weekend. The drive-to-spa time drops to zero. Check treatment availability and any guest inclusions directly with the hotel. For a two-night stay, pair one Jackalope spa morning with one Alba Thermal Springs afternoon session and you have the complete Peninsula wellness sequence.

## The village end: Endota Mornington

Endota Mornington is a main-street day spa: short treatments, reliable therapists, bookable around a lunch rather than as a full weekend anchor. The stay logic is different: base yourself in the village, add a massage.

### Mornington stays paired with Endota Mornington

Mornington town has several bayside hotel options, all within walking distance of <a href="/explore/spas-and-wellness/#endota-spa-mornington">Endota Mornington</a>. For a weekend centred on Main Street dining and foreshore walks, this is a compact choice: the spa adds polish rather than defining the trip.

## The three stay-and-soak weekends worth treating as products

### 1. "The serious thermal weekend": two nights
Jackalope Hotel. One morning session at Alba Thermal Springs. One treatment at Spa by Jackalope. One long lunch on the Red Hill ridge. One dinner at Doot Doot Doot. Drive home Sunday relaxed. Check current room, spa and dining prices before deciding.

### 2. "The full hot springs ritual": two nights
Peninsula Hot Springs Glamping on Friday. Evening bathhouse session. Saturday morning long breakfast, midday Spa Dreaming Centre treatment, afternoon pool circuit. Sunday morning slower breakfast, ask about checkout time, then drive home. Compare the full glamping, bathing and treatment price with a hotel-based trip before deciding.

### 3. "The southern tip resort weekend": two nights
RACV Cape Schanck Resort. One spa half-day at One Spa. One round at RACV Cape Schanck Golf Course (if golfing) or the lighthouse walk (if not). Dinner on-property or, with a separate drive and reservation, at Jackalope. Sunday morning hot springs drive-over to Peninsula Hot Springs or Alba. Compare current room, spa and dining prices; packages and weekend rates vary.

> Prices may change. Confirm current rates directly with the venue or operator before booking.

## Before you book

- **Check both bookings before paying.** Popular bathing sessions and rooms can both fill. Check availability for both before paying for either, then confirm the bookings together.
- **Check package deals.** Operators may offer packages with specific dates and inclusions. Compare the current package with separate room and spa prices; RACV's named spa offer may be limited to weekdays.
- **Compare midweek dates.** Compare weekday and weekend rates if your dates are flexible, and check which restaurants open midweek.
- **Check seasonal conditions.** Bathing can work in any season. Check weather, availability and current rates for the dates you have in mind.

Sorrento in August belongs to the people who live there. The back beach is empty, the dining rooms take walk-ins, and the village finally feels like a village rather than a car park.

Most Sorrento weekends are built around the summer version of the town: crowded, bright, too warm to walk far. This plan inverts that. It assumes you are here in the cold months, and it treats the village as the base rather than a stop.

Two nights. Nine stops. About 55 minutes of driving across the whole weekend.

## The shape of it

The tip of the Peninsula gives you four landscapes inside a short radius: the bay-side village, the back beach, the heath corridor between them, and the national park at the very end. The off-season weekend works because you can move between all four without queueing for anything, and because the order is set by the light rather than by opening hours.

Friday afternoon orients you. Saturday does the long walk. Sunday leaves gently.

## Friday

### The Continental Sorrento

Check in by 3pm. Leave time to walk the village before the first drink. The front lawn is the right pre-dinner space on a calm evening.

### Sorrento Ocean Baths

A short drive to the back-beach side. Walk the boardwalk above the baths at last light. This is the most dramatic orientation move you can make in Sorrento, and it settles the geography of the whole weekend in about twenty minutes: bay behind you, Bass Strait in front.

### Hotel Sorrento

Dinner somewhere walkable. Hotel Sorrento's bistro is the right call when you have driven all afternoon and want the bay view to do the work.

## Saturday

### Sorrento Back Beach

Early, before nine. Morning light on the basalt reefs is the photograph people keep trying to take. Walk end to end.

### Stringers Sorrento

Breakfast after the walk. Coffee and something from the counter, a few minutes in the village sun.

### Point Nepean Fort Walk

The afternoon move, and the one the weekend hinges on. Shuttle out, walk back. Three hours minimum. Give it the whole afternoon rather than squeezing it around a late lunch, because the walk back is the part that turns two days in a village into a weekend in a landscape.

### Pt. Leo Estate

Drive east for the second night's dinner. The sculpture park at dusk, then a dining room that treats the occasion seriously. This is the only real drive of the weekend.

## Sunday

### Coppins Track

A final morning heath walk through the narrow corridor between the front beach and the back coast. One hour, easy pace.

### Sorrento Gelato

One last village move before the drive home. Gelato counts as breakfast at the end of a weekend.

## What to book, and when

Three things need locking in: the room, and the two dinner tables. The Continental Sorrento and Hotel Sorrento both take direct bookings, and Pt. Leo Estate is worth securing for the Saturday night. Everything else in the plan is a walk, a beach, or a counter, and the off-season is the stretch of the year when those need nothing from you in advance.

If the forecast turns, the fort walk is the piece to move rather than drop. It reads well in weather, and the shuttle takes out the most exposed stretch.

## The plan

Two nights, one village, and nothing to book but a table.

*19 September to 4 October 2026, checked 20 September.* Pick one booked or confirmed anchor activity, then leave space for a beach walk, playground stop or slow lunch nearby. Program listings are not ticket availability, so check the direct page before leaving.

## Rosebud: make a fairy door

[Rosebud Library’s Make Your Own Fairy Door](https://library.mornpen.vic.gov.au/Whats-On/Events/School-Holidays-Make-Your-Own-Fairy-Door-Rosebud-Library) runs Monday 21 September, 11am to noon, for ages 5 to 10. It is free, but booking is essential, and a parent must remain. A gentle, creative holiday anchor with plenty of time left for the foreshore.

## Arthurs Seat Eagle: art from above

At the [Arthurs Seat Eagle Spring Takes Flight program](https://aseagle.com.au/spring-takes-flight/), see the earthwork artwork from the gondola and pick up an activity sheet. The holiday program runs daily, 10am to 5pm.

For the native animal show, the currently useful option is [29 September at 11.30am](https://aseagle.com.au/spring-takes-flight/). Places were limited when checked on 20 September. Select 29 September at 11.30am on the linked booking calendar and make a separate booking if it still suits.

## Enchanted Adventure: an outdoor-day anchor

[Enchanted Adventure](https://www.enchantedadventure.com.au/before-you-visit/) is open daily 9am to 5pm from September to April. Book ahead, bring a picnic if that helps the day run smoothly, and treat Tree Surfing as a separate upgrade with its own availability and conditions.

## Sorrento: make something to take home

The [Sorrento spring school holiday program](https://www.mornpen.vic.gov.au/Things-to-do/Events/Whats-on/Spring-School-Holidays-2026) runs 21 September to 2 October, with pottery, printing, jewellery, painting and mosaics. Bookings are essential: confirm the individual activity’s age guidance and time before committing the family calendar.

## Youth hubs: flexible local sessions

The [Youth Services school holiday program](https://www.mornpen.vic.gov.au/Services/Communities/Youth-services/Youth-programs/Youth-services-school-holiday-program) is for ages 10 to 17, with games and art across Hastings, Mornington and Rosebud hubs. Register directly and check session details before attending.

Devilbend brings a working farm, brewery and taproom together in Tuerong. The operator says it grows its own barley and hops, with beer poured through a ten-tap bar. That makes the farm itself part of the reason to choose this brewery stop.

For an ordinary visit, use the venue's table-booking option. VineHop has separate admission and transport arrangements.

990 Stumpy Gully Road, Tuerong. [Official venue and bookings](https://devilbend.beer/).

Researched guide, not a first-hand review. Operator information checked 16 November 2026.

Plan a walk and a picnic at The Briars in Mount Martha, with a wildlife sanctuary, visitor centre and picnic areas at the permanent precinct. VineHop's Festival Hub is a temporary event use of the property, with separate admission and arrangements.

The council currently lists the Heritage Homestead as closed for restoration and the Balcombe Estuary boardwalk as closed after storm damage. The sanctuary closes in winds above 35km/h, severe-weather warnings or total fire bans. The Community Forest also closes for severe-weather warnings or total fire bans.

450 Nepean Highway, Mount Martha. [Council visitor information and closure notices](https://www.mornpen.vic.gov.au/Things-to-do/The-Briars/About-The-Briars).

Based on published council information, checked 16 November 2026. This guide does not report a visit.

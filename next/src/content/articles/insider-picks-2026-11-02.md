**Balnarring Bakehouse** earns the detour on any spring morning when you are already heading out towards Emu Plains or the Greens Bush trails. The beef-and-mushroom pie is the reason to go - dense filling, short pastry that holds together on the walk back to the car.

The bakehouse sits in Balnarring Village, which means you are in and out quickly without navigating a car park. Order a custard tart alongside the pie. Both are better warm, and the custard tart does not survive the journey home with any dignity. The Saturday breakfast sandwich has its followers, but the pie-and-pastry combination is the cleaner call. There is no seating ceremony here; you eat at the bench outside or in the car with the windows down.

Allow fifteen minutes. No bookings, no apps. Find it in the village on Balnarring Road.

Pair a stop here with the Emu Plains Market one Saturday, since both are within easy walking distance of each other.

---

**Greens Bush**, the largest remnant bushland on the Peninsula, is worth a morning right now. The dry sclerophyll forest along the Greens Bush trail network holds sun orchids, trigger plants, and ground-level heath in bloom through November - the kind of flowering you notice at ankle height rather than from the path.

The main loop from the Boneo Road trailhead takes roughly ninety minutes at a relaxed pace. The junction near the old stock route is where the orchid density increases; slow down there rather than pressing on. Early morning is quieter and the light through the stringybark canopy is better before eleven. Carry water - the bush is drier than the fern gullies on the ocean side.

Access is via Greens Bush Road off Boneo Road near Rosebud. Free entry, no permit required. Allow two hours if you want to take the longer ridge loop.

Pair a Greens Bush walk with the Balnarring Bakehouse beforehand, or the Emu Plains Market on the drive back.

---

**Emu Plains Market** at Balnarring Reserve runs every Saturday through 19 December 2026, 9am to 2pm, and in spring it is one of the better local producer markets on the Peninsula. The stall mix leans towards growers, small-batch preserves, and local honey rather than the craft-market format that has taken over some of the larger Peninsula markets.

The reserve setting keeps it from feeling like a carpark event. Go between nine and ten-thirty for the freshest produce and the best chance at the better bread and pastry stalls. If you are arriving from Greens Bush, the timing works well - finish the walk by nine-thirty and reach Balnarring by ten.

Emu Plains Reserve, Balnarring. Saturdays 9am–2pm, running to 19 December 2026. See emuplainsmarket.com.au. No entry fee.

Pair the market with a Balnarring Bakehouse stop beforehand, then use whatever you picked up to assemble lunch at Merricks Beach.

Most guides to the Mornington Peninsula are written by people who visited once.

They rank the same ten wineries everyone else ranks, recommend the same three beaches, and get the details wrong in ways that only become obvious when you are standing in a car park that no longer exists, outside a cafe that closed in 2023.

We live here. And for years we have wanted a guide that worked the way locals actually use the Peninsula - by season, by mood, by which side of the water you feel like today. One that knows the good table at the pub is the one by the fire, that the bakery sells out by eleven, and that the best time for the back beaches is a weekday in February when the crowds have gone home.

So we built it.

## What Peninsula Insider is

Peninsula Insider is a complete, independent guide to the Mornington Peninsula - every town, every walk, every cellar door, bakery, boat ramp and beach, researched and verified by people who live here.

It is organised the way you actually plan a day:

- **Eat** - restaurants, cafes, bakeries, pubs and producers, from the Sorrento strip to the Flinders village
- **Wine** - cellar doors and vineyards across Red Hill, Main Ridge, Merricks and beyond
- **Stay** - hotels, cottages, glamping and retreats we would genuinely book ourselves
- **Explore** - walks, beaches, lookouts, golf, gardens and the places in between
- **What's On** - markets, festivals and events, kept current week to week
- **Journal** - longer guides: the perfect winter weekend, the Peninsula with kids, with dogs, in a day

Every listing tells you what you actually need to know: when to go, what to order, where to park, and what the catch is. If somewhere is overrated, we say so. If the little place nobody writes about is the best meal on the Peninsula, that is the one we will send you to.

## How we work

Three rules govern everything on this site.

**We go there.** Listings are based on real visits, not press releases. Details - opening hours, menus, prices, access - are verified directly and re-checked on a rolling basis.

**We are independent.** Nobody pays for coverage. If a venue appears here, it is because we think it is worth your time. Where we have commercial relationships, they are disclosed and they never influence what we write.

**We correct the record.** The Peninsula changes fast - chefs move, cellar doors change hands, tracks close for the season. When something on the site is wrong, we fix it. If you spot something, there is a corrections page and we read every submission.

## Where to start

If you are new here, three good doors in:

- **Planning a day trip?** Start with the day-trip guide in the Journal - a realistic one-day loop, not a fantasy itinerary.
- **Here for the weekend?** The winter and autumn weekend guides will steer you right whatever the season.
- **Live locally?** The What's On calendar and the local secrets collection are where the good stuff hides.

The Peninsula has been our home for a long time. Now it has the guide it deserves.

Welcome to Peninsula Insider.

**Many Little, Red Hill South - the sharing menu now, while the spring stone fruit arrives**

Many Little sits inside Polperro Winery and runs the most unexpected menu on the ridge: Sri Lankan-influenced dishes built from Peninsula produce, matched to natural wines chosen specifically to handle tamarind, curry leaf, and coconut sambol. That pairing discipline is what separates it from other winery dining rooms. In spring, the kitchen moves toward lighter sambols and hoppers made with fresh coconut milk, before summer heat shifts the menu again. Order the full sharing format and ask the floor team to pair the wines - the experience only coheres completely when both sides of the equation are working. The room is small and Saturday lunch fills early.

Book ahead through the Many Little website. Allow two hours minimum. Many Little is at Polperro Winery, 150 Red Hill Road, Red Hill South.

Pair with a walk through the Polperro estate vines before you sit down.

**Peninsula VineHop Festival, Saturday 21 November - a self-guided circuit across six Mount Martha venues**

The VineHop Festival runs one day a year and Saturday 21 November is it. The hub is at The Briars in Mount Martha, with five additional linked venues on the circuit. The format is self-guided: a single ticket covers entry and tastings across all six stops between 11:00am and 7:00pm. What makes it worth doing in spring specifically is the outdoor component - the Bay coast venues open their terraces, the light holds until evening, and the new vintage releases are on the table for the first time. This is not a tasting masterclass. It is a relaxed day of moving between places, talking to winemakers when they appear, and eating and drinking at your own pace.

Tickets at events.humanitix.com/peninsula-vinehop-festival-2026/tickets - book before Saturday. The Briars, 450 Nepean Highway, Mount Martha. Gates open 11:00am.

Pair with a walk along the foreshore between venues if your circuit takes you near the Bay.

**Emu Plains Market, Balnarring - Sunday 22 November is worth the drive**

Emu Plains Reserve sits back from the highway in Balnarring on flat grazing land, and the Sunday market there is one of the quieter ones on the Peninsula. No food truck corridor, no amplified music. Stalls lean toward small producers: cut flowers from local growers, preserves, farm eggs, early season stone fruit from hinterland orchards. In late November the stone fruit stalls start appearing, which makes this the right time to come before the Christmas crowds shift the character of every market on the Peninsula. The market runs 9:00am to 2:00pm. Go before 11:00am for the best selection and to avoid the midday heat.

No booking needed. Emu Plains Reserve, Balnarring. Runs Saturdays and Sundays through 19 December 2026, 9:00am–2:00pm.

Pair with a detour to The Briars afterwards if you are heading north on Nepean Highway.

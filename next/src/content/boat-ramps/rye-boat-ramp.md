## What this ramp is

Rye Boat Ramp sits off Point Nepean Road on the southern bay shore. [Mornington Peninsula Shire lists Rye as a Shire-managed ramp and confirms that its ramps are free to use and park at](https://www.mornpen.vic.gov.au/Things-to-do/Sport-and-activities/Boating/Boat-ramps). Use the linked Boating Victoria ramp finder on that page for current facility and condition information.

Conditions at a coastal ramp can change with tide, weather and maintenance. Confirm the current condition and vessel suitability before towing to the ramp.

## Tide dependence

Use the BOM-listed Rosebud Jetty station in the [BOM tidal predictions](https://www.bom.gov.au/australia/tides/) before driving here with a trailer. Tide predictions are planning information, not a guarantee that the ramp is suitable for a particular vessel; check the current ramp condition as well.

## Parking and pressure

Check the current facility listing and signs for parking arrangements. No live occupancy or guaranteed-space information is published, so allow extra time and identify an alternative before departure.

## Best-paired locations and species

Use the dedicated species guides for planning rather than treating a ramp page as fishing advice: [snapper](/fishing/species/snapper/), [King George whiting](/fishing/species/king-george-whiting/) and [squid](/fishing/species/squid/). [Rye Pier](/fishing/locations/rye-pier/) has its own location guide.

[I'm Hooked Fishing Charters](/fishing/charters/im-hooked-fishing-charters/) advertises departures from various Port Phillip and Western Port locations. Confirm the exact ramp directly with the operator before travelling; Rye is not guaranteed.

## Permits and fees

The [Shire's current boat-ramp page](https://www.mornpen.vic.gov.au/Things-to-do/Sport-and-activities/Boating/Boat-ramps) says no permit or fee is required to use and park at a Shire-managed ramp. Check signs on arrival for any precinct-specific restrictions. A [Victorian Recreational Fishing Licence](https://www.vfa.vic.gov.au/recreational-fishing/fishing-licence) is required for recreational fishing unless an exemption applies.

## Safety notes

**Conditions.** Check official tide predictions, marine forecasts, warnings and current ramp information for both departure and return. Do not rely on a generic tide or wind rule from a guide.

**Navigation.** Check current official navigation rules and local notices before operating toward Port Phillip Heads; do not use this page as a substitute for them.

**Return-to-ramp alternative.** [Sorrento Boat Ramp](/boating/ramps/sorrento-boat-ramp/) is the nearest Shire-listed alternative on the same bay shore. Plan alternatives before departure and check current conditions at the alternative too.

## What this ramp is

Warneet Boat Ramp sits on Rutherford Parade on Western Port's eastern shore. [Better Boating Victoria's current Warneet project page](https://betterboating.vic.gov.au/ramp-upgrades/warneet-boating-precinct/) says planning and design for future ramp, car-park and pontoon work is underway and timing depends on future funding; it also records the North and South jetty rebuilds as complete. The published precinct overview identifies the Warneet Foreshore Reserve Committee of Management as the delegated manager of the boat ramp and car park, with Parks Victoria managing the jetties.

Better Boating Victoria records the North and South jetties as complete, while detailed design for future ramp, car-park and pontoon work continues. Because several organisations manage assets in the precinct, confirm current ramp and parking conditions before travelling.

## Tide dependence

The current project source does not publish a fixed safe launch window. Check the BOM-listed Western Port (Stony Point) station in the [BOM tidal predictions](https://www.bom.gov.au/australia/tides/), the forecast and current ramp information. Suitability depends on vessel draft, tide, weather and any works.

## Parking and pressure

Check the current facility information and signs for parking arrangements; no live occupancy is published. The future precinct design includes car-park changes, so confirm current access with the managing authority before departure.

## Best-paired locations and species

Use the dedicated species guides for planning rather than treating a ramp page as fishing advice: [King George whiting](/fishing/species/king-george-whiting/), [squid](/fishing/species/squid/), [gummy shark](/fishing/species/gummy-shark/), [mulloway](/fishing/species/mulloway/) and [pink snapper](/fishing/species/pinkies/).

## Permits and fees

Confirm current fees and parking conditions with the managing authority before travelling. A [Victorian Recreational Fishing Licence](https://www.vfa.vic.gov.au/recreational-fishing/fishing-licence) is required unless an exemption applies.

## Safety notes

**Tidal range.** Western Port changes substantially across the tide. Channel depth, current strength and shallow-water positions shift, so check predictions and current conditions.

**Conditions.** Check official tide predictions, marine forecasts, warnings and current ramp information for both departure and return. Do not rely on a generic current rule from a guide.

**Return-to-ramp alternative.** [Hastings Boat Ramp](/boating/ramps/hastings-boat-ramp/) is the primary nearby Western Port alternative and is listed by Better Boating Victoria as upgraded and open. Plan alternatives before departure; do not improvise a new destination in deteriorating conditions.

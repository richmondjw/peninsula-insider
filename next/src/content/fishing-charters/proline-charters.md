## What this charter actually is

Proline has operated out of Sorrento Pier since 1991, making it one of the longest-running fishing-charter businesses on the Peninsula. The product is defined by the vessels: *Due South*, a 60-foot Westcoaster Sportfish, and *Strait Shooter*, a 41-foot Steber Sportfish, the largest fishing-charter platforms in Victoria.

Fleet size means Proline operates in a segment no other Peninsula operator reaches. Groups of 15 to 20 passengers, corporate functions, shark fishing into Bass Strait, multi-day offshore itineraries to King Island and Portland. The standard bay product (five-hour snapper and whiting sessions departing Sorrento Pier) is solid, but the real differentiator is what becomes possible when you need a vessel large enough for a substantial group or a serious offshore trip.

## Why we chose this charter

One specific reason: Proline is the only operator on the Peninsula that can put 15 or more people on the water together, on a vessel purpose-built for offshore work, with a three-decade track record. If your group exceeds 10 passengers or you want to fish Bass Strait for mako sharks, there is no comparable alternative at Sorrento.

The caveat is cost structure. The group-booking model means solo anglers or couples are paying a premium unless the session fills. The per-person rate at capacity is competitive ($100 to $290 per person depending on session); at half-capacity it is not. For groups who can fill the boat or split the cost between an even number, the value proposition is clear.

> Prices may change. Confirm current rates directly with the venue or operator before booking.

## The trip format

The standard bay session is five hours from Sorrento Pier, typically morning or afternoon. The target on bay sessions is snapper during October to December, shifting to KGW, calamari, salmon, and flathead in the off-season. The snapper method is anchor and burley over known structure in Port Phillip Bay south and southwest of Sorrento. The vessel's size means it can hold position in conditions that push smaller trailer boats off their marks.

Shark trips (8 to 10 hours) depart Sorrento and run into Bass Strait targeting gummy shark, mako, and blue shark. These sessions require offshore comfort and extended travel time to the grounds. Return timing is at the skipper's discretion based on sea conditions.

King Island trips (January to March) and Portland tuna runs (March to July) are multi-day itineraries; contact the operator directly for current scheduling and pricing.

Guests arrive at Berth 1, Sorrento Pier, at least 15 minutes before departure for gear allocation and safety briefing.

## Operator and vessel

The operation traces a family fishing heritage to the 1840s; the current Proline business was established in 1991. Both vessels hold Marine Safety Victoria commercial vessel registration. 35 years at the same berth at Sorrento Pier is itself an editorial signal: a charter business does not persist at a premium location through successive tourism cycles without a reliable product.

The specific strength is the offshore capability. Both vessels can cross Bass Strait safely in conditions that would prevent smaller charter boats from departing. Both are described as suitable for wheelchair access; confirm specific boarding requirements at Sorrento Pier with the operator at booking.

## What's included

Rods, reels, bait, and tackle on standard sessions. On shark trips, heavy tackle and traces are provided. Food and drinks are not included on standard sessions; the operator can advise on what provisions to bring for longer offshore trips. A tip of $10 to $20 per person for a productive session is customary but never expected.

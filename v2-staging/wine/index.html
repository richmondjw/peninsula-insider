Wine Country Cellar doors · Pinot noir · Cool-climate producers

The Peninsula's best bottles start on the ridge.

Pinot noir and chardonnay built on basalt, plus the breweries and distilleries that now round out the region. This hub sorts them by what they're actually for — long lunches, appointment tastings, and afternoons that earn the drive.

— By The Peninsula Insider Editors Read the shortlist 8 min
Editor's Letter

Three ways a Peninsula wine afternoon works.

Either you anchor the day to one benchmark cellar door with a long lunch and let the rest of the afternoon be slow driving. Or you book two appointment-only tastings, one either side of a small village meal. Or you skip the wineries altogether and use a brewery, distillery, or orchard to make the day feel a little less formal.

The question is no longer whether the drive is worth it. It's which cellar doors to open, and in what order.

This list is editorial. Every entry has been visited and the wine has been drunk at the source. The list is ordered by editorial authority and Halliday score where applicable: benchmark producers first, then the smaller rooms doing quiet, interesting work below them.

—
The Editors

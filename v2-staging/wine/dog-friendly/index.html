Wine Country Confirmed · April 2026

Dog-friendly wineries on the Mornington Peninsula.

Confirmed outdoor areas at Stonier, Main Ridge Estate, Kerri Greens, Green Olive, and Montalto Piazza. Policies change — always call ahead.

— By The Editors
Editor's Note

Genuinely possible, but limited.

Dog policies change. This list is based on confirmed outdoor-area policies as of April 2026. Call or email ahead before visiting with a dog.

Dog-friendly winery visits on the Peninsula are genuinely possible but limited. Most cellar doors and winery restaurants do not permit dogs. The exceptions are outdoor garden areas and casual dining spaces at a small number of estates. Main Ridge Estate requires pre-booking before arriving with a dog — do not assume you can walk in.

The clearest options: Stonier Wines (lawn), Kerri Greens (outdoor), Green Olive at Red Hill (garden and restaurant), and Montalto Piazza (casual outdoor dining, walk-in).

For confirmed non-dog-friendly venues: Foxeys Hangout and Quealy Winemakers explicitly do not permit dogs.

Last fact-verified: 23 April 2026

Frequently asked

Questions before you arrive.

Which wineries allow dogs?

Confirmed as of April 2026: Stonier (lawn), Main Ridge Estate (on-lead, pre-book required), Kerri Greens (outdoor), Green Olive at Red Hill (garden). Most Peninsula cellar doors do not permit dogs. Foxeys Hangout and Quealy Winemakers explicitly exclude dogs.

Do I need to call ahead?

Yes, always. Main Ridge Estate explicitly requires pre-booking before arriving with a dog. For all other venues, call or email ahead as policies change seasonally.

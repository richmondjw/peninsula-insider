Best cellar doors on the Mornington Peninsula.

Walk-in and appointment-only tastings ranked by editorial authority. Montalto, Foxeys, Paringa, Ten Minutes by Tractor, and more.

— By The Editors
Editor's Letter

More than forty cellar doors. Not all are worth the stop.

The Peninsula has more than forty cellar doors. Not all of them are worth the stop, and the ones worth visiting have different formats — some are walk-in, some work better with a booking, and a handful are appointment-only operations where the wines are good enough that you should plan around them rather than drop in.

The walk-in group is the easiest entry point. Montalto is the most complete estate: tasting room, Piazza dining, kitchen garden, and a sculpture trail. Foxeys Hangout is the most genuinely informal and biodynamically committed. Paringa Estate is the clearest value-for-tasting stop on the ridge.

The appointment wineries — Ten Minutes by Tractor, Pt. Leo Estate, Kooyong, Yabby Lake — require more planning but produce the Peninsula's most serious wines.

If you are interested in what the region's best terroir-driven production actually tastes like, build a trip around at least one of them.

Frequently asked

Questions before you book.

Which cellar doors can I visit without a booking?

Montalto, Foxeys Hangout (no bookings taken at all), Paringa Estate, T'Gallant, Red Hill Estate, Polperro, Green Olive at Red Hill, Ocean Eight, Crittenden Estate all welcome walk-ins for groups of 1–8.

Best for a first visit?

Montalto — the most complete first-visit estate. Paringa Estate and Foxeys Hangout for a more focused single-estate experience.

What is a typical tasting fee?

$15–$25 per person, usually redeemable on a purchase. Foxeys Hangout charges no tasting fee. Ten Minutes by Tractor and Pt. Leo Estate have formal tasting experiences at higher price points.

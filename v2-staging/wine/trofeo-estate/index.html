Trofeo Estate

The Peninsula's only amphora winery, terracotta-aged Sangiovese, Fiano, and Nebbiolo, and a cellar worth visiting on its own terms.

The Insider's note

On Trofeo Estate.

Trofeo Estate is the Peninsula's one and only amphora winery. The team ages a meaningful portion of the estate's production in terracotta pots rather than oak barrels, and the resulting wines, Sangiovese, Fiano, Nebbiolo, have a textural depth and a savoury edge that nothing else in the region produces. The ancient method sits oddly against the Peninsula's cool-climate reputation and that is exactly why it works: the contrast is the conversation.

The barrel room itself is worth the visit. Stepping into a Peninsula cellar and finding it lined with huge clay amphora rather than the expected rows of French oak is the kind of small shock that reorients how you think about the region's possibilities. The tasting is generous and the team is happy to explain the winemaking in detail, the how and the why of terracotta versus oak.

Book the cellar tour. Taste the Sangiovese and the Fiano back-to-back. Bring home whichever one most surprises you.

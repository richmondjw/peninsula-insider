Wine Country Subregion · Balnarring

Balnarring wineries.

A quieter corner of the Mornington Peninsula wine region — south-facing slopes above Western Port Bay, cooler and more wind-exposed than the interior plateau.

— By The Editors
Editor's Note

The quieter corner of the Peninsula.

Balnarring sits on the south-facing slopes above Western Port Bay, south of Merricks. The subregion is cooler and more wind-exposed than the interior plateau, with a character that rewards explorers willing to look beyond the well-signposted cellar doors further north.

The producers here are typically smaller operations — often appointment-only or open on limited days — but they represent some of the most focused winemaking on the Peninsula. The village itself has a good bakery and pub, making a half-day of it straightforward.

—
The Editors

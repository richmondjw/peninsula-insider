Dog-Friendly Peninsula with a dog

The Peninsula, properly, with the dog.

Beaches, wineries, cafés, stays, walks, and backup options that actually work with a dog in the plan. No fluffy pet-travel clichés — just useful local trip logic.

Read this first

The national park bans dogs.

Mornington Peninsula National Park prohibits dogs entirely — including Cape Schanck, Point Nepean, and most of the Peninsula's best walks. Greens Bush and Arthurs Seat State Park are the same.

The bay-side foreshores, most cafés with outdoor seating, and a reasonable number of cellar doors and holiday rentals are the Peninsula dogs can enjoy — and genuinely enjoy. Plan around those.

Dogs banned from the park. Welcomed on the foreshore. Both are true.

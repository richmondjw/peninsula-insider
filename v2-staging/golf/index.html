Golf on the Peninsula.

World-ranked public access. Serious course architecture. Hot springs within fifteen minutes. Australia's best golf destination for a weekend that still works for non-golfers.

Editor's note

Honest, locally literate, useful.

Not every respected course on the Peninsula is equally useful to a visitor. This guide tells you which rounds are worth building a weekend around, which carry more aspiration than access, and how golf fits into a Peninsula trip that still works for the rest of the group.

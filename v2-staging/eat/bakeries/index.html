Eat & Drink Bakeries — Updated April 2026

Best bakeries on the Mornington Peninsula.

Six honest bakeries, where each one is, and what each does well. The sell-out-by-noon problem is real — arrive before ten on weekends.

— By The Peninsula Insider Editors Jump to the list 6 bakeries
Editor's Letter

Small, serious, and sold out by noon.

The Peninsula bakery scene is smaller than the cafe scene and better for it. There are six bakeries worth knowing about, scattered across the Peninsula from Mornington to Flinders. Each has a reason: a particular loaf, a particular morning, a particular detour that makes it worth the drive from wherever you are staying.

The sell-out-by-noon problem is real across most of them. Arriving before ten on weekends is strongly advised. None of them have the inventory depth of a city bakery — they make what they make and stop when it is gone. That is largely the point.

Flinders Sourdough is the benchmark. Everything else maps from there by location and what you need the stop to do for the rest of the day.
—
The Editors

Frequently Asked

The questions we get asked.

What is the best bakery on the Mornington Peninsula?
Flinders Sourdough — closed Monday and Tuesday, sells out by early afternoon on weekends. Arrive before 9am for the best selection. Johnny Ripe in Mornington is the best option on the main strip. Sourdough Kitchen in Balnarring and Red Hill Bakery are strong hinterland alternatives.

When do Peninsula bakeries open?
Most open between 7am and 8am. Weekend sell-outs are common. Arriving before 10am is strongly advised across all six.

Where is the best sourdough on the Peninsula?
Flinders Sourdough is the benchmark. The miche and seeded rye are the loaves to buy. Sourdough Kitchen (Balnarring) and Red Hill Bakery are strong alternatives with slightly different styles.

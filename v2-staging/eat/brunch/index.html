Eat & Drink Brunch — Updated April 2026

Best brunch on the Mornington Peninsula.

Commonfolk Coffee in Mornington, Many Little in Red Hill, and the village cafes worth planning a morning around.

— By The Peninsula Insider Editors Jump to the list 9 spots
Editor's Letter

The first half of a Peninsula day.

Brunch on the Mornington Peninsula means either a specialty coffee destination in Mornington or a village cafe you would drive forty minutes for if you were not already nearby. The Peninsula's brunch scene is not a destination category in itself — it is the first half of a Peninsula day, the thing you do before the cellar door or the beach walk.

Commonfolk Coffee (Mornington) is the benchmark. Everything else organises around it by geography. Somers General for the southern Mornington Shire bayside corridor. Merricks General for the ridge. Flinders General for the ocean coast. Many Little if you want something that reads more as a proper restaurant with a daytime offer.

The weekend queues are real at the popular spots. Arriving before 9:30am solves most of them.
—
The Editors

Frequently Asked

The questions we get asked.

Where is the best brunch on the Mornington Peninsula?
Commonfolk Coffee (Mornington, 16 Progress Street) is the specialty coffee benchmark. For a more elevated daytime offer, Many Little Bar & Dining (Red Hill South) is the hatted-restaurant alternative. Merricks General Wine Store is the most beautiful setting on the Peninsula for a morning meal.

Which brunch spots are dog-friendly?
Commonfolk Coffee (back courtyard), Somers General Store, and Flinders General Store all have confirmed dog-friendly outdoor seating. Check directly with venues as policies can change.

Do I need to book?
Many Little takes bookings and can fill on weekends. Most village cafes on this list are walk-in. Arriving before 9:30am avoids the main weekend queues.

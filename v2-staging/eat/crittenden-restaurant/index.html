Crittenden Restaurant

Lakeside dining under a pergola at one of the Peninsula's founding wineries — now operating as Crittenden Restaurant under Head Chef Brunno Melo.

The Insider's note

On Crittenden Restaurant.

Crittenden was there before most of the cellar doors that now define the Peninsula, and the restaurant at its heart is one of the most quietly excellent dining rooms on the plateau — set beside a still lake under a pergola strung with vines. Now operating as Crittenden Restaurant under Head Chef Brunno Melo, the kitchen brings a produce-forward menu that celebrates local growers without the preachy apparatus.

The wine list is a generous walk through the estate's own catalogue — Garry and Rollo Crittenden's pioneering range including Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, and the Spanish-leaning Los Hermanos wines — with smart additions from elsewhere. Order whatever fish the kitchen is running that week. Take the lake walk afterwards.

A reliable weekday lunch and one of the best mid-priced meals on the plateau. The room's best trick is that it still feels entirely unhurried.

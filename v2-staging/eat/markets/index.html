Eat & Drink Markets — Updated April 2026

Best markets on the Mornington Peninsula.

Red Hill on the first Saturday, Mornington every Wednesday, Balnarring seasonally — the three Peninsula food markets worth planning a morning around.

— By The Peninsula Insider Editors Jump to the list 3 markets
Editor's Letter

Three markets, three characters.

The Peninsula has three established food markets, each with a different character. Red Hill Market is the one worth planning a morning around — a large monthly market on the first Saturday of the month at Red Hill Showgrounds, with Peninsula produce, artisan food, and a coffee and brunch scene that makes the visit feel like an event rather than a shopping errand.

Mornington Farmers' Market runs every Wednesday at Craig's Reserve and is the best option for fresh local produce during the week — smaller, more focused, and less tourist-facing than Red Hill. Balnarring Farmers' Market is the most genuinely local of the three — a village-scale market with strong produce and a relaxed pace.

—
The Editors

Frequently Asked

The questions we get asked.

Best market on the Mornington Peninsula?
Red Hill Market (first Saturday of the month, Red Hill Showgrounds). Mornington Farmers' Market (every Wednesday, Craig's Reserve) for weekday produce. Balnarring for the most local village-scale experience.

When is Red Hill Market?
First Saturday of every month. Gates open 8am, most stalls wind down by 1pm. Arrive by 9am for best produce. Dogs welcome on leashes.

Are markets dog-friendly?
Red Hill (leashed dogs welcome) and Balnarring (outdoor, generally dog-friendly). Verify before visiting.

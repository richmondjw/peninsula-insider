Explore 5 day-trip routes

Day trips that repay the drive.

Melbourne to Peninsula in 90 minutes. These are the day-trip routes worth making — sequenced as itineraries, not lists, so the drive, the meal, and the walk actually fit together.

The editor's note

Don't try to see everything.

The best Peninsula day trip picks one thread — coast, wine, hot springs, foreshore, panorama — and commits to it. The failure mode is trying to combine two: a morning winery visit plus an afternoon at Cape Schanck plus sunset at Sorrento means too much driving, rushed lunches, and a long evening back to Melbourne.

Each of the routes below is a complete day. Pick one; do it properly. Drive times between the anchor points are short enough that you will cover more ground than you expect even when you move slowly.

One Peninsula day, done well, is worth two done in a hurry.
Sorrento → Cape Schanck

The Coastal Morning

  1. Breakfast at Sorrento Ocean Beach or a coffee on Ocean Beach Road.
  2. Walk the Coppins Track toward Diamond Bay (as far as you like).
  3. Drive to Cape Schanck; do the boardwalk to the lighthouse platform.
  4. Lunch back in Sorrento town or at Portsea Hotel on the terrace.
  5. Afternoon beach time at Shelly Beach for the calm side, or Sorrento Back Beach for drama.
Red Hill and Main Ridge

The Cellar Door Day

  1. Arrive at 11am. First stop: Foxeys Hangout for the view.
  2. Lunch at Montalto — the Peninsula's best complete winery-lunch experience. Book ahead.
  3. Afternoon cellar doors: Ten Minutes by Tractor or Polperro.
  4. Finish with a sculpture-garden walk at Pt. Leo Estate (time permitting).
  5. Drive home before dusk; arrange a driver if you are seriously tasting.
Cape Schanck & Fingal

The Hot Springs Reset

  1. Walk Bushrangers Bay from Cape Schanck (5km return, 2 hrs). Arrive 8.30am.
  2. Mid-morning coffee at the Cape Schanck Lighthouse café, or drive to Rye.
  3. Pre-book a Twilight or afternoon session at Peninsula Hot Springs or Alba. Three hours.
  4. Light lunch at the springs or drive to Rye/St Andrews for a café meal.
  5. Drive back to Melbourne late afternoon; you will want an early night.
Mornington and Mount Martha

The Mornington Foreshore Loop

  1. Breakfast at Commonfolk in Mornington (or Bayview in Mount Martha for a quieter start).
  2. Walk the Mornington foreshore past the bathing boxes to Schnapper Point.
  3. Coffee on the pier. Browse Main Street.
  4. Drive to Mount Martha and walk the clifftop section for a southern view.
  5. Lunch at Mount Martha Beach or in Mornington town on the return.
Arthurs Seat and Red Hill

The Arthurs Seat Panorama

  1. Drive up or take the Arthurs Seat Eagle gondola.
  2. Walk the TC McKeller summit circuit — 1.8km, 1 hr, big views.
  3. Late morning: drive into the Red Hill plateau. Coffee at Red Hill Brewery or a cellar door.
  4. Lunch: Tedesca Osteria (book ahead) or Merricks General Wine Store for something more relaxed.
  5. Afternoon: one or two cellar doors, or a sculpture-park walk at Pt. Leo Estate.

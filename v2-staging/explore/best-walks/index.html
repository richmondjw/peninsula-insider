Explore 11 walks · honestly ranked

The Peninsula walks worth the drive.

Not a trail database. An editorial ranking — which walks actually deliver the day they promise, and which ones are famous for reasons other than the walking.

The editor's note

On ranking walks.

Parks Victoria has the facts. AllTrails has the reviews. Neither will tell you that Bushrangers Bay is the walk most visitors should be doing instead of the Cape Schanck boardwalk they have heard of, or that the Long Point Circuit in Greens Bush is the Peninsula's most under-visited good walk. This list will.

Dogs are banned from Mornington Peninsula National Park and from Greens Bush. That eliminates most of the entries below for dog owners. We flag this clearly on each walk rather than burying it — see the dog-friendly guide for the alternatives that genuinely work.

The walk that is famous is not always the walk that is best.

If you only do one

First-time visitor: Bushrangers Bay.

Twenty minutes: Cape Schanck boardwalk.

Full day: Two Bays Trail (or at least one segment).

With a coffee: Coppins Track from Sorrento.

The ranking

Eleven walks, editorially ranked.

  1. 01

    Bushrangers Bay Walk

    Cape Schanck · 5km return · 2 hrs · Easy–Moderate

    The walk that consistently surprises first-time visitors. Basalt cliffs, a creek-to-sea bay where almost no one is there after 11am on weekdays. The Peninsula walk to clear the morning for.

  2. 02

    Coppins Track

    Sorrento → Diamond Bay · 4km one-way · 90 min · Easy

    The strongest town-adjacent clifftop walk on the Peninsula. Flat clifftop path above Bass Strait, with Diamond Bay as a genuine endpoint. Coffee in Sorrento either side.

  3. 03

    Point Nepean Walk

    Point Nepean · 13.5km · 4–5 hrs · Easy–Moderate

    Military fortifications from 1878, the quarantine station, Cheviot Beach where PM Harold Holt disappeared. The history competes with the view, which is the highest compliment a walk can earn.

  4. 04

    Two Bays Walking Trail

    Dromana → Bushrangers Bay · 26km · 1–2 days · Moderate

    The Peninsula's definitive long walk. Ancient grasstrees, eucalypt forest, fern gullies, grassland. Best split across two days with overnight camping at Lightwood Creek.

  5. 05

    Greens Bush — Baldrys Long Circuit

    Main Ridge · 3.6km · 1.5 hrs · Easy

    Victoria's largest surviving coastal heathland remnant. Ancient grasstrees and a reasonable chance of kangaroos at dusk. The Peninsula's most under-visited good walk.

  6. 06

    Cape Schanck Boardwalk

    Cape Schanck · 20 min return · Accessible

    Short, surfaced, and genuinely spectacular. The lighthouse platform above Bass Strait is the best short-stop on the southern Peninsula. Do not dismiss it because it is brief.

  7. 07

    Farnsworth Track

    London Bridge → Portsea Ocean Beach · 1.5km one-way · 30 min · Easy

    The late-afternoon clifftop walk. Best done at 4pm in autumn or winter for the light. Not for anyone uncomfortable near exposed edges.

  8. 08

    Arthurs Seat Summit Circuit

    Arthurs Seat · 1.8km · 1 hr · Moderate

    TC McKeller Walk, William Ricketts sculptures, and views to Melbourne on a clear day. Combine with the Eagle gondola for a one-way walk.

  9. 09

    The Coastal Walk

    Cape Schanck → London Bridge · 26–30km · 2 days · Moderate–Hard

    The Peninsula's wildest walking. Bass Strait clifftops, Bay of Islands, St Paul's, Diamond Bay, Jubilee Point. Exposed and demanding; autumn or spring only.

  10. 10

    Mornington Foreshore

    Mornington · Varies · Flat · Dogs on lead

    Flat path between Mornington Pier and Schnapper Point, past the bathing boxes. Reliably good, any weather, any fitness level. Cafés at both ends.

  11. 11

    Mornington Peninsula Walk (Full Circuit)

    Peninsula-wide · 97.5km · 8–10 days

    The full circuit — Two Bays, Coastal Walk, Point Nepean, Bay Trail. More enjoyable in sections across several visits than as a single time trial. Not a race.

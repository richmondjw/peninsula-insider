Explore Autumn field guide

The Peninsula, experienced.

Not a list of things to do. A field guide to the walks, beaches, markets and rituals that make the Peninsula a place worth returning to — matched to the kind of day you are actually trying to have.

By the editors · Vintage on the ridge, pinot lunches in low sun, and cellar doors with a fire going in the corner.

The editor's note

On exploring properly.

The Peninsula rewards people who know how to read it. The Cape Schanck boardwalk is twenty minutes and genuinely unmissable. The Bushrangers Bay walk is an hour from the same carpark and most visitors never do it. The first gets recommended; the second changes the day.

What follows is not a catalogue. It is a filter — short walks that earn the drive, beaches that repay the effort of finding them, markets worth timing a weekend around, and the rituals (spa, lookout, long coffee) that stitch it all together.

The honest walk recommendation is more useful than the famous one.

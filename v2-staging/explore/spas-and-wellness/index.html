Spa & Wellness 5 venues · ranked and compared

The Peninsula spa guide.

Two serious geothermal bathhouses. A clifftop resort spa. A design-hotel treatment programme. Five wellness venues on the Peninsula, honestly ranked and matched to the weekend you are trying to have.

The editor's note

Alba or Peninsula Hot Springs.

The question every first-time visitor asks. Both are excellent. They are different experiences and the right answer depends on the weekend.

Choose Alba if you value design, calm, and adult-only sessions. Alba is architecturally tighter, quieter, and session-capped — which is its strength. For couples, design enthusiasts, and for people who want a bathhouse where you can hear yourself think, Alba is the stronger choice.

Choose Peninsula Hot Springs if you want the fuller bathing circuit, more pool variety, and a programme that rewards a longer session. Peninsula Hot Springs is the more complete wellness destination — and can feel crowded on weekends. Book the Twilight session or an early-morning weekday to get the best of it.

Doing both in one day is not recommended. The second bathhouse blurs into the first; your skin notices. Do one per visit, or two visits across a two-night stay with a full day between them.

One bathhouse per visit. Both across two visits.
The ranking

Five venues, ranked and matched.

  1. 01

    Alba Thermal Springs

    Fingal · Geothermal bathhouse · Adults

    Opened 2022. Architecturally tight, quieter than Peninsula Hot Springs, adult-focused with session caps that protect the atmosphere. The strongest single booking on the Peninsula for first-time visitors who value design and calm over volume. Book mid-week Twilight sessions for the best experience.

  2. 02

    Peninsula Hot Springs

    Fingal · Geothermal wellness complex

    Opened 2005, expanded steadily since. The larger, more varied Peninsula bathhouse. More pools, more treatment options, more program depth — and more crowds. Best approached with a plan rather than wandered through. Avoid peak weekends; book the Bath House Twilight or early-morning sessions for the fullest experience.

  3. 03

    One Spa at RACV Cape Schanck

    Cape Schanck · Resort day spa

    Clifftop views, resort infrastructure, strong treatment programme. The Peninsula's best resort-style day spa when you want a structured treatment rather than a bathing session. Works particularly well combined with a round at the RACV golf course.

  4. 04

    Spa by Jackalope

    Merricks North · Boutique hotel spa

    Design-led treatment rooms inside the Peninsula's most architecturally distinctive boutique hotel. Small, intimate, and more about the treatment than the facility. Strongest when paired with an overnight stay at Jackalope.

  5. 05

    Endota Spa (Various)

    Mornington, Sorrento · Day spa · Treatment rooms

    The reliable day-spa option — bookable without much notice, treatment-focused rather than bathing-focused. Works as a shorter wellness booking between other Peninsula activities. Less distinctive than the flagship spas but practical and consistent.

Planning

How to book properly.

Session length. A proper bathhouse visit is three to four hours. An hour is not enough to do the circuit properly or recover from it. Block the time.

Timing. Midweek and early morning or Twilight sessions are significantly better than Saturday midday. Booking ahead is required at both Alba and Peninsula Hot Springs — walk-ups are increasingly rare.

Pair with a walk. Cape Schanck walks are 15 minutes from both bathhouses. The combination — walk in the morning, spa in the afternoon — is the Peninsula's strongest single-day format.

Pair with accommodation. For a full wellness reset, stay within 15 minutes of the bathhouse. Fingal, Cape Schanck and the Rye / St Andrews corridor all work.

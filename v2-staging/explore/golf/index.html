Golf 11 courses · public and private

The Peninsula golf guide.

World-ranked public access. Serious course architecture. Hot springs fifteen minutes away. Australia's most complete golf destination for a weekend that still works for non-golfers.

The editor's note

Why this list matters.

Australia has world-class golf; very little of it is genuinely public access. The Peninsula is the exception. St Andrews Beach is bookable online, consistently listed in the world top 100, and you do not need a member connection to play it. The Dunes, Moonah Links, and Cape Schanck RACV similarly accept public bookings at courses that would be private clubs in most other markets.

We do not publish green fees — they change, pricing tiers shift, and dynamic pricing has arrived. Check course websites directly before booking. Weekends at the top courses book 3–4 weeks ahead; the most-referenced times (Saturday morning at St Andrews Beach) book further out still.

World top-100, bookable online. Do not take that for granted.
The ranking

Eleven courses, honestly ranked.

  1. 01

    St Andrews Beach Golf Course

    Fingal · Public

    Tom Doak-designed links, ranked in the world top 100 by every serious publication that ranks them. The strongest public-access round on the Peninsula by a considerable margin. Book online; 3–4 weeks ahead for weekends.

  2. 02

    The National Golf Club

    Cape Schanck · Private

    Australia's best 54-hole property. Three courses across a serious property. Member-only or member-guest; not part of most weekend plans unless you have a connection.

  3. 03

    The Dunes

    Rye · Public

    Serious coastal links character, bookable online without a member connection. Consistently strong in the national rankings. The right second choice when St Andrews Beach is booked out.

  4. 04

    Moonah Links

    Fingal · Public

    36 holes including the Open Course — genuinely tournament-standard. Hosts the Victorian Open. The better choice for golfers who want two rounds in a weekend without repeating a course.

  5. 05

    Cape Schanck Golf Course (RACV)

    Cape Schanck · Public

    The resort course at RACV Cape Schanck. Drops towards Bass Strait with dramatic coastal holes. Strong for a group staying onsite or combining with the spa and walking corridor.

  6. 06

    Rosebud Country Club

    Rosebud · Public

    Two courses at a serious Peninsula club. Strong mid-tier public option — less dramatic than the coastal links, more forgiving for casual rounds.

  7. 07

    Eagle Ridge

    Boneo · Public

    Flatter, more forgiving course. The right choice for mixed-ability groups or for golfers wanting a reliable round without the coastal-links exposure.

  8. 08

    Mornington Golf Club

    Mornington · Public

    The closest Peninsula course to Melbourne. Works well for a round before lunch in Mornington town. Less distinctive than the southern courses but genuinely useful.

  9. 09

    Flinders Golf Club

    Flinders · Public

    Clifftop course overlooking Bass Strait. Shorter than the links courses but with some of the Peninsula's most dramatic views on a coastal hole.

  10. 10

    Portsea Golf Club

    Portsea · Public

    At the tip of the Peninsula. Less prestigious than the coastal links of Fingal and Rye, but a pleasant course for a round combined with lunch at Portsea Hotel.

  11. 11

    Sorrento Golf Club

    Sorrento · Public (restricted)

    A compact coastal club. Access rules vary; check before booking. Best for golfers basing themselves in Sorrento who want a walk-distance round.

Public access matrix

Booking access, summarised.

Public-access, world-ranked: St Andrews Beach. The rare combination.

Public-access, serious architecture: The Dunes, Moonah Links (Open Course), Cape Schanck (RACV).

Public-access, reliable: Rosebud Country Club, Eagle Ridge, Mornington Golf Club, Flinders, Portsea, Sorrento.

Private / member-guest only: The National Golf Club.

Most public courses accept online bookings directly through the course website. For weekend mornings in peak season (October–April), book three to four weeks ahead; for the flagship courses, plan further out. Winter weekends are almost always available at short notice — and links courses like St Andrews Beach drain faster than inland courses, staying open when others close.

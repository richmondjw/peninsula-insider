Explore Free Peninsula

The Peninsula, for free.

Beaches, walks, lookouts, markets and foreshore — the Peninsula's best experiences without an entry fee. The paid experiences are excellent too, but plenty of the best moments cost nothing.

The editor's note

Free does not mean second-tier.

The Cape Schanck boardwalk is free. Bushrangers Bay is free. The Mornington Farmers Market is free to enter. Arthurs Seat's summit views are free. Every Peninsula beach is free. Most of what defines the Peninsula as a destination costs nothing beyond the drive in and a parking spot.

The paid experiences — Peninsula Hot Springs, hatted restaurants, the Arthurs Seat Eagle gondola — are excellent and deserve their prices. But a Peninsula day that stacks several free experiences together reliably produces one of the better weekends of the year.

Beaches

Free beaches.

  • Every Peninsula beach is free to access. Pick bay side for swimming, back side for drama.
  • Sorrento Back Beach — free parking (limited on summer Saturdays). The Peninsula's most consequential back beach.
  • Mills Beach, Mornington — bathing boxes, flat sand, free foreshore parking most of the year.
  • Shelly Beach, Portsea — short easy walk from the carpark; one of the Peninsula's best free swim beaches.
  • Safety Beach — long, broad, quiet. Free parking and easy access.
Walks

Free walks.

  • Cape Schanck boardwalk — 20 min return, free, accessible. Park fees apply at the Mornington Peninsula National Park carpark.
  • Mornington foreshore — flat, unfenced, 3km of beachfront walking. Free.
  • Coppins Track from Sorrento — free, accessible clifftop walking as far as you want to go.
  • Arthurs Seat summit walks — free once you're at the top (parking is free for short stays).
  • Baldrys Short Circuit, Greens Bush — free Parks Victoria access point.
Lookouts

Free lookouts.

  • Arthurs Seat summit — free, panoramic views to Melbourne on clear days.
  • Cape Schanck Lighthouse platform — free access via the boardwalk.
  • Diamond Bay lookout — free clifftop platform at the end of Coppins Track.
  • Point Nepean clifftop lookouts — free once inside the park (small entry fee may apply).
  • Mount Martha clifftop path — free roadside parking, dramatic southern views.
Markets

Free markets.

  • Mornington Farmers Market — 2nd Saturday, free entry.
  • Red Hill Community Market — 1st Saturday Sept–May, free entry.
  • Balnarring Racecourse Community Market — 3rd Saturday, free entry.
  • Markets cost nothing to browse — bring cash if you plan to buy.
Foreshore

Free foreshore.

  • The foreshore between Dromana and Rosebud — 15km of continuous bay-side walking.
  • Bay Trail cycling path — shared path along Port Phillip Bay; free to use.
  • Sorrento foreshore and pier — free evening walks; one of the Peninsula's best sunset spots.
  • Portsea pier and foreshore — quieter than Sorrento, same coastal feel, free access.

Best Golf Courses on the Mornington Peninsula — The Tier Guide

— By [object Object] 5 min read

Not every respected golf course on the Peninsula is equally useful to a reader. Some are destination rounds you can book. Some are excellent but private. Some are better as part of a stay-and-play weekend than as a standalone mission.

This guide uses tiers rather than a one-to-fifteen ladder because access matters as much as quality. A course you cannot play sits in a different category to one you can build a weekend around.

Tier 1: The destination-makers

St Andrews Beach Golf Course

The lead story. Serious architecture by Tom Doak, public access, genuine global reputation (regular appearances in world top-100 lists), and one of the clearest reasons to make a Peninsula golf trip happen. Near Cape Schanck on the southern end: pair with a hot springs session and you have a complete day. Read the full course review in our St Andrews Beach profile.

The National Golf Club

Arguably the strongest overall golf property in the region, though private access changes how it sits on a shortlist. Three courses on one property (Old, Moonah, Gunnamatta) designed by Greg Norman, Peter Thomson, and Robert Trent Jones Jr. Important and impressive, but not a bookable recommendation unless you know a member.

Tier 2: The serious public-access weekender set

Moonah Links

A proper anchor for a golf weekend, especially when the resort layer and stay options are part of the plan. Two championship courses (Open and Legends), on-site accommodation, and the kind of integrated infrastructure a small group needs.

The Dunes

One of the Peninsula’s most respected public-access experiences and still central to any serious shortlist. Windswept coastal links feel, and the kind of course that rewards playing twice in a weekend.

Rosebud Country Club

Less architectural mythology than the top pair, but highly relevant because it is practical, playable, and widely used. Three 9-hole courses mix-and-match into 18. Good value for a casual round.

Tier 3: Strong rounds, narrower fit

Mornington Golf Club

Short, scenic, close to town. Good for a relaxed round between other Peninsula activities. Low bar to entry.

RACV Cape Schanck

Part of the Cape Schanck Resort. Scenic coastal holes and full resort integration for members and guests.

Portsea Golf Club

Classic Peninsula member’s club on the tip. Beautiful setting. Access depends on membership or reciprocal rights.

Flinders Golf Club

Compact but scenic. Sits above the southern coast in Flinders. More a local favourite than a destination round.

Sorrento Golf Club

Parkland course in Sorrento. Pleasant, manicured, social. A friendly member’s club rather than a bucket-list round.

This is the band where context matters. Some are scenic, some are useful if you are already staying nearby, some are stronger socially than architecturally, and some need access or pricing clarity before you can make a clean recommendation.

Tier 4: Community and local-play value

This is where the broader landscape matters, but not every course deserves destination billing. These courses can still be useful for locals, casual players, or practical trip planning. They just are not the reason to cross the city.

How to use this guide

If you are a serious golfer with one round to play, start with St Andrews Beach. If you are building a golf weekend, the shortlist is St Andrews Beach, Moonah Links, and The Dunes. If you are chasing club prestige, check access before you build the weekend around it.

If the point is a Peninsula weekend that still works for the rest of your group, the more useful question is which round gives the best shape for the whole trip, not which course ranks highest.

Before you book

  • Green fees are highly variable and should be checked directly before booking.
  • Private and semi-private access rules shift, so old golf-forum wisdom is worth re-checking before you plan around it.
  • Shoulder season is often the smartest time to play the Peninsula if you care as much about the weekend as the round: autumn and spring deliver the best combination of weather, availability, and pricing.
  • Book at least 3–4 weeks ahead for weekends at St Andrews Beach and The Dunes.

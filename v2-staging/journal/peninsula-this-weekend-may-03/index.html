Peninsula This Weekend — 9 to 10 May

— By [object Object] 5 min read

Peninsula This Weekend is our weekly dispatch: the plan we’d actually make for the weekend ahead, the booking worth locking in, and the bit of weekend thinking we’d happily do for you.

The second weekend of May has a clear organising principle.

Mother’s Day is the calendar this Sunday, and the most rewarding version of the weekend lets it be that. Book the lunch, or book the active morning, and let everything else taper around it.

The main event - Mother’s Day lunch, properly framed

If you are booking one thing this weekend, book Sunday first.

Three versions look strongest on paper.

  • Flinders Hotel is the family-shaped option: live jazz, face painting, and enough built-in entertainment to stop lunch from sagging.
  • Pier 10 is the vineyard-table option: the cleaner choice if the brief is simply a long lunch in Shoreham and no unnecessary theatre.
  • Jetty Road Brewery is the easier north-side option if you want Mother’s Day to feel relaxed rather than formally orchestrated.

Pick the lunch, get there unhurried, and let the rest of Sunday follow from that.

The active version - Mother’s Day Classic at Moonah Links

If your family does better with something to do before the table, the Mother’s Day Classic at Moonah Links is the cleanest active move on the Peninsula this Sunday.

It gives the day a proper shape - walk or run first, lunch second - and the distances are broad enough that different ages and energy levels can make it work. If you are heading south anyway, this is the best version of a Mother’s Day morning with a point to it.

One practical note: the Mornington Racecourse Market is cancelled this Sunday due to a race day. Worth knowing before you build the loop.

The slower Saturday

Saturday does not need to compete with Sunday.

If you want one actual Saturday booking, Kodomo no Hi at Peninsula Hot Springs is the sharpest family event on the board: specific, weather-proof, and structured enough to carry the day. Otherwise Saturday rewards a lighter hand - one walk, one late lunch, an early night.

A local note before you plan

May weekends here are easier than summer, but the Peninsula still rewards a settled base. Pick north or south. Stay there long enough for the day to feel deliberate, and let Sunday’s lunch be the thing the rest of the weekend leans into.

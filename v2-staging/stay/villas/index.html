Villa stays on the Mornington Peninsula.

Less about scale than privacy, setting, and how well they carry a long weekend. The stay should hold the trip together without forcing hotel behaviour.

— By The Peninsula Insider Editors Jump to the list 4 villas
Editor's Note

Privacy, setting, duration.

The Peninsula's best villa stays are less about scale than privacy, setting, and how well they carry a long weekend. Vineyard villas in Merricks and Red Hill work differently from coastal villas in Sorrento, but the principle is the same: the stay should hold the trip together without forcing hotel behaviour onto a trip that wants more room to breathe.

Stay Glamping — 2026

Glamping on the Mornington Peninsula.

Not a huge Peninsula category yet — which makes the current answer simple. Less a crowded field than a strategic one, built around soaking and switching off.

— By The Peninsula Insider Editors Jump to the list 1 stay
Editor's Note

Strategic, not crowded.

Glamping is not a huge Peninsula category yet, which makes the current answer simple: if you want a proper glamping stay on the Mornington Peninsula, Peninsula Hot Springs Glamping is the defining option. It works because it pairs the accommodation format with an experience the region already owns, rather than trying to sell canvas alone as the whole idea.

This is less a crowded category than a strategic one. The reason to stay this way is not just novelty. It is to shape a thermal, slower, more sealed-off weekend around Fingal and the back-beach side of the Peninsula.

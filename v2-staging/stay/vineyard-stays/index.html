Stay Vineyard stays — 2026

Vineyard stays on the Mornington Peninsula.

A 20-kilometre belt of plateau running through Red Hill, Main Ridge, and Merricks North. Staying here changes what the weekend is.

— By The Peninsula Insider Editors Jump to the list 8 stays
Editor's Note

Cellar doors as a walk, not a drive.

The Peninsula's wine country is a 20-kilometre belt of plateau running through Red Hill, Main Ridge, and Merricks North. Staying in this zone changes what the weekend is — cellar doors become a morning walk rather than a drive, vineyard lunches are the main event rather than a detour, and the ridge light at dusk is what you came for.

Jackalope is the design-hotel benchmark: a black-steel structure set inside a working vineyard with one of Victoria's most ambitious dining rooms. Polperro and Point Leo Estate Villas offer the more private version — self-contained on a working estate, close to serious winemaking. Lindenderry and Hillview Cottages give the boutique-hotel and cottage register that suits those who want character over spectacle.

If you're spending two nights on the Peninsula and wine is the main reason, these are the properties that make the trip hold together.

Frequently Asked

Frequently asked.

Which winery has accommodation on the Peninsula?
Jackalope Hotel (Merricks North vineyard), Polperro Villas (Red Hill estate), Point Leo Estate Villas (sculpture park and winery), Lindenderry at Red Hill (boutique vineyard hotel), and Crittenden Estate (self-contained villas on the estate).

Which part of the Peninsula is wine country?
Red Hill, Main Ridge, and Merricks North on the central plateau. The elevation and cool-climate conditions here are what produces Mornington Pinot Noir and Chardonnay with the character that's made the region internationally credentialled. Staying in this zone puts you within minutes of 20+ cellar doors.

How long do I need?
Two nights. Day one: arrive, afternoon cellar door session, dinner on the property. Day two: morning walk, two or three cellar doors, a long vineyard lunch (book ahead). Drive home Sunday evening. One night is doable but leaves most of Saturday dedicated to travel.

Stay Hotels · Vineyard stays · Coastal weekends

Choose the base, and the whole weekend changes.

The Peninsula is a region of different moods packed close together. A Red Hill country-house stay produces a different trip from a Sorrento main-street hotel. This hub sorts the beds by the kind of weekend you actually want.

— By The Peninsula Insider Editors Jump to picks 17 stays
How to choose

Start with the geography, then pick the room.

Red Hill suits couples who want vineyard calm and long lunches nearby. Sorrento works when you want the social theatre of the tip and late light on the coast. Flinders is the quieter southern reset, better for one-night decompressions and ocean weather.

Vintage on the ridge, pinot lunches in low sun, and cellar doors with a fire going in the corner.

—

Sandpit · Choose your base

Work out where to wake up, and the rest gets easier.

This is the first stay-planning test. Instead of asking people to browse beds blindly, start with the pocket of the Peninsula that makes the whole weekend feel coherent.

What people need: A fast answer to whether the weekend is a Red Hill lunch trip, a Sorrento village trip, a Flinders reset, or a simple north-end base.
What the map does: It turns abstract suburb names into a visual choice. You can immediately see whether the trip clusters around the ridge, the tip, or the northern bay side.
What follows: Once the base is obvious, the room choice becomes much less noisy.

Red Hill / Merricks

Best for: Long lunches, fireplaces, two-night couples weekends.

Unlocks: Jackalope, Laura, Pt. Leo, Montalto, Main Ridge bakery runs.

Pace: Best when the meal is the hero stop and the rest of the day stays slow.

7 coastal stays Sorrento / Blairgowrie

Sorrento / Blairgowrie

Best for: Village weekends, beach access, easy people-watching, one-night resets.

Unlocks: Hotel Sorrento, back beach walks, ferry edge, late bay light, tip dinners.

Pace: Best when you want to park the car and let the southern end carry the mood.

Southern reset Flinders / Cape Schanck

Flinders / Cape Schanck

Best for: Ocean weather, slower village energy, smarter one-night decompressions.

Unlocks: Flinders Hotel, pier walks, Cape Schanck, southern wineries without the crowd.

Pace: Best when you want the Peninsula to feel quieter and slightly wilder.

3 bay-side bases Mornington / Mount Martha

Mornington / Mount Martha

Best for: Shorter drives from Melbourne, practical family stays, easy first-timer access.

Unlocks: Cliff walks, cafés, practical entry point, north-end flexibility.

Pace: Best when the trip needs to feel easy from the first hour, not heroic.

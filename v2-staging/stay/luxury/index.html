Stay Luxury — 2026

Luxury accommodation on the Mornington Peninsula.

The Peninsula does luxury best when it stops trying to impersonate an international resort strip. The strongest stays are rooted in the landscape — vineyard, ridge, ocean edge, village main street.

— By The Peninsula Insider Editors Jump to the list 8 stays
Editor's Note

Rooted in the landscape.

The Peninsula does luxury best when it stops trying to impersonate an international resort strip. The strongest stays here are either rooted in the landscape — vineyard, ridge, ocean edge, village main street — or they are not especially interesting. A good Peninsula luxury stay should shape the whole weekend, not just provide a handsome room key.

That usually means one of two modes. The first is hinterland luxury, where the trip leans into wine, long lunches, fireplaces, and slower mornings, with Jackalope, Lindenderry, Point Leo Estate Villas, and Polperro Villas doing the heavy lifting. The second is coastal luxury, where the base matters because it decides the rhythm of the trip, and The Continental and Hotel Sorrento become the right answer.

The question is not simply what is most expensive. It is what gives the Peninsula weekend the right shape.

How to choose well

How to choose well.

Red Hill or Sorrento?
Red Hill is the right base when the weekend is about wine, long lunches, and a slower food-led Peninsula. Sorrento is the right base when the weekend is about village atmosphere, walking, bay and ocean access, and a stronger sense of arrival.

Hotel or villa?
Choose a hotel if you want service, restaurant access, and easier flow. Choose a villa if privacy, vineyard setting, and a more self-contained long-weekend rhythm matter more than formal hotel infrastructure.

The publication's view.
Jackalope is still the Peninsula's most singular luxury hotel expression. Lindenderry is the calmer, more classic counterpoint. Point Leo Estate Villas and Polperro Villas make the strongest case for luxury through place rather than spectacle.

The Peninsula Golf Weekend: Two Nights, One Serious Round, Room for the Rest

One public-access course with genuine ranking credibility, a long lunch on the ridge, a hot springs session, and a stay within ten minutes of the first tee. For groups where not everyone plays.

— For Friends 120 min driving
Editor's note

Why this plan, in this order.

The Peninsula golf weekend that actually makes sense for a group where not everyone plays. One serious round at St Andrews Beach — Tom Doak architecture, public access, world top-100 credibility — anchors Saturday. The rest of the weekend shapes around it: ridge stays, vineyard lunches, coastal walks for the non-golfers, and a Sunday that sends you home slowly through Red Hill. Not a 36-hole grind. A shaped weekend that uses golf as the hero round rather than the only event. Book St Andrews Beach 4+ weeks out; plan the lunch at Montalto the same day.

The sequence

Stop by stop.

Day 1

  1. Afternoon

    Jackalope Hotel

    Arrive Friday afternoon, check in, settle. The ridge stay positions both golf days and the non-golf days properly — Cape Schanck is 15 minutes south, Red Hill restaurants are at your door.

  2. Evening

    Doot Doot Doot

    Friday dinner at the hotel restaurant. Sets the weekend up as an event rather than a golf grind.

Day 2

  1. Morning

    St Andrews Beach Golf Course

    Saturday morning tee time at St Andrews Beach. Book 4+ weeks ahead. Links country, firm fairways, Tom Doak greens. Allow 4.5 hours for 18 with walk-ups. Non-golfers drive to Cape Schanck for the boardwalk and Bushrangers Bay.

  2. Midday

    Montalto Vineyard & Olive Grove

    Late lunch at Montalto piazza — relaxed, no dress code required, close enough to the course to work as a recovery meal. The whole group meets here whether they played or not.

  3. Afternoon

    Bushrangers Bay Walk

    Late afternoon walk for the non-golfers (or an easy option for everyone). Southern Ocean cliffs and the light at 5pm. Optional — skip and rest if the morning round was tiring.

Day 3

  1. Morning

    Commonfolk Coffee

    Sunday brunch in Mornington. Strong coffee, good eggs, no queue before 9am. Shake out the Saturday legs.

  2. Midday

    Ten Minutes by Tractor

    One cellar door before the drive home. Serious pinot noir and chardonnay. The ridge sending you off.

Escape Itineraries — weekends — slow travel

Good Peninsula weekends are built in the sequence.

The region is small enough to overdo and varied enough to get wrong. These escape plans are our answer: fewer bookings, better pacing, and just enough structure that the whole thing feels inevitable rather than overplanned.

— By The Editors 6 plans
Three rules

What a good Peninsula weekend actually looks like.

Pick one geography per day. Red Hill, Sorrento, Flinders, and Cape Schanck all ask for different tempos. Stringing them together without thought is how good weekends turn into traffic.

Let lunch or dinner be the event, not both. The Peninsula's strongest move is still daylight dining. If lunch is the headline, keep dinner softer. If dinner matters, keep the afternoon light.

Always leave room for weather — the region gets more interesting when the wind shifts, the sky changes, or dusk suddenly looks too good to waste indoors.

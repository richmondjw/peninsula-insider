The Insider's take

On Tuerong.

Tuerong is where some of the Peninsula's best bottles come from roads most visitors never take. Yabby Lake is the anchor — Halliday Top 100, relaxed cellar-door program — and the same back roads hold Dexter Wines and a cluster of low-volume producers whose fruit ends up in Melbourne's better wine lists. Quiet, specific, worth the detour.

Tuerong is a rural locality best known to wine enthusiasts as the address of Yabby Lake Vineyard and Dromana Estate.
  • Best for: serious wine visitors; walkers at Devilbend Reservoir; travellers who want hinterland quiet without driving all the way to Red Hill.
  • Half a day; usually paired with Moorooduc or Mornington.

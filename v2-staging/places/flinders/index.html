The Insider's take

On Flinders.

Flinders sits on the wild southern edge of the Peninsula and carries itself with a kind of salt-air confidence. The village is compact but smart - a bakery, a handful of good shops, an old pub, and a coastline that feels a world away from the bay-facing polish further north. Base yourself here if you want your mornings to begin with wind off Bass Strait, your afternoons with a drive back inland for lunch, and your evenings under a harder, cleaner sky.

Flinders is the Peninsula's most complete ocean-coast village — a bakery, a pub, a world-class golf course, and a marine sanctuary immediately offshore.
  • Best for: serious golfers; divers after the Flinders Blowhole; anyone who wants a southern-edge village base without driving to the tip.
  • Signature experience: walk the coastal reserve past the blowhole at high tide, then a long lunch in the village.
  • Full day; Flinders rewards staying rather than passing through.

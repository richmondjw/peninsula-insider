Main Street Mornington Festival 2026

Annual free street festival on Mornington's Main Street: 7 stages, live music, local wine and craft beer, food stalls, artisan crafts, kids' activities, roving performers, 11am to 5pm on the third Sunday of October. 2026 contingent on sponsorship after 2025 was cancelled.

— 18 October 2026
Editor's verdict

On Main Street Mornington Festival 2026.

If it runs, it's the year's biggest Main Street day. 2025 was cancelled. Check the organiser before you plan around it.

Main Street Mornington Festival is the annual third-Sunday-of-October street takeover: 7 stages, live music, local wine and craft beer, food stalls and roving performers from 11am to 5pm. When it lands, it's the biggest single day Main Street sees all year.

The complication: 2025 was cancelled when the funding gap couldn't be closed, and the official site (as of late 2025) listed 2026 as 'seeking sponsors'. VicFomo and OnlyMelbourne project 18 October 2026 based on the annual pattern, but treat it as unconfirmed until the organiser announces.

If you're planning travel around it, contact the Northern Mornington Peninsula Tourism Association before you book. We'll update this entry as the status firms up.

Kids Grade A

Built for families when it runs: flat Main Street, kids' activities, roving performers, multiple food options. Pram-friendly throughout.

Bring a pram, bring a toddler, bring a teen - everyone will be happy.

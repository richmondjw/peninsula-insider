Editor's verdict

On Mornington King's Birthday Race Day.

Mornington King's Birthday race day is local racing at its most social.

The big winter day on the Mornington Racecourse calendar, run every King's Birthday Monday. Feature races, on-course entertainment, the betting ring, and the kind of long-weekend crowd that brings out the proper winter coats.

Who it's for: groups, work shouts, anyone after a winter day out that isn't a cellar door. Dining packages are the move, the general admission lawn is exposed and cold by 4pm.

The practical move: book a lunch package early (the marquees sell out), wear layers under the smart coat, and pair the weekend with the Mornington Winter Music Festival which runs the same weekend. Stay overnight in Mornington so the post-races crawl through Main Street is walkable. Check the MRC site closer to the date for the full card and ticket tiers.

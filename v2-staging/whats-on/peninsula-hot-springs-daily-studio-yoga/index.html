Editor's verdict

On Peninsula Hot Springs Daily Studio Yoga.

A useful $20 add-on if you're already booked in to bathe, not a reason to drive out on its own.

A 45-minute guided yoga session in the Wellness Studio, open to guests aged 16 and over. It is sold as a $20 add-on to your bathing admission, not a standalone class.

The move: book a 7:30am or evening bathing slot when the pools are quieter, do yoga first, then bathe. Avoid the 10am to 4pm window when day-trippers crowd the studio booking. Wellness Studio is indoors so it runs in any weather.

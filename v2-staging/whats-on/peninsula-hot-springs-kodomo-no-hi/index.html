Editor's verdict

On Peninsula Hot Springs - Kodomo no Hi (Children's Day).

The most culturally specific family event on the Peninsula in May. Book early — PHS family programming fills fast.

Peninsula Hot Springs does cultural programming better than most Peninsula venues because it has the infrastructure to absorb a full-day crowd without collapsing under it. Kodomo no Hi — Japan's Children's Day, celebrated on 5 May each year and marked here on 9 May — brings traditional carp streamers (koinobori), cultural activities alongside the regular bathing access, and the kind of specific occasion that makes a May weekend feel earned rather than improvised. PHS has a long relationship with Japanese bathing culture and this event is a natural fit for the space. Book early: the family-oriented programming days at PHS fill several weeks out, and the May school holiday framing means demand is higher than a regular weekend.

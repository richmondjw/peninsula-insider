Ninch Nabs: Samples & Seconds Makers Market

One-day samples and seconds makers market at The Village Church Mount Eliza: local artists and small businesses moving handmade goods at accessible prices.

— 13 June 2026
Editor's verdict

On Ninch Nabs: Samples & Seconds Makers Market.

Samples and seconds means real bargains from real makers. Half the price, all the craft, none of the gloss.

Ninch Nabs is the local makers' end-of-line sale: artists and small businesses clearing samples, seconds and overruns at prices that actually work. Held inside and around The Village Church on Canadian Bay Road.

It's a one-day occasional drop, not a monthly fixture, so treat it as a get-in-and-out trip. The crowd is locals and regulars, the goods are genuinely handmade, and the best buys go in the first ninety minutes.

Worth the trip if you're after Christmas gifts on the cheap or filling out a homewares shelf. Skip if you want produce or food trucks; this is a craft-and-textiles affair.

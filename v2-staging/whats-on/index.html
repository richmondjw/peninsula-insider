Department · II

Markets, Ranked

The Peninsula runs eight recurring markets. Here they are in the order we'd actually attend them.

17 events
Markets
6 May – 28 April 2027 Weekly

Mornington Wednesday Market (Main Street Market)

Forty years of Wednesdays on Main Street. Not a tourist event, just the way Mornington shops midweek.

Markets
7 May – 29 April 2027 Weekly

Hastings Thursday Street Market

Hastings does its market on a Thursday morning, which is exactly the kind of inconvenient timing that keeps it real.

Markets
30 May 2026 Monthly

Dromana Community Market

Dromana's last-Saturday market is a community Saturday, not a destination. Useful if you're already heading to the beach.

Markets Worth the drive
31 May 2026 One-off

The Enchanted Market at The Briars

A market that doubles as a bushwalk. The setting inside The Briars is the reason to make the trip.

Markets Worth the drive
6 June 2026 One-off

Point Nepean Portsea Market

The setting alone earns the drive. Tea trees, old Quarantine Station buildings, and a strict make-or-grow rule that keeps the stalls honest.

Markets Worth the drive
6 June 2026 Monthly

Red Hill Market - First Saturday, Every Month

If you only ever do one Peninsula market, do this one. Everything else is an understudy.

Department · III

Cellar Door Events

Release weekends, winemaker dinners and tastings — the region's strongest category, rarely treated as events.

3 events
Cellar Doors Worth the drive
6 June – 8 June 2026 Annual

Crittenden Wines, King's Birthday Wine Weekend Events

If you take wine seriously, this is the King's Birthday booking to make. Seated tastings of 25+ pours, the new restaurant, and the option to not drive home.

Cellar Doors Worth the drive
6 June – 8 June 2026 Annual

Stonier Pies & Pinot (King's Birthday Weekend)

A glass of the new-release 2025 Pinot, a duck pie, the fire going. There is no better King's Birthday move on the Peninsula.

Cellar Doors Worth the drive
6 June – 8 June 2026 Annual

Mornington Peninsula Winter Wine Weekend

The best weekend of the Peninsula year for committed wine people. Cooler weather, smaller crowds, and the winemakers are often the ones pouring.

Department · IV

Live Music

Sunday sessions, ridge vineyard sets, and the recurring gigs no other Peninsula site tracks.

5 events
Live Music
1 May – 30 September 2026 Monthly

Peninsula Hot Springs, Sound Healing Sessions

Peninsula Hot Springs sound healing isn't a gimmick, it's a genuine 90-minute reset, and the venue is the right one for it.

Live Music Worth the drive
3 May – 28 June 2026 Weekly

Peninsula Hot Springs Sunday Sessions

Worth checking each week, PHS now names the artist in advance, which makes the booking decision much easier. If the lineup appeals, it's one of the better soft-Sunday plays on the Peninsula.

Live Music Worth the drive
5 June – 8 June 2026 One-off

Mornington Winter Music Festival 2026

Mornington Winter Music Festival is the off-season weekend Main Street built for itself, and now (12 years in) one of the few Peninsula festivals that consistently delivers.

Live Music Worth the drive
2 January – 11 January 2027 One-off

Peninsula Summer Music Festival 2027

PSMF is the festival that puts world-class chamber music in a winery and somehow makes both better. Save the date, book when tickets release.

Live Music Worth the drive
2 January – 11 January 2027 Annual

Peninsula Summer Music Festival 2027, Save the Date

Save-the-date for the festival that puts world-class chamber music inside a winery. Tickets release expected October 2026.

Department · V

Wellness & Retreats

Thermal circuits, sound baths, and the ongoing programming at Alba and the Hot Springs.

4 events
Wellness
1 May – 30 April 2027 One-off

Peninsula Hot Springs Daily Studio Yoga

A useful $20 add-on if you're already booked in to bathe, not a reason to drive out on its own.

Wellness
1 May – 30 April 2027 One-off

Peninsula Hot Springs Yoga (Complimentary)

Free yoga before a hot springs bathe is the move locals know about and tourists don't book.

Wellness Worth the drive
1 May – 30 April 2027 One-off

Restore & Pamper Retreat at Polperro Farmhouse

A retreat that takes wellness seriously without the wellness-industrial-complex theatrics.

Wellness
1 May – 30 April 2027 Monthly

Sound Circle: Full Moon Sound Journey at Peninsula Hot Springs

Not woo, just 90 quiet minutes that genuinely reset the week.

Department · VI

Kids & Family

School-holiday programmes graded A/B/C. We've been to most of them. We'll tell you which ones.

5 events
Kids & Family
30 June – 2 July 2026 Annual

Winter Camp 2026, The Ranch

The school-holiday move when the kids need to be properly tired and you need three days off.

Kids & Family
1 July – 10 July 2026 Ongoing

Mornington Peninsula Regional Gallery, School Holiday Workshops

The school-holiday move when the kids need something built-in, indoors, and actually good rather than just available.

Kids & Family
1 July – 10 July 2026 Ongoing

Youth Services School Holiday Program

The under-the-radar one that parents of older kids underuse: free, supervised, and actually well-run by people who do this for a living.

Kids & Family
5 July 2026 One-off

Mornington Tourist Railway, School Holiday Special Runs

Simple, unironic, properly run: a heritage steam ride that doesn't pretend to be anything more than what it is, which is exactly the appeal.

Kids & Family
6 December – 20 December 2026 Annual

Mornington Tourist Railway, Santa Specials

The Christmas outing that pays off harder than it has any right to: a real steam train, a real Santa, and an hour of small-child wonder.

Peninsula Hot Springs – Bathe-in Cinema (Thursdays)

Department · VIII

Exhibitions & Galleries

The region's serious public art, a strong rainy-day category nobody leans into.

7 events
Exhibitions
28 February – 31 May 2026 Annual

Michael Vale Exhibition at MPRG

Worth an hour if you're already in Mornington, a special trip only if Vale is on your list.

Exhibitions Worth the drive
28 February – 31 May 2026 One-off

New Wave 26 at MPRG

New Wave 26 is MPRG doing what MPRG does best: a tightly curated contemporary survey that punches well above the building it's in.

Exhibitions Worth the drive
1 April – 30 June 2026 Seasonal

Mornington Peninsula Regional Gallery - Autumn Exhibition

The Peninsula's best rainy-day cultural hour. Free, well curated, five minutes from Main Street. No excuse to skip it.

Exhibitions
25 April – 31 May 2026 Seasonal

TRACE Duo Exhibition

Worth thirty minutes if you're already in the area, paired with MPRG up the road.

Exhibitions
25 April – 31 May 2026 One-off

TRACE Duo Exhibition at Lander-Se

An independent two-hander worth pairing with MPRG, not a destination on its own.

Exhibitions
29 April – 7 June 2026 One-off

Tall Poppy, the first Melbourne Design Week exhibition on the Peninsula

Tall Poppy is doing two things at once. It is a fifteen-designer survey of where Australian making sits in 2026, and, the gallery believes, the first time NGV's Melbourne Design Week has programmed an exhibition on the Mornington Peninsula. We would treat the claim as close to true rather than confirmed, and the show as worth a Saturday irrespective.

Sorrento Solstice Festival 2026

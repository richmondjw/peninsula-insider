Racing & Sport

The Peninsula's racing days and endurance weekends — the single category Melbourne visitors under-plan.

4 events
Racing & Sport
22 May 2026 One-off

Coastrek Mornington Peninsula 2026

Team hiking challenge supporting Beyond Blue mental health. Choose 20km (Sorrento to Point Nepean), 30km (Point Nepean loop), or 50km (Cape Schanck to Point Nepean). Teams of 2 or more. Coastal bush trails and clifftop tracks. 6am start.

Racing & Sport
30 May 2026 One-off

Harry Baker 2K Sailing Regatta

Annual sailing regatta hosted by Sorrento Sailing Couta Boat Club. Signature short coastal race from Sorrento foreshore. Part of the club's active summer–autumn racing calendar.

Racing & Sport
8 June 2026 One-off

Mornington King's Birthday Race Day

Annual King's Birthday public holiday thoroughbred race day at Mornington Racecourse. Feature races, on-course entertainment, betting facilities, dining packages. Picturesque coastal setting racecourse on the Peninsula.

Racing & Sport
25 October 2026 One-off

The Bloody Long Walk – Mornington Peninsula 2026

35km scenic charity walk from Point Nepean National Park Quarantine Station to Martha Cove Marina. Raises funds for Mito (mitochondrial disease research). Walk jog or run. Spectacular coastal scenery. Limited spots.

Markets, Ranked

The Peninsula runs eight recurring markets. Here they are in the order we'd actually attend them.

23 events
Markets
6 May – 28 April 2027 Weekly

Mornington Wednesday Market (Main Street Market)

Victoria's longest-running street market – operating over 40 years every Wednesday on Main Street Mornington. 120+ stalls of handmade crafts, home-baked treats, home-grown plants and produce. Rain, hail or shine (high-wind policy applies). Vibrant weekly community gathering.

Markets Worth the drive
7 May – 29 April 2027 Weekly

Hastings Thursday Street Market

Weekly outdoor street market on High Street Hastings every Thursday. 40+ stalls featuring homemade, homegrown and craft goods. Fresh produce, handmade goods, plants. Local food options available on the street.

Markets
9 May 2026 Monthly

Crib Point Community Market

Community market at Crib Point every second Saturday of the month. Spirit of community support and inclusion; promotes locally made handcrafted products and local produce.

Markets
9 May 2026 Monthly

Heart of the Community Market (Rosebud)

Community market at Rosebud on the second Saturday of the month. Approximately 160 stalls with craft, handmade and new products, fresh produce and plants. No used goods policy. Located near Rosebud foreshore.

Tall Poppy is doing two things at once. We would treat the claim as close to true rather than confirmed, and the show as worth a Saturday irrespective.

Youth Services School Holiday Program

Free school holiday drop-in program for 10 to 17 year olds across four Youth Hubs: free wifi, games, art, food, and a weekly excursion. Operoo registration required, no booking for centre days.

— 1 July – 10 July 2026
Editor's verdict

On Youth Services School Holiday Program.

The under-the-radar one that parents of older kids underuse: free, supervised, and actually well-run by people who do this for a living.

The Shire runs free school holiday drop-in days at four Youth Hubs (Hastings, Mornington, Rosebud, Somerville). Qualified youth workers, free wifi, video games, pool tables, art supplies, board games, food. Weekly excursions go on the program but places are limited and need an expression of interest.

For day-to-day attendance there is no booking required, but every young person needs Operoo registration done once with a parent or guardian (do this before the holidays, not at the door).

For teens who are over the kid-friendly stuff but not quite ready to be left alone for a week, this is the right shape of thing.

Mother's Day Classic 2026 — Peppers Moonah Links

The Peninsula's clearest active Mother's Day morning: a community walk-run at Moonah Links with 2.5km, 7km and 15km options before the lunch bookings take over.

— 10 May 2026
Editor's verdict

On Mother's Day Classic 2026 — Peppers Moonah Links.

The cleanest active Mother's Day morning on the Peninsula. Do this if your family likes a day with an actual shape before lunch.

Mother's Day often collapses into a single long meal. The Moonah Links Classic gives the day a better rhythm: move first, eat second, and let the rest of the afternoon soften naturally. The charity angle matters, but the real planning value is structural — it stops the day from becoming all booking and no momentum. If you are already heading south, this is the strongest way to begin Sunday.

Kids Grade B

A good family morning if the kids can handle a walk start. Better for school-age children than toddlers.

One age range event - good if you've got 5-to-10s, risky otherwise.

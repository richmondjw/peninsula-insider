Peninsula Hot Springs - Allara Briggs Pattison

A named live performance at Peninsula Hot Springs amphitheatre pool — contemporary folk from Yorta Yorta/Dja Dja Wurrung artist Allara Briggs Pattison, included with bathing.

Editor's verdict

On Peninsula Hot Springs - Allara Briggs Pattison.

The strongest single Peninsula event for the first weekend of May. Book the afternoon bathing session.

This is not a generic Sunday Sessions listing — Allara Briggs Pattison is a named artist with a real catalogue, and the amphitheatre pool at Peninsula Hot Springs is one of the better live music settings in Victoria when the conditions are right. You are in thermal water, the stone stage is directly in front of you, and the late-afternoon light over the tea-tree does the rest. Included with standard bathing, which makes the value proposition straightforward. Book the afternoon session and arrive early enough to secure the stone steps near the stage. If you are building the first weekend of May around Red Hill Market in the morning, this is the clean evening anchor that makes the day feel complete rather than arbitrarily extended.

Kids Grade B

Good for families but the amphitheatre crowd is primarily adults. Confident swimmers 8+ fine.

One age range event - good if you've got 5-to-10s, risky otherwise.

# Baillieu Vineyard, the Merricks vine walk, and National Works on Paper closing soon

Baillieu Vineyard's Chardonnay opens up with time, the Merricks vine walk is at its best in new-growth spring, and the National Works on Paper show at MPRG closes 22 November.

Insider Picks  Spring

Mornington Peninsula Regional Gallery, Mornington Peninsula   Photo · Robert Blackburn, courtesy of Visit Victoria

**WINE - Baillieu Vineyard, Merricks**

Baillieu’s cellar door is the one the Peninsula wine crowd mentions in lowered voices. Not a secret - it has been there for years - but under-visited in a way that works entirely in your favour this weekend. Spring is the right moment to come: the vines are in full new growth, the room is not at summer capacity, and the wines need a bit of time and conversation to show what they are. The Chardonnay in particular does not announce itself; it opens gradually, and the team here will give you the space to let that happen. Book a tasting rather than dropping in, and stay long enough for a proper glass after the flight. The vine walk through the estate is worth doing before you sit down - twenty minutes at most, and the rows look as good as they will all year. Bookings essential on Saturday; check the website for current spring hours.

Pair the afternoon with a detour to Merricks General Wine Store for something to take home.

**WALK - Merricks Creek Trail, Merricks to Coolart**

The Merricks Creek corridor is at its best right now. The wetland grasses along the lower Coolart section have flushed green after the October rains, and the paperbark stands that frame the Coolart Wetlands are dense and fragrant in a way that only lasts a few weeks in late spring. Walk from the Merricks foreshore reserve south toward Coolart Wetlands and Wildlife Reserve - allow around ninety minutes return for the full wetland loop. Keep an eye on the shallow reed margins in the first hour of morning light; the resident swamp harriers hunt low this time of year and are almost impossible to miss. No formal booking needed, but Coolart Homestead has specific opening days, so check their website if you want to combine the walk with a visit inside. Bring water and decent footwear; the Coolart loop goes soft in places after rain.

Pair the walk with lunch at Merricks General Wine Store, ten minutes back up the hill.

**DISCOVERY - National Works on Paper 2026, MPRG, Mornington - closes 22 November**

The National Works on Paper show at Mornington Peninsula Regional Gallery has been running since September and closes on Sunday 22 November. That gives you two more weekends, including this one. NWOP is an acquisitive prize exhibition - the gallery purchases works from finalists - so the selection reflects what paper-based practice in Australia looks like right now, not a curated retrospective. The range runs from finely detailed graphite works to large-format screen prints to pieces where the paper itself has been worked, torn, or layered until it barely resembles a flat surface. NWOP is worth seeing specifically because it rewards slow looking; the works are not competing for Instagram attention, and the gallery at Dunns Road is quiet enough on a weekday morning that you can take your time. Open Thursday to Sunday, 11am–4pm. No booking needed, free entry.

Pair the gallery visit with a walk along the Mornington foreshore before or after.

## FAQ

When is Baillieu Vineyard cellar door open?

Baillieu Vineyard is on Tuerong Road, Merricks. Check their website for current spring hours and to book a tasting - bookings are recommended on weekends.

When does the National Works on Paper exhibition close at Mornington Peninsula Regional Gallery?

The National Works on Paper 2026 show closes Sunday 22 November 2026. Mornington Peninsula Regional Gallery is at Civic Reserve, Dunns Road, Mornington. Open Thursday to Sunday, 11am–4pm.

Can I walk the vines at Baillieu Vineyard?

Yes. Baillieu Vineyard has a vine walk through the Merricks estate. Access is linked to the cellar door visit - book ahead and ask the team to point you toward the walk before or after your tasting.

# Avani Syrah, a Seawinds Garden walk, and pub carols in Main Ridge

Taste Syrah at Avani in Red Hill South, walk to the bay lookouts at Seawinds Garden, or plan a December dinner and singalong in Main Ridge.

Keep this for later

- By **The Peninsula Insider**

**Avani Wines: 98 Stanleys Road, Red Hill South**

Go for a seated tasting if you want to compare Avani’s wines rather than pick a bottle at the counter. The cellar door lists a Syrah flight spanning three vintages from its museum releases. It opens Thursday to Monday, 10 am to 5 pm. Book ahead if the tasting is the anchor of your afternoon; the winery also welcomes walk-ins.

**Seawinds Garden: enter via Purves Road, Arthurs Seat**

Make for the Bay and Northern lookouts for views over Port Phillip, or take the garden paths past indigenous and exotic plantings and William Ricketts sculptures. If you want a timed walk, Parks Victoria lists the TC McKeller Walk as a 1 km, 45-minute circuit from the Seawinds information shelter. That’s a named trail, not an estimate for a wander around the garden. Pet dogs are not permitted. Assistance dogs are welcome subject to [Parks Victoria’s entry requirements](<https://www.parks.vic.gov.au/get-into-nature/all-abilities-access/assistance-dogs>).

**Pub Carols at The Pig & Whistle: 365 Purves Road, Main Ridge**

This is a singalong with dinner, not a drop-in carols session. The organiser says the band teaches each table part of the same song, then brings the room together for the finale. Sessions are 6 pm to 10 pm AEDT on Thursdays 3, 10 and 17 December. Tickets include a main and dessert. [Check ticket availability on Humanitix](<https://events.humanitix.com/pub-carols>).

## FAQ

Where is Avani Wines and when is the cellar door open?

Avani Wines is at 98 Stanleys Road, Red Hill South. The cellar door opens Thursday to Monday, 10 am to 5 pm. Bookings are recommended and walk-ins are welcome.

How do I get to Seawinds Garden, and can I bring my dog?

Enter off Purves Road near Arthurs Seat Summit. Pet dogs are not permitted. Parks Victoria's entry requirements apply to assistance dogs.

When is Pub Carols at The Pig & Whistle and what does the ticket include?

Pub Carols runs on 3, 10 and 17 December 2026, from 6 pm to 10 pm AEDT, at 365 Purves Road, Main Ridge. Check availability on Humanitix. The ticket includes a main and dessert.

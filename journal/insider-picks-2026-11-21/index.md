# Foxeys Hangout, the Arthurs Seat ridge in flower, and the VineHop Festival

Biodynamic sparkling at Foxeys Hangout in Red Hill, the wattle-and-teatree ridge walk on Arthurs Seat in full spring colour, and the Peninsula VineHop Festival across six Mount Martha venues on Saturday.

## Foxeys Hangout - biodynamic sparkling flight, Red Hill

The sparkling program at Foxeys is the most honest argument for biodynamic viticulture on the Peninsula: not a philosophy pitch, just a row of glasses that taste like somewhere specific. The cellar door sits on a north-facing Red Hill block with a deck wide enough to feel genuinely unhurried, and in spring the vines are at the green-sharp stage that makes the view worth something. Order the sparkling flight before you commit to a bottle. Chris Strickland has been building toward a house style that is dry, textural, and nothing like the Peninsula’s earlier sparkling era, and the flight makes that progression legible in a way a single pour doesn’t. The all-day deck is at its best before noon on a Saturday, before the ridge road fills up.

**Foxeys Hangout**, 827 Mornington–Flinders Road, Red Hill. Open Thursday–Monday; check the website for current hours. Bookings recommended on weekends.

Pair the visit with a stop at Eldridge Estate five minutes south for the MV6 Pinot, then eat at The Red Hill Baker on the way back.

## Arthurs Seat ridge circuit - spring wildflowers, Arthurs Seat State Park

The ridge walk above Arthurs Seat earns its detour in October and November, when the understorey switches from winter grey to the specific gold-and-white combination of wattle finishing and teatree starting. The circuit from the Eagle gondola top station runs through the state park’s dry sclerophyll section before dropping into a section of damp gully where ground orchids appear beside the path in the third week of November. The timing is not guaranteed, but this is the week to check. Keep left at the junction 400 metres from the gondola station to stay on the high ridgeline rather than dropping early to the car park. The whole circuit takes around 90 minutes at a relaxed pace. Carry water; the top of the seat is exposed and gets warm by midday.

**Arthurs Seat State Park**, Arthurs Seat Road, Arthurs Seat. No booking required. Check Parks Victoria for current track conditions before you leave.

Finish at Foxeys Hangout for the afternoon session, or drop into Merricks Estate on the way back toward the highway.

## Peninsula VineHop Festival - six Mount Martha venues, Saturday 21 November

The VineHop is one of the Peninsula calendar events that works because it is designed around movement rather than sitting at one venue all day. Six wineries link up under a single ticket, the hub sits at The Briars in Mount Martha, and the format lets you self-sequence the route rather than following a set shuttle timetable. That independence is the point. Saturday 21 November, 11am to 7pm. The mid-afternoon window between 1pm and 3pm is when most visitors are at peak venue capacity, so experienced attendees start at the furthest venue from the hub and work back. This is not a secret known to everyone. Tickets are on Humanitix and have sold out in past years; check availability now rather than Saturday morning.

**Peninsula VineHop Festival**, hub at The Briars, Mount Martha. Saturday 21 November 2026, 11am–7pm. Book at events.humanitix.com/peninsula-vinehop-festival-2026/tickets.

Pair the day with the Mt Eliza Farmers Market the following morning, Sunday 22 November, 9am–1pm, while the weekend is still in motion.

## FAQ

When is Foxeys Hangout open and do I need to book?

Foxeys Hangout is at 827 Mornington–Flinders Road, Red Hill. Open Thursday to Monday; hours vary seasonally so check the website before you go. Bookings recommended for weekend visits.

What is the Peninsula VineHop Festival and how do I get tickets?

The Peninsula VineHop Festival runs Saturday 21 November 2026, 11am–7pm, with the festival hub at The Briars, Mount Martha, and six linked venues. Book tickets at events.humanitix.com/peninsula-vinehop-festival-2026/tickets before they sell out.

Where do I park for the Arthurs Seat ridge walk?

Use the Arthurs Seat State Park car park off Arthurs Seat Road, Arthurs Seat. The Eagle gondola top station is a convenient starting point for the ridge circuit. No booking required; check Parks Victoria for current track conditions.

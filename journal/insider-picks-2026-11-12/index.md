# Main Ridge Dairy, the Arthurs Seat wildflower loop, and the NWOP's last weekend

Goat-milk gelato at a working farmgate, spring orchids on the Arthurs Seat Eagle trail network, and the National Works on Paper exhibition closing Sunday at Mornington Peninsula Regional Gallery.

Insider Picks  Spring

Trofeo Estate, Dromana, Mornington Peninsula   Photo · Courtesy of Visit Victoria

**Main Ridge Dairy** earns its farmgate reputation in spring, when the goats are in full milk and the cheese boards reflect it.

The cheese board here is not an afterthought. It arrives with fresh chèvre, an aged round, and usually something seasonal the dairy is trialling. You can watch the herd from the tasting area, which makes the whole proposition unusually legible. Kids grasp it immediately; adults slow down in a way they don’t at conventional cellar doors. The goat-milk gelato is the item that brings people back - lighter than dairy, clean, and made on site. Spring is the right season: kid goats are still visible, the pasture is green, and the dairy is running at full capacity rather than the leaner winter schedule.

Allow 45 minutes minimum. It is a working farm, not a café - timing your visit for mid-morning gives you the fullest selection. Check their current opening days on the website before driving out.

Pair it with a glass of Pinot Gris at Main Ridge Estate, ten minutes up Mornington-Flinders Road.

**The Arthurs Seat trail network** in the state park is worth visiting any time, but mid-November is a specific window for spider orchids and sun orchids on the exposed ridge sections.

The trail system above the gondola terminal links through dry sclerophyll forest to open heathland. The orchid flowering here is concentrated in the heathland patches between the ridge lookout and the lower fire track junction - a section most visitors skip because they turn back at the first viewpoint. Don’t. The ground flora in this corridor includes at least three native orchid species in November. Bring water; the exposed sections are unshaded after 10am. The views to Port Phillip and Western Port are incidental to the botany this time of year, but they’re real. Leash your dog on the orchid sections - Parks Victoria signage marks the sensitive zones.

Arthurs Seat State Park, Arthurs Seat Road. No booking needed. Allow 90 minutes for the full ridge loop. Download the Parks Victoria trail map before you go.

Pair the visit with a coffee at Flinders General Store on your way south, or a farmgate stop at Main Ridge Dairy if you are staying in the hills.

**National Works on Paper 2026** at Mornington Peninsula Regional Gallery closes this Sunday, 22 November. This is the last weekend.

NWOP is a serious national survey - not a local group show dressed up in prize language. The 2026 edition drew over 3,000 entries nationally, and the hung works span everything from monotype to cut-paper installation. The Saturday floor talk (11:30am, 14 November) with the curator walks through the prize selection process and naming criteria, which is worth attending if you want to understand why particular works were chosen over technically stronger ones. The talk runs one hour and is free with gallery entry. After Sunday the show disappears entirely; the works return to their makers or go to private collections.

Mornington Peninsula Regional Gallery, Civic Reserve, Dunns Road, Mornington. Open 11am–4pm through Sunday 22 November. Free entry. Book the Saturday floor talk via the gallery website.

Pair the gallery visit with lunch at one of the Mornington foreshore venues before the summer crowds compress the parking.

## FAQ

Where is Main Ridge Dairy and when is it open?

Main Ridge Dairy is at 14 Stanleys Road, Main Ridge. Hours vary seasonally - check their website or social channels before visiting, as farmgate openings depend on the production schedule.

When does the National Works on Paper 2026 exhibition close?

National Works on Paper 2026 (NWOP) closes Sunday 22 November 2026 at Mornington Peninsula Regional Gallery, Civic Reserve, Dunns Road, Mornington. Open 11am–4pm. Entry is free.

Where do I start the Arthurs Seat trail network?

The main trailhead is at Arthurs Seat State Park, off Arthurs Seat Road, Arthurs Seat. Parks Victoria's trail map is available at the Eagle gondola terminal and on the Parks Victoria website.

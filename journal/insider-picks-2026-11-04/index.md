# Red Hill Brewery's tasting paddle, the Boneo Plains wildflower flush, and the Mt Eliza Farmers Market

**Red Hill Brewery - tasting paddle beside the hop yard**

The estate hop garden at Red Hill Brewery is in full growth right now, bines climbing their strings above the beer garden. That context matters: this is Victoria’s oldest craft brewery, and the Belgian-style ales it produces taste different when you can see where the hops come from. The tasting paddle is the right starting point. It runs across the core range and usually includes at least one seasonal, letting you compare the wit and the saison before you commit to a full pint. The kitchen runs a changing lunch menu on weekends - expect substantial plates, not bar snacks. This is a full afternoon, not a quick stop.

Located at 88 Shoreham Road, Red Hill South. Open for lunch on weekends; check the website for weekday hours. Allow two to three hours minimum. Bookings recommended for groups.

Pair the afternoon with a walk through the nearby Arthurs Seat State Park before you arrive.

**Boneo Plains Regional Park - grassland orchid flush**

The spider orchids and donkey orchids on Boneo Plains do not get the attention of the Greens Bush wildflower walks, which means the tracks are quieter and the plants are less trampled. The grassland here is a different habitat entirely - open, low, dry underfoot - and in the second week of November the density of flowering ground orchids across the paddock margins is as good as it gets anywhere on the Peninsula. Early morning is better: the light is lower, the wind is down, and the heath off the southern edge of the reserve adds a second layer of colour. Closed shoes are non-negotiable; the grass is tussocky and uneven.

Access via Boneo Road near Rosebud West. No entry fee, no booking. Allow ninety minutes for a thorough circuit. Bring water - there are no facilities on site.

Pair the walk with lunch afterwards at St Andrews Beach Brewery in Fingal, twenty minutes south.

**Mt Eliza Farmers Market - running Sundays until 22 November**

The Mt Eliza Farmers Market is in its final weeks of the spring run, closing 22 November. It operates Sunday mornings, 9am to 1pm, at the Mt Eliza Village Precinct, and it is a genuinely local-stall market: stone fruit from the Langwarrin growers, early-season stonefruit jams, Peninsula olive oil, and a rotating set of small producers that shifts week to week. The crowd thins noticeably after 11am but the best stalls sell out earlier - the mushroom grower from Tyabb is usually gone by 10:15. This is not a destination food court; it is a working market worth arriving early for.

Mt Eliza Village Precinct, Mount Eliza. Sundays 9am–1pm, now through 22 November 2026. No booking required. Allow forty-five minutes to an hour.

Pair the market with a coffee at one of the Main Street cafés immediately adjacent to the precinct.

## FAQ

When is Red Hill Brewery open and do I need to book?

Red Hill Brewery is open for lunch on weekends and select weekdays at 88 Shoreham Road, Red Hill South. Bookings are recommended for groups; check redhillbrewery.com.au for current trading hours.

Where exactly is the Boneo Plains grassland and when is the orchid bloom at its best?

Boneo Plains Regional Park is accessible via Boneo Road near Rosebud West. The spider and donkey orchid flush typically peaks in mid-to-late spring - early-to-mid November is a reliable window. No booking required; wear closed shoes and stay on the marked tracks.

When does the Mt Eliza Farmers Market run and where is it?

The Mt Eliza Farmers Market runs Sundays 9am–1pm at Mt Eliza Village Precinct through 22 November 2026. No booking required; arrive before 11am for the best produce selection.

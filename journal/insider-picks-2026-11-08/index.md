# Portsea Hotel fish and chips, the Bushranger Bay snorkel trail, and live music at Moonah Links

The Portsea Hotel's front-beach deck before the summer crowds, the Bushranger Bay underwater trail at peak spring visibility, and acoustic sets running every Saturday at Peppers Moonah Links through December.

**EAT - Portsea Hotel, Portsea**

The Portsea Hotel has one of the best pub views in Victoria, and right now - before the summer school-holiday machine arrives - you can actually get a table on the front-beach deck without planning a week in advance. The view across Port Phillip toward the Heads is the same in November as it is in January. The crowd isn’t.

Order the fish and chips. It is what this pub does, it is what the setting calls for, and it has been consistent for long enough that recommending anything else feels like a distraction. Eat outside. The water is close enough that you can hear it. The ferries pass. That is the whole point.

November is the gap between quiet autumn and peak summer. It closes fast. This weekend is a better bet than the one after it.

Portsea Hotel, 3746 Point Nepean Road, Portsea. Allow 1.5–2 hours. Book ahead via the hotel’s website, particularly for weekend lunch on the deck. Pairs well with a walk out to London Bridge or the Portsea back beach before you sit down.

**EXPERIENCE - Bushranger Bay snorkel trail, Cape Schanck**

Spring is the best window for snorkelling at Bushranger Bay. Water temperatures have come off their winter low, visibility in the shallows runs to four or five metres on a calm day, and the kelp forest along the southern reef is growing fast. By February the water is warmer but murkier; by July it’s clear but cold enough to shorten the session.

The snorkel trail follows the rocky reef at the southern end of the bay. Leafy seadragons have been sighted along here regularly - not guaranteed, but the habitat is right and spring is when they’re most active. The access track from the Bushrangers Bay car park off Browns Road is about twenty minutes each way, mostly flat, with a short scramble at the end.

Bring your own gear. There is no hire nearby. Go on a morning with low swell from the south - check the Bureau forecast before you leave. Allow two to three hours for the full return walk and time in the water. Parks Victoria’s website has current track and conditions notes. No booking required. Pairs well with a coffee stop at Flinders on the way home.

**DISCOVERY - Acoustic Saturdays at Peppers Moonah Links, Fingal**

This series has been running at the Spike Bar since June and it closes out on 26 December. The format is straightforward: local and regional acoustic acts, the bar open, the golf course rolling out behind the windows as the light drops. It is not a festival and it is not trying to be.

What makes it worth noting now specifically is the spring timing. The deck at Moonah Links faces west over the course. November sunsets from that deck - the light across the fairways going gold around 7:30 pm - are the reason to come this month rather than waiting until the Christmas run. The music is the excuse. The light is the draw.

Sessions run every Saturday, 5:00 pm to 8:00 pm, through to 26 December 2026. No booking listed for general entry but check the Moonah Links website for the current artist and any ticketed nights. Peppers Moonah Links, 55 Peter Thomson Drive, Fingal. Allow the full three hours. Pairs well with dinner afterward at a Sorrento restaurant on the way back down the Peninsula.

## FAQ

When is the best time to visit the Portsea Hotel to avoid summer crowds?

October and November are the sweet spot - the weather is warm enough for the deck but the summer crowds haven't arrived. The Portsea Hotel is at 3746 Point Nepean Road, Portsea. Check the hotel's website for current trading hours and table bookings.

Where is the Bushranger Bay snorkel trail and what do I need to bring?

Bushranger Bay is accessed via a track from the Bushrangers Bay car park off Browns Road, Cape Schanck. The snorkel trail runs along the rocky reef at the southern end of the bay. Bring your own mask and fins - there is no hire on site. The walk in takes about 20 minutes each way. Check conditions before you go; Parks Victoria has current water and track information.

What time do the Acoustic Saturdays sessions run at Peppers Moonah Links?

Acoustic Saturdays run every Saturday from 5:00 pm to 8:00 pm at the Spike Bar, Peppers Moonah Links, 55 Peter Thomson Drive, Fingal. The series continues until 26 December 2026. Check the Moonah Links website for the current artist lineup.

# Commonfolk Coffee, Cape Schanck Boardwalk, and Wine and Cheese Night at Hastings

Commonfolk's roastery brunch earns a Mornington morning, the Cape Schanck boardwalk is at its coastal heath peak, and Wine Lovers Warehouse runs a Friday-night pour in Hastings.

**EAT - Commonfolk Coffee, Mornington**

Commonfolk roasts its own beans on-site, and that alone separates it from most Peninsula cafes. The courtyard is dog-friendly and open in spring, which matters when October delivers the kind of morning that makes sitting outside the obvious choice. Order from the brunch menu - the kitchen runs a focused seasonal card, not a sprawling laminated one - and pair it with whatever single-origin filter they are currently pouring. The retail room carries bags to take home, and the Brew Room is worth five minutes if you are particular about equipment. This is a Mornington local’s default, not a destination cafe marketed at weekenders, and the difference is felt. It is busy on weekend mornings; arriving before 9 am on Saturday or Sunday removes most of the wait. Barkly Street, Mornington. Allow 45 minutes. No booking for walk-ins; check their site for current hours.

Pair this morning with a browse through Mornington’s main strip before the weekend crowds arrive.

**WALK - Cape Schanck Boardwalk, Cape Schanck**

The boardwalk from Cape Schanck lighthouse to Bushrangers Bay is a 30-to-40-minute walk each way, and late October is the correct time to do it. The coastal heath on either side - banksias, tea-tree, native pigface running across the dune faces - is in active flower right now. By February it will be scorched and grey. The track descends from the lighthouse carpark, crosses the headland, and drops toward the rocky bay platform at the end. It is exposed and the surface is uneven in sections; wear proper footwear. Go in the morning before the southerly fills in. The lighthouse itself is run by Parks Victoria and is worth a quick look before you descend. Cape Schanck Road, Cape Schanck. Entry to the park requires a parks pass; check the Parks Victoria website. Allow two hours return.

The Balnarring Bakehouse is a straightforward detour on the way back up the Peninsula if you want something to eat after.

**DISCOVERY - Wine and Cheese Night, Wine Lovers Warehouse, Hastings**

Wine Lovers Warehouse is a Hastings bottle shop that occasionally runs structured tasting nights, and this Friday’s wine-and-cheese format is the kind of low-key local event that does not require a special occasion to justify going. It runs Friday 30 October, 6:00 pm to 8:00 pm - two hours, standing format, producer or importer pours alongside matched cheeses. Hastings is the less-visited end of the Peninsula and the crowd at these events is genuinely local, not tourist-adjacent. Bookings are essential and the Eventbrite listing suggests it will sell out; the link is in the footer. This is not a masterclass and it is not a degustaton dinner. It is a Friday-night drink with a bit of structure around it, which is sometimes exactly the right thing. Wine Lovers Warehouse, Hastings. Book ahead via Eventbrite.

If you are making a night of it, Hastings foreshore is a short walk for a post-event stretch along the Western Port waterfront.

## FAQ

Where is Commonfolk Coffee and when does it open?

Commonfolk Coffee is on Barkly Street, Mornington. It opens early for breakfast and brunch daily - check their website or socials for current hours before you go.

When is the Wine and Cheese Night at Wine Lovers Warehouse Hastings?

Friday 30 October 2026, 6:00 pm–8:00 pm, at Wine Lovers Warehouse, Hastings. Book via Eventbrite: eventbrite.com.au/e/wine-lovers-warehouse-wine-and-cheese-night-hastings-tickets-2002007929610

What is the Cape Schanck boardwalk like in spring and how long does it take?

The Cape Schanck Boardwalk Trail runs from the lighthouse carpark to Bushrangers Bay, roughly 30–40 minutes one way. In October and November the coastal heath - particularly the tea-tree and banksias - is in flower. Go in the morning before the sea breeze picks up.

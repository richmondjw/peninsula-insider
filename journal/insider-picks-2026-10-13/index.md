# Somers General on a Spring Sunday, the Bushrangers Bay return walk, and National Works on Paper at MPRG

A Western Port brunch stop with Peninsula pantry goods, a return walk from Cape Schanck to Bushrangers Bay, and a national paper art survey open until 22 November at Mornington Peninsula Regional Gallery.

## EAT - Somers General, Somers

Somers General runs a weekend brunch menu that has no business being this considered in a village this quiet - and that is precisely why it is worth the drive to The Boulevard on a Sunday morning. The general store format means you eat well and leave carrying something: the Peninsula wine shelf stocks producers you won’t find in a Dan Murphy’s, and the pantry section is genuinely curated rather than decorative. Spring is the right time because the verandah opens up, the village road is still calm before the summer crowds arrive from Christmas, and the kitchen runs through the brunch menu at full stretch on a Saturday or Sunday. Order whatever the eggs dish is - it changes - and stay long enough to browse the store shelves before you drive out. Pair the visit with a run up to Balnarring Bakehouse, ten minutes north, for something to take home.

**Somers General, 2 The Boulevard, Somers. Allow 45–60 minutes. Bookings are available through the operator website; check current availability.**

## EXPERIENCE - Bushrangers Bay return walk, Cape Schanck

Start at the eastern Cape Schanck car park for the [Bushrangers Bay Track](<https://www.parks.vic.gov.au/places-to-see/parks/mornington-peninsula-national-park/things-to-do/Bushrangers-Bay-Track>). Walk to the bay and return along the same track: 5 km in total, with steep stairs and narrow, sandy, unsealed paths. Pet dogs are prohibited; [assistance-dog entry requirements](<https://www.parks.vic.gov.au/get-into-nature/all-abilities-access/assistance-dogs>) are separate. Bring drinking water and check the park manager’s live conditions before travelling. The beach is unpatrolled and swimming is not recommended. Pair the walk with a stop at Flinders Sourdough for a Beach Bun while stocks last.

**Bushrangers Bay Track, eastern Cape Schanck car park, Cape Schanck. 5 km return along the same track. Steep stairs; pet dogs prohibited.**

## DISCOVERY - National Works on Paper 2026, Mornington Peninsula Regional Gallery

The National Works on Paper survey - MPRG’s annually contested open prize exhibition - is currently on the gallery’s main walls and runs until Sunday 22 November 2026. This is the show where you can calibrate exactly where Australian practice on paper sits right now: drawing, printmaking, collage, mixed media, all under one prize rubric, all wall-ready. The selection this year spans artists you’ll recognise from interstate prize circuits alongside several you won’t have seen before. It rewards slow looking - give it an hour rather than twenty minutes. The gallery is also running two paper-focused workshops on Saturday 17 October: Jacky Cheng’s Tessellation Folding Workshop (9:30am–12:30pm, bookings essential via Eventbrite) and the free Orizome paper-dyeing session (1:30pm–3:30pm, bookings via Eventbrite). Both sit neatly alongside the exhibition. Pair the gallery visit with a walk down Mornington’s main street for coffee before or after.

**Mornington Peninsula Regional Gallery, Civic Reserve, Dunns Road, Mornington. Open daily 11am–4pm. Free entry. Exhibition closes 22 November 2026.**

## FAQ

Where is Somers General and when is it open?

Somers General is at 2 The Boulevard, Somers. It operates as a cafe and general store - check their Instagram or website for current trading hours before you go, as weekend hours can shift in spring.

Where does the Bushrangers Bay trail start and how long is it?

Start at the eastern Cape Schanck car park. Bushrangers Bay Track is 5 km return along the same track, with steep stairs and narrow, sandy, unsealed paths. Pet dogs are prohibited.

When does National Works on Paper 2026 close at Mornington Peninsula Regional Gallery?

The exhibition runs until Sunday 22 November 2026. The gallery is at Civic Reserve, Dunns Road, Mornington, open daily 11am–4pm. Entry is free.

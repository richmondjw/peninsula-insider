# Lindenderry's garden walk, the NWOP's final weekend, and live music at Fingal

Red Hill estate gardens at their spring peak, the National Works on Paper closing Sunday at Mornington Peninsula Regional Gallery, and acoustic sets at Peppers Moonah Links in Fingal.

**EAT / DRINK - Lindenderry at Red Hill, Red Hill**

Spring is the one time the 34-acre estate at Lindenderry earns its keep as a reason to show up before or after a meal. The gardens come into their own between October and December, with wisteria finishing and roses opening along the paths between vineyard rows. The dining room sources close - expect asparagus and new-season peas on the specials board in November - and the weekend cellar door pours estate reds and whites without the summer crowds that arrive after Christmas. This is the right fortnight to book a table, walk the grounds before dinner, and stay the night rather than rushing back up the hill. The kitchen is not trying to be a destination restaurant in the Doot Doot Doot mould; it’s a confident country dining room that suits a long Saturday lunch. Reserve the dining room and a room directly with the property; both fill on spring weekends. Book ahead.

Pair with a Saturday morning at the Emu Plains Market in Balnarring before heading up to Red Hill.

**EXPERIENCE / WALK - Arthurs Seat State Park, Arthurs Seat**

The spur trails off the main Arthurs Seat ridge are at their clearest in mid-November, after the worst of the spring growth has softened the scrub without yet closing the sight lines. The walk from Sexton’s Hill picnic area down through the Enchanted Adventure Garden boundary and back via the Stringer’s Creek track takes roughly 90 minutes and passes two stretches of native bush where spider orchids are still showing this week. Go before 10am to get the ridge views before sea haze builds. The track surface is dry and firm after the last run of rain drained through. Carry water - there’s none on the lower loop. The Arthurs Seat Eagle gondola gives you an easy descent option if legs are done; check its current operating hours before you go. No booking, free access. Allow two hours door to door including the top lookout.

Pair with a late lunch at Lindenderry, ten minutes down Arthurs Seat Road.

**DISCOVERY - National Works on Paper 2026, Mornington Peninsula Regional Gallery**

The National Works on Paper exhibition at Mornington Peninsula Regional Gallery closes this Sunday, 22 November. This is a touring national survey - 51 finalists selected from open entry - and the gallery is showing the full prize collection through its last weekend. The works range from large-format ink drawings to delicate gouache pieces, and the prize itself carries enough weight that the selection is genuinely competitive rather than regional-survey polite. On Saturday 14 November at 11:30am, one of the curators walks the floor for an hour and talks through how the selection was made and what the judges were looking for. That talk is worth the trip alone if you have any interest in how prize exhibitions actually work. Book the floor talk at mprg.mornpen.vic.gov.au. General viewing is free, 11am–4pm daily through Sunday. The gallery is at Civic Reserve, Mornington.

Pair with a coffee on Main Street before the 11:30am floor talk.

**BONUS - Acoustic Saturdays, Peppers Moonah Links, Fingal**

If the weekend needs a third stop, the Acoustic Saturdays series at Peppers Moonah Links runs every Saturday 5–8pm through 26 December. The Spike Bar at Moonah Links has an easy-going setup - golf resort bar with live acoustic acts, no cover charge, no need to book a tee time. It’s the right size for a drink and a listen without committing to a full dinner. Check moonahlinks.com.au for the current performer.

## FAQ

What are Lindenderry at Red Hill's dining and cellar door hours?

Lindenderry at Red Hill is on Arthurs Seat Road, Red Hill. The cellar door operates on weekends; the dining room serves guests and visitors. Confirm current hours and reserve directly with the property before travelling.

When does the National Works on Paper exhibition close at Mornington Peninsula Regional Gallery?

The National Works on Paper 2026 closes Sunday 22 November. The gallery is at Civic Reserve, Mornington. Open daily 11am–4pm. Entry is free; no booking required for general viewing. The curator floor talk is Saturday 14 November, 11:30am–12:30pm and requires a booking at mprg.mornpen.vic.gov.au.

What time does Acoustic Saturdays run at Peppers Moonah Links?

Acoustic Saturdays at Peppers Moonah Links, Fingal, runs every Saturday 5pm–8pm through to 26 December 2026. No booking required for the music; check the website for dining reservations.

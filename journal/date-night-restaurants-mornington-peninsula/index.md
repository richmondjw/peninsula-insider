# Date Night on the Mornington Peninsula: Where to Book for Two

A sunset drink, a room worth dressing for and no long drive home. The Peninsula's best dinners for two, from a Sri Lankan wine bar on the ridge to a set menu in Flinders, and how to build the evening around them.

## The short version

- A guide to date-night and dinner-for-two restaurants on the Mornington Peninsula, with notes on which nights each serves dinner.

- Picks: Many Little (Sri Lankan, Red Hill South), Moke Dining (weekly set menu, Flinders), Bistro Elba (Southern French, Sorrento), Mr Vincenzo's (Mornington), Laura at Pt. Leo (Saturday dinner), Doot Doot Doot at Jackalope, The Baths (Sorrento) and The Rocks (Mornington) for the view.

- Editorial argument: the Peninsula is lunch-led, so a great date night needs a dinner-open restaurant, a west-facing sunset drink and a plan for getting home.

The Mornington Peninsula is a lunch region. Most of its famous dining rooms are built around the middle of the day, with the vines and the bay in full light, and many of them are closed by dinner time.

That makes date night here a slightly different art. It is not hard to have a wonderful evening for two on the Peninsula, but it does not happen by accident. You need a restaurant that is actually serving dinner on the night you want, a drink somewhere west-facing while the sun goes down, and a plan for the drive home. Get those three right and the Peninsula has some of the most memorable evenings in Victoria.

## The original choice: Many Little, Red Hill South

From the team behind Polperro, Many Little is the most distinctive room on the ridge: a European-style wine bar with a menu built on Sri Lankan cooking. Curry leaf, tamarind, coconut and spice, plated with the care of a modern dining room, and a wine list chosen to stand up to it. There is nothing else like it on the Peninsula.

Dinner runs Thursday to Sunday from 5pm, with lunch on weekends. There is a five-course chef’s tasting menu alongside an à la carte list. On a date, take the tasting menu and let the room lead.

**Why it works for two:** it is lively without being loud, and the food gives you something to talk about all night.

## The serious one: Moke Dining, Flinders

A compact, family-run room on Cook Street where the chef cooks a set menu that changes weekly, sometimes daily, around what the Peninsula is producing. The cooking blends European technique with brighter, Asian-inflected flavours. The chef comes out to talk to the room, which tells you the scale and the spirit of the place.

Dinner is Thursday to Saturday from 6pm, with lunch on Saturday and Sunday. Book well ahead.

**Why it works for two:** a small room, one menu and no decisions to make. Pair it with a walk on the Flinders foreshore before dinner.

## The bistro night: Bistro Elba, Sorrento

Southern French cooking on Ocean Beach Road, with lunch and dinner listed seven days and set and prix fixe options alongside the à la carte. The useful detail for a date: the restaurant sets a 90-minute window for small groups on earlier reservations and suggests booking after 7.30pm if you want more time. Take the later table.

**Why it works for two:** it is in the middle of Sorrento, so a sunset drink beforehand and a walk afterwards need no car.

## The town option: Mr Vincenzo’s, Mornington

A restaurant and wine bar on the Esplanade, just off Main Street, with views towards the park and the bay. Seasonal shared dishes, handmade pasta and house focaccia. It is mainly a dinner restaurant, with Sunday lunch; check the current days when you book.

**Why it works for two:** the easiest serious dinner for anyone coming from Melbourne, and close to Mornington’s harbour for the sunset.

## For the big occasion

Some nights call for more than a good dinner.

**Laura at Pt. Leo, Merricks.** The estate’s fine-dining room, for up to 40 guests, with four-course and eight-course menus. Dinner is on Saturday from 6pm; lunch runs Friday to Monday. Reservations are required on weekends. This is the anniversary booking. Our [three dinners comparison](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/journal/three-italian-dinners/>) weighs it against Tedesca and Ten Minutes by Tractor.

**Doot Doot Doot at Jackalope, Merricks North.** Jackalope’s signature multi-course dining room, one of the most dramatic spaces on the Peninsula. Book it with a night at the hotel and the whole evening takes care of itself. Check current service dates and dietary arrangements with Jackalope before booking.

## For the view

If the evening is about the water, sit on it.

**The Baths, Sorrento.** On the sand of the Sorrento foreshore with Port Phillip Bay at your feet. Seafood, oysters and one of the most photographed dining positions on the Peninsula. Book for sunset in the warmer months, and check hours, which are reduced outside peak season.

**The Rocks, Mornington.** At the end of Mornington pier, with bay views on almost every side and a terrace that catches the evening. Seafood-led and classical. Book a window table timed for the last of the light.

Our [waterfront restaurants guide](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/journal/waterfront-restaurants-mornington-peninsula/>) covers both in more detail.

## Build the evening, not just the booking

**Start west.** The bay side gets the sunset. The Continental Sorrento has a Sunset Terrace for drinks with a view; The Baths and The Rocks are on the water themselves. In summer the sun sets after 8.30pm, so a 7pm drink and an 8.30pm dinner works; in winter, flip it. [The Sunset Hour](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/journal/the-sunset-hour/>) has more places to stand.

**Check the night.** The most common date-night mistake on the Peninsula is choosing a restaurant that does not serve dinner that evening. Many rooms are closed early in the week. Thursday to Saturday is the safe window.

**Solve the drive.** Country roads, dark hills and a bottle of wine do not mix. Stay where you eat, or near it. Sorrento and Mornington are walkable towns; Jackalope is a hotel, so dinner and bed are on the same property. Otherwise, arrange a taxi or transfer before you go out, because they are scarce on busy nights.

## Which one?

- **First date or early in the relationship:** Many Little.

- **A food-focused couple:** Moke Dining.

- **A Sorrento weekend:** Bistro Elba after a sunset drink.

- **Anniversary:** Laura on a Saturday, or Doot Doot Doot with a room at Jackalope.

- **Sunset on the water:** The Baths or The Rocks.

## FAQ

What is the most romantic restaurant on the Mornington Peninsula?

For a special-occasion dinner, Laura at Pt. Leo Estate (Saturday dinner) and Doot Doot Doot at Jackalope are the Peninsula's most dramatic rooms. For an intimate, original evening, Many Little in Red Hill South and Moke Dining in Flinders are our picks. For a view, The Baths at Sorrento and The Rocks at Mornington sit right on the bay.

Which Mornington Peninsula restaurants are open for dinner?

Many Peninsula restaurants are lunch-led, so check before you plan an evening. Many Little serves dinner Thursday to Sunday; Moke Dining Thursday to Saturday; Laura at Pt. Leo on Saturday; Mr Vincenzo's in Mornington is mainly a dinner restaurant; Bistro Elba in Sorrento lists lunch and dinner seven days. Confirm current days when booking.

Where can we have a sunset drink before dinner on the Peninsula?

The bay side faces west. The Continental Sorrento's Sunset Terrace, The Baths on the Sorrento foreshore and The Rocks at the end of Mornington pier all catch the evening light. See our Sunset Hour guide for more.

How do we avoid driving after dinner on the Peninsula?

Stay where you eat or close to it. Jackalope pairs a hotel with Doot Doot Doot; in Sorrento, the hotels of The Continental precinct and the town's restaurants are within walking distance of each other. Otherwise book a local taxi or transfer before you arrive, because they are scarce on busy nights.

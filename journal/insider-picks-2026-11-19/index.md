# Tar Barrel paddles, the Peninsula VineHop loop, and the last day of NWOP 2026

A beer-and-spirits paddle at Tar Barrel Brewery in Mornington, the six-venue VineHop Festival across Mount Martha on Saturday, and the final hours of National Works on Paper at Mornington Peninsula Regional Gallery on Sunday.

**EAT / DRINK - Tar Barrel Brewery & Distillery, Mornington**

Spring has the taproom at 72 Watt Road working harder than any other time of year - the beer garden fills by midday and the wood-fired pizza oven runs hot from open. The paddle is the right move: Tar Barrel produces both beer and spirits on-site, so a mixed selection across the two gives you a proper read of what they’re doing right now. Ask staff which release is freshest - the range rotates and there’s usually something new on the board in November. This isn’t the loudest venue on the Peninsula, which is exactly why it works as a Friday-evening or Saturday-lunch stop without the bayside pub scrum. Plan an hour minimum; two if you’re eating. Walk-ins are fine for the taproom. Tar Barrel Brewery & Distillery, 72 Watt Road, Mornington.

Pair it with a walk along the Mornington cliff tops before or after.

**EXPERIENCE / OUTDOORS - Peninsula VineHop Festival, Mount Martha**

The VineHop format - a hop-on, hop-off route linking six separate venues - is better than a fixed festival ground because you move at your own pace through actual Peninsula properties. This year’s hub is at The Briars, Mount Martha, with five further venues on the loop. Saturday 21 November, 11am to 7pm. The smart play is to start at The Briars early, before the route gets crowded mid-afternoon, then work your way along as the day cools. November light on the Mount Martha ridgeline at 5pm is worth staying for. Book transport in advance - driving between venues is not the idea here, and parking near The Briars fills fast on event days. Tickets at the Humanitix link; bookings are essential and this event has sold out before. Festival Hub: The Briars, Mount Martha.

Pair it with a late lunch at any of the linked venues - most serve food on the day.

**DISCOVERY - National Works on Paper 2026, Mornington Peninsula Regional Gallery**

National Works on Paper closes Sunday 22 November at 4pm - that is the hard deadline. NWOP is a genuinely competitive acquisitive prize show: works in the exhibition are for sale and the gallery purchases from the finalists, so what you’re looking at is work that passed a serious national selection process, not a regional survey. The 2026 edition at Mornington Peninsula Regional Gallery (Dunns Road, Mornington) has been running since September; this is the last weekend to see it. Admission is free. Allow 45 minutes to an hour. The gallery sits inside Civic Reserve, so you can extend the morning with a walk through the reserve itself - the European elms along the reserve paths are in full leaf through November. No booking required. Open 11am–4pm Saturday and Sunday.

Pair it with coffee at Store Ten on Main Street, a ten-minute walk away.

## FAQ

When does the Peninsula VineHop Festival run, and how do I get tickets?

Peninsula VineHop Festival 2026 runs Saturday 21 November, 11am–7pm, with the festival hub at The Briars, Mount Martha. Tickets at events.humanitix.com/peninsula-vinehop-festival-2026/tickets. Book ahead - it has sold out in previous years.

When does the National Works on Paper exhibition close at Mornington Peninsula Regional Gallery?

National Works on Paper 2026 closes Sunday 22 November. The gallery is at Civic Reserve, Dunns Road, Mornington. Open 11am–4pm. Free entry.

Where is Tar Barrel Brewery and what should I order?

Tar Barrel Brewery & Distillery is at 72 Watt Road, Mornington. Open daily. Order a beer or spirit paddle from the current range and check the wood-fired pizza menu on arrival. No booking required for walk-ins to the taproom.

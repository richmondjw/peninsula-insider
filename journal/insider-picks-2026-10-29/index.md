# The Bay Hotel's vault room, Greens Bush in flower, and Wine and Cheese Night in Hastings

The Bay Hotel Mornington's private vault dining room earns a booking, Greens Bush trails are at peak wildflower density, and Wine Lovers Warehouse Hastings runs a guided cheese-and-glass night on Friday.

**EAT / The Bay Hotel Mornington**

The old Commonwealth Bank vault on Main Street has been converted into the most specific private dining room on the Peninsula. Exposed brick, a heavy original door, and a table for up to ten - it reads nothing like a standard gastropub booking, which is exactly the point. The kitchen runs a Peninsula-produce-led menu that changes with what is available, so the conversation to have when you book is which local wines and seasonal dishes the team is working with that week. Spring means the specials board moves fast: expect asparagus, new-season lamb, and lighter treatments than the winter slate. This is the pick for a group occasion that needs a room with actual character rather than a cordoned section of the main floor. Book ahead and ask them to build the food around current Peninsula produce. Allow two to three hours. The Bay Hotel is on Main Street, Mornington.

Pair it with a pre-dinner walk along Mornington Pier as the light drops.

**WALK / Greens Bush, Boneo Road**

Greens Bush is the largest remnant of old-growth woodland on the Peninsula, and late October is the best window to walk it. The understorey is at full density right now: ground orchids, guinea flowers, and spreading wattles that finished flowering give way to the first of the spring greenhoods along the shadier sections of the loop. The canopy stays cool even on warm days, which matters when you are walking in direct sun for the approach sections. The main loop from the Boneo Road trailhead takes around 90 minutes at a relaxed pace. Go in the morning before the day heats up. The trail surface is mostly firm earth with tree-root sections, so closed shoes are the right call. No entry fee, no booking. Greens Bush is managed by Parks Victoria, trailhead off Boneo Road near Rosebud. Allow two hours including the drive in and the car park walk.

Pair it with a post-walk lunch at the Rye Hotel’s deck, eight minutes north on Rye-Rosebud Road.

**DISCOVERY / Wine and Cheese Night, Wine Lovers Warehouse Hastings**

Wine Lovers Warehouse in Hastings runs a structured wine-and-cheese matching night on **Friday 30 October, 6:00 pm to 8:00 pm**. This is not a passive tasting: the format works through specific pairings with explanation, which is the thing that separates it from a standing-around-with-a-glass event. Hastings sits on the western edge of the Peninsula, easy to reach from Frankston or the Westernport side without the Nepean Highway weekend crawl. The format suits two people with an interest in why a washed-rind cheese and a cold-climate Chardonnay do or don’t work, rather than a large group looking for a party. Seats are limited - this sells before the weekend. Book via Eventbrite before Friday morning. Wine Lovers Warehouse Hastings, High Street, Hastings. The session runs two hours; no further plans needed for the evening.

Pair it with a Saturday morning at Emu Plains Market in Balnarring the following day, nine minutes south.

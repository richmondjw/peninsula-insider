# Mock Red Hill: the orchard farmgate and cider lounge

Fruit, juice and cider put the family orchard at the centre of this Red Hill stop.

Keep this for later

- By **The Peninsula Insider**

Food  Cider

A converted 1945 cool store houses Mock Red Hill’s farmgate shop and cider lounge. The family orchard produces fruit, juice and cider, giving this Red Hill stop a different focus from the surrounding cellar doors.

Choose it for an orchard-produce stop or a cider-lounge visit. VineHop is a separate, adults-only event.

1103 Mornington-Flinders Road, Red Hill. [Official farmgate and visitor information](<https://www.mockredhill.com.au/>).

## Peninsula Hot Springs: what it is

The key choice is between [Revitalise Bath House entry](<https://www.peninsulahotsprings.com/bathe/bath-house/revitalise>) and [Reset all-of-site bathing](<https://www.peninsulahotsprings.com/bathe/spa-dreaming-centre/reset-all-of-site-bathing>).

**Revitalise** is the starting point for the all-ages Bath House. The venue lists more than 50 bathing and wellness experiences. Towel, robe and locker hire is available subject to availability, so include any hire you need when comparing the total cost.

**Reset** adds the Spa Dreaming Centre and is for guests aged 16 and over. It includes towel, robe and locker use. The advertised 70-plus total refers to bathing experiences across the site, not 70 swimming pools. Treatments and private bathing have their own packages; read the inclusions rather than assuming they come with Reset.

An early or evening arrival can be worth comparing with daytime entry if your plans are flexible. Use the live booking calendar for eligible sessions and rates. No session choice guarantees empty pools.

## Alba Thermal Springs: what it is

Alba Thermal Springs, Fingal. Photo: Alba Thermal Springs.

Alba offers shared springs, [private springs experiences](<https://albathermalsprings.com.au/alba-experiences/hot-springs/private-springs-experience/>) and spa treatments. Start with general entry if shared bathing is what you want; check the separate private options if having a pool to yourselves is the reason for the trip.

The [Alba visitor FAQ](<https://albathermalsprings.com.au/faqs/>) confirms included towel, bathrobe and locker use. Under-16s need an adult in the general springs; private springs are 16+. Spa guests aged 16 to 18 need an accompanying adult in the treatment room. Phones, smart watches and laptops are prohibited in the springs, spa and restaurant.

Plan dining and any treatment around your booked arrival. A restaurant stop or private experience may have its own reservation time. Check current offers directly rather than building the day around a seasonal promotion from a previous visit.

## Before you book

- **Price the complete visit.** Compare the same day, group size and inclusions. Check the final total and public-holiday terms before paying.

- **Read cancellation terms.** Peninsula Hot Springs states a 24-hour cancellation and amendment window for bookings other than accommodation. [Alba’s standard window](<https://albathermalsprings.com.au/info/>) is 48 hours, with seven days for retreats and full-day spa packages and separate group terms. Late cancellation can cost the full booking.

- **Check access needs and maintenance.** Contact the venue about the specific pools and facilities you need before committing to travel.

- **Pack for your ticket.** Bring swimwear, suitable footwear and warm clothes for afterwards. Check whether you need your own towel or hire.

- **Check every add-on.** A bathing ticket, private spring, treatment and dining reservation may each have different timing and terms.

## FAQ

Should I book Peninsula Hot Springs or Alba Thermal Springs?

Start with the package: Peninsula Hot Springs Revitalise covers the all-ages Bath House; Reset adds the Spa Dreaming Centre for guests aged 16 and over. Alba includes towel, robe and locker use with springs entry, with private springs and spa experiences booked separately. Choose the access and inclusions that suit your group, then compare prices for the same date.

Are towels and robes included at Peninsula Hot Springs and Alba?

At Peninsula Hot Springs, Revitalise offers towel, robe and locker hire subject to availability; Reset includes their use. Alba includes a towel, bathrobe and locker in its pricing. Check the exact package before paying.

Is Alba Thermal Springs adults-only?

No. Under-16s may use the general springs with an adult. Private springs are for guests aged 16 and over. Spa guests aged 16 to 18 must be accompanied by an adult, with treatments in the same room.

Does Peninsula Hot Springs have 70 pools?

The venue advertises more than 70 bathing experiences across the Bath House and Spa Dreaming Centre, and more than 50 bathing and wellness experiences in the Bath House. Those are experience counts, not pool counts.

Which hot springs is cheaper?

Compare live quotes for the same date, arrival time and inclusions. Revitalise hire costs can change the total, while Reset and Alba include towel, robe and locker use. Early or evening offers and surcharges can also change the comparison.

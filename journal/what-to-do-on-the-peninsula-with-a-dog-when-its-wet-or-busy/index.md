# What to Do on the Peninsula With a Dog When It’s Wet or Busy

The Peninsula gets harder with a dog when the weather turns or the obvious places get packed. That is exactly when a good guide becomes useful. The answer is usually not to force the postcard version of the day. It is to reshape the day around space, shelter, and lower-friction stops.

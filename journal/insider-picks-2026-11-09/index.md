# St Andrews Beach Brewery, the Cape Schanck heathland, and the Mt Eliza Farmers Market

A Sunday roast in a converted racehorse stable, spring wildflowers at their densest on the Bushranger Bay heathland trail, and the last Saturday of the Mt Eliza market run.

**EAT / DRINK - St Andrews Beach Brewery, Fingal**

The Sunday roast at St Andrews Beach Brewery is the reason to rearrange your weekend. The brewery occupies former racehorse training stables on the ocean side of the Peninsula - timber beams, open lawns running out to what was the exercise track, and a kitchen that treats the roast as a proper event rather than a pub afterthought. Spring is exactly the right moment: the lawns are dry enough to claim a table outside, the afternoon light is long, and the core beer range - pale ale, lager, red ale, session sour - gives you something to work through while you wait. Order one of each on arrival. The red ale holds up particularly well against whatever comes off the roast. The building alone is worth the drive down Fingal Road.

Book ahead via the website. Allow two to three hours minimum; the lawn invites lingering. The brewery is at 65 Meakins Road, Fingal.

Pair the afternoon with a walk along the Rye ocean beach before or after - ten minutes’ drive west.

**EXPERIENCE / WALK - Cape Schanck Heathland Circuit, Cape Schanck**

The coastal heathland between Cape Schanck Lighthouse and Bushrangers Bay is at its peak density of wildflower cover through November. Ground orchids, sun orchids, and tea-tree in full white flower line both sides of the boardwalk before it drops toward the basalt platforms. The light at this end of the Peninsula is different from the bay side - harder, cleaner, and in spring the offshore wind is light enough that the platforms are walkable at low tide without getting pushed around. The circuit from the lighthouse carpark out to the Bushrangers Bay lookout and back takes about 90 minutes at an easy pace. Go before 10am to get the low-angle light on the rock platforms and to avoid the mid-morning coach groups that arrive at the lighthouse from late October onward.

Cape Schanck Lighthouse Road, Cape Schanck. Free to walk the heathland circuit; lighthouse entry is separate. Check tide times before you go.

After the walk, St Andrews Beach Brewery is 15 minutes north on Fingal Road - an easy pairing for the afternoon.

**DISCOVERY / MARKET - Mt Eliza Farmers Market, Mount Eliza**

The Mt Eliza Farmers Market finishes its current Sunday run on 22 November - that leaves two Sundays including this one. The market runs in the Mt Eliza Village Precinct from 9am to 1pm, and it draws growers and producers from across the northern Peninsula rather than the resellers that fill out some of the larger weekend markets. In spring the stone fruit and berry stalls are coming into early season, and the cut-flower growers are worth the trip alone. It is a smaller market than Emu Plains or Mornington Park, which is precisely what makes it manageable: one loop, genuine producers, done in an hour. Bring a bag. The coffee queue moves quickly.

Mt Eliza Village Precinct, Mount Eliza. Runs Sundays 9am–1pm; final date in this run is Sunday 22 November 2026. Check mtelizafarmersmarket.com.au to confirm.

The market pairs well with a walk down to Canadian Bay Reserve afterward - five minutes on foot from the precinct.

## FAQ

When does St Andrews Beach Brewery serve its Sunday roast, and do I need to book?

The Sunday roast runs on Sundays from open. Bookings are strongly recommended - check the website at standrewsbeachbrewery.com.au and book ahead, especially through spring and summer.

When does the Mt Eliza Farmers Market run, and where exactly is it held?

The market runs at Mt Eliza Village Precinct on Sundays 9am–1pm, with the current run ending 22 November 2026. Check mtelizafarmersmarket.com.au for the confirmed Sunday schedule.

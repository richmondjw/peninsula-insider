## Choose your ramp

Shire-managed ramps are currently free to use; confirm Warneet's conditions with its precinct manager. Tide, weather, maintenance and vessel draft all affect suitability. Heading for the Heads? Read [Before you launch](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/boating/#before-you-launch>) first.

## No boat? Hire one, or go with a skipper

Sorrento Long Pier, Mornington Peninsula   Photo · Robert Blackburn, courtesy of Visit Victoria

### Self-drive hire

[Mornington Boat Hire](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/boating/hire/mornington-boat-hire/>) runs tinnies and polycraft for the protected bay from Schnapper Point Drive, Mornington. The operator advertises this fleet as no-licence hire.

The operator limits the boats to its surveyed area within 2 nautical miles of Mornington Pier. They must not approach the Rip. Expect a cancellation if the operator judges the weather unsafe. Open year-round when the weather allows; book ahead in the summer holidays and on long weekends.

### Fish from a pier

No boat at all? Our [fishing spots guide](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/fishing/locations/>) covers piers and jetties, with tide notes for each and any current closure.

### Skippered fishing charters

The skipper handles the boat and finds the fish. These four are reviewed in our fishing guide.

- [Proline Charters](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/fishing/charters/proline-charters/>)

From Sorrento Pier since 1991, on two of the largest charter boats in Victoria.

## Before you launch

**Plan for current conditions.** Check official marine forecasts, warnings, navigation rules and local notices before departure. Do not use this guide as a substitute for current official safety information.

- ### Read the forecast twice

Check the [Bureau of Meteorology forecast for the Peninsula](<https://www.bom.gov.au/vic/forecasts/mornington-peninsula.shtml>), wind included, the night before and again on the morning you go.

- ### Use the right tide station

Use Rosebud Jetty for Sorrento and Rye, Mornington for Safety Beach and Mornington, and Western Port (Stony Point) for Hastings and Warneet. [Willyweather](<https://tides.willyweather.com.au/>) and the [Bureau](<https://www.bom.gov.au/australia/tides/>) both publish them.

- ### Know your second ramp

Pick an alternative before you leave home, in case the first is full or the tide turns against you.

 [Tides and safety in full](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/boating/tides-safety/>)

Conditions and access can change. [Report changed ramp or safety information](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/contact/?type=correction&page=%2Fboating%2F&message=Something%20has%20changed%20in%20the%20boating%20ramp%2C%20hire%20or%20safety%20guidance.>).

### Do not use a generic launch window

Check the BOM-listed station shown for the ramp, then confirm the current ramp condition and your vessel's limits. A tide prediction does not guarantee safe access, and maintenance can change conditions.

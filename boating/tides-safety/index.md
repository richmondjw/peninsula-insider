# Tides, weather, and safety on the Peninsula

Pre-trip planning for Port Phillip Bay and Western Port. The reference stations to use, the conditions to read, the alternatives to know about. The kind of session that does not feature in the news is the one that started with the right pre-trip check.

Last fact-verified Fri, 20 Nov 2026.

## Tidal range, by water body

Port Phillip Bay has a smaller tidal range than Western Port. Western Port's stronger tidal movement directly affects channel depth, ramp access, and fish behaviour. The two systems demand different pre-trip thinking; use the current BOM-listed station shown for the ramp rather than a legacy station name.

### Port Phillip Bay reference stations

- **Rosebud Jetty** for the southern bay ramps in this guide (Sorrento and Rye).

- **Mornington** for Mornington and Safety Beach.

### Western Port reference station

- **Western Port (Stony Point)** for Hastings and Warneet. BOM also lists Flinders Jetty, Tooradin, Cowes Jetty and San Remo; choose the station closest to your actual route.

Look up tides at [Willyweather](<https://tides.willyweather.com.au/>) or [the Bureau of Meteorology](<https://www.bom.gov.au/australia/tides/>). The night before and morning of every trip.

## The Rip at Port Phillip Heads

The Rip, the tidal passage between Point Nepean and Queenscliff, is one of Australia's most dangerous tidal passages for small vessels. South and south-westerly swell entering on a strong ebb tide creates standing waves that have killed experienced operators on adequate vessels. **Do not attempt to cross the Rip in unsuitable conditions regardless of vessel size or experience.** Boats departing Sorrento must stay well clear of the channel.

## Marine forecasts

Read the Bureau of Meteorology local waters forecast for [Port Phillip](<https://www.bom.gov.au/vic/forecasts/portphillip.shtml>) or [Western Port](<https://www.bom.gov.au/vic/forecasts/westernport.shtml>). It covers wind, weather and seas. Check it the night before and again on the morning of your trip because conditions can change quickly. Check [Safe Transport Victoria](<https://safetransport.vic.gov.au/on-the-water/>) for current safety notices and licensing requirements.

## Lifejackets and licensing

Carry a correctly fitted lifejacket for every person. On a powered vessel up to and including 4.8 metres, everyone in an open area must wear the specified lifejacket while underway. On powered vessels over 4.8 metres and under 12 metres, it must be worn in heightened-risk situations. Children under 12 must wear one in an open area whenever the vessel is underway. Port Phillip Heads, within three nautical miles of Point Lonsdale, is a designated hazardous area where everyone on vessels under 12 metres must wear one. Check the current [lifejacket rules](<https://safetransport.vic.gov.au/on-the-water/recreational-boating/wear-a-lifejacket/>) before departure. For hire-and-drive boats, a marine licence is required when the vessel is capable of more than 10 knots; the owner must brief hirers on the vessel's operating requirements. See [Safe Transport Victoria's licensing guidance](<https://safetransport.vic.gov.au/on-the-water/marine-licence-and-vessel-registration/licensing-and-registration/>).

## Return-to-ramp planning

Before you launch, identify your nearest alternative ramp in case conditions deteriorate. A boat that cannot safely return to its launch ramp needs an alternative. This is not hypothetical; it is a basic pre-trip planning step. Useful alternative pairings:

- [Safety Beach](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/boating/ramps/safety-beach-boat-ramp/>) → check [Mornington Park](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/boating/ramps/mornington-park-boat-ramp/>) or [Sorrento](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/boating/ramps/sorrento-boat-ramp/>).

- [Sorrento](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/boating/ramps/sorrento-boat-ramp/>) → check [Rye](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/boating/ramps/rye-boat-ramp/>).

- [Warneet](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/boating/ramps/warneet-boat-ramp/>) → check [Hastings](<https://peninsulainsider.com.au/boating/ramps/hastings-boat-ramp/>).

These are geographic pairings, not live suitability endorsements. Recheck tide, weather, maintenance and ramp access for both locations before departure.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do Port Phillip Bay and Western Port differ?

Port Phillip Bay has a smaller tidal range than Western Port. The practical effect is that current at Western Port ramps and channels can be substantially faster, and ramps like Warneet and Tooradin need more care around low water. Use the BOM-listed station shown for the ramp: Rosebud Jetty, Mornington, or Western Port (Stony Point).

### How do I check tides for the Peninsula?

Use BOM tidal predictions and select a station that appears in its current Victorian list: Rosebud Jetty for Sorrento and Rye, Mornington for Safety Beach and Mornington, Western Port (Stony Point) for Hastings and Warneet, or Flinders Jetty for Flinders. Confirm local ramp conditions before launching.

### What is the Rip and why does it matter?

The Rip is the tidal passage between Point Nepean and Queenscliff at the entrance of Port Phillip Bay. Strong tidal currents combined with south or south-westerly swell create dangerous standing waves. The Rip is one of Australia's most dangerous tidal passages for small vessels. Do not attempt to cross in unsuitable conditions regardless of vessel size or experience. Boats departing Sorrento Boat Ramp or operating in the southern bay must stay well clear of the channel.

### What pre-trip checks should I do before a session?

Tide forecast for the relevant reference station. Marine wind and swell forecast (BOM Marine Forecasts for Victoria). The location of your nearest alternative ramp in case conditions deteriorate. Vehicle check (fuel, lights, trailer secure). Vessel check (battery, fuel, safety equipment, EPIRB if offshore). Weather is checked the night before and again on the morning of the trip; conditions on Port Phillip Bay can change quickly with afternoon sea breezes from the north-west in summer and southerly change fronts in spring.

### What safety equipment must I carry?

Requirements depend on vessel size and operating area. Carry a correctly fitted lifejacket for every person and check Safe Transport Victoria before departure for the current carriage and wearing rules. Children under 12 must wear a lifejacket whenever a vessel is underway.

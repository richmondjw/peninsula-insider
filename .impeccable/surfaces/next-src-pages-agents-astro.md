# Agent welcome guide

## Scope and visitor mode

- Project: Peninsula Insider. Route: `/agents/`. Source: `next/src/pages/agents.astro`.
- Mode: Read. This is an ordinary extension of the incumbent Harbour system.
- Audience: agents and the people building them who use public Peninsula information to help someone plan a visit.
- Job: choose the appropriate public route or format, retain editorial context, interpret dates correctly, respect access permissions and report corrections.
- Primary action: follow a task-specific starting point; secondary actions open compact formats and trust guidance.
- Content: a welcoming opening, seven task rows, public-format links, date meanings, uncertainty, permissions and correction guidance.
- Constraints: preserve the shared shell and current DESIGN.md and design.json; no imagery, new identity, account-data access or expanded reuse rights. Keep this brief development-only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Welcome agents into the publication with useful routes and clear responsibilities, using a readable editorial guide.

OWN-WORLD: Preserve Harbour blue, white paper, Sora headings, Figtree body, restrained rules and underlined links from the incumbent system. No new visual identity or imagery.

STORY: Understand the welcome, choose one of seven tasks, select a public format, then retain context and interpret dates and permissions before using the information.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Shared masthead above a large left-aligned welcome heading, brief introductory copy and three section links. The task section follows directly, with names and explanations paired in rows.

FORM: Read-mode editorial guide with task rows, paired explanatory sections and a prose closing; columns stack on mobile. The form is fixed by the approved incumbent-extension direction. No form ordering or generated seed key was supplied; none is invented here.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

# Mornington Wednesday Market (Main Street Market)

Mornington's long-running artisan market fills Main Street every Wednesday, 9am to 3pm. Expect local makers, produce and food; severe weather can cancel the market or limit stalls.

First timer

Editor's verdict

Forty years of Wednesdays on Main Street. Not a tourist event, just the way Mornington shops midweek.

An artisan street market on Main Street each Wednesday, 9am to 3pm. The organiser may limit stalls in high wind or heat and cancel for a severe weather warning. Check its current notice before travelling.

The move: combine the market with a Main Street cafe and a foreshore walk.

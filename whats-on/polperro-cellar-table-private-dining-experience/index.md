# Polperro Cellar Table, Private Dining Experience

Polperro's private dining room below the winery. Six to eight guests, five courses, the latest Polperro and Even Keel cuvees poured by the people who made them.

Editor's verdict

The most exclusive table at Polperro and the room you book when an occasion has to land. Worth the spend.

Six to eight seats, one shared table in the cellar room beneath the winery. Sculptural lighting, five courses from the kitchen, and matched flights of the current Polperro and Even Keel cuvee releases. Available Saturday evenings, by arrangement.

This is the occasion-only Polperro: anniversaries, milestone birthdays, a small group who actually wants to talk about wine while they drink it. Not a date-night casual book. Book three to four weeks ahead minimum.

Make a weekend of it. Truffle hunt at Red Hill Truffles in the morning, lunch at Many Little, the Cellar Table that evening. Polperro has four villas, each sleeping two. Check accommodation availability at https://www.polperrowines.com.au/escape/ before relying on an overnight stay. Restaurant and cellar door are normally closed on Mondays and Tuesdays; confirm private-event arrangements directly.

# Oktoberfest at The Continental Sorrento

Conti Bar becomes a German beer hall from 15 to 22 October, with Weihenstephaner on tap and a Bavarian menu. Free entry.

All weather

Editor's verdict

Weihenstephaner and a Bavarian menu for a week, with no ticket at the door. Walk in, order a stein.

Conti Bar takes Bavarian styling for a week, with Weihenstephaner on tap and a German menu to match.

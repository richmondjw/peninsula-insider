Friday 13 November

  • National Works on Paper 2026 (NWOP)

    Start with the award-winning works, then take time with the wider survey of contemporary art on paper.

    11am · Mornington · Exhibitions · Recurring weekly

  • Books and Ideas: Barrie Cassidy at Port Phillip Estate

    Barrie Cassidy joins Wheeler Centre CEO Erin Vincent to discuss his career and Australian politics over a two-course dinner at Port Phillip Estate.

    6.30pm · Red Hill South · Writers & Ideas · One-day event

  • Live Music at Flinders Hotel

    Friday-night live music at Flinders Hotel, running from 7pm to 10pm. The venue welcomes families, friends and leashed dogs on the deck, with table bookings recommended when you want a guaranteed place.

    7pm · Flinders · Live Music · One-day event

  • Smooth Operators Live at Peninsula Beer Garden

    Local covers group Smooth Operators plays a three-hour Friday set at Peninsula Beer Garden, moving through jazz, blues, dance, rock and pop from the 1960s to current favourites. The venue says no booking is required for this performance.

    5pm · Boneo · Live Music · One-day event

  • Soul Night Market at Dromana Estate

    A Friday-evening market at Dromana Estate with more than 90 maker and food stalls, an outdoor food lawn and live soul-inspired music. Bring a picnic rug or folding chair if you want to settle on the grass between browsing stalls.

    5pm · Tuerong · Markets · One-day event

Saturday 14 November

  • Crib Point Community Market

    Indoor and outdoor community market at Crib Point Community House on the second Saturday of the month, 9am to 1pm. Expect produce, plants, food and a children's treasure hunt.

    9am · Crib Point · Markets · Recurring monthly

  • National Works on Paper 2026 (NWOP)

    Start with the award-winning works, then take time with the wider survey of contemporary art on paper.

    11am · Mornington · Exhibitions · Recurring weekly

  • Acoustic Saturdays at Peppers Moonah Links

    Saturday acoustic music at Spike Bar, from 5pm to 8pm. Make it an evening stop after a day around Fingal or Cape Schanck.

    5pm · Fingal · Live Music · Recurring weekly

  • Beginner Alteration and Repair Sewing Class with Pete Sews

    A three-hour beginner class for adults and teens who want to alter, repair and personalise their own clothes. Pete Sews keeps group classes to six people and supplies the workshop machines and equipment.

    1pm · Capel Sound · Arts · One-day event

  • Curators floor talk: The Making of a Prize Exhibition

    A useful way into the National Works on Paper exhibition if you want to understand the judging decisions, with the curator and panel member leading the conversation.

    11.30am · Mornington · Arts · One-day event

  • Jamie MacDowell Live at Jetty Road Brewery

    Each weekend at Jetty Road Brewery you can enjoy brews and bites, accompanied by local artists playing cool tunes. Full menu available from 12pm, Jamie McDowell will be live from 7pm.

    6pm · Dromana · Live Music · One-day event

  • Jesse Lewis Live at Peppers Moonah Links Resort

    Jesse Lewis plays an acoustic Saturday session on the Spike Bar terrace at Moonah Links from 5pm to 8pm. The organiser says no booking is required for this sunset set.

    5pm · Fingal · Live Music · One-day event

  • REwind Lane Live at Tar Barrel

    Melbourne acoustic duo Rewind Lane plays a two-hour Saturday afternoon set at Tar Barrel, built around close harmonies and nostalgic, feel-good covers.

    1pm · Mornington · Live Music · One-day event

  • Spring Winery Walk at Red Hill Recreation Reserve

    A guided 10-kilometre route through Red Hill vineyards and private tracks, with five winery stops pairing new-release wines with small snacks. The organiser describes steep sections and paddock walking, so moderate fitness and proper walking shoes are required.

    8am · Red Hill South · Food & Wine · One-day event

  • The Calmer Miles Live at Peninsula Beer Garden

    Mornington Peninsula acoustic duo The Calmer Miles plays a three-hour Saturday set at Peninsula Beer Garden, mixing original songs with folk, pop and rock covers. The venue operates on a walk-in basis.

    5pm · Boneo · Live Music · One-day event

Sunday 15 November

  • National Works on Paper 2026 (NWOP)

    Start with the award-winning works, then take time with the wider survey of contemporary art on paper.

    11am · Mornington · Exhibitions · Recurring weekly

  • Bennsong Live at Peninsula Beer Garden

    Enjoy live music at Peninsula Beer Garden! Bennsong (Benn Manie) is a versatile entertainer who has been involved in the music industry for many years.

    12pm · Boneo · Live Music · One-day event

  • Festive Wreath Making at Heronswood House and Garden

    A two-hour workshop with botanical designer Domenica Vavala, using fresh and dried locally sourced foliage to make a sustainable festive wreath to take home.

    10.30am · Dromana · Arts · One-day event

  • Jimmy & Niki Live at Jetty Road Brewery

    Each weekend at Jetty Road Brewery you can enjoy brews and bites, accompanied by local artists playing cool tunes. Full menu available from 12pm, Jimmy & Niki will be live from 1pm.

    1pm · Dromana · Live Music · One-day event

  • Magnum Bonum at Polperro Winery

    A casual Sunday long-table session on the Polperro lawn, pairing large-format wines with generous shared dishes. It is a walk-in event running from midday to 3pm.

    12pm · Red Hill · Food & Wine · One-day event

  • Michael Charles Live at The Pig & Whistle Tavern

    Chicago Blues Hall of Fame inductee Michael Charles brings his Where To Next? tour to the Pig & Whistle's intimate Shed Theatre, supported by Dylan Walker. Doors open at 2.30pm.

    Main Ridge · Live Music · One-day event

  • Mr Morpheus Live at Tar Barrel

    Mr Morpheus plays Tar Barrel's Sunday live-music session from 1.30pm to 4.30pm at the Mornington brewery and distillery.

    1.30pm · Mornington · Live Music · One-day event

  • Sourdough Masterclass at Peninsula Gourmet Cooking School

    Get Your Sourdough Perfect Every Time Your class will take place in our brand new, purpose built facility in Dromana on the gorgeous Mornington Peninsula.

    11am · Dromana · Food & Wine · One-day event

  • Sunday sessions at Peninsula Hot Springs Eco Lodges

    Sunday sessions at Bath House Café, complimentary with bathing. Starts 12pm.

    Fingal · Wellness · One-day event

  • The Peninsula Retreat at Alba Thermal Springs & Spa

    Embrace the natural beauty of the Peninsula and the healing powers of water and nature with Alba’s two day Peninsula Retreat. The retreat includes a guided coastal hike, hot springs bathing across two days, wellness activities and all meals.

    Fingal · Food & Wine · On during your dates · runs to Mon, 16 Nov

Markets

  • Crib Point Community Market

    Indoor and outdoor community market at Crib Point Community House on the second Saturday of the month, 9am to 1pm. Expect produce, plants, food and a children's treasure hunt.

    9am · Crib Point · Markets

  • Mt Eliza Farmers Market

    A VFMA-accredited village market with local growers and specialty makers, supported by the Mt Eliza Chamber of Commerce.

    9am · Mount Eliza · Markets

  • Emu Plains Market

    Emu Plains is the artisan market that actually feels like the Peninsula: stringybarks, music, makers you'd buy from twice.

    9am · Balnarring · Markets

  • Mornington Wednesday Market (Main Street Market)

    Forty years of Wednesdays on Main Street. Not a tourist event, just the way Mornington shops midweek.

    9am · Mornington · Markets

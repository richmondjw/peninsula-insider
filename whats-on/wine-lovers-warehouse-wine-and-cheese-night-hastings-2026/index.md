# Wine and Cheese Night at Wine Lovers Warehouse Hastings

Wine Lovers Warehouse and Peninsula Cheese host a wine and cheese evening in Hastings on Friday 30 October, 6–8pm, with live music and food. This is an adults-only, ticketed event.

[Book or check details](<https://www.eventbrite.com.au/e/wine-lovers-warehouse-wine-and-cheese-night-hastings-tickets-2002007929610>)

Editor's verdict

A relaxed Friday wine-and-cheese tasting in Hastings with local cheesemakers and live music. Check the current ticket terms before you go.

The organiser advertises wine tastings from Wine Lovers Warehouse, cheeses from Peninsula Cheese, live music and food including wood-fired pizza and a Rotary Club barbecue. This is described as a social evening rather than a seated tasting.

The event runs from 6pm to 8pm on Friday 30 October at 2080 Frankston-Flinders Road, Hastings, and is for adults aged 18 and over. Tickets are paid. Check the event page for current admission and availability before travelling, and arrange transport if you plan to taste wine.

# Mt Eliza Farmers’ Market Twilight Edition

The Mt Eliza Farmers’ Market moves to Friday afternoon and evening for its December edition, with local growers and specialty food stalls on the Village Green and Mt Eliza Way service road.

[Check latest details](<https://www.mtelizafarmersmarket.com.au/>)

Editor's verdict

An afternoon and evening market for local produce and specialty food, not the usual Sunday morning session.

The December edition of the Mt Eliza Farmers’ Market is on Friday 18 December 2026, from 3pm to 8pm. This is the twilight edition, not another Sunday morning market. The market organiser publishes those hours on its own calendar.

Look for the market on the Village Green and along the Mt Eliza Way service road, near the corner of Canadian Bay Road. The market’s usual mix includes local produce and specialty food suppliers, although the organiser does not list which stalls will attend this edition. The organiser suggests parking behind the Mt Eliza Way shops or at the back of Mt Eliza Square.

The market is outdoors. Check the organiser’s website again before you leave if the weather looks uncertain. If you need step-free access or accessible parking, check with the organiser first, as the market’s location page does not give event-specific access details.

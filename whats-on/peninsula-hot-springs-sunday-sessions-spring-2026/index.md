# Sunday Sessions at Peninsula Hot Springs

Live music every Sunday from midday at Peninsula Hot Springs, played to the pools and included with bathing. Brad Kennedy on 20 September, The Calmer Miles on 27 September.

All weather

Editor's verdict

Book the bathe and the music comes with it. The easiest good Sunday on this end of the Peninsula.

Live music runs from midday every Sunday, played to the pools rather than ticketed separately. Entry is by bathing session, so book a bathing time and the music comes with it.

The line-up rolls week to week. Brad Kennedy plays 20 September and The Calmer Miles play 27 September; the October performers are not yet listed, so check the venue's events page for the current Sunday before you go.

It pairs an afternoon soak with a set, which makes it an easy plan for the spring school-holiday Sundays and the weeks after.

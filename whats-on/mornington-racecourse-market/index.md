# Mornington Racecourse Market

A makers market at Mornington Racecourse on Sunday 11 October, 9am to 2pm. Browse handmade, homegrown and Australian-designed goods, street food and live music. Entry is free; paid parking applies and dogs are not permitted.

Editor's verdict

A useful Sunday stop for makers, food and music, with room to extend the trip into Mornington. Check the exact market date before leaving because racing can change the usual cadence.

What you will find: Craft Markets Australia describes a market for handmade, homemade, homegrown and Australian-designed goods. Food vendors serve takeaway meals, and the Shire lists live music on the lawn. The mix of individual stalls can change, so treat those categories as the market's brief rather than a promise that a particular maker will attend.

Plan for Sunday 11 October 2026: The Shire lists the market from 9am to 2pm at Mornington Racecourse, 320 Racecourse Road. Entry is free. Paid parking applies, with a small surcharge if you pay electronically. Check the official event page for current charges. Dogs are not permitted in the market area. No booking is listed as required.

Before you go: This is an outdoor event. Check the organiser's current notice if the weather is uncertain, and ask Craft Markets Australia directly about accessible parking or routes if you need them. The usual second-Sunday rhythm can move because of racing; a recurrence rule is not confirmation of a future date.

Make a day of it: You can continue into Mornington after the market for Main Street dining or a foreshore walk. Those are independent stops, not part of the market programme. Event details were checked against the Mornington Peninsula Shire listing on 6 October 2026.

# Peninsula Hot Springs Yoga (Complimentary)

Free 25-minute outdoor yoga by the amphitheatre pools, included with bathing admission. Daily at 9:15am, all ages, no booking.

First timer

Editor's verdict

Free yoga before a hot springs bathe is the move locals know about and tourists don't book.

A 25-minute outdoor yoga session in the amphitheatre pools area, included with your bathing admission, no booking required. Runs daily at 9:15am.

Who it's for: anyone already booked in for a morning bathe who wants a soft start. Show up to the amphitheatre five minutes before, mat provided. Outdoor and exposed, so dress warm in winter, you'll bathe immediately after. This is the value-add nobody mentions on the PHS website, but it is the simplest way to make a standard bathing session feel like a proper morning out.

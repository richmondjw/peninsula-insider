# Pub Carols at The Pig & Whistle

A group carols night with a live band and two-course meal at The Pig & Whistle in Main Ridge. The organiser lists Thursday 3, 10 and 17 December, each from 6pm to 10pm.

[Book or check details](<https://events.humanitix.com/pub-carols>)

Editor's verdict

A participatory carols evening for groups rather than a background music dinner. Choose a Thursday and check Humanitix for current ticket terms.

The Pig & Whistle's Pub Carols nights combine a two-course meal with a live band. The organiser says the band teaches each table part of a song before bringing the room together for a final performance. It is presented as a group or end-of-year party evening.

Humanitix lists Thursday 3, 10 and 17 December, 6–10pm, at 365 Purves Road, Main Ridge. Select the correct date on the booking page and check the current price, remaining places and group arrangements before travelling.

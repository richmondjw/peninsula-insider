# Peninsula Hot Springs, Sound Healing Sessions

Monthly sound healing immersion at Peninsula Hot Springs through autumn and winter. Thermal bathing paired with a guided sound session. Adults, bookings essential.

Editor's verdict

Peninsula Hot Springs sound healing isn't a gimmick, it's a genuine 90-minute reset, and the venue is the right one for it.

Monthly sound bath sessions at Peninsula Hot Springs through autumn and winter, run alongside thermal bathing. The combination is the point: warm water first, sound second, the rest of the day looking different.

Book well ahead, sessions are small and PHS regulars treat the calendar like a release schedule. The exact dates need checking month by month on the events page, the rhythm is roughly monthly through May to September.

Go on a wet weekday if you can. The bathhouse is at its best when the crowd is thin and the steam is rising off the cold pools.

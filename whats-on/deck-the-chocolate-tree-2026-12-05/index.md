# Deck the Chocolate Tree

Decorate a chocolate Christmas tree to take home in Flinders on 5–6 or 12–24 December. Drop in between 9am and 4pm; participation is subject to availability.

[Book or check details](<https://www.mpchoc.com.au/events/deck-the-chocolate-tree/>)

This is a drop-in chocolate-decorating activity at the Mornington Peninsula Chocolaterie in Flinders. You decorate a milk chocolate Christmas tree and take it home in a gift box. It suits a stop for an activity rather than a booked workshop with an allocated session.

The operator lists two date blocks: 5–6 December and 12–24 December 2026, from 9am to 4pm. There are no listed sessions on 7–11 December. Booking is not required, but participation depends on availability. Check the official page for the current price and any changes before travelling.

# Foxeys Hangout Vegetable Feast (Morning Sun Vineyard)

Chef Bernard McCarthy's all-vegetable, multi-course feast at Morning Sun Vineyard in Main Ridge, matched to Foxeys wines. Ticketed, shared table, outdoors at a working vineyard.

Editor's verdict

A ticketed vegetable-led feast with matched Foxeys wines at a working vineyard. The value is genuinely silly.

Bernard McCarthy cooks a multi-course vegetable menu (think pumpkin risotto grown at the Kentucky Road block) at Morning Sun Vineyard in Main Ridge, with Foxeys pouring the matches. Shared table, outdoor, intimate. The recurring series doesn't publish dates far in advance, you have to follow @foxeyshangout on Instagram to catch the next announcement.

Best for couples and small groups of food-and-wine people. Vegetarians especially. Bring a warm layer between April and September, the vineyard sits exposed.

Line it up with a morning Terroir Masterclass at Ten Minutes by Tractor (ten minutes away) or an afternoon at Merricks General Store and you have a full Main Ridge day.

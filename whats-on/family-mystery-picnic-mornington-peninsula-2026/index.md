# Family Mystery Picnic on the Mornington Peninsula

Self-guided family scavenger journey: solve smartphone clues, collect picnic goodies from local providers, hit activity stops, and feast at a scenic picnic location. Runs Thursday to Sunday weekly.

Editor's verdict

Makes a proper day of it for families in a way that a single activity doesn't. The scavenger structure keeps kids engaged across the whole route.

AmazingCo runs this self-guided experience for families. You get digital clues on your smartphone, solve them to find the next stop, collect picnic food from local providers along the way, hit activity stops, and finish at a scenic picnic location.

Date-specific availability is shown on the live booking calendar. Allow two to four hours and book ahead so the picnic food is ready at the collection points.

The picnic is mostly outdoors, so check the forecast and dress for the current season.

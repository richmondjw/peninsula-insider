# National Works on Paper 2026 (NWOP)

National Works on Paper 2026 is MPRG's survey of contemporary Australian art made on and with paper. Visit 5 September to 22 November, Tuesday to Sunday, 11am to 4pm; the gallery is closed Mondays.

Editor's verdict

Start with the award-winning works, then take time with the wider survey of contemporary art on paper.

National Works on Paper 2026 is Mornington Peninsula Regional Gallery's survey of contemporary Australian art made on and with paper. The gallery names Adam Lee as winner of the major acquisitive award for You are the wind, I am the song, and Emma Bingham as Emerging Artist Award winner for Holding pattern. Their works are useful starting points before you explore the wider exhibition.

The exhibition runs from 5 September to 22 November 2026 at Civic Reserve, Dunns Road, Mornington. Visit during the gallery's regular Tuesday-to-Sunday hours, 11am to 4pm. The gallery is closed on Mondays. These are opening days within an exhibition run, not a daily appointment from the first date to the last.

Use the official exhibition page to check for a change to normal hours before travelling. The published pages used for this listing do not establish current admission terms or individual accessibility arrangements, so confirm those with the gallery if they matter to your visit. This is an indoor option when the Peninsula weather turns, with time to look closely rather than rush through a single performance.

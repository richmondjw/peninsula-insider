# Curators floor talk: The Making of a Prize Exhibition

Walk through the National Works on Paper exhibition with MPRG curator and judging-panel member Rebecca Owens, who will discuss how works were selected for the prize.

[Book or check details](<https://mprg.mornpen.vic.gov.au/Events/Curators-floor-talk-The-Making-of-a-Prize-Exhibition>)

Editor's verdict

A useful way into the National Works on Paper exhibition if you want to understand the judging decisions, with the curator and panel member leading the conversation.

Rebecca Owens, MPRG curator and a member of the National Works on Paper judging panel, leads a one-hour floor talk through the exhibition. The gallery says she will discuss the selection process and works that stood out to the judges.

The talk is listed for Saturday 14 November, 11:30am–12:30pm at the Mornington Peninsula Regional Gallery. MPRG lists it as free and recommends booking. Check the gallery's event page for the current registration link before travelling.

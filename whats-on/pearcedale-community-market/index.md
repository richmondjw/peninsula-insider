# Pearcedale Community Market

Monthly third-Saturday community market under the eucalypts at Pearcedale: fresh bread, honey, jam, craft, plants, coffee. Dogs on lead welcome.

Editor's verdict

A genuinely local Saturday morning, not a destination market. That's the whole point.

Pearcedale Community Market runs the third Saturday of every month at the community centre, under the gum trees, year-round. The mix is what you'd hope for: a baker, a honey guy, jam, potted plants, craft, coffee, a few food vans.

It's not Red Hill and doesn't try to be. The pace is gentle, parking is fine, dogs are welcome on lead, and an 8am arrival means warm bread before the locals beat you to it.

Pair it with Moonlit Sanctuary at Pearcedale or a loop out to Coolart Wetlands. The right call for a slow Saturday that doesn't require an itinerary.

# Sound Circle: Full Moon Sound Journey at Peninsula Hot Springs

Monthly full-moon sound journey in the PHS Wellness Studio followed by an afternoon in the hot springs. All-inclusive ticket covers the sound bath plus tea plus bathing from midday. Hosted by Lucy Ennis (Sound Circle).

Editor's verdict

Not woo, just 90 quiet minutes that genuinely reset the week.

A 90-minute guided sound journey in the PHS Wellness Studio, run on full-moon dates by Lucy Ennis of Sound Circle. The ticket bundles the sound bath, herbal tea and superfood balls afterwards, and hot springs access from midday until close.

Who it's for: anyone who already likes meditation, breathwork or floats, and wants a deeper version with thermal bathing built in. Show up at 9:45am for the 10am start, bring layers (you lie down on the floor), and stay all afternoon in the pools.

Dates rotate with the lunar calendar, so check Eventbrite for the next session, they sell out a week or two ahead.

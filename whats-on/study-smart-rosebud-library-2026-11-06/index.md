Editor's verdict

A practical local space for students who want to set aside time for their own work.

Rosebud Library lists this Study Smart session for VCE and tertiary students on Friday 6 November, from 4pm to 5.45pm. The programme offers a dedicated space to concentrate on assignments and exam preparation alongside other students.

The library says students can bring snacks and make use of its resources and staff support. It also describes study tips and subject-specific study sessions as part of the wider programme. Do not assume a particular subject workshop or tutor will be present at this dated session unless the library confirms it.

The session is free and no bookings are required. The venue is Rosebud Library on McDowell Street. Bring the materials you need for your own work and check the official listing before travelling. Ask the library directly about accessibility or any support you need. Other programme dates appear on the source page; this entry covers only Friday 6 November.

# Hastings Thursday Street Market

Weekly outdoor street market on High Street Hastings every Thursday. 40+ stalls featuring homemade, homegrown and craft goods. Fresh produce, handmade goods, plants. Local food options available on the street.

Editor's verdict

Hastings does its market on a Thursday morning, which is exactly the kind of inconvenient timing that keeps it real.

Forty-plus stalls along High Street, every Thursday year-round, weather permitting. Homemade, homegrown, craft and produce, the locals' shop rather than a tourist destination. The Thursday timing is the whole point: this is for people who live here, and the price of entry is taking a weekday morning off.

Go if you're working from home in the Western Port half of the Peninsula and want a midweek excuse to walk. Combine with a Hastings foreshore stroll. Skip if you're chasing the weekend artisan-market experience: this isn't that.

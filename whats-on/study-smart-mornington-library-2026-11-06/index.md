# Study Smart at Mornington Library

VCE and tertiary students can use a dedicated study space at Mornington Library on Friday 6 November, from 4pm to 5.45pm, without booking ahead.

Editor's verdict

A practical local space for students who want to set aside time for their own work.

Mornington Library lists a Study Smart session for VCE and tertiary students on Friday 6 November, from 4pm to 5.45pm. It provides a library space for focused assignment work and exam preparation in the company of other students.

The operator says students may bring snacks and use library resources, with staff available to help find information. The wider programme includes study tips and subject-specific sessions. Check with the library if you are looking for a particular subject or type of assistance; this listing does not promise a named tutor or workshop.

The session is free and no booking is required. Mornington Library is on Vancouver Street in Mornington. Bring your own study materials and check the primary listing for changes before setting out. Contact the library about access requirements or specific support. Rosebud runs a separate session at another venue, so check the location carefully when making plans.

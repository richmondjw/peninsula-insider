# Tessellation Folding Workshop with Jacky Cheng

Learn paper-folding techniques with National Works on Paper artist Jacky Cheng in a three-hour workshop at Mornington Peninsula Regional Gallery. Bookings are essential.

Editor's verdict

Choose this for a structured morning making session with National Works on Paper artist Jacky Cheng. Allow time to explore the gallery afterwards.

Jacky Cheng leads a three-hour workshop on tessellation and paper-folding methods at Mornington Peninsula Regional Gallery on Saturday 17 October. The gallery says no previous experience is needed, materials and morning tea are included, and bookings are essential because places are limited.

The session runs from 9:30am to 12:30pm at Civic Reserve, Dunns Road, Mornington. The gallery is also showing National Works on Paper 2026, but admission to the exhibition is separate from the workshop listing. Check the organiser's booking page for current ticket terms and availability before travelling.

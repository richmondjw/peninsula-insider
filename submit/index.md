# The places we'd never find without you.

The Peninsula Insider editorial desk visits a lot of venues. We don't know everything. If you've got a hidden corner that doesn't get the coverage it deserves - a back-bay swimming hole, a producer's farm-gate stall, a local pub with a roast that's worth the drive - we'd like to hear about it. Send it through, and if it lands we'll publish it with your name.

How it works

- **You submit** the spot, the story, and your name.

- **We visit** if it's somewhere we haven't been. Otherwise we cross-reference with our notes.

- **We publish** the strongest submissions in the Local Secrets stream with your name as contributor.

- **We disclose** any commercial relationships transparently; reader submissions are never paid placements.

House rules

- One spot per submission. If you have three, send three.

- It must be on the Mornington Peninsula proper - Mount Martha to Portsea, plus Western Port fringe.

- If you own or work at the venue, say so. We won't publish it as a "secret" but we may cover it editorially.

- Photos are optional. If you submit one, you're confirming you took it or have permission to share it.

- No deceased-author plagiarism. Use your own words.

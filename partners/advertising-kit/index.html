About Peninsula Insider

A modern newsroom for the Peninsula.

Local first. Editor-led. Published every Thursday. Every claim verified. Every commercial relationship disclosed. Built for digital, not converted to it.

We cover the Peninsula across five pillars: Eat & Drink, Stay, Do, Plan, and the Insider's Journal. The Journal carries a weekly cover story, longform features, interviews with the chefs and winemakers shaping the region, and seasonal guides that go deeper than the destination-marketing copy currently on the internet for this place.

Two products carry the brand. The publication itself, at peninsulainsider.com.au. And The Insider's Letter, a weekly five-minute read sent every Thursday morning, written and signed by the editor.

The publication is founded and edited by James and Emma, with a structured editorial desk handling commissioning, research, drafting, fact-checking, copy, and scheduling.

Peninsula Insider II.The firewall
II

The firewall

Three rules hold the line.

Peninsula Insider takes commercial partnerships. We are open about that. The publication is funded in part by the businesses who appear on it. What we do not do is let those businesses influence the editorial.

i.

Curated commercial

Only Peninsula businesses appear on PI. No programmatic, no off-region brands, no Booking.com retargeting filling our sidebar. If a reader sees a partner story or a sponsor placement here, the venue is one we know.

ii.

Editor-led

James and Emma sign the editorial. They pick the cover. They never appear at advertiser-only events. The masthead is theirs. The rate card is the publisher's. The two do not meet.

iii.

Disclosed by default

Every paid item carries a Partner Content eyebrow. Every founding partner is publicly listed. Every conflict is declared in the article that touches it. Once a year we publish a one-page firewall audit, naming what we ran, what we declined, and where the line strained.

What we will not run

  • Programmatic display advertising
  • Off-region brands
  • Off-tone advertisers, including any business whose offer would embarrass the publication or its readers
  • Sponsored editorial that contradicts our published view of the same venue or category
  • Any commercial item that fails our editor's reader-benefit test

We say no more often than we say yes. That is the point.

Peninsula Insider III.The newsroom
III

The newsroom

Built as a publication, not a blog.

PI is built as a structured publication, not a one-person blog.

Editorial commissioning runs through a desk model: research, drafting, fact-checking, copy, scheduling, editorial sign-off, each handled as separate functions with structured handoffs. Every factual claim in a feature is logged with a primary source, a confidence rating, and an expiry date before draft. Every cover passes editor sign-off before publish.

For a partner, that means the difference between commissioning a story in autumn and seeing it published in time for the season you wanted to capture, versus waiting until the season has passed.

Audience

Honest about scale. Pricing for permanence.

Peninsula Insider launched in early 2026 and is in its first year. We are honest about scale. The publication is small now and growing.

The investment thesis for a partnership at this stage is not based on impressions. Standards we hold

Every line of this is held.

No advertiser influences our editorial recommendations or rankings. The default test for any commercial item is reader benefit, with the burden on the commercial side to show it.

Every paid item is labelled. Every Founders' Circle partner is named publicly. Every conflict is declared in the article that touches it.
Annual audit
Once a year, we publish a one-page firewall audit naming what we ran, what we declined, and where the line strained. The first audit publishes November 2026.
No fake bylines
Every PI piece carries either a byline from James, a byline from Emma, the byline of a named human contributor, or the Peninsula Insider house byline. We do not invent writers.
No surprise pricing
Everything in this kit is the price you will pay. No hidden production fees, no rate-card-versus-actual gap.

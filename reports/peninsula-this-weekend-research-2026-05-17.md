## Editorial observations

### 1. The mushroom moment is the sharpest angle this weekend
Two separate mushroom foraging experiences on the same Saturday — The Kitchen's guided forage+lunch (11am, $85pp, max 15) and the independent Mush-Meander (9:30am, $30, Humanitix). May is the Peninsula's peak forage season. The Kitchen's spots are likely near-capacity — this is the second-to-last Saturday of the series. If The Kitchen is sold out, the Mush-Meander becomes the active alternative. Worth cross-checking both.

### 2. MPRG closing pressure — penultimate weekend
New Wave 26 and Michael Vale exhibitions both close May 31. This is the second-to-last weekend to see them. The MPRG loop (gallery + Mornington Main Street lunch or coffee) is the cleanest all-weather option for both days. Free, no booking, curated, accessible. Under-pushed in the dispatch context given how consistently it performs.

### 3. Rocky Road Festival Sunday is the finale for ticketed sessions
May 24 is the last Sunday of the Rocky Road Festival tasting sessions at Flinders Chocolaterie ($28pp). The free general entry runs until May 31, but the structured tasting experience — sampling 8 flavours, making your own rocky road — only runs Sundays in May. Last chance.

### 4. PHS has live music both evenings
Saturday: Chloe Gill (from 7pm, complimentary with bathing). Sunday: Sunday Sessions series (2-5pm, complimentary with bathing). PHS has quietly built a strong live-music-in-water program through late autumn. Both are complimentary, which removes a separate ticket barrier. The Saturday evening framing is new and worth surfacing.

### 5. Market thinness creates a different kind of Saturday
The Peninsula's regular Saturday market circuit is dark this weekend — no Red Hill, no Boneo, no Emu Plains, no Pearcedale. The only Saturday market is Tootgarook (large, 250 stalls, community-run). This is a meaningful gap in the usual Peninsula Saturday shape. Visitors who come expecting a market-then-winery day will need to route differently. The dispatch should address this rather than ignore it.

### 6. The waiting game: truffle anticipation
Red Hill Truffles opens May 30 — one week away. Flinders opens in June. This weekend is the last one before the truffle season arrives. For editorial texture: the Peninsula is in the final days before its most winter-specific experience. Not actionable yet, but it gives the weekend a "last quiet breath before winter proper" quality.
